# Foundations

> **In one sentence:** Aion 2 measures your character with three separate
> numbers — item level, Combat Power and your account-wide collection progress —
> and almost every mistake a new player makes comes from confusing them.

## Why it matters

Every other chapter in this vault describes a system that spends resources and
gives power back. This chapter describes the yardsticks those systems are
measured against. Without them you cannot answer the two questions that decide
what you do each evening: *what content am I allowed to enter*, and *what
content will other players actually take me into*. Those are different
questions with different answers, and Aion 2 gives them different numbers.

The chapter also exists to correct a specific, expensive misreading. Korean
community guides written between the KR launch on 2025-11-19 and 2026-03-18
use the word "Combat Power" (전투력) to mean what the game calls item level.
Guides written after 2026-03-18 use the same word to mean the new official
Combat Power stat, which is roughly two orders of magnitude larger. A guide
that says "get to 2,000 Combat Power" and a guide that says "you need 500K
Combat Power" are both correct and are talking about completely different
things. If you read Korean or machine-translated sources — and for Aion 2 you
have to, because the best information is Korean — you need this distinction
before anything else.

## How it works

### The three-number model

| Number | Korean term | Typical range | What it is | Who checks it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Item level (Gear Score) | 아이템 레벨, 템렙 | **0 to 3,800 at Season 1** | A sum of your equipped gear's item levels plus every enchant, breakthrough and Daevanion point | The game, at dungeon entrances |
| Combat Power (CP) | 전투력 | about 46,000 to over 850,000 | A server-side weighted score over your actual stat sheet | Other players, in the group finder |
| Account-wide collection progress | 내실 | no single number | Pantheon, pets, traces, wardrobe, titles — permanent, shared, never re-earned | You, over months |

**Item level is a key, not a measure of strength.** It is close to a plain
addition: base item level of each equipped piece, plus one per enchant level,
plus one per breakthrough level, plus one per Daevanion crystal point spent on
the boards. That means a full set of high-level gear with terrible stat rolls
and empty sockets produces exactly the same item level as the same set rolled
perfectly. The game does not care, and will let both characters through the
door. See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]] for the arithmetic and the gate table.

**Combat Power is a measure of strength, not a key.** It was introduced in KR
on 2026-03-18 and is not used as an entry condition for any content. It reads
your finished stat sheet — attack, defense, damage amplification, HP, and so
on — and weights each stat. Because it reads the sheet rather than the gear, it
picks up everything item level ignores: manastone rolls, soul imprint options,
stigma levels, skill levels, pet understanding, titles, wings and Arcana. It is
the number party leaders ask for, because it is the number you cannot fake.

**Account-wide progress is the power you keep.** The Pantheon, your pet
collection and race understanding, Empyrean Lord's Traces, the wardrobe and
your titles are shared across characters on the same server, and none of them
are ever replaced by a later patch. Gear is a treadmill; these are not. A
character with modest gear and finished collection progress can hold its own
against much better-geared players, and Korean guides are emphatic that
neglecting this layer is the single biggest cause of two players at the same
level having wildly different real performance.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- Item level is the thing to push while you are locked out of content, because
  it is what unlocks content. Once you are inside the content you want to run,
  push Combat Power instead, because that is what determines whether you clear.
- Anything one-time and account-wide beats anything repeatable. Sealed dungeons,
  strongholds, Empyrean Lord's Traces, hidden cubes and Kibelisk registrations
  are all first-clear rewards. They are not going anywhere, but every hour you
  delay them is an hour you spent at lower power than you needed to be.
- Do not optimise Combat Power as a number. The CP formula is known to weight
  Accuracy and Critical Hit low relative to their real value, because it was
  tuned around endgame characters that already sit above the useful thresholds.
  Stripping Accuracy to raise CP is the classic post-2026-03-18 mistake.

## Costs and math

The only arithmetic in this chapter is the item level identity, which is worth
memorising because it makes gear decisions trivial:

```
Item level = Σ (base item level of each equipped piece)
           + Σ (enchant levels)
           + Σ (breakthrough levels)
           + (Daevanion crystal points spent)
```

Every +1 anywhere in that expression is exactly +1 item level. The in-game
number reads slightly higher than this because it also folds in manastones,
soul-bind sub-stats, pets and titles, which are not itemised per gear piece.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Reading "전투력" as Combat Power in an old Korean guide.** Any Korean guide
  with target numbers in the 1,000 to 2,900 range is talking about item level,
  whatever the machine translation says.
- **Chasing item level with badly rolled gear.** It gets you through the door
  into content you cannot contribute to. Party leaders screen on CP precisely
  to filter this out.
- **Treating the level cap as the goal.** Levelling to 45 takes roughly seven
  to ten hours and 45 to 50 another three to four. The game is what happens
  afterwards, and the resources you skipped on the way up are still owed.
- **Deferring the account-wide layer.** It is the slowest-moving and the only
  permanent layer, so it is the one that most rewards starting early.

## Global launch notes

The global release is on PC via Steam and PURPLE on **2026-10-05**, with five
days of early access from 2026-09-30 for Founder's Pack holders, and a Launch
Scale Test on 2026-09-17 and 18. NC announced this date at gamescom on
2026-08-25; some Steam storefronts still display a "September 2026" header,
which is out of date. The game is free-to-play with an optional monthly
membership that unlocks the player market and the Kinah/Quna exchange.

**Season 1 global launches at character level cap 45**, item-level gates 0 to
about 3,800, and without Heroic-grade equipment. The Korean service is now on
Chapter 1 with a level 50 cap, six stigma slots, Stat Arcana and four Sanctuary
raids — that is the live KR game, not the launch game. Every number in this
chapter is a KR/TW number; treat gate values as the shape of the curve rather
than as literal global values until launch-day data exists. See
[[Global Launch Differences]] and [[Season 1 System Availability]].
