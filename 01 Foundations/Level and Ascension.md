# Level and Ascension

> **In one sentence:** Character level is a short, cheap gate that unlocks
> systems rather than power, and the red Ascension gauge sitting above your
> quest log is the mechanism that stops you racing through it.

## Why it matters

Levelling in Aion 2 is not the game. It is the tutorial for the game, and it is
deliberately fast — roughly seven to ten focused hours from 1 to 45. What
levelling actually does is hand you systems on a schedule: wings at 5, Stigmas
and the Pantheon at 22, soul imprint at 32, the entire endgame and the Season 1
level cap at 45. The raise to 50 and the sixth stigma slot are Chapter 1 and
are out. Miss the quest that opens a system and the system stays closed.

The Ascension gauge matters more than the level number. It is the single most
common reason a new player believes the game is bugged: the main story simply
stops offering the next quest, usually around level 21, and there is no error
message explaining why. The gauge is the explanation, and the fix is to go do
the side content the gauge counts.

## How it works

### The level curve

The cap was 45 at the KR/TW launch on 2025-11-19 and was raised to 50 by
Chapter 1 "Land of Sand and Frost" on 2026-07-01. Community guides split the
curve into five bands, which is also how the aion2hub route guides are
organised:

| Band | Region (Elyos / Asmodian) | What opens | Rough time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 10 | Poeta, Verteron / Ishalgen, Altgard | Ascension, first sealed dungeons | about 1 hour |
| 10 to 25 | Cantas Valley / Altgard | Daevanion board (level 12), Stigmas (2nd Ascension) | 2 to 3 hours |
| 25 to 40 | Canyon, Aullaeu, Fire Temple (level 37) / equivalent | Team dungeons, soul imprint at 32 | 3 to 4 hours |
| 40 to 45 | Scarlet Forest and the final campaign | 4th Ascension, endgame | 1 to 2 hours |
| 45 to 50 | Eltnen / Morheim (Chapter 1) | Sixth stigma slot, upper-grade stigma shards | 3 to 4 hours |

Levels stop mattering after 45. From that point progression is driven entirely
by item level, and the level 45 to 50 band is best read as an item level and
stigma band that happens to also award levels. See
[[Item Level vs Combat Power]].

### The red Ascension gauge

The gauge (각성 게이지) sits above the quest log and fills as you complete side
content. It must read 100 percent before the next Ascension quest — and with it
the next segment of the main story — becomes available. Three activity types
fill it:

- **Regional missions / side quests** (지역 임무, the green and light-blue
  markers on the world map).
- **Sealed dungeons** (봉인 던전, also translated "hideouts"), which are
  instanced solo or small-group caves marked with a question mark on the
  minimap. Each is a one-time clear with no reset.
- **Strongholds** (주둔지, also translated "garrisons" or "bastions"), the
  open-world fort objectives. These are the only source of the Noble Belt
  Enhance Scrolls your belt needs, and each also hands over Lesser Manastones.

The gauge is not a soft suggestion. Documented stall points are around level
21 to 22, again at 32, and again at 37, where players following only the yellow
main story run out of quest. In every case the answer is the same: clear side
content until the gauge fills.

### The four Ascension quests

| Ascension | Level | Quest name | Grants |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | 5 (Elyos) / 6 (Asmodian) | "Fledgling Wings" / "Spread Your Wings", to Asahr in the Ascension Subspace | Daeva status, flight, Daeva's Lesser Wings |
| 2nd | 22 | 2nd Ascension quest | [[Stigmas]] (skill slots 4 to 8) and the [[Pantheon]], including your first statues; Daeva's Intermediate Wings |
| 3rd | 32 | 3rd Ascension quest | Daeva's Superior Wings; soul imprint becomes available at this level |
| 4th | 45 | Final episode chain | Daeva's Supreme Wings, the Ascension Bracelet, a Unique-grade class weapon, the title "Facing the Origin of Zikel" |

The wing rewards are concrete and worth knowing, because wings are a real gear
slot that feeds Combat Power. Values below are from an Inven community
compilation; the first figure group is the equipped effect and the second the
collection effect. See [[Wings Acquisition]].

| Wings | Equipped | Collection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daeva's Lesser Wings | HP +200 | Flight 2,000, HP +50 |
| Daeva's Intermediate Wings | Defense +200, HP +300 | Flight 500, Attack +10 |
| Daeva's Superior Wings | Defense +300, HP +400, MP +200 | Flight 500, Defense +100 |
| Daeva's Supreme Wings | Defense +400, HP +500, MP +250, Penetration +500 | Flight 500, Penetration +100, Accuracy +20 |

The 3rd Ascension level is the one figure in this table I would not bet money
on. English roadmap sources consistently say level 32, and soul imprint
unlocking at 32 supports that reading, but at least one Korean guide title
frames the band as "2nd Ascension to level 35", which could mean the 3rd
Ascension quest actually lands at 35. Treat 32 as confidence medium.

### XP sources, ranked

1. **Main story (yellow / "Hero") quests.** Six episodes at the KR launch, and
   the overwhelming majority of your experience. Quest XP scales up sharply
   after level 37; a single main quest can be worth one to two levels in the
   38 to 45 band. Completing the final main quest leaves you around level 44.5.
2. **Regional missions.** Secondary XP, but the primary source of Stigma Shard
   Points and Daevanion Points, and the thing that fills the Ascension gauge.
3. **Sealed dungeons and strongholds.** First-clear rewards include Kinah,
   Enhance Stones, Daevanion Crystals, wing materials and statue scraps. The
   first Cantas Valley hideout alone pays 15,000 Kinah, 500 Enhance Stones and
   2 Daevanion Crystals.
4. **Team dungeons from level 25** (Ancient Temple, Distorted Cave and
   similar), around 20 to 25 minutes per run for a 2 to 3 player group, and the
   high-XP Abyss Gate dungeon from level 38.
5. **Multipliers**: XP potions, rested-energy accumulation, server hot-time
   buffs and guild XP bonuses. These matter far more on an alt than on a main.
6. **Open-world mob grinding.** Explicitly not a meaningful XP source; use it
   only to top off the last fraction of a level.

## Progression path

1. **Levels 1 to 10.** Follow the main story only. Take the Ascension quest the
   moment it appears. At 10, clear the first sealed dungeons for your first
   Enhance Stones and Daevanion Crystals.
2. **Levels 10 to 22.** Main story plus enough regional missions to keep the
   gauge ahead of you. Open the Daevanion board at 12 and spend points on your
   main damage skills.
3. **Level 22.** Fill the gauge, take the 2nd Ascension. Equip a damage Stigma
   first — utility Stigmas are better later, when you have shards to spare.
   Start placing Pantheon collectibles as they drop; the Pantheon is
   account-relevant and never resets.
4. **Levels 22 to 40.** Expect stalls at 32 and 37. Clear sealed dungeons and
   strongholds as they unlock rather than banking them: the belt scrolls and
   Daevanion Crystals compound. Fire Temple around 37 is your first real story
   dungeon.
5. **Levels 40 to 45.** Finish the campaign. The last chain awards a
   guaranteed Unique-grade class weapon, the Ascension Bracelet and the Supreme
   Wings. Do not stop before the episode is fully complete — the final quests
   often require the previous region at 100 percent completion, which means
   more regional missions and sealed dungeons.
6. **Levels 45 to 50.** Cross into Eltnen or Morheim. Three to four hours. This
   band also delivers the new sealed dungeons, strongholds and Monoliths, and
   at 50 your sixth stigma slot.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- The Ascension gauge is never optional, so treat it as part of the main story
  rather than as side content you might skip. Front-load it: doing the regional
  missions in a region while you are already there costs far less travel than
  coming back for them at 45.
- The level 45 episode completion is the single highest-value action in the
  levelling phase. A free Unique weapon, the Ascension Bracelet and the best
  early wings in one chain.
- Skill specialisation slots open at skill levels 8, 12 and 16, with a further
  slot reported at 20. Sources disagree on whether the third slot is at 16 or
  20; either way the community convention is to stop main damage skills at 8 or
  12 during levelling rather than spreading points thin. Resets are free, so
  experiment. See [[Skills and Mastery Points]].
- Do not enchant green or common gear while levelling. The consensus is to
  start light enchanting (+4 to +6) only once you have a Heritage-grade (blue)
  weapon, and to save serious enchanting for Unique gear after 45.

## Costs and math

- 1 to 45: about 7 to 10 hours if you follow the story and clear only the side
  content the gauge demands. Vortex Gaming's beginner guide claims the cap is
  reachable "within 7 hours".
- 45 to 50: about 3 to 4 hours, per the KR summer showcase.
- Final main quest completion leaves you at roughly level 44.5; the remainder
  comes from any repeatable content.
- Stigma reset costs Kinah, but Chapter 1 made stigma reset permanently free.
- Item level after main story completion and the 4th Ascension, with no side
  content done, is about 1,000 to 1,100. With sealed dungeons, strongholds and
  Empyrean Lord's Traces completed it reaches about 1,400 with no dungeon
  farming at all. That 300 to 400 item level difference is the measurable cost
  of skipping the gauge content.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Assuming the story is bugged at level 21.** It is the gauge. Check it first.
- **Banking sealed dungeons and strongholds "for later".** They are one-time
  clears with no reset, so nothing is lost permanently, but you spend the whole
  levelling curve weaker than you needed to be and you will have to re-travel
  every region.
- **Skipping the level 45 episode to start farming dungeons.** The episode
  reward weapon is Unique grade and free; farming a comparable one is hours of
  Draupnir.
- **Levelling past 45 on an alt without a plan.** See the alt section below;
  most alts have a natural stopping point well short of your main.
- **Over-investing in the Clash Runes you get handed during levelling.**
  They add item level, but enchanting them carries a destruction chance from
  stage 2 upward, and every stone spent there is a stone not spent on a gear
  slot that cannot break.

## Alts and second characters

Alts are not optional at the top end of Aion 2, because the daily Kinah earn
cap is per character. Community figures put the cap at about 2 million Kinah
per character per day, so two or three active alts roughly triple your income.
Alts also each carry their own Expedition dungeon entries and their own
Odium Storehouse daily, which is the main pet-soul source.

What makes an alt fast:

- **Server-shared progress.** Pets, pet race understanding, the wardrobe and
  Empyrean Lord's Traces were changed to server-shared. Pet counts across
  characters are summed and race understanding uses the highest character's
  level, so an alt inherits your main's collection stats immediately. Special
  race pets remain per character.
- **Warehouse sharing.** Push high-enchant gear, doping consumables and
  crafting materials down from the main. A pre-supplied alt can be crafted to
  level 18 quickly and then push the main story with crafted gear.
- **Skipping cutscenes and dialogue**, plus XP potions, rested energy, hot-time
  buffs and guild bonuses.
- **Class choice.** Ranged classes are the community pick for alts — Ranger
  and Spiritmaster are named most often — because they clear field content with
  the least attention.

Common alt stopping points, in ascending order of commitment:

| Target | Why stop there |
| --- | --- |
| Level 37 | Fire Temple expedition entry; cheap gear and Kinah |
| Level 45, item level 1,000 | Abyss entry and conquest tickets start accruing; the standard alt |
| Item level about 2,000 | Vakron armour and Fire Temple farming; the alt becomes a materials source, and its bound Unique drops become soul-imprint tuning material for the main |

## Global launch notes

**The Season 1 level cap is 45.** That is the planning number for this vault:
four Ascensions, four stigma slots, **Azphel at 45 (Ariel missing in the Launch
Scale Test)**, and no Eltnen or
Morheim. The Korean raise to 50 on 2026-07-01, the sixth stigma slot, and the
Chapter 1 "growth support" packages are roadmap. The Ascension gauge mechanic
itself has been present since KR launch and is very unlikely to change.

Two KR quality-of-life changes are worth watching for, because their absence
would make levelling noticeably worse: free stigma resets, and the "growth
support" packages Chapter 1 gave new and returning players, one of which
bundles gear, tier 4 equipment, five stigmas at level 15 and pet race
understanding level 10.
