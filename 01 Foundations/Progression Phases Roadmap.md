# Progression Phases Roadmap

> **In one sentence:** Aion 2 is a sequence of seven phases in which the correct
> action changes completely, and the single skill that separates fast players
> from slow ones is knowing which systems to deliberately ignore right now.

## Why it matters

Aion 2 opens about twenty progression systems and gives you meaningful
resources for maybe four of them at a time. The systems do not announce when
they become worthwhile. Enchanting is available from level 10 and is a waste of
materials until level 45. Transcendence is technically enterable at item level
1,500 and is the best use of your time from 1,600 to 2,000, then stops being
the priority. Soul imprint unlocks at level 32 and should not be seriously used
until past item level 2,000.

This is why two players with identical playtime end up hundreds of thousands of
Combat Power apart. It is almost never that one of them worked harder. It is
that one of them spent Enhance Stones on gear that got replaced, Odd Energy on
Expeditions instead of Transcendence, and Kinah on Superior manastones five
times more expensive than the Intermediate ones that would have rolled better.

The organising principle behind every phase below is a hierarchy of
permanence:

1. **One-time content that never resets** — sealed dungeons, strongholds,
   Empyrean Lord's Traces, Monoliths, hidden cubes, Kibelisks. Do these first
   in any phase where they are available, because they pay once and forever.
2. **Account-wide or server-shared progress** — Pantheon, pets and race
   understanding, wardrobe, titles. Slow to build, never lost, benefits every
   character you will ever make.
3. **Gear tier replacements** — the largest single power jumps, and the reason
   not to over-invest in the tier you are about to leave.
4. **Enchants, breakthroughs and tuning within a tier** — the last thing you
   do, because it is the thing that gets thrown away.

Read that list twice. It explains every "invest" and "ignore" call below.

## How it works

The phase boundaries are item level, not character level, because after 45
character level does almost nothing. Each phase below states its goal, what to
spend on, what to leave alone, and the exit criterion that tells you to move on.

## Progression path

### Phase 0 — Levels 1 to 45

**Goal:** reach the cap and finish the main story episode. Seven to ten hours.

**Invest in:**

- **The main story, exclusively, as the default.** It carries the vast majority
  of your experience and it is what unlocks systems.
- **The red Ascension gauge**, via regional missions, sealed dungeons and
  strongholds, whenever the story stalls — expect stalls around level 21, 32
  and 37. This is not optional content; it is a hard gate.
- **Daevanion board points** on your two or three main damage skills. Every
  point is +1 item level permanently, and the board is the cheapest item level
  in the game.
- **Sealed dungeons and strongholds as they unlock**, not later. Strongholds
  are the only source of Noble Belt Enhance Scrolls, and each also hands over
  Lesser Manastones.
- **Pantheon placement.** Put collectibles in as they drop. The Pantheon is
  account-relevant and never resets.
- **The highest item level piece in every slot**, from quest rewards and
  vendors. Empty slots are the most expensive mistake in the game.

**Ignore:**

- **Enchanting anything below Heritage grade (blue).** Everything you are
  wearing gets replaced at 45. The community convention is to start light
  enchants (+4 to +6) only once a blue weapon drops.
- **Manastones and spirit stones.** You cannot craft Intermediate ones yet, and
  Lesser ones on gear you will discard is pure waste.
- **Arcana, soul imprint tuning, PvP, the wardrobe, crafting beyond the
  minimum, alts.** All either locked or worthless at this power level.
- **Clash Runes past a couple of light enchants.** They gain a destruction
  chance from enchant stage 2, and every stone burnt there is a stone not spent
  on a slot that cannot break.

**Reasoning:** nothing you own at level 30 survives the next gear tier at 45.
On global, **45 is the character cap**, so "level 50" in Korean guides is
Chapter 1 roadmap only. The only things
that survive Phase 0 are Daevanion points, Pantheon entries, Empyrean Lord's
Traces, unlocked systems and the map knowledge. Spend on those and on nothing
else.

**Exit:** level 45, main episode complete, holding the free Unique-grade class
weapon, the Ascension Bracelet and Daeva's Supreme Wings. Item level around
1,000 to 1,100 if you did only the story.

### Phase 1 — At level 45 (global cap), item level 1,000 to 1,400

**Goal:** sweep every remaining piece of one-time content. This phase requires
no dungeon farming at all and is worth roughly 300 to 400 item level. On
**global Season 1 you are already at the character level cap** when this phase
starts — there is no Eltnen/Morheim levelling band and no sixth stigma slot.

**Invest in:**

- **Every remaining regional mission** in every region, to 100 percent
  completion. Some later main quests require the previous region complete.
- **Every remaining sealed dungeon and stronghold.**
- **Empyrean Lord's Traces** (주신의 흔적), collected while you are already
  crossing each map. They pay Skill Points, Revelation Amulet enhancement
  materials and titles. They do not raise item level directly, which is exactly
  why people skip them and then regret it.
- **The Belt of Nobility and the Revelation Amulet.** Both are full gear slots.
  Push both to Unique grade at +0 minimum; the KR consensus is that these two
  are mandatory because you cannot trade for them.
- **Monolith resonance** in your faction's starting base.
- **Wings**, aiming for a flight value around 140 as an early target.
- **Alchemy crafting to level 20 and then 25**, which unlocks Intermediate
  manastones and Intermediate spirit stones. Use the free alchemy tables in
  towns rather than the Kinah-charging craft menu.

**Roadmap (Chapter 1, out at global launch):** Eltnen or Morheim 45→50, sixth
stigma slot — see [[Level and Ascension]].

**Ignore:**

- **High enchant levels.** Enhance Stones are scarce and your gear is about to
  be replaced by Conquest drops.
- **Superior manastones.** Intermediate stones roll a higher per-option value
  (13 against 10) and cost roughly a fifth as much to reach the same total.
- **Soul imprint option rerolling.** Do not start the expensive reroll grind
  until you are holding gear you intend to keep.
- **PvP gear, Arcana refinement, alts.**

**Reasoning:** this is the highest power-per-hour phase in the entire game and
it is the one most often skipped, because none of it looks like progression.
Korean guides are blunt about it: a character that did the foundation content
and one that did not are separated by an enormous margin at the same item
level.

**Exit:** item level about 1,400, all one-time content cleared, Intermediate
manastone and spirit stone crafting available.

### Phase 2 — Item level 1,400 to 1,700

**Goal:** replace your quest gear with Unique-grade dungeon and market gear.

**Invest in:**

- **Conquest difficulty Krao Cave and Draupnir.** Use cubes for the guaranteed
  rewards. Opening the Draupnir reward 14 times guarantees one Unique armour
  piece. Once you have taken the guaranteed reward, stop running it.
- **The market for the slots you did not fill.** Item level 55 Unique armour and
  accessories are cheap; Heritage-grade item level 59 pieces from Urugugu
  Canyon and Vakron's Sky Island run roughly 150,000 to 300,000 Kinah.
- **Even enchanting**, compromising at +10 to +12 on the weapon and +5 to +7 on
  armour, because Enhance Stones are the bottleneck.
- **Intermediate manastones on weapon and armour, Intermediate spirit stones on
  accessories.** Keep any high-grade option that rolls, even a useless one — it
  still counts toward Combat Power. The precision reroll grind comes later.
- **Shugo Festa** attendance, for the currency that buys Skill Points and
  Daevanion node points.

**Ignore:**

- **Fire Temple item level 67 gear off the market.** It works, but at 1.5 to 2
  million Kinah per piece it is a bad trade at this stage.
- **Ascension Trial on Easy.** Easy opens at item level 1,000 but Normal opens
  at 1,500 and is far more rewarding. Do not burn weekly attempts early if you
  are close to 1,500.
- **Chasing perfect manastone options.**

**Reasoning:** grade upgrades are the largest single item level jumps
available, and until your set is Unique-grade there is nothing worth enchanting
heavily or tuning at all.

**Exit:** item level 1,600 to 1,700. Expedition Conquest at 1,600 and
Transcendence become available.

### Phase 3 — Item level 1,600 to 2,000

**Goal:** Arcana. This is the fastest Combat Power growth in the game.

**Invest in:**

- **Transcendence dungeons, with essentially all of your Odd Energy.** This is
  the single most emphatic recommendation in the Korean guides. Deus Research
  Base and Shattered Arcanis; stages 1 to 2 drop Common Arcana and stage 3
  drops Rare (green). Target stage 3 S-rank repeatedly.
- **Filling all five Arcana slots before upgrading any of them**, to activate
  the set effects. A Common Arcana is +20 item level, a Rare +40, across five
  slots.
- **Arcana skill levels.** Arcana raise skill levels, which is how you reach
  skill level 16 and its specialisation slot, and later 20.
- **Abyss Points for Decurion accessories** — rings, earrings and necklace are
  the best value. Abyss Points are not a PvP-only currency: Corridors (a
  five-minute PvE mob-killing instance available when your faction holds an
  artifact) can pay over 5,000 each, plus urgent requests, mission quests,
  Shugo Festa and Abyss field mobs.
- **Nightmare Mode**, once tickets start accumulating. Attempts recharge two
  per day and cap at 14, so let none expire. Clear every stage in sequence each
  week for Daevanion points.
- **Ascension Trial Normal**, weekly, for Silentium and Daevanion materials.
- **Rift events and the Shugo Festa** as they come up.

**Ignore:**

- **Spending Odd Energy on Expeditions.** Even though Expedition Conquest opens
  at 1,600, Arcana return more power per Odd Energy point. Vakron and Urugugu
  wait for Phase 4.
- **Superior manastones and precise soul imprint work**, still.
- **Pushing past Transcendence stage 3 or 4** if it costs you clears. Unlock
  stage 4 and farm stage 3.

**Reasoning:** Arcana are a fresh gear category with five empty slots. Filling
empty slots always beats improving filled ones, and Arcana simultaneously give
item level, Empyrean Lord attributes, set effects and skill levels — four
returns from one currency.

**Exit:** item level 2,000, five Rare Arcana equipped with a set effect active,
Decurion accessories on.

### Phase 4 — Item level 2,000 to 2,400

**Goal:** a full Expedition armour set, and the beginning of real optimisation.

**Invest in:**

- **Vakron's Sky Island Conquest** for the Guarder and armour set. Prefer it
  over Urugugu Canyon: accessories have many acquisition routes and armour has
  very few, and Vakron also drops the Guarder.
- **Breakthroughs**, prioritising the offensive slots. Weapon, Guarder and
  accessories to 5 breakthroughs before armour; a Korean analysis recommends
  armour breakthroughs 1 to 2 only after offensive gear is at 3 or more.
- **Soul imprint tuning**, starting now and in this order: Decurion Guarder at
  2 to 3 breakthroughs first, then the crafted or dungeon weapon, then gloves
  and chest, then shoulders, boots and finally the ring. On weapons and belts,
  the recommended tuned options are Combat Speed, Weapon Damage Amplification
  and multi-hit accuracy.
- **Arcana refinement toward Unique grade**, starting with the Chalice.
  Mysterious Crystals come from Arcana extraction, the season shop and season
  missions; five crystals craft one Unique card.
- **Pet collection and race understanding.** Pets are server-shared and their
  soul costs were halved. Run the Odium Storehouse daily on every character.
- **Alts**, starting now. The daily Kinah cap is per character, and each alt
  carries its own Expedition entries and its own Odium Storehouse.

**Ignore:**

- **Over-breakthrough on armour**, specifically the Nua and Vakron sets, which
  are transitional.
- **Godstone imprinting**, which is destroyed on removal and belongs on gear
  you will keep.

**Reasoning:** this is the first phase where your gear is worth keeping, which
is the precondition for every optimisation system. Tuning, breakthroughs and
godstones are all wasted on transitional gear, and all mandatory on final gear.

**Exit:** item level 2,400. Krao Cave Hard, Fire Temple Conquest and Sanctuary
range.

### Phase 5 — Item level 2,400 to 3,800 (Season 1 ceiling)

**Goal:** Season 1 endgame gear, Heritage then Unique Arcana, and Sanctuary 1.
Stop at 3,800. There is no Phase 6 at launch.

**Invest in:**

- **Fire Temple Conquest** for the Enraged-series weapons, and **Ferocious Horn
  Den** to close remaining gear gaps.
- **Transcendence stage 6 and above** for Heritage-grade Arcana. Stage 6
  S-rank repeated is the recommended farm. Stages 9–10 sit around 2,800–2,900,
  still inside 3,800.
- **Unique belt crafting**, which is probabilistic and needs the Master's
  Shining Orichalcum Girdle.
- **Godstone imprinting** on weapon and belt, now that the gear is final.
- **Sanctuary 1 (Ludra / Rudra)** around item level 2,700. Eight players at
  launch. Sanctuary 2 at 3,500 is a later Korean season — inside the number
  3,800, not in the launch raid roster.
- **Wings restoration and wing skill levels.**
- **Titles**, chosen for their stats.
- **Wardrobe collection effects.**
- **Stigmas to 20 on all four slots** (300 shards). Not 25 — that is Chapter 1.

**Ignore:**

- Anything whose tooltip says 4,000, 4,500 or 4,800.
- Sanctuary 2/3/4, Castle of the Fallen Daeva, Abyssal Horn Den, Doom Trial,
  Stat Arcana, level 50.

**Reasoning:** past ~2,700 you are no longer unlocking new Season 1 doors. The
rest of the 0–3,800 bar is finishing systems. See
[[Item Level vs Combat Power]].

**Exit:** item level 3,800, Unique set complete, four stigmas at 20. That is
Season 1 done.

### Phase 6 — Roadmap only (after Season 1)

Not launch. Korean Chapter 1 raised the cap to 50 and **rescaled** the bar so
that new dungeons start at 3,800 as a *floor* and run to 4,800. Guides written
after 2026-07-01 that tell you to enter Castle of the Fallen Daeva at 3,800 /
4,500, Abyssal Horn Den at 4,100–4,800, Doom at 4,000, or Muspel at 4,500 are
describing that later game. File them until global gets the next season.

## Priorities and breakpoints

The short version, if you read nothing else:

| If your item level is | Do this one thing |
| --- | --- |
| Below 1,000 | Main story, and fill the Ascension gauge when it stalls |
| 1,000 to 1,400 | Every sealed dungeon, stronghold, regional mission and Trace |
| 1,400 to 1,700 | Conquest Krao and Draupnir, fill every slot with Unique gear |
| 1,600 to 2,000 | Transcendence for Arcana; all Odd Energy goes here |
| 2,000 to 2,400 | Vakron armour set, then breakthroughs and soul imprint |
| 2,400 to 3,800 | Fire Temple, Ludra, four stigmas to 20, Unique Arcana. This is Season 1 done |
| 4,000 and up | Chapter 1. Ignore until global gets the next season |

Cross-cutting rules that hold in every phase:

- **Never leave a gear slot or a socket empty.** It is the cheapest power in
  the game at every stage.
- **Spread enchants evenly.** Same item level, much lower cost and risk.
- **Let nothing with a cap expire.** Odd Energy, Nightmare attempts (cap 14),
  conquest tickets, Ascension Trial attempts, weekly Sanctuary attempts.
- **Do the daily missions and supply requests every day.** They are the base
  layer of Enhance Stones, Abyss Points and Daevanion materials.

## Costs and math

- Levelling 1 to 45: 7 to 10 hours. There is no 45 to 50 at Season 1.
- Main story only, no side content: item level about 1,000 to 1,100. Adding all
  foundation content with no dungeon farming: about 1,400. Adding Draupnir
  guaranteed rewards and market fill-in: 1,600 to 1,700.
- Arcana: Common +20 item level each, Rare +40, Heritage +60, Unique +80,
  across five slots. Even one stage 1 Transcendence clear is worth +20.
- Full Vakron set swap: about +150 item level.
- Marchutan Daevanion board completed: over +150 item level for over 230 points.
- Unique Arcana refinement is slow. With four skills selected on a Chalice you
  can refine about three per week, and the expected time to a fully finished
  Chalice ranges from under a month for a loose target to over six months for a
  specific two-skill combination. The Libra part is roughly twice as expensive
  per refinement and correspondingly slower.
- Kinah income is capped per character per day, reported at about 2 million,
  which is the entire reason alts exist.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Doing systems in the order the game unlocks them.** The unlock order and
  the useful order are different. Enchanting unlocks at 10 and is useful at 45.
  Soul imprint unlocks at 32 and is useful at 2,000.
- **Skipping the foundation sweep in Phase 1 because it does not feel like
  progress.** It is 300 to 400 item level and the largest single block of free
  power in the game.
- **Spending Odd Energy on Expeditions during Phase 3.** Arcana return more per
  point. This is the most commonly cited efficiency mistake in KR guides.
- **Over-investing in transitional gear.** Breakthroughs, godstones and soul
  imprint work on gear you are about to replace is the most expensive form of
  wasted effort available, because those systems consume the scarcest
  materials.
- **Buying Superior manastones before your set is final.**
- **Burning Ascension Trial attempts on Easy at item level 1,000** when Normal
  at 1,500 is close.
- **Optimising for the Combat Power number in the late phases.** The formula
  undervalues Accuracy and Critical Hit and — until it is patched —
  massively overvalues Penetration. Build for the stat thresholds in
  [[Stat Reference]], then let the CP number follow.
- **Believing Combat Power is the entry requirement.** Party leaders ask for CP,
  but the actual failure mode in Sanctuary content is mechanics. Multiple KR
  clear reports say a 500,000 CP player who executes out-performs a higher-CP
  player who does not, and recommend cutting high-CP players who repeat
  mistakes.

## Global launch notes

The phase structure is content-dependent, so how much of this roadmap applies
at global launch depends on what content state global ships with. Phases 0
through 4 map onto KR launch content and should apply immediately. Phases 5 and
6 depend on Season 2, Season 3 and Chapter 1 content existing.

The sequencing logic is content-independent and is the part worth carrying
over: one-time content first, then fill empty slots, then upgrade grades, then
Arcana, and only then optimise within a tier. That ordering follows from the
item level formula and the permanence hierarchy rather than from any specific
patch.

Two things global players should watch for on day one. First, whether the
Combat Power display exists; if not, expect a third-party aggregator to become
the de facto party standard, exactly as Atull did in KR. Second, whether the
"growth support" packages exist, since one of them explicitly targets 300,000
Combat Power and would compress Phases 0 through 3 substantially.

**Launch Scale Test changes Phase 0 specifically.** Skill Points come from
**side quests and Sealed Dungeons**, not from a feather sweep. Do the story
and the sealed-dungeon clears; leave the horizontal feather route until after
45. Daevanion still pays +1 item level per point, but **Ariel is not in the
test client** and percentage nodes are smaller, so the "boards are the cheapest
gate" line is weaker than in KR. Conquest opens at **700**, not 1,000. See
[[Global Launch Differences]].

Global launch is 2026-10-05 on Steam and PURPLE, with Founder's Pack early
access from 2026-09-30. See [[Global Launch Differences]].
