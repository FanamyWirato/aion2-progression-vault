# Essence Extraction

> **In one sentence:** Essence Extraction is real and its Korean name is
> **정기 추출**, but it is the **gathering skill**, not a combat progression
> track — so this note is a signpost, and the system itself lives in
> [[Gathering Nodes and Plants]].

## Verdict

Earlier research in this vault flagged "Essence Extraction" as a possible
undocumented parallel progression system, described as having 100 levels, Common
and Specialty Skill Points, a point budget too small to fill, and a 50,000 Kinah
respec. That came from a single source chain, so the question was whether it
existed at all.

**It exists, every mechanical detail in that description is correct, and the
only thing wrong was the category.** It is not a second combat progression
track. It is the gathering profession — one skill covering ore, gems, logs,
herbs, cooking ingredients and Odyle — and it is already fully documented in
this vault under chapter 13.

| Claim from the earlier chain | Verdict | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 levels | **Correct** | Two tiers of 50: Novice (입문) 1–50, then Professional (전문) 1–50 |
| Common and Specialty Skill Points | **Correct** | NC's own terminology. Three Common skills, three Specialty nodes per node family |
| The point budget cannot cover everything | **Correct** | 100 levels pay **65 Common and 14 Specialty**; filling every node needs **90 and 42** |
| 50,000 Kinah respec | **Correct** | Available at any time. Note that *combat* skill resets were made free on 2025-11-21 while life-skill resets were not |
| A parallel *progression* system | **Wrong** | It is a life skill. It grants no stats, no Combat Power and no item level |

So this was not a hallucinated system or a mistranslation of gear extraction —
it was a real system filed under the wrong kind. The name was also one syllable
off: 정기 (vital essence) rather than 정수 (essence of a thing).

## Where it actually lives

- **[[Gathering Nodes and Plants]]** — the system note. The skill tree with all
  Korean names, the two-tier level structure, the Novice-50 Shining Ruby
  promotion quest, the point budget, the four quality tiers, node families, XP
  values and zone-by-zone density.
- **[[Gathering and Professions]]** — the chapter map, and where Essence
  Extraction sits relative to crafting, conversion and farming.
- **[[Odyle and Special Resources]]** — Odyle, the one gathered material that
  matters most, and the naming mess around it.
- **[[Material Conversion]]** — what gathered materials turn into, including the
  weekly Odyle Energy conversion that is the real payoff.

Nothing about Essence Extraction belongs in chapter 02. It shares only the words
"skill points" with [[Skills and Mastery Points]] and [[Stigmas]], and those
points come from a different pool, buy different things and reset under different
rules.

## Four things called extraction

This is the other half of the confusion, and it is worth keeping straight
because three of these four are not this note.

| Korean | English in circulation | What it is | Note that owns it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **정기 추출** | Essence Extraction, Gathering, Harvesting | The gathering skill. Ore, gems, logs, herbs, cooking ingredients, Odyle | [[Gathering Nodes and Plants]] |
| **추출** | Extraction, Dismantling, Salvage | Breaking down gear to recover Enhancement Stones and register skins. Auto-extraction added 2026-03-25 under Cube → Extract → Settings | [[Extraction and Refunds]] |
| **아르카나 추출** | Arcana Extraction | Dissolving Arcana cards for Mysterious Crystals | [[Arcana Refinement and Farming]] |
| **영혼의 서** | Soul Codex, Soul Engraving | Re-rolling hidden option lines on gear. Not extraction at all, but the "Soul Codex: Reset" item name causes constant confusion | [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]] |

Rule of thumb: if a guide says "extraction" and talks about Enhancement Stones
coming back, it means the second row. If it talks about ore and herbs, it means
the first. The Korean client uses two distinct words; English translations
collapse them.

## If you came here looking for hidden character power

Essence Extraction is not it. The systems that actually add stats and are easy
to miss are [[Monolith Resonance]], [[Garrisons and Sealed Dungeons]],
[[Daevanion Boards]] and [[Pantheon Stats]].

What gathering *does* buy is supply: Odyle into Odyle Energy, which gates dungeon
reward cubes, plus Daevanion Crystals, wing materials and tradable Kinah. That
argument is made properly in [[Gathering and Professions]].
