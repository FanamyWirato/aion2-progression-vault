# Skills and Talents

> **In one sentence:** your skill levels are the single densest source of
> Combat Power in Aion 2, and you buy them from four separate systems whose
> currencies do not convert into each other — so the whole chapter is about
> which of the four to spend on at any given moment.

## Why it matters

Every other chapter in this vault buys stats. This one buys *skill levels*, and
skill levels are worth more than their stat contribution suggests, because
Aion 2 attaches conditional effects to specific level thresholds. A skill at
level 15 and the same skill at level 16 are not 7 percent apart — the level 16
version has an extra specialisation effect bolted onto it that may add a whole
extra proc, remove a cast time, or convert the skill into a mobile one. Korean
guides do not talk about "levelling skills"; they talk about *hitting 16* and
*hitting 20*, because those are the two places the curve steps.

The scale is worth stating up front. An Inven community member measuring Combat
Power deltas on the day the CP display launched found that taking **one stigma
skill from level 1 to level 20 was worth 10,000 to 15,000 Combat Power** — more
than swapping a dungeon accessory for a fully broken-through crafted one, and
roughly half a weapon tier upgrade. A separate translated Korean video guide
independently reported stigma level bands worth about 900, 2,000, 3,500 and
6,000 CP across levels 1–5, 5–10, 10–15 and 15–20, which sums to about 12,400
and lands inside the same range. Two independent measurements agreeing is rare
in this game's data, so treat this as the firmest number in the chapter.

The trap is that the four systems that supply skill levels have wildly
different costs, and the game gives you no hint of that. Mastery Points are
free and resettable at zero cost. Daevanion points are cheap and permanent but
also count as item level, which makes them the best value in the game. Arcana
are expensive and slow. Stigma Shards are the most expensive resource in the
chapter and are bought with the PvP currency. Spending them in the wrong order
is the defining early-game mistake, and it is a mistake you make silently.

## How it works

### The four layers, and what each one can give you

A mastery skill's level is the sum of contributions from four independent
sources. Only the first is bought with Skill Points.

| Layer | What it does | Range on a single skill | Cost | Reversible? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mastery Points** (Skill Points, 스킬 포인트) | Raises a skill from 1 to 10 | levels 1–10 | Free; earned from level-ups and Wisdom Stones | **Yes, reset is free** |
| **Daevanion nodes** (데바니온) | Blue nodes give +1 active skill level, green nodes +1 passive level | up to **+4** | Daevanion Crystals, cheap and abundant | Yes, but the refund costs Kinah |
| **Arcana cards** (아르카나) | Each card carries 1 to 4 skill lines by grade | up to **+4 per card**, stackable | Odd Energy, Mysterious Crystals, months | Only by re-refining, which is expensive |
| **Gear soul imprint** (영혼 각인) | A rolled option granting a skill level, on weapon, Guard, ring and earring only | **+1 to +2** per line | Soul Imprint rerolls and Sage's Stones | Yes, by re-attuning |

The arithmetic that follows from this is the most useful thing on this page,
and it agrees with the version in [[Arcana Cards and Types]]:

```
Mastery Points alone        →  level 10
+ a fully invested Daevanion →  level 14
+ Arcana and gear            →  level 16 needs +2 more
                                level 20 needs +6 more
```

So **levels 1 to 10 are free, levels 11 to 14 are cheap, and levels 15 to 20
are the entire cost of the system.** Anyone who tells you skill levels are a
slow grind is describing the last six levels of a handful of skills.

Stigma skills are a fifth layer that plays by different rules entirely: they
have their own level track from 1 to 20, they are raised only with Stigma
Shards, and Mastery Points, Daevanion nodes, Arcana and gear cannot touch them.
See [[Stigmas]].

### The specialisation ladder, and the 16-versus-20 confusion

Every mastery active skill has **five specialisation options** (특화) and a
number of **slots** to put them in. These are two separate ladders on the same
skill, and conflating them is why English sources disagree about whether the
third breakpoint is 16 or 20. Both numbers are right; they describe different
things.

| Skill level | Specialisation options unlocked | Slots you can fill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | **3 of the 5** options unlock | 1 |
| 12 | 4th option unlocks | 2 |
| 16 | **5th and final option unlocks** — all five now available | 2 (unchanged) |
| 20 | none left to unlock | **3** |

Read that table twice, because it drives every priority call in this chapter.
Level 16 is where you finish *unlocking* choices; level 20 is where you get the
third *slot* to use them in. An Inven class guide states it flatly: "at level 16
all specialisations open but you can only use 2 slots; at level 20 you can use
3." That is the resolution, and it means neither the "16" sources nor the "20"
sources were wrong — they were counting different ladders.

Practical consequence: **16 is the cheap breakpoint and 20 is the expensive
one.** Getting a skill to 16 costs +2 over a maxed Daevanion; getting it to 20
costs +6 and usually consumes a Unique Arcana line. The Korean convention is
therefore many skills at 16, and only two to four skills at 20.

Stigma skills use the same idea with different numbers, and they are simpler:
specialisation effects **activate automatically** at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20,
with no slots and no choices. That is why stigma investment is a pure
"concentrate, do not spread" problem.

### Where the four currencies come from

| Currency | Korean | Main sources | Spent on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skill Point | 스킬 포인트 | Level-ups; **Wisdom Stones** (지혜의 돌) from Monolith resonance, the Nightmare shop and the Shugo Festa shop | Mastery levels 1–10 |
| Daevanion Crystal | 데바니온 결정 | Level-ups, region quests, achievements, sealed dungeons, Shugo Festa shop (7 per character) | Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel, Triniel nodes |
| Daevanion Crystal: Ariel / Azphel | 데바니온 결정: 아리엘 / 아스펠 | Mission quests, Nightmare shop, Awakening Battle, Abyss and Arena shops, the broker | The two level 45 specialised boards |
| Stigma Shard | 스티그마 샤드 | Ascension and Rift quests (8 guaranteed), Abyss exchange shop at **25,000 AP each**, and Unstable Shards converted from dungeon drops | Stigma levels only |
| Odd Energy → Arcana | 오드 에너지 | Transcendence reward cubes | Arcana skill lines |

The one non-obvious entry there is **Wisdom Stones from Monolith feather
donations**. Collecting Empyrean Lord's Traces (주신의 흔적) and resonating them
at a Monolith pays out **112 Wisdom Stones across resonance levels 1 to 30** —
that is 112 permanent Skill Points that no amount of money can buy, and it is
the single largest block of Skill Points in the game. Multiple Korean sources
call this out as the reason two characters at the same level can have visibly
different skill trees. See [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] for collecting them
and [[Monolith Resonance]] for the reward ladder.

## Progression path

### Which layer to spend on, by stage

This is the chapter's actual answer, and it is deliberately blunt.

| Stage | Spend on | Deliberately ignore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Levels 1–44** | Mastery Points into your left-click basic attack and right-click chain skill, to level 12. Daevanion blue (active) nodes on those same two or three skills. | Stigma Shards entirely. Arcana does not exist yet. |
| **Level 45, item level 1,000–1,400** | Empyrean Lord's Traces for Wisdom Stones. Finish Nezekan's Combat Speed and Cooldown Reduction orange nodes. Sweep sealed dungeons and strongholds for crystals. | Stigmas beyond whatever levels the quests handed you free. |
| **Item level 1,400–2,000** | Arcana, with essentially all Odd Energy, because Arcana is the only layer that gets you past skill level 14. Keep feeding Daevanion crystals. | Stigma Shards, still — the shard economy is AP-limited and your AP has better early uses. |
| **Item level 2,000–2,400** | Open Ariel with dedicated crystals once your armour is around +12. Start the stigma project: pick your four and commit. | Spreading shards across more than four stigmas — Season 1 has four slots. |
| **Item level 2,400+** | Stigmas to 20, one at a time. Unique Arcana refinement for the level-20 skill targets. | Nothing much; this is where all four layers run in parallel. |

The reasoning behind the ordering is that the layers have very different
returns per unit of effort, and the ranking flips over time:

1. **Mastery Points are free and resettable, so there is no wrong answer.**
   Spend them immediately, on whatever you are actually pressing.
2. **Daevanion is the best value in the game and it is not close**, because
   every point spent is simultaneously +1 item level, roughly +1 Combat Power,
   the node's own stats, and possibly a skill level. Nothing else pays four
   ways.
3. **Arcana is the only route past skill level 14**, so it becomes mandatory
   exactly when you start caring about the level 16 and 20 breakpoints.
4. **Stigmas are last, not because they are weak — they are the densest CP in
   the chapter — but because their currency is the scarcest.** 300 shards, or
   7,500,000 Abyss Points, to bring all four Season 1 stigmas to 20.

### Total skill level reachable at Season 1 versus later

| Metric | Global launch (Season 1) | KR now (Chapter 1 and later) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mastery skill level ceiling | **20** | 20 |
| Specialisation slots at max | 3 | 3 |
| Stigma level ceiling | **20** | **25** |
| Stigma slots equipped at once | **4** | 6 |
| Stigma specialisations reachable | **4 of 5** | 5 of 5 |
| Total Skill Points obtainable | **lower than 423** — the published 423 figure includes Chapter 1's one-time content | **423** |
| Daevanion boards available | 6 certain, 8 possible | 8 |
| General Daevanion points obtainable | **553** | 709 |

The headline is that **the mastery ceiling does not move**: 20 is 20 at launch
and 20 today. What later Korean content adds is *stigma* headroom (20 → 25),
one more stigma slot, more Skill Points, and two more Daevanion boards. So a
global launch character can reach the same per-skill mastery ceiling as a
Korean character, and will have fewer skills sitting at that ceiling because
Arcana supply and Skill Point supply are both smaller.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Level 12 on your two most-pressed skills before anything else.** The
  specialisation that unlocks at 12 is described by Korean guides as
  disproportionately strong, and cumulative damage from a basic attack you press
  constantly beats a cooldown skill you press occasionally.
- **Then 16 on your three or four core skills.** This is the cheap breakpoint.
- **Then 20 on two to four skills only.** Which ones is class-specific; the
  Korean class boards on Inven are the correct reference and are more current
  than any English source.
- **Never spread Stigma Shards.** Decide your five before you spend one.
- **Reset your mastery tree freely.** It is free. Korean beginner guides
  explicitly recommend experimenting because of this.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Buying Stigma Shards with Abyss Points before item level 2,000.** Your AP
  buys Decurion accessories that are worth far more at that stage, and the shard
  price does not go up while you wait.
- **Reading "16" and "20" as competing claims.** They are two different ladders;
  see the specialisation table above.
- **Treating mastery reset as risky.** It is free. The expensive resets are
  Daevanion node refunds (Kinah) and, before Chapter 1, stigma resets.
- **Levelling every skill evenly.** Skill Points cap a skill at 10, so an even
  spread guarantees that *nothing* reaches a specialisation breakpoint above 12.
- **Skipping Empyrean Lord's Traces.** They do not raise item level, so they
  look like flavour content. They are 112 Skill Points.
- **Planning Daevanion routes by eye.** Nodes open one square at a time, so a
  careless route permanently wastes points until a reset is offered.

## Global launch notes

The launch baseline this chapter is written against: **global releases
2026-10-05 on Steam and PURPLE, with Founder's Pack early access from
2026-09-30**, running as global Season 1 with roughly the Korean content state
immediately before Chapter 1. Unlocks and gates run from 0 to about 3,800,
Heroic-grade equipment is not in the build, and the eight original classes are
playable with no Brawler.

What that means per system:

- **Mastery skills and the specialisation ladder ship intact.** They are
  launch-era systems, unchanged since KR launch except for the free reset, which
  was itself a launch-week change.
- **Stigmas ship with four slots and a level cap of 20.** This is settled
  against the Korean patch record: the fifth slot arrived 2026-03-25 and the
  sixth slot plus level cap 25 arrived with Chapter 1 on 2026-07-01. The
  consequence to remember is that **Korean stigma loadout guides written after
  March 2026 cannot be followed as written**, because a five- or six-stigma set
  minus one is not a four-stigma set. Full budget in [[Stigmas]]: a finished
  Season 1 set is 300 shards.
- **Six Daevanion boards are certain; Marchutan and Yustiel are the open
  question.** They were added in KR Season 2 and Season 3 respectively, both
  before Chapter 1, so the pre-Chapter-1 baseline implies they are present —
  but their point supply is tied to specific Season 2 and Season 3 dungeons and
  season shops, and NC has published no global board list. [[Daevanion Boards]]
  treats them as roadmap with the reasoning spelled out.
- **The Chapter 1 Daevanion board expansion is out.** So the four general boards
  are the smaller pre-Chapter-1 versions, needing roughly 596 points rather
  than 840, and there is no free reset event to rescue a bad route.

Note that [[PvP Boards and Transformations]] was written on the assumption that
global launches *after* Chapter 1 and therefore ships all eight boards plus the
Learn All Nodes convenience feature. Under the launch baseline above that
assumption does not hold. Both notes flag it as an open question; the board
roster itself is not in dispute, only which boards are in the launch client.
