# Stigmas

> **In one sentence:** stigmas are the densest Combat Power in the game — one
> stigma taken from level 1 to 20 is worth 10,000 to 15,000 CP — and they are
> paid for with the scarcest currency in the game, so the only correct play is
> to pick four and never spread.

## Why it matters

Stigma skills (각인 스킬 in Korean, 烙印技能 in Traditional Chinese) are the
extra skills you slot on top of your class kit. You have a pool of roughly
twelve per class, you equip a handful of them, and you raise those with a
dedicated currency.

The reason this note exists is a single number. When Aion 2 added its Combat
Power display, an Inven player measured the CP delta of every upgrade available
to them and found that **taking one stigma from level 1 to level 20 was worth
10,000 to 15,000 Combat Power** — comparable to half a weapon tier, and more
than most accessory upgrades. A translated Korean video guide independently
broke stigma levels into bands worth roughly 900, 2,000, 3,500 and 6,000 CP for
levels 1–5, 5–10, 10–15 and 15–20, summing to about 12,400. Two independent
measurements landing in the same range makes this the most reliable power figure
in the chapter.

The counterweight is cost. Stigma Shards come mostly from the **Abyss exchange
shop at 25,000 Abyss Points each**, and a finished Season 1 set — four stigmas
at the level-20 cap — costs **300 shards, or 7,500,000 Abyss Points.** That is a
multi-month project. So stigmas are simultaneously the best thing you can buy
and the last thing you should start buying, which is the tension the whole note
is about.

Critically, **stigma levels are a closed system.** Mastery Points, Daevanion
nodes, Arcana skill lines and gear soul imprint cannot raise a stigma level.
Only Stigma Shards can. Every other layer in [[Skills and Talents]] is
irrelevant here.

## How it works

### Season 1 has four slots and a level cap of 20

This is settled. **Global Season 1 gives you four stigma slots and a stigma
level ceiling of 20**, and both numbers rest on two independent lines of
evidence that agree.

The first is NC's own Purple Lounge material describing the system at the Korean
launch: a pool of about twelve stigma skills per class, of which **four** can be
equipped. That is the basis for the verdict already recorded in
[[Season 1 System Availability]].

The second is the Korean patch record, which shows exactly when each expansion
arrived and therefore what the pre-Chapter-1 build contained. The 2026-03-25
patch notes state 스티그마 스킬을 최대 5개까지 장착할 수 있도록 변경됩니다 —
"changed so that up to 5 stigma skills can be equipped" — which only makes sense
as a change *from* four, and Inven's live-broadcast summary writes it explicitly
as 스티그마 최대 4개 → 5개. The level cap moved separately: Korean press
covering the 2026-07-01 Chapter 1 update quotes NC as raising the character cap
from 45 to 50 and 스티그마 스킬 최고 레벨을 20에서 25로 확장 — the stigma
maximum from 20 to 25.

So four slots and cap 20 are not an inference from a content window. They are
the state the system was in before two dated patches changed it, and both of
those patches fall outside the launch build.

> **The warning that matters most in this note.** Korean stigma guides written
> after March 2026 assume five slots, and anything written after July 2026
> assumes six slots and level 25. **Neither loadout transfers.** A five-stigma
> rotation is not a four-stigma rotation with one skill removed — the guide
> author chose those five to cover each other, and dropping one usually leaves a
> gap they were relying on. Likewise, any recommendation that leans on a
> level-25 effect is describing a skill you cannot build. When you read a Korean
> class guide, check its date first: **before 2026-03-25 it is directly usable,
> and after that it needs translating down.**

### Roadmap: how capacity grew in Korea

| Date (KR) | Change | Status for Season 1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2025-11-19 (launch) | Four slots, level cap 20, pool of ~12 per class | **This is Season 1** |
| **2026-03-25** | Fifth slot; mastery and stigma slots unified so either type can go in any slot; one new stigma per class; extra shard sources | Roadmap |
| **2026-07-01** (Chapter 1) | Sixth slot at the new level 50; cap raised to 25 via Advanced Stigma Shards; fifth specialisation opens at 25; stigma resets made permanently free | Roadmap |

Two details from that table are worth carrying forward. The 2026-03-25 change
was not only a slot — it **merged the mastery and stigma slot pools**, so in
current Korea any skill can occupy any slot except the fixed left-click basic
attack. At Season 1 the stigma slots are still a separate, dedicated set of
four. And the Chapter 1 patch is where **free stigma resets** arrived, which is
why launch-era resets should be assumed to cost.

The same 2026-03-25 patch added **per-preset slot conditions**, which is what
lets a Korean player keep a separate PvE and PvP stigma arrangement. That is
roadmap too, so do not assume a frictionless mode swap at Season 1 — see the
PvE versus PvP discussion below for what to do instead.

### Levels, and the automatic specialisation ladder

Stigmas run from level 1 to **20** at Season 1. Unlike mastery skills, there is
no choosing: **specialisation effects apply automatically at levels 5, 10, 15
and 20.** No slots, no options, no decisions.

| Stigma level | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Granted when you learn the stigma |
| **5** | First specialisation effect applies automatically |
| **10** | Second effect |
| **15** | Third effect |
| **20** | Fourth effect — **the Season 1 ceiling** |
| 21–24 *(roadmap)* | Chapter 1 extension, requires **Advanced Stigma Shards** |
| **25** *(roadmap)* | Fifth and final specialisation effect |

A useful thing falls out of the Chapter 1 patch record: NC's Chapter 1 preview
describes level 25 as opening the **fifth** specialisation. So a stigma has five
specialisation effects in total, and **a Season 1 stigma at its level-20 ceiling
has four of the five.** You are not missing a whole tier of power, you are
missing the last of five steps — which is a much smaller gap than the level
numbers suggest, and it is worth knowing before you read a Korean guide that
treats level 25 as the baseline.

The design consequence people miss is that because the effects are automatic and
land on a fixed ladder, **stigma investment has no build diversity.** There is
no "wrong" specialisation choice to make. The only decision is *which* stigmas
and *how deep*, and the breakpoints are what you are buying. A stigma at 14 has
bought you two of four effects; two stigmas at 5 have bought you two of eight.

### Shard costs

The per-level cost doubles every five levels, in four bands of five levels each.
That makes the last five levels cost more than the first fifteen combined.

| Level band | Shards per level | Band total | Cumulative shards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 → 5 | 1 | 5 | **5** |
| 6 → 10 | 2 | 10 | **15** |
| 11 → 15 | 4 | 20 | **35** |
| 16 → 20 | 8 | 40 | **75** |

Those cumulative figures are exactly what the Inven cost table reports, so the
per-level ladder and the totals corroborate each other. At **25,000 Abyss Points
per shard** from the Abyss exchange:

| Target | Shards | Abyss Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One stigma to 10 | 15 | 375,000 |
| One stigma to 15 | 35 | 875,000 |
| **One stigma to 20** | **75** | **1,875,000** |

One source lists the cumulative cost to level 5 as 100,000 AP rather than the
125,000 that five shards imply, which would mean level 1 arrives free with the
skill and you buy four levels. The other three rows in that source match the
table above exactly, so I have used the clean five-shard reading; the difference
is one shard and it does not affect anything downstream.

### The finished Season 1 set

Because Season 1 caps you at **four slots and level 20**, the stigma project has
a finish line, which is not true of current Korea. These are the two numbers to
budget against:

| Milestone | Shards | Abyss Points | Kinah, if converted instead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Four stigmas to 15** | **140** | 3,500,000 | 28,000,000 |
| **Four stigmas to 20 — complete** | **300** | **7,500,000** | 60,000,000 |

The Kinah column assumes every shard comes from an Unstable Stigma Shard
converted at the guaranteed 200,000 Kinah rate rather than bought with Abyss
Points. In practice you will mix the two, and the mix is heavily in Kinah's
favour — which is the single biggest lever on how long this takes. See Inputs
below.

**300 shards is the whole system.** No fifth slot to feed, no level 25 to chase.
That is worth saying plainly because Korean guides describe an open-ended sink,
and at Season 1 it is a finite one.

### Why breadth strictly beats depth, until it does not

The cap-20, four-slot shape makes the spending order provable rather than a
heuristic. Divide each band's cost by the one specialisation effect it buys:

| Effect | Reached at level | Shards for that effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | 5 | **5** |
| 2nd | 10 | **10** |
| 3rd | 15 | **20** |
| 4th | 20 | **40** |

The cost per effect rises monotonically and is identical across all four
stigmas, so the cheapest next effect is *always* the lowest band you have not
yet filled on all four skills. The optimal order is therefore rigid: **all four
to 5, then all four to 10, then all four to 15, then all four to 20.**

The important consequence of only having four slots is that this breadth-first
logic **runs out at 140 shards.** In Korea, 40 shards spent pushing one stigma
from 15 to 20 competes with bringing a fifth or sixth stigma from 1 to 15 for
35 — so the trade stays live indefinitely. At Season 1 there is no fifth slot,
so once all four sit at 15 the only remaining purchase *is* the expensive
16→20 push. After 140 shards, depth is not a choice, it is the only shelf left.

### Resets and refunds

| When | Reset cost | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KR launch (2025-11) | Expensive; **no shard refund** | Medium |
| From 2026-01-28 | Reduced | Medium |
| From Chapter 1 (2026-07-01) | **Free** | Medium |

A figure circulating in this vault's earlier research puts the reset at
**20,000 Kinah per level for stigma levels 1–4 and 40,000 per level for levels
5–10**. **Confidence low** — I could not trace it to a primary Korean source,
and it may describe the reduced post-2026-01-28 cost rather than the launch
cost. If it is right, the shape is what matters: the per-level fee doubles as
the levels climb, so unwinding a deep stigma is expensive in Kinah on top of
whatever happens to the shards.

This is the sharpest contrast with mastery skills, where the reset has been free
since launch week. Stigma resets started out as a real cost and only became free
with Chapter 1 — which is **not** in the global launch build. So under the
launch baseline, **assume a stigma reset costs you something and possibly does
not return your shards at all.**

That asymmetry is the entire argument for the conservative approach in this
note. You can rearrange your mastery tree on a whim. You cannot rearrange 75
shards.

## Unlock and gates

| Gate | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 2nd Ascension, around level 22 | Stigmas become available as a system |
| A mission quest in the **level 20–35** band | The actual unlock; also grants 8 free shards |
| **Level 45, four slots, cap 20** | **The Season 1 ceiling** |
| Level 46+ *(roadmap)* | Advanced Stigma Shards begin dropping, one per level from 45 to 50 plus one per stigma already at 20 |
| Level 50 *(roadmap)* | Sixth slot, and level 25 with the fifth specialisation |

The official KR announcement places the unlock quest in the level 20 to 35
window rather than at a fixed level, which is why sources quote different
numbers. Treat "some time in your twenties" as the answer.

## Inputs: currencies, materials, sources

| Source | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ascension and Rift mission quests** | **8 guaranteed** | The unlock questline itself; free and unmissable |
| **Abyss exchange shop** | unlimited at **25,000 AP** each | The main supply. See [[Abyss Points and Ranks]] |
| **Unstable Stigma Shards** from dungeons | convert to real shards | Drop currency; conversion is a gamble, below |
| Arena and season shops | varies | Season 3 added further exchange routes |
| **Kinah** | 200,000 per guaranteed conversion | Also pays launch-era reset fees |

**Unstable Stigma Shard (불안정한 스티그마 샤드)** is the dungeon-drop version
and it does not work directly. You convert it, and the conversion has two
prices: a cheap attempt with roughly a **25% success chance**, or an expensive
attempt at **100%**. The Taiwanese patch notes for 2026-02-25 record the
guaranteed conversion price dropping from **1,000,000 Kinah to 200,000 Kinah**,
an 80% cut, and a Korean source describes the same change as reducing the cost
to 20% of its previous value. After that change, buying the guaranteed
conversion is straightforwardly correct — at 200,000 Kinah a shard is far
cheaper in Kinah than in Abyss Points, and Kinah is the currency you have most
of.

That is the practical takeaway: **convert every Unstable Shard you get at the
guaranteed rate, and treat the Abyss shop as the top-up rather than the primary
route.** Korean guides quoting 9,375,000 AP are pricing five maxed stigmas —
their slot count, not yours — and pricing the worst case where every shard is
bought rather than converted. See
[[Currencies and Economy]] for whether the Kinah you hold is spendable on this.

## Progression path

The ordering below is deliberately conservative, because shards are
irreversible under launch-era rules.

1. **On unlock (level ~20–35).** Take the 8 free quest shards. Do not buy any.
   Read your class's stigma pool and pick your set on paper.
2. **Levels 20–45.** Spend nothing. Your Abyss Points have better uses at this
   stage — the AP accessories described in [[Abyss Points and Ranks]] are worth
   more per point than a level-3 stigma. Bank Unstable Shards from dungeons.
3. **Item level 1,400–2,000.** Still nothing, or nearly nothing. This is the
   Arcana window per [[Progression Phases Roadmap]], and Arcana is the only
   layer that unlocks skill levels 16 and 20.
4. **Item level ~2,000.** Start. Convert every banked Unstable Shard at the
   guaranteed rate. Bring **all four** chosen stigmas to **level 10** — 60
   shards total buys eight automatic effects, which is the best shard-to-power
   ratio available anywhere in the note.
5. **Item level 2,000–2,400.** Push all four to **15**. That is 140 shards
   cumulative and it is the milestone worth aiming at, because it is the last
   point at which shards are cheap.
6. **Item level 2,400+.** Now spend the expensive 40 shards per skill to reach
   **20**, one skill at a time, starting with whichever stigma your rotation
   presses most. 160 shards for the four pushes, 300 cumulative, and then the
   system is finished for Season 1.

The reason step 2 and 3 say "spend nothing" so emphatically: the shard price
does not rise while you wait, the shards do not expire, and Abyss Points spent
on early accessories compound into faster clears which produce more of
everything. Waiting is free. Spending early is not.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Four stigmas, full stop.** A fifth partially-levelled stigma is pure waste,
  because the fifth slot is a 2026-03-25 Korean change that Season 1 does not
  have.
- **Level 10 on all four before 15 on any of them. Level 15 on all four before
  20 on any of them.** This is the strict optimum, not a rule of thumb — see the
  cost-per-effect table above.
- **140 shards is the milestone; 300 finishes the system.**
- **Convert Unstable Shards at the guaranteed 200,000 Kinah rate.** Do not
  gamble at 25%.
- **Do not start before item level ~2,000.** Abyss Points buy more elsewhere.
- **Check the date on any Korean guide you read.** Post-March 2026 guides assume
  five slots and post-July 2026 guides assume six and level 25.

### PvE versus PvP picks

I am not going to invent class-by-class lists, because the reliable sources for
this are the Korean class boards on Inven and they change with every balance
patch. What generalises is the selection *criteria*, which the Korean guides are
consistent about:

| Role | Prioritise in PvE | Prioritise in PvP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tank** | Threat generation, damage reduction, and the party-wide mitigation stigmas — you are buying survivability the group depends on | Crowd control and CC resistance. A tank that cannot be locked down is the win condition in group PvP |
| **Healer** | Throughput and efficiency: group heals, heal-over-time, and anything reducing cast time | Dispels, shields, anti-CC and mobility. Raw healing loses to being killed |
| **Damage** | Sustained single-target damage and anything that raises your uptime on a boss | Burst windows, gap closers and escapes. Damage that does not land inside a stun is wasted |

Two structural points that do generalise:

- **Because specialisation effects are automatic, a stigma is either worth 75
  shards or it is not.** There is no salvaging a mediocre pick with a clever
  specialisation choice, the way there is with mastery skills.
- **Presets would let you split PvE from PvP without splitting your shards**,
  since the three skill presets in [[Skills and Mastery Points]] each save their
  own stigma arrangement. Note that presets arrived in Korea on 2026-01-07,
  after launch, so whether Season 1 has them is itself uncertain. Either way the
  *levels* are shared — a PvP-only stigma at 20 costs the same 75 shards as a
  PvE one, and with only four slots that is a real price. **Prefer stigmas that
  earn their 75 shards in both modes**, which matters more at four slots than it
  would at six.

Consult your class's Inven board before committing. For Cleric and Templar in
particular, the Inven class-board guides cited below carry current
recommendations.

### How much power stigmas represent, in context

| System | What one unit buys | Item level? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One stigma, 1 → 20** | **10,000–15,000 CP**, 75 shards | No |
| One Daevanion point | ~1 CP and **+1 item level**, plus the node's stats | **Yes** |
| Arcana skill line | +1 to +4 skill levels toward the 16 and 20 breakpoints | Yes, via the card |
| Mastery Point | levels 1–10 only | No |

Stigmas are pure Combat Power and contribute **no item level**, which is why
they cannot substitute for gear when you are trying to pass an entry gate. That
is the honest framing of their place: stigmas make you stronger inside content
you can already enter, and Daevanion points make you *able to enter* it. See
[[Item Level vs Combat Power]] for why conflating those two is the most common
progression mistake in the game.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Buying shards before item level 2,000.** The most common and most expensive
  error. Abyss Points spent on Decurion accessories at that stage return far
  more.
- **Spreading shards across more than four stigmas.** You have exactly four
  slots at Season 1 and the value is entirely in the 5/10/15/20 breakpoints.
- **Pushing one stigma to 20 before all four reach 15.** Levels 16–20 cost 40
  shards; the same 40 shards spent lower down buys two effects instead of one.
- **Copying a Korean loadout without checking its date.** A five-stigma build
  minus one stigma is not a four-stigma build.
- **Gambling on the 25% Unstable Shard conversion.** Since the guaranteed price
  fell to 200,000 Kinah, the gamble is bad expected value.
- **Assuming a free reset.** Free stigma resets are Chapter 1, which is not in
  the launch build. Launch-era resets cost, and may not refund shards.
- **Following Korean endgame stigma guides literally.** They assume level 25 and
  six slots. Neither exists at global launch.
- **Expecting Arcana or Daevanion to help.** They cannot touch stigma levels.

## Global launch notes

Against the **2026-10-05** global release (Founder's access **2026-09-30**),
running Season 1 on roughly the pre-Chapter-1 Korean content with a gate ceiling
around 3,800 and no Heroic gear:

**In the build:**
- **Four stigma slots** and a **level cap of 20**
- Unlock ladder in the Launch Scale Test: 2nd slot **level 27**, 3rd **32**,
  4th **37** (older notes said "quest between 20 and 35")
- Dedicated stigma slots, separate from mastery slots
- Shard ladder still the planning cost model, but **sources widened**:
  Nightmare, Arena, Shugo, Battlefield. Shugo shop sold **35 shards per
  character**. Radiant Odyle **three times a month per character**. Abyss shop
  still **25,000 AP** per shard; Welps expects that to fall
- Costly stigma resets — the free reset is a Chapter 1 change

**Roadmap, with dates:**
- **2026-03-25:** fifth slot; mastery and stigma slot pools merged; one new
  stigma per class; extra shard sources including Awakening Battle and Abyss
  directives
- **2026-07-01 (Chapter 1):** sixth slot at level 50; level cap 25 via Advanced
  Stigma Shards; fifth specialisation at 25; stigma resets made free

The practical effect of the two roadmap rows is a **hard ceiling on the whole
system at 300 shards**, and the reading warning above: date-check every Korean
guide, because the loadouts they recommend were built for capacities you do not
have.
