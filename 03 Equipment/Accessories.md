# Accessories

> **In one sentence:** the accessory group is really eight separate progression
> systems sharing a tooltip category, and two of them — the Noble Belt and the
> Revelation Amulet — are the best long-term investments in the game because you
> never replace them.

## Why it matters

Most equipment in AION 2 is temporary. You will replace your weapon four or
five times and your armor set three times. The Belt and the Amulet you keep
from the early story to the end, upgrading them **in place** through grade
evolution. That changes the arithmetic completely: an Enhancement Stone spent on
the Belt is never wasted, and an Enhancement Stone spent on a Unique armor piece
you replace in a month mostly is.

Accessories also hold the character's [[Manastones Spirit Stones and Godstones]]
**Spirit Stone** sockets — manastones go in weapons and armor, spirit stones go
in accessories. And rings specifically roll Mastery skill levels on Soul
Imprint, which is why one Korean guide singles the crafted ring out as the one
accessory worth crafting even when the rest come from dungeons.

## How it works

### The eight accessory-style slots and their tracks

| Slot | Track type | Progresses by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amulet | Permanent, grade evolution | Enhance Scrolls from Monoliths, then Substance Morph |
| Belt | Permanent, grade evolution | Enhance Scrolls from Strongholds, then Substance Morph |
| Pendant | Permanent-ish, quality evolution | Quest materials plus an Awakening Gauge, then Draconic Essence |
| Bracelet | Replacement ladder | Quest, craft, dungeon, then Sanctuary conversion |
| Brooch | Replacement ladder | Craft, or Equipment Change Ticket conversion |
| Necklace | Replacement ladder | Dungeon drop, craft, or Abyss shop |
| Earrings | Replacement ladder | Dungeon drop, craft, or Abyss shop |
| Ring | Replacement ladder | Dungeon drop, craft, or Abyss shop |

The first three behave nothing like the last five. Learn the distinction before
you spend anything.

### Track 1 — Revelation Amulet (계시의 아뮬렛) and Fierce Battle Amulet

The amulet slot has two parallel items, a PvE one and a PvP one. Both use their
own dedicated enhancement scroll, so ordinary Enhancement Stones alone will not
raise them.

| Item | Purpose | Scroll | Scroll source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revelation Amulet (Amulet of Revelation) | PvE | Revelation Amulet Enhance Scroll | Zone Monolith level-ups in Verteron, Altgard, Eltnen, Morheim |
| Fierce Battle Amulet (Amulet of Battle) | PvP | Fierce Battle Amulet Enhance Scroll | Reshanta Monolith level-ups, and the Abyss shop for the Heroic version |

The scrolls come from **Monolith donations**, which is the
[[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] system: each zone scatters Empyrean Lord's
Traces, marked on the map with a feather icon, and turning them in at the zone's
Monolith raises the Monolith level. Each level pays stat gains plus items, and
almost every level of the Verteron and Altgard Monoliths includes one Revelation
Amulet Enhance Scroll. The Altgard Monolith has 30 levels costing a cumulative
560 tokens and pays roughly 31 scrolls across the whole ladder; the Chapter 1
Eltnen and Morheim Monoliths have 15 levels each. The Reshanta Monolith pays
Fierce Battle Amulet Enhance Scrolls instead, alongside Daevanion Crystals and
Amplify Stone Fragments.

One frequently repeated number is that **140 feathers** exist across the map and
collecting all of them takes the Revelation Amulet to Unique +10. That figure
comes from a single low-trust aggregator, so treat it as **low confidence**; the
mechanism is well corroborated but the count is not.

Amulet attributes by grade, from the community wiki:

| Grade | Revelation Amulet lines |
| --- | --- |
| Rare | Weapon Damage Boost, Penetration |
| Epic | Weapon Damage Boost, Penetration, Power Shard Damage Bonus |
| Unique | Weapon Damage Boost, Perfect Penetration, Power Shard Damage Bonus |
| Heroic | Added 2026-07-01; converted from a Unique +10 amulet |

Weapon Damage Boost is one of the two best stats in the game, which is why the
amulet punches above its slot.

### Track 2 — Noble Belt (고결의 허리띠)

Same mechanic, different scroll and different stats. You receive a green Noble
Belt in the early main story. It is permanent, cannot be replaced, and is
progressed with **Belt Enhance Scrolls** from **Strongholds** (also translated
Garrison Dungeons, the map markers shown as question marks). Some regional and
exploration quests, and Rift of Time and Space runs into the opposing faction's
map, also award them.

| Grade | Noble Belt lines |
| --- | --- |
| Rare | HP Potion Recovery % Increase, HP |
| Epic | HP Potion Recovery % Increase, HP, Defense |
| Unique | HP Potion Recovery % Increase, Regeneration, HP, Defense |
| Heroic | Added 2026-07-01; converted from a Unique +10 belt |

### The grade evolution loop (Substance Morph)

Both the Amulet and the Belt use the same cycle, and it is the single most
important mechanic in this note.

1. Enhance the item to **+10** using its dedicated scroll.
2. **Unequip it.** Substance Morph will not accept an equipped item.
3. Open Substance Morph (Alt + H) and morph it into the next grade. The new item
   arrives at **+0** with much higher base stats.
4. Re-equip, farm more scrolls, return to +10, repeat.

The ladder is Common (green) → Rare (blue) → Epic (purple) → Unique (gold) →
Heroic. The Epic and Unique Amulet and Belt are **morph-locked**: Substance
Morph is the only way to obtain them, which is why the loop is mandatory rather
than optional. The colour-to-grade mapping above is the one two independent
sources agree on; a third low-trust source gives a different mapping, so treat
the colours as **medium confidence** and the grade names as high.

**Stop at Unique +10.** Every guide, Korean and English, says the same thing:
beyond Unique +10 the stones-per-level cost rises steeply while the stat gain
does not, and the Heroic conversion only became available in July 2026 with its
own scroll economy.

### Track 3 — Draconic Pendant and the Awakening Gauge

The Pendant slot was added to the KR client on **2026-07-01** with Chapter 1.
Per the official note, Rare and "Heritage" grade pendants and their enhancement
materials come from Eltnen and Morheim quests, and Unique-grade and above
pendants are enhanced with Kinah, **Breakthrough Stones**, **Draconic Essence**
and **Radiant Draconic Essence** rather than ordinary Enhancement Stones.
Draconic Essence is the consolidated form of the old Horn / Hide / Scale
Draconic materials from Expedition and Sanctuary.

A separate English description of the pendant track, which I could only trace to
one aggregator, gives more mechanical detail: a green Draconic Pendant from the
level 45 to 50 main questline, evolving to blue at +10 and to Unique with
further enhancement, where **after each quality evolution an Awakening Gauge
must be filled to 100% to unlock the material quest for the next upgrade**. The
gauge fills from main quests, side quests, Sealed Dungeon objectives and
Exploration Outpost missions. The same source describes a "talisman" on an
identical +10-then-evolve track with scrolls from Exploration Outposts.

Two cautions. First, that account is **low confidence** — single-source and
partly contradicted by the patch note's description of quest-sourced Rare and
Heritage pendants. Second, no Korean source I found uses a term matching
"talisman" as a distinct slot; it is most likely a rendering of the Amulet or of
the Brooch. Do not plan around a talisman slot until you see it in the client.

### Track 4 — Bracelets

The bracelet is a straightforward replacement ladder, and it is the one
accessory slot with a clean, well-documented sequence.

| Step | Item | Grade | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pilgrim Bracelet | Epic | Crafted |
| 2 | **Ascension Bracelet** | Unique | The **level 45 Ascension quest** reward |
| 3 | Abyssal Bracelet | Unique | Abyssal Forge Sanctuary, ~3.2% per roll |
| 4 | Ludra's Bracelet | Heroic | Abyssal Forge, 2% per roll, **or** 2× Equipment Change Ticket: Rudra + 20,000,000 Kinah |
| 5 | Later Sanctuary Heroic bracelets | Heroic | Corroded Decontamination Facility and Muspel's Chalice |

Ascension quests fire at levels 5, 22, 32 and 45; the level 45 one is the
important one because it hands you a Unique bracelet outright. See
[[Level and Ascension]].

Ludra's Bracelet is Heroic at per-item Item Lv 98 with Attack 74 and Defense
260. The ticket conversion is the correct route — the 2% cube drop is not worth
waiting for, and the April 2026 patch specifically doubled the Rudra's Heart and
Rudra's Bracelet weighting inside the Heroic drop pool to make the slot less
punishing.

The Vortex Gaming translation of a Korean gearing video puts bracelets fourth in
the attack-power enhancement order, behind weapon, sub-weapon and ring, which is
higher than most Western guides place them.

### Track 5 — Brooch

A new slot added **2026-05-27** with Muspel's Chalice. Obtained by crafting or
by material-converting an Equipment Change Ticket from Muspel's Chalice plus
Kinah. The known dropped brooch is the **Kaldrix Brooch**, Unique, from Chalice
of Muspel at 10% on Hard and 5% on Normal — a generous rate by AION 2 standards,
because a brand-new slot with nothing in it is worth a lot of item level.

### Track 6 — Necklace, Earrings, Ring

Ordinary replacement slots with three competing sources:

- **Dungeon drops.** Accessories have the highest drop rates of any gear type:
  rings land at 20 to 39% per roll on Epic tiers and 3 to 9% on Unique tiers.
  Rings are always the most common item in a boss pool, necklaces and earrings
  next. This is deliberate — it makes accessories the cheapest way to raise item
  level early.
- **Crafted Dragon Lord accessories.** Crafted under the accessory profession
  (Handicrafting / Jewelcrafting). Genesis and Nemesis Dragon Lord accessories
  arrived 2026-08-12, crafted by inheriting Splendent Noble / Horned Dragon Lord
  accessories into them.
- **Abyss shop accessories**, bought with Abyss Points and Merit Medals, running
  Decanus → Centurion → Tribunus and High Commander → Praetorian Captain. These
  carry PvP stats and the −5% PvE Damage Boost set penalty. See [[PvP Gear]] and
  [[Abyss Points and Ranks]].

**The ring exception.** Korean setting guides recommend crafted rings even for
new players who otherwise use dungeon accessories, because ring Soul Imprint
rolls Mastery skill levels and the crafted ring carries one more imprint line
than the dropped ring. Everything else in this group can wait: Korean press
explicitly notes that for necklaces and earrings the gap between high and low
tier items is not meaningful enough to rush.

### AP-purchased accessories

Abyss Points buy accessories before they buy armor or weapons, and that is the
recommended entry point for PvP because accessories are the cheapest AP
purchase per point of PvP power. One Korean video guide singles out a
"10-Star General Ring" as the recommended AP buy. A frequently quoted figure of
roughly 715,000 AP for a full Guardian Decanus set comes from a low-trust site
and should be treated as **low confidence**.

Abyss accessories additionally have their own **Potential** amplification, added
2026-04-08: each of four stages adds +0.5% PvP Damage Boost for accessories,
+0.5% PvP Damage Resistance for armor, and +2.5% PvP Damage Boost for the weapon
and Guard, using Potential Amplifiers dropped by Abyss monsters plus Abyss
Points.

## Progression path

1. **Do not sell the green Noble Belt.** Start the scroll loop the first time you
   clear a Stronghold.
2. **Collect Empyrean Traces as you level.** They are one-time and they are the
   only Revelation Amulet scroll source. Clear every Stronghold and Sealed
   Dungeon once on **both** faction maps — Rift of Time and Space gives you
   access to the other side's instances.
3. **Level 45: claim the Ascension Bracelet.** If the quest does not appear,
   check the Ascension Gauge above the quest log.
4. **Fill the remaining accessory slots from dungeon drops.** Rings, earrings and
   necklaces drop constantly; take whatever raises item level and do not invest.
5. **Craft your ring.** The extra Soul Imprint line and Mastery skill roll make
   this the one accessory worth real money early.
6. **Run the Belt and Amulet loop to Unique +10** and stop. This is the largest
   permanent power block available to a mid-game character.
7. **Buy Abyss accessories with your first Abyss Points** if you intend to PvP
   at all. Accessories first, then armor, then weapon.
8. **Slot into the new slots as they open.** Brooch and Pendant are free item
   level because they started empty.
9. **Heroic conversions last.** Ludra's Bracelet via tickets, then the Heroic
   Amulet and Belt conversions from Unique +10.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Belt and Amulet before anything else that is not the weapon.** They are
  permanent; nothing else in the accessory group is.
- **Unique +10 is the stop line** on both. Do not push further until the rest of
  the character is stable.
- **Rings before necklaces and earrings.** Mastery skill rolls and the extra
  crafted imprint line.
- **New slots before upgrading old ones.** An empty Brooch or Pendant slot
  contributes zero; a filled one at low grade contributes a lot.
- **Accessories before armor in the AP queue** for PvP.
- Do not push a dungeon accessory past +10 in the early game. The cost is high
  and the piece is replaceable.

## Costs and math

- Ludra's Bracelet by conversion: 2× Equipment Change Ticket: Rudra plus
  **20,000,000 Kinah**.
- Abyssal Forge Weapon Selection Box, for comparison of ticket value: 4×
  Equipment Change Ticket: Ludra plus **50,000,000 Kinah** (2026-07-01).
- A Season 3 Korean guide gives the new Sanctuary Heroic bracelet as roughly
  **40 million Kinah to take from +0 to +20**, versus about **50 million** to
  inherit an existing +5 bracelet into it — so for bracelets, enhancing fresh is
  cheaper than spending a Succession attempt. The specific numbers are
  **medium confidence** (one blog) but the conclusion matches the general Korean
  advice that low-enhancement, easy-to-tune slots should not consume Succession
  attempts.
- Beyond Unique +10 on the Belt and Amulet, community reports describe "tens of
  thousands of enhancement stones per level". Directionally right, no verified
  table.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Selling or discarding the starting Noble Belt.** It cannot be replaced.
- **Forgetting to unequip before Substance Morph.** The +10 item will not appear
  in the material list while equipped.
- **Forgetting to unregister from an equipment preset.** Same failure, different
  cause, and it also blocks Succession.
- **Pushing the Belt or Amulet past Unique +10 in the mid-game.** The most
  expensive avoidable mistake in this note.
- **Ignoring the second faction's map.** Half the Strongholds, Sealed Dungeons
  and Traces, and therefore half the scrolls, are over there.
- **Spending a limited Advanced Succession attempt on a bracelet or a ring.**
  These slots are cheap to re-enhance and easy to re-tune; the attempts belong on
  the weapon and Guard.
- **Wearing Abyss accessories in a dungeon.** The −5% PvE Damage Boost penalty
  is on the set at every tier.

## Global launch notes

Belt, Amulet, Ascension Bracelet and dungeon accessory drops are launch-era.
Pendant and Brooch are later KR and remain out.

**Global:** **Unique (gold) is the grade ceiling** for Belt of Nobility and
Revelation Amulet — **Heroic conversion is not implemented.** Progress on those
slots is **enhancement (+1…+10)** with Belt Enhance Scrolls and Revelation
Amulet Enhance Scrolls, not a further Substance Morph tier. Character sites
often show **+N next to the item name**; that is enchant level only. A gold
**Edler Gürtel +0** is max grade, not an unupgraded white item.

On KR live, Unique +10 is still the sensible stop before Heroic (July 2026).
On global, **Unique +10 is the end of the belt/amulet track** until a Chapter 1
patch adds Heroic.

**Launch Scale Test — accessories are not a KR clone:**

- **Earring:** Move Speed, **Might** (attack), **Precision** (crit + accuracy).
  Might and Move Speed are must-have; Precision matters early, not late.
- **Necklace:** Combat Speed, Might, Precision.
- **Ring:** Might and Precision. Rings are no longer "just active-skill lines".
- Crafted accessories still exist to buy the **extra soul-bind line** those
  stats sit on.
- Conquest **pity boxes include accessories and armour**, not weapons only.

See [[Stat Reference]] and [[Global Launch Differences]].
