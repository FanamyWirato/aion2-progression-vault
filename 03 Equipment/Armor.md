# Armor

> **In one sentence:** armor is seven slots that carry most of your HP and
> Defense but only a minority of your offensive power, which is why the correct
> strategy is to raise all seven to a competent floor from dungeon drops and
> spend the expensive resources on three of them.

## Why it matters

Armor decides whether you survive a boss mechanic, and survival is a hard gate:
you cannot farm content you keep wiping in. But per point of Kinah, armor is
worse than the weapon and worse than the permanent accessories. English guides
that recommend "dungeon armor is perfectly viable because its defensive
differences are relatively minor" are describing a real property of the game —
the gap between dropped and crafted armor is much smaller than the gap between
dropped and crafted weapons.

There is one exception that changes the shape of the priority list. Armor slots
each roll a **different** best Soul Imprint line, and three of those lines are
offensive and large: Damage Boost on the chest, Combat Speed on the gloves, and
Critical Damage Boost on the pauldrons. Those three slots are effectively
damage slots wearing an armor label. See
[[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]] and [[Stat Reference]].

### Are there armor classes?

No. AION 2 does not carry the plate / chain / leather / cloth proficiency split
that AION 1 used. In the live item data each gear set contains exactly one
Breastplate, one Helm and so on, with no material variants, and armor pieces do
not carry a class restriction the way weapons do. The practical effect is that
you can mix pieces from different sets freely and choose by stats rather than by
class. Confidence **medium-high**: this is inferred from the set data plus one
English source stating it explicitly, rather than from a patch note.

Do not carry AION 1 armor-type knowledge into AION 2. Almost every English
search result for "Aion armor types" returns AION 1 material.

### Armor stat profile

A top-tier crafted armor piece carries:

- Defense and HP as the headline pair
- A fixed additional option — crafted Draconic armor carries 5% PvE Damage
  Tolerance per piece
- Five manastone sockets (no Godstone socket; that is weapon-only)
- Six selectable Soul Imprint lines, drawn from a pool that differs by slot
- Enchantable to +20, adding flat Defense and HP (the Splendent Nemesis Dragon
  Lord Breastplate gains Defense 700 and HP 400 at +20)
- Exceed (Breakthrough) to +5, adding flat Defense and HP plus a 5% Defense and
  5% HP increase

Armor and accessory enhancement had its Defense and HP contribution **doubled**
in the 2025-12-31 patch, which is why older guides understate armor
enhancement's value.

### Set effects

Set bonuses exist but, in the current live data, they belong almost entirely to
the **Abyss / PvP** sets, not to PvE dungeon or crafted sets. There are 17
registered set bonuses: 10 equipment sets, all of them the Guardian and Archon
military-rank Abyss sets, plus 7 [[Arcana Sets and Meta]] sets.

Each Abyss equipment set has 11 distinct pieces (Guard, Necklace, Earrings,
Ring, Breastplate, Greaves, Helm, Pauldrons, Gloves, Boots, Cloak) and bonus
tiers at 2, 5, 8 and 12 pieces. Representative numbers, from the Guardian High
Commander Tribulation set:

| Pieces | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | +10% Abyss PvE Damage Boost, +5% PvP Damage Boost, −5% PvE Damage Boost |
| 5 | +12.5% Abyss PvE Damage Boost, +5% PvP Damage Tolerance, −5% PvE Damage Boost |
| 8 | +15% Abyss PvE Damage Boost, +10% PvP Damage Boost, −5% PvE Damage Boost |
| 12 | +20% Abyss PvE Damage Boost, +10% PvP Damage Tolerance, −5% PvE Damage Boost |

Two things to read out of that table. First, the **−5% PvE Damage Boost penalty
applies at every tier**, so Abyss armor is a dedicated PvP set and wearing it in
a dungeon is an active loss. Second, the 8- and 12-piece damage amplification
was **doubled** in the 2026-08-26 patch, which pushed full-set Abyss builds
further ahead for PvP.

PvE dungeon armor sets — `Anguish`, `Apex`, `Black Claw`, `Divine Canyon`,
`Division`, `Dramata`, `Unyielding`, `Faded Shadow`, `Lava Heart`, `Ancient
Spirit` and so on — share a naming prefix across slots but do not appear in the
set-bonus registry. Their value is in base stats, additional options and Soul
Imprint, not in set completion. So there is no reason to keep a matched PvE set;
mix by slot. Confidence **medium** — the absence of PvE set bonuses is inferred
from the set database being exhaustive at 17 entries.

## Progression path

1. **Story and field armor** to level 45. Enhance nothing past +3.
2. **Epic Expedition armor.** `Faith`, `Judicator` and `Red Agate` from Easy;
   `Corrupted Judicator`, `Black Claw`, `Divine Canyon`, `Division`, `Anguish`,
   `Unyielding`, `Dramata` and `Apex` from Normal and Hard. Drop rates for armor
   pieces are generous — cloaks and gloves land at 15 to 30% per roll on the
   higher difficulties — so a full Epic set arrives quickly.
3. **Unique Expedition and Sanctuary armor.** `Bakarma` (Draupnir and Abyssal
   Forge), `Durvati` (Dying Dramata's Nest — this is the 두르바티 set the Korean
   guides pair with the Bakarma weapon), `Nuakum` (Ferocious Horn Den), `Vakron`
   (Vakron Sky Island), `Basilus` (Citadel of the Fallen Daeva), `Awakened
   Alturadon` and `Nathara` (Chalice of Muspel, Hall of Illusion), `Abyssal`
   (Abyssal Forge). Unique armor drop rates are roughly 1 to 8% per roll, so
   this stage is where the guaranteed "ceiling" rewards and Gear Change Vouchers
   start to matter more than the per-roll chance.
4. **Unique crafted Draconic armor**, the Dragon Lord family, crafted under
   Tailoring.
5. **Heroic dropped armor.** `Ancient Spirit` (Abyssal Forge Normal, roughly
   0.75 to 2% per roll), `Faded Shadow` (Chalice of Muspel Normal, roughly 0.6
   to 1.5%) — this is the 침식된 잔영 set Korean guides recommend as the
   budget Heroic option — and `Lava Heart` (Citadel of the Fallen Daeva and
   Consumed Deus Research Base Hard, roughly 0.13 to 0.27%).
6. **Heroic crafted Draconic armor.** `Noble Dragon Lord` (Elyos) and
   `Horned Dragon Lord` (Asmodian), then `Genesis Dragon Lord` and
   `Nemesis Dragon Lord` added 2026-07-01, each with a Splendent version.

### Crafted versus dropped Heroic armor

This is the live decision for a Korean player right now, and the Korean press
answer is specific. Crafted Dragon Lord armor carries **5% PvE Damage Tolerance
per piece and one extra Soul Imprint line** compared with the dropped Faded
Shadow set. Dropped armor is much cheaper to obtain. The recommended
compromise is to craft the three slots that matter — **Helm, Breastplate and
Cloak** — and fill the remaining four with dropped Heroic pieces.

## Priorities and breakpoints

**Slot priority inside armor:**

1. **Breastplate** — the highest Damage Boost value of any armor slot.
2. **Cloak** — carries class passive-skill levels, which can be worth more than
   any stat line if it hits a key passive.
3. **Helm** — rolls Smite and Attack increase alongside HP.
4. **Gloves** — the only armor Combat Speed source, so it is a damage slot for
   any class whose rotation is attack-speed bound.
5. **Pauldrons** — Critical Damage Boost, valuable once your crit rate is high.
6. **Greaves and Boots** — fill last, except that Boots matter early for PvP
   and for movement-heavy boss fights.

**Survivability breakpoints that matter for content entry:**

- Get every armor slot to roughly +3 to +5 during the story. Korean and English
  guides both describe this as removing "paper character syndrome" — it is not
  about optimisation, it is about the story staying efficient.
- Standardise the whole set to +8 to +10 before you start pushing Hard
  difficulty. Below +10 each level is 1 Combat Power; above +10 it is 2, so +10
  is where enhancement starts paying double.
- Damage Resistance is capped at 80% damage reduction since Chapter 1 removed
  the individual stat caps, so stacking resistance has a hard ceiling.
- Attack Power and Defense Power stat caps were raised from 100% to 120% in
  April 2026.
- Enhance evenly, not deeply: all pieces to +1, then all to +2, and so on.
  Uneven armor wastes the doubled post-+10 Combat Power rate.

**How much of the power budget is armor?**

There is no published breakdown, so this is a judgement call from the
priorities every source agrees on. The weapon and Guard together are the
largest offensive block; the permanent Belt and Amulet are the best
Kinah-per-point purchases; armor is seven slots of mostly defensive value with
three offensive Soul Imprint lines hidden in it. The MMOEXP-style enhancement
order of weapon, then accessories, then armor is directionally right for a DPS
class, and the Vortex Gaming translation gives weapon, sub-weapon, ring,
bracelet as the attack-power order — armor is not in the top four. Treat any
specific percentage split you see quoted as invented.

## Costs and math

- Heroic Breastplate recipe, from the live data: 5× Artisan's Splendent
  Orichalcum Breastplate, 33× Artisan's Ultimate Refining Stone, 81× Tanned
  Robust Balaur Leather, 12× Wrathful Mind, 18× Wrathful Will, 28× Wrathful Ego,
  13× Maddened Wrathful Curse, 81× Radiant Orichalcum Ore, 47× Radiant Odyle.
  Combo chance 25%, Tailoring mastery requirement listed as Intermediate Lv 95.
- Korean guides note that Heroic **armor** direct-enhancement costs were cut
  substantially before Season 3, which is why the current advice is often to
  enhance a fresh Heroic armor piece from scratch rather than spend a limited
  Advanced Succession attempt on it.
- Season 3 Advanced Succession budget is three attempts. Two go to weapon and
  Guard; the third, if you spend it on armor, should go to Helm, Breastplate or
  Cloak.

## Traps and mistakes

- Wearing an Abyss set in PvE. The −5% PvE Damage Boost line is on every tier.
- Trying to complete a matched PvE armor set. PvE sets have no set bonus.
- Spending an Advanced Succession attempt on armor before the weapon and Guard.
- Deep-enhancing one armor piece while the rest sit at +2. The Combat Power
  formula rewards a flat set.
- Discarding Unique-grade armor duplicates. They are Succession Stone material
  and Soul Binding re-roll fodder — see [[Extraction and Refunds]].
- Assuming armor is class-restricted and passing on a good piece.

## Global launch notes

Unique is the ceiling. Abyss armour still exists as an AP purchase.

**Launch Scale Test:** base Defense is **much higher** than KR (example: level-86
Nokim helm **551 vs 260**). PvE armour craft still skippable. **Chest and helm
are PvP must-crafts** — Weapon Damage Tolerance sits on their soul-bind lines,
plus incoming-heal / endurance / status-resist options. See [[Crafting]] and
[[Global Launch Differences]].
