# Brooch, Pendant and Seal Slots

> **In one sentence:** three new equipment slots that NC added to Korean AION 2
> across 2026, one per Sanctuary release, and **none of them will exist for a
> Season 1 global player** — this note is a roadmap, not a to-do list.

## Why it matters

Read the global-launch verdict first and the mechanics second. Everything here
is Korean Season 3 and later content. If you are planning a global character for
2026-10-05, the correct action for all three systems is **none**.

It still matters for two reasons. First, it tells you what the ceiling looks
like: roughly 200 item level from a pair of top-grade Brooches, plus a Pendant
that eats hundreds of millions of Kinah, plus a Seal. That is a large amount of
power sitting outside the slots described in [[Accessories]], and it reframes
what "finished gear" means. Second, **NC has said out loud what the pattern is**
— Bracelet, Brooch and Seal are being rotated as Sanctuary rewards — which makes
the rest of the year predictable in a way most MMO roadmaps are not.

## The rotation pattern

NC's stated intent is that each new **Sanctuary** release brings a special
equipment slot as its headline reward, rotating through Bracelet, Brooch and
Seal. Observed sequence:

| Date (KR) | Sanctuary / patch | New slot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-05-27 | Chalice of Muspel (무스펠의 성배) | **Brooch** |
| 2026-07-01 | — | **Pendant** |
| 2026-09-09 | Snowfield of Sorrow | **Seal** (인장) |

The Bracelet predates this note and already exists in [[Accessories]]; it is the
first entry in the same rotation. Expect the pattern to continue: a new Sanctuary
roughly every six to ten weeks, each carrying a slot or a slot upgrade.

**Confidence high** on the three dates. **Confidence medium** on the rotation
being an ongoing commitment rather than a description of what has happened so
far — NC stated the intent, but stated intent and shipped roadmap diverge.

## Brooch (KR 2026-05-27)

The Brooch arrived with the Chalice of Muspel Sanctuary, the same patch that
brought Philosopher's Stone: Authority and the Genesis/Nemesis Dragon Lord gear
(see [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]]).

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Slot type | **Paired** — you equip two |
| Item level contribution | **~200 for the pair at top grade** |
| Acquisition | **Crafted**, gated behind **Jewelcrafting** |
| Introduced | KR 2026-05-27 |

The paired design is what makes it significant: 200 item level for two slots is
comparable to upgrading a whole armour tier, and it arrives all at once for
anyone with the crafting profession levelled.

The Jewelcrafting gate is the interesting part economically. It means Brooch
supply is controlled by crafters rather than by drop tables, which historically
in AION 2 has meant a period of extreme prices followed by a collapse as the
profession saturates. Players who levelled Jewelcrafting speculatively before
2026-05-27 did very well.

**Confidence medium** on the ~200 item level figure and on the Jewelcrafting
gate. Both come from community reporting rather than a patch note I could read
directly; the date is firm from the patch archive and NC's own English release.

## Pendant (KR 2026-07-01)

The Pendant is the best-documented of the three, because its enhancement cost
generated exactly the kind of Korean cost thread that makes Inven valuable.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Slot type | Single |
| Acquisition | **Quest-awarded**, not crafted or dropped |
| Enhancement ceiling | **+20** |
| Enhancement material | **Draconic Essence** (용의 정수) |
| Reported average cost to +20 | **~726 million Kinah** |
| Introduced | KR 2026-07-01 |

The quest acquisition matters: unlike the Brooch, everyone gets the base item.
The differentiation is entirely in the enhancement track, and that track is
brutally expensive.

**The ~726 million Kinah figure is an expected value, not a bill.** It comes from
a Korean cost analysis that works step by step from +0 to +20 with per-step
success rates and material costs, producing an average total. Half of players
will pay less; the tail pays far more. Do not budget 726M and expect to finish —
budget it and expect a coin flip.

For scale, 726 million Kinah is a very large number in the Korean economy: it is
the sort of figure that appears in "is this worth it?" threads rather than in
progression guides. The community answer has generally been that the Pendant is
worth pushing to a middle enhancement level and then leaving, with +20 reserved
for players who are otherwise finished.

This is one of the few places in the vault where a hard Kinah figure exists for
an endgame project, so it is worth recording precisely even though no global
player can act on it. Compare with the enhancement cost models in
[[Enchanting]].

**Confidence medium-high** on the ~726M average, which comes from a single
detailed source with a full per-step table; **high** on the date, the quest
acquisition, the +20 ceiling and Draconic Essence as the material.

## Seal / Sigil (인장, KR 2026-09-09)

The newest slot, arriving with the **Snowfield of Sorrow** Sanctuary.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Korean | 인장 |
| English renderings | **Seal**, also **Sigil** |
| Introduced | Announced for KR **2026-09-09** |
| Status as of 2026-09-06 | **Announced, not yet live** |

Almost nothing mechanical is documented, because at the time of writing the
patch has not shipped. Expect Sanctuary-gated acquisition consistent with the
Brooch and the rotation pattern, and expect an enhancement track.

**This note should be revisited after 2026-09-09.** It is the one section here
with a known expiry date.

**Confidence high** on the announcement and the date; **confidence low** on
everything else, because there is nothing else yet.

## Global launch notes

This is the section that answers the planning question, so it is stated flatly.

| Slot | KR date | In a Season 1 global build on 2026-10-05? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bracelet** | Pre-dates this note | **Yes** — see [[Accessories]] |
| **Brooch** | 2026-05-27 | **No** |
| **Pendant** | 2026-07-01 | **No** |
| **Seal** | 2026-09-09 | **No** |

All three arrived deep into Korean Season 3 and later, six to ten months after
the Korean launch. They sit far outside the 0–3,800 unlock and gate range that
[[Global Launch Differences]] records for the global Season 1 build, and the
Brooch in particular is tied to Genesis/Nemesis Dragon Lord-era content that also
depends on Heroic-grade equipment — which the launch build reportedly does not
include.

**Confidence high** on all three "no" verdicts.

Practical consequences for a global launch character:

- **Do not level Jewelcrafting for Brooches.** There will be no Brooches. Level
  it for what it does in Season 1 or not at all.
- **Your accessory slots are the Season 1 set only** — see [[Accessories]] for
  what actually exists.
- **Kinah planning is much simpler at launch.** No 726-million-Kinah sink exists,
  which means [[Enchanting]] and [[Succession and Transfer]] are the real Kinah
  competitors in your first months.
- **Do not hoard Draconic Essence** if a launch-build item by that name appears —
  its Pendant use will not exist yet, and it may have unrelated uses.

**When will they arrive for global?** Unknown. If global follows the Korean
content order at a compressed pace, the Brooch is the first of the three and
would land with whatever global calls its third or fourth content wave. No
schedule has been announced; [[Global Launch Differences]] records none.

## Progression path

For a Korean or Taiwanese player, in order:

1. **Bracelet** — already yours, and the Empyrean Lord attributes roll on it at
   an unusually high rate, so it rewards soul-imprint work
   ([[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]]).
2. **Pendant** — do the quest, take it to a middle enhancement level. Everyone
   can get the base item, so this is the cheapest item level of the three.
3. **Brooch pair** — if you have Jewelcrafting or the Kinah to buy from someone
   who does. ~200 item level for the pair is the largest single jump here.
4. **Seal** — when it ships.
5. **Pendant to +20** — last, and only if you are otherwise finished. ~726M
   average.

For a global Season 1 player: **none of the above.** Return to this note when the
Brooch is announced.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Planning a global launch character around any of these slots.** They will not
  be there.
- **Budgeting 726M Kinah as a fixed Pendant cost.** It is an average with a long
  tail.
- **Pushing the Pendant to +20 before the rest of your set is finished.** It is
  the worst Kinah-per-item-level rate in the game until everything else is done.
- **Levelling Jewelcrafting speculatively on global** on the strength of the
  Korean Brooch economy. The timing is wrong by many months.
- **Confusing the Pendant with the Necklace.** They are separate slots; the
  Necklace is in [[Accessories]] and exists at launch.
- **Confusing Seal (인장) with Sigil in other MMOs' vocabulary.** Both English
  words are in use for the same Korean item; there is only one slot.
