# Equipment

> **In one sentence:** equipment is the largest single block of your character's
> power in AION 2, and it is not one system but roughly seventeen slots layered
> with five or six independent upgrade systems each, so the whole chapter is
> really a resource-allocation problem rather than a gear-acquisition problem.

## Why it matters

Every hard gate in AION 2 is expressed as an item level or a Combat Power
number, and both are dominated by equipment. Current Korean live content asks
for item level 3,800 to enter the newest Expedition on Normal, 4,500 for Hard
difficulty, and 4,500 plus 500.0k Combat Power for the Rift of Time and Space
Contest. Skills, [[Arcana]], [[Pantheon]] and [[Daevanion Boards]] all
contribute, but none of them move the number as fast as replacing a weapon or
taking a slot from Unique to Heroic grade.

The trap is that equipment competes with itself. Enhancement Stones, Kinah,
Attunement Stones and Succession Stones are all shared across every slot, so
spending on a piece you will replace in two weeks is the single most common way
players stall. The chapter therefore has two jobs: tell you what exists, and
tell you the order to buy it in.

### The layered model of gear power

Think of any single equipped item as a stack. Each layer is a separate system
with its own currency and its own failure mode.

1. **Grade.** Common, Rare, Epic, Unique, Heroic. Grade sets the size of every
   other layer: how many Soul Imprint lines the item can hold, how many
   manastone sockets it has, and whether it is eligible for Breakthrough at all.
   See [[Gear Tiers and Sources]].
2. **Item level.** A per-item number printed on the tooltip, not the same thing
   as your character item level. Ludra's Bracelet is Heroic at item level 98,
   the Noble Dragon Lord Fist is Heroic at 108, and the Splendent Nemesis Dragon
   Lord Breastplate is Heroic at 134, so grade alone does not fix item level.
   See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]].
3. **Base stats.** Min and Max Attack on weapons, Defense and HP on armor,
   Attack and Defense on accessories. Fixed by the item.
4. **Additional options.** The fixed bonus lines the item carries by virtue of
   its set or source, for example the flat 5% PvE Damage Boost on crafted
   Draconic weapons or the 5% PvE Damage Tolerance on crafted Draconic armor.
5. **Enhancement** to +20 — see [[Enchanting]].
6. **Breakthrough / Exceed** to +5, available only from Unique grade upward —
   see [[Breakthrough]].
7. **Soul Imprint and Soul Binding**, which choose six random sub-stat lines out
   of a large pool and let you re-roll them — see
   [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]]. This is where the real long-term grind
   lives; the base item is comparatively easy to obtain.
8. **Manastones** (weapons and armor) and **Spirit Stones** (accessories) in
   five sockets, plus one Godstone socket on weapons — see
   [[Manastones Spirit Stones and Godstones]].
9. **Potential**, an Abyss-gear and dungeon-gear amplification layer introduced
   in March 2026 that adds PvP Damage Boost or Tolerance in four stages.
10. **Succession and Transfer**, the systems that move layers 5 through 7 from
    an old item onto a new one so that replacing gear does not reset your
    investment — see [[Succession and Transfer]].

The practical consequence is that a fresh Heroic drop is usually *worse* than
your fully invested Unique piece until you inherit into it. Never equip a new
item on grade alone.

## Progression path

This is the shape of the whole chapter. Each stage assumes the previous one.

1. **Story gear to level 45.** Take whatever drops, enhance nothing deeply.
   Start the Noble Belt and Revelation Amulet immediately because they are
   permanent.
2. **Level 45 baseline.** The main-story quest weapon, the level 45 Ascension
   quest bracelet, and Epic dungeon armor from the Easy and Normal Expedition
   tiers. Clear every Sealed Dungeon and Stronghold once on both faction maps
   and collect every Empyrean Trace — these are one-time, they never come back
   cheaper, and they are the only source of Belt and Amulet scrolls.
3. **Unique dungeon and Abyss set.** Expedition Conquest gear plus Abyss-point
   accessories. This is where your character becomes farm-capable.
4. **Unique crafted Draconic gear** for the slots where crafted beats dropped —
   weapon, Guard, and rings above all.
5. **Heroic transition.** Sanctuary weapons via Equipment Change Tickets,
   Heroic crafted Draconic weapons and armor, and Advanced Succession to carry
   your enhancement and Soul Imprint work up a grade.
6. **Optimisation forever.** Soul Imprint re-rolls, manastone packages,
   Potential, and a second set for the other side of the PvE/PvP split.

## Priorities and breakpoints

Every Korean guide, the Vortex Gaming translations and the English community
agree on the same ordering, and it has not changed across three seasons.

**Order of investment across slots:**

1. **Main-hand weapon.** It multiplies every skill coefficient and it is the
   only slot that both damage and clear-speed scale off directly.
2. **Guard (sub-weapon).** Treated as a second weapon in KR guides; it is the
   other slot that can roll Weapon Damage Boost and Combat Speed on Soul
   Imprint, which are the two best lines in the game.
3. **Noble Belt and Revelation Amulet.** Not because they are strong per point,
   but because they are permanent. You will never replace them, so every point
   spent is kept forever. The Korean consensus is to push both to Unique +10
   and then stop.
4. **Rings**, because ring Soul Imprint rolls Mastery skill levels, and the
   crafted ring gets one extra imprint line over the dropped ring.
5. **Chest, then Cloak and Helm**, the three armor slots with the highest
   value per point.
6. **Everything else**, evenly, to whatever level clears the content you are
   actually running.

**Breakpoints worth stopping at:**

- +10 on anything is the first real milestone; below +10 each enhancement level
  gives 1 Combat Power, above +10 it gives 2.
- Unique +10 on the Belt and the Amulet is the documented cost-efficiency
  ceiling for early endgame.
- +20 is the enhancement cap, and Breakthrough +5 sits on top of it.
- Do not push Rare-grade equipment past +5.

## Costs and math

- A Heroic crafted Draconic weapon costs roughly 280 to 300 million Kinah if
  every material is bought on the broker (KR, Season 3). Gathering and season
  shop materials cut this substantially.
- Converting 2 Equipment Change Tickets plus 20,000,000 Kinah yields Ludra's
  Bracelet outright, which is far cheaper than farming the 2% drop.
- Advanced Succession has a fixed maximum cost of 50,000,000 Kinah since
  Chapter 1.
- Ordinary Succession success rates were 100% for the first two attempts, then
  80/60/40/20% — until Chapter 1 removed the count cap and made it 100%.
  Advanced Succession retains a per-season attempt limit.

Exact per-level Enhancement Stone and Kinah tables belong in [[Enchanting]] and
[[Breakthrough]].

## Traps and mistakes

- Equipping a fresh higher-grade drop before inheriting your old investment
  into it. You lose enhancement, Breakthrough and Soul Imprint at once.
- Spreading Enhancement Stones evenly across seventeen slots in the first week.
- Deep-enhancing the level 20 to 30 story gear.
- Soul Imprinting a tradable item you intended to sell — imprinting makes items
  untradable permanently.
- Extracting or converting Unique-grade duplicates before checking whether they
  are Succession Stone or Soul Binding fodder.
- Assuming PvE gear works in the Abyss. Abyss sets carry an explicit
  −5% PvE Damage Boost line alongside their Abyss bonuses.

## Global launch notes

Everything above is Korean live service as of the 2026-09-04 patch. The global
release is 2026-10-05 with five-day Advance Access from 2026-09-30. Two
equipment-relevant differences are being reported for the global build, both at
medium confidence because the sourcing is thin: Heroic-grade equipment will not
be available at launch from drops or crafting and is planned for a later patch,
and Hard-mode Conquest dungeons will not be present at launch. If both hold,
the global ceiling at launch is Unique grade, which makes the Belt, the Amulet
and crafted Unique Draconic gear relatively more important and makes Advanced
Succession irrelevant for a while.

The item-level *bar* at launch is **0–3,800**. Dungeon *doors* are the Korean
Season 1 set (200 / 1,000 / 1,600 / 2,200 / 2,400 / ~2,700). 3,800 is what you
fill with enchants, boards and Arcana after Ludra — not a Chapter 1 dungeon
floor. 4,000+ is out. See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]].

      consumables in the item database.

      No Korean source uses a matching term.
