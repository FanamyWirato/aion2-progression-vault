# Potential Enhancement

> **In one sentence:** Potential (잠재력) is a small, guaranteed, four-step
> multiplier bolted onto raid-grade gear — no failure, no randomness, no
> destruction — and it is the only gear system in AION 2 where spending the
> material always works.

## Why it matters

Every other system in this chapter is a gamble. [[Enchanting]] can fail,
[[Breakthrough]] can fail, [[Runes]] can destroy the item, imprint lines roll
random. Potential does none of that: you feed it stones, it advances a step, and
the step is worth a fixed percentage.

It matters for two reasons beyond the numbers. First, **it is a sink for
duplicate raid gear** that competes with attunement fodder and extraction, so it
changes what you do with drops. Second, **its steps do not survive
[[Succession and Transfer]]**, which means it is the one investment you must
deliberately *not* make on a stepping-stone item.

The values are individually small. Four steps on a weapon is +2.4% PvE damage.
Across a full set it is a real but unspectacular multiplier — the kind of thing
that matters at a progression wall and nowhere else.

## How it works

Potential is a track on the item, separate from its enhancement level, its
breakthrough state and its soul imprint. It has **four steps**, each of which is
**guaranteed** when you pay for it.

| Slot type | Value per step | Value at four steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Weapon** | +0.6% PvE damage | **+2.4%** |
| **Armour** | +0.3% PvE resistance | **+1.2%** |
| **Accessories** | +0.3% PvE damage | **+1.2%** |

Note that these are explicitly **PvE-tagged** values. The PvP equivalent is a
separate track — see Abyss Potential below.

| Rule | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible items | **Unique grade or higher**, obtained from **Expedition Conquest** or a **Sanctuary** |
| Failure | **None.** Each step is guaranteed |
| Destruction | None |
| Steps | Four, then the track is complete |
| Material | **Potential Amplification Stone** (잠재력 증폭석) |
| Inheritance | **Steps do not carry across.** The new item starts at zero |

### Where the stones come from

Potential Amplification Stones are produced by **[[Material Conversion]]** from
**equipment of the same difficulty tier**. That is the design intent and it is
the important part: the system is a laundering mechanism for gear you cannot use.
Cleared a Hard-difficulty expedition and got the wrong class's Unique chest?
Convert it into stones and push your weapon's Potential instead.

This puts Potential in direct competition with two other uses for the same
duplicate: attunement fodder ([[Attunement and Option Rerolls]]) and material
extraction ([[Extraction and Refunds]]). In Korean practice, attunement fodder
wins while your option lines are unfinished, and Potential wins afterwards.

### Abyss Potential (PvP)

Added **2026-04-08**, roughly five weeks after the PvE system. Same structure,
separate track, separate material: **Potential Amplification Stone: Abyss**
(잠재력 증폭석: 어비스), applied to Abyss PvP equipment and granting PvP-tagged
values rather than PvE ones.

The material comes from Abyss content, which ties it to
[[Abyss Points and Ranks]]. The split exists because AION 2 keeps PvE and PvP
stat tags rigorously separate throughout — see [[Stat Reference]] — and a single
Potential track would have leaked PvE power into the Abyss.

**Confidence medium** on the Abyss variant's details. The date and the item name
are firm; the per-step values are assumed to mirror the PvE table and I did not
find them stated.

## Unlock and gates

| Thing | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| The system existing | KR patch **2026-03-04** |
| Eligible item | Unique or higher, from Expedition Conquest or Sanctuary |
| Ineligible | Anything below Unique; anything from other sources (crafted, quest, field) |
| Material | Potential Amplification Stones, made by material conversion from same-difficulty gear |
| Abyss variant | KR **2026-04-08**, Abyss equipment, Abyss stones |

The source restriction is the sharpest gate. A crafted Unique weapon — the item
most Korean guides tell you to build — is not obviously eligible under a strict
reading of "from Expedition Conquest or Sanctuary". I could not confirm whether
crafted Unique gear can take Potential; see Open questions.

## Inputs: currencies, materials, sources

| Resource | Feeds | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Potential Amplification Stone** | PvE Potential steps | [[Material Conversion]] from same-difficulty Unique-or-above equipment |
| **Potential Amplification Stone: Abyss** | Abyss Potential steps | Abyss PvP content |
| **Amplification Stone Fragment** | Converts up into stones | Nightmare exchange shop and other content rewards |
| Kinah | Conversion and application fees | Everything |

The fragment sub-unit appears in the [[Nightmare Mode]] exchange shop alongside
Soul Codex: Reset, which is worth knowing when you are budgeting Phantasmal
Fragments — the same currency buys reroll consumables and Potential materials,
and they are not equally valuable to you at every stage.

## Progression path

1. **Do not touch Potential until your set is final.** Steps are destroyed by
   inheritance, so any Potential on a stepping-stone item is thrown away.
2. **Weapon first.** +0.6% per step is double the armour and accessory rate, and
   the weapon is the item you keep longest.
3. **Accessories next** (+0.3% damage), then armour (+0.3% resistance) if you are
   pushing a defensive wall.
4. **Convert duplicates as they arrive**, but only once your attunement work is
   done — before that, duplicates are worth more as fodder.
5. **Run the Abyss track separately** if you PvP seriously; the materials do not
   cross over.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Weapon to four steps is the only unambiguous priority.** +2.4% PvE damage
  for guaranteed materials is the best rate in the system.
- **Armour Potential is the last thing to do in the entire chapter.** +1.2%
  resistance across a set is real but is beaten by essentially every other
  investment.
- **Free-to-play players should do the weapon and stop.** The material supply is
  gated on clearing content you can convert, which is exactly the content
  free-to-play players run least.
- **There is no reason to stop below four steps** on an item you have decided to
  keep, because there is no failure and no escalating risk — only the material
  cost.

## Costs and math

**No public cost table exists.** The number of Potential Amplification Stones per
step, the conversion ratio from equipment to stones, and any Kinah fee are not
published in any source I could reach. This is the single largest gap in this
note and it is why the frontmatter confidence is medium-high rather than high.

What is firm:

- **Four steps, guaranteed, no failure.** So the total cost is deterministic once
  the per-step price is known — unlike every other system in this chapter, there
  is no expected-value calculation to do.
- **+2.4% / +1.2% / +1.2%** at full track, PvE-tagged.
- **The real cost is opportunity cost**, not Kinah: every duplicate converted
  into Potential stones is a duplicate not spent on attunement.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Applying Potential before Inheritance.** The steps do not transfer. This is
  the expensive mistake and it is easy to make, because Potential feels like
  enhancement, and enhancement levels *do* transfer.
- **Converting duplicates while your imprint lines are unfinished.** Fodder first,
  Potential later.
- **Assuming PvE Potential helps in the Abyss.** The values are PvE-tagged. You
  need the Abyss track and the Abyss stones.
- **Spending Nightmare Phantasmal Fragments on Amplification Stone Fragments
  early.** Soul Codex: Reset is the better buy while your options are still bad.
- **Expecting it to matter.** +2.4% on a weapon is a tiebreaker, not a
  transformation. Finish [[Enchanting]], [[Breakthrough]] and your imprint lines
  first.

## Global launch notes

**Reopened.** This vault marked Potential out because it arrived in Korea on
**2026-03-04**. The Launch Scale Test showed the Potential UI on **more gear
types, including crafted weapons**. Built-in PvE Damage Boost was missing from
those same pieces, which is the obvious reason to move the percentage onto a
stone.

Welps could not find a PvE Potential-stone source and wondered whether PvP
stones apply. **Do not budget stones** until Advance Access shows a drop or
shop row. If it ships, duplicate Unique gear has a third use again and the
"crafted has no PvE% so dungeon wins" argument from Korean mid-game guides
does not transfer. Abyss Potential (2026-04-08) is still later.

See [[Crafting]] and [[Global Launch Differences]].

## Open questions

- [ ] **Cost per step.** How many Potential Amplification Stones does each of the
      four steps take, and does the cost escalate across steps? Nothing published.
- [ ] **The material conversion ratio.** How much same-difficulty equipment
      converts into one stone. The aion2hub conversion browser would answer this.
- [ ] **Whether crafted Unique gear is eligible.** The stated gate is "Unique or
      above from Expedition Conquest or Sanctuary". Crafted gear is the main
      endgame target for many players; if it is ineligible, that reshapes the
      whole priority list.
- [ ] **Abyss Potential per-step values.** Assumed to mirror the PvE table
      (+0.6% / +0.3% / +0.3%, PvP-tagged); not confirmed.
- [ ] **Whether Potential survives Breakthrough or Transfer**, as distinct from
      Inheritance. Only the Inheritance case is documented.
- [ ] **Whether the four-step cap is per item or per slot**, i.e. whether a
      replacement item in the same slot starts fresh. Almost certainly per item,
      but the Inheritance rule is the only evidence.
