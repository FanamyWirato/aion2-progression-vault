# Weapons

> **In one sentence:** the main hand and the Guard are the two slots that
> multiply every skill you press, they are the first thing you upgrade in every
> phase of the game, and because they are class-locked they are also the two
> slots where a wrong purchase is hardest to recover from.

## Why it matters

Weapon Attack feeds directly into skill coefficients, so a weapon upgrade
raises every ability at once rather than adding a flat bonus. Korean guides put
it more bluntly: the main hand and the Guard decide your hunting speed, and
compromising on their options makes every farm loop measurably slower.

There is a second, mechanical reason weapons come first. The two strongest Soul
Imprint lines in the game — **Weapon Damage Boost** and **Combat Speed** — only
appear on the weapon and the Guard. No armor slot except Gloves can roll Combat
Speed at all, and nothing else can roll Weapon Damage Boost. So the weapon and
Guard are not merely the biggest slots, they are the only route to two stats
that scale everything else. See [[Stat Reference]].

The weapon also carries the only Godstone socket on the character, which makes
it the delivery mechanism for that system — see
[[Manastones Spirit Stones and Godstones]].

## How it works

### The Guard (sub-weapon)

The Guard, written 가더 in Korean and sometimes transliterated "Guarder", is a
universal off-hand equipped by all nine classes — it is not a shield restricted
to tanks. It appears in every gear set alongside the class weapons and it is
crafted by the same profession as melee weapons.

Korean setting guides treat the Guard as a co-equal first priority with the main
hand, and Season 3 succession advice is explicitly "spend your first two
Advanced Succession attempts on the weapon and the Guard". Do not treat it as an
accessory.

### Weapon stat profile

From the live item data, a top-tier crafted weapon carries:

- Min Attack and Max Attack as the headline pair
- Accuracy, Critical Hit, Block
- Parry Damage Reduction Rate and Amount
- A fixed additional option, for example 5% PvE Damage Boost on crafted
  Draconic weapons
- Five manastone sockets and one Godstone socket
- Six selectable Soul Imprint lines out of a pool that includes Combat Speed,
  Weapon Damage Boost, Status Effect Chance, Damage Boost, Multi-hit Chance,
  Might, Precision, Attack, Critical Hit, Critical Attack, Back Attack, Front
  Attack, Accuracy, Block, HP, MP, and one class skill at level 1
- Enchantable to +20, which adds a further flat Min and Max Attack
- Exceed (Breakthrough) to +5, which adds flat Attack plus a percentage Attack
  increase

The Soul Imprint priority for weapon and Guard, from the Korean consensus, is
Weapon Damage Boost and Combat Speed first; then Might, Multi-hit Chance or
Critical Hit; then Accuracy or elemental damage if you are missing in bosses.

## Progression path

The ladder below is reconstructed from the Korean patch chronology and the live
item database. Names are the English client names where I could confirm them.

### Levelling and first endgame (launch era, still the global-launch shape)

1. Story and field drops, Common through Epic.
2. Epic Expedition weapons on Easy and Normal difficulty: the `Faith`,
   `Red Agate`, `Judicator` tier from Urugugu Canyon, Krao Cave and Fire Temple
   Easy, then `Corrupted Judicator`, `Black Claw`, `Divine Canyon`, `Division`,
   `Anguish` and `Unyielding` from Normal and Hard.
3. **The level 45 main-story quest weapon**, a Unique, and the first weapon
   worth enhancing.
4. Unique Expedition weapons: `Kromede` and `Enraged Kromede` from Fire Temple,
   `Rupture` from Krao Cave, `Aulamus` from Urugugu Canyon.

### Season 2 and 3 (KR, Jan to Jun 2026)

5. `Bakarma` and `Afflicted Bakarma` Unique weapons, from Draupnir, Cradle of
   Nihility and the Abyssal Forge Sanctuary. This is the tier English SEO
   articles usually mean when they say "Crimson or Obsidian weapon".
6. `Nathara` Unique weapons from the Season 3 Hall of Illusion expedition
   (환영의 회랑) and Chalice of Muspel.
7. **Unique crafted Draconic weapons**, the "Dragon Lord" family. Each tier has
   an Elyos name and an Asmodian name: `Star` / `Dark`, `Wise` / `Ebony`,
   `True` / `White` Dragon Lord, plus the April 2026 additions the KR notes call
   "Mysterious Dragon" and "Red Dragon". Each has a `Splendent` version produced
   by a 25% combo proc.
8. **Heroic Sanctuary weapons**, obtained by cube drop or, far more reliably,
   by converting Equipment Change Tickets. The chain is
   `Ludra's` (Abyssal Forge: Ludra) → `Eroded Ruler` (Corroded Decontamination
   Facility) → `Lava Heart` (Muspel's Chalice), and Succession Crafting can walk
   a weapon up that chain rather than re-farming each step. The Ludra's weapons
   are named per class: Blade of Extinction, Scale Sword, Claw, Fatal Bow, Fatal
   Mace, Fist of Extinction, Grimoire, Heavenbound Staff, Abyssal Jade, plus
   Ludra's Heart as the Guard.
9. **Heroic crafted Draconic weapons**: `Noble Dragon Lord` (Elyos) and
   `Horned Dragon Lord` (Asmodian), and their Splendent versions. "Horn Dragon
   weapon" in English guides is this tier.

### Chapter 1 and later (KR, Jul 2026 onward)

10. `Genesis Dragon Lord` (Elyos) and `Nemesis Dragon Lord` (Asmodian) weapons
    and Guards, added 2026-05-27, crafted by inheriting the Splendent
    Noble/Horned gear into them. Splendent versions of these are the current
    ceiling, at per-item Item Lv 134 for the armor equivalent.
11. Abyss weapons run a parallel military-rank ladder that Succession Crafting
    also walks: `Decanus` → `Centurion` → `Tribunus`, and separately
    `Legion Commander` → `Elite Legion Commander` → `Praetorian Captain`
    (translated in some notes as Royal Guard Captain or Guard Captain). These
    are Abyss-Point purchases with an explicit −5% PvE Damage Boost penalty, so
    they are a PvP set, not an upgrade to your PvE weapon. See [[PvP Gear]] and
    [[Abyss Points and Ranks]].

### A naming caution

English aggregator sites frequently cite a "Crimson → Obsidian → Horn Dragon"
weapon path. I could not find items with the literal names Crimson or Obsidian
in either the aion2hub or questlog item databases. The most likely reading is
that Crimson and Obsidian are loose renderings of the Red Dragon and
Ebony/Dark Dragon Lord crafted tiers, and Horn Dragon is Horned Dragon Lord.
Treat that specific three-step path as **low confidence** and use the Dragon
Lord family names above instead.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Weapon first, always, with one exception.** If you are dying to normal boss
  patterns rather than failing a damage check, fix survivability first. This is
  the single caveat in an otherwise unanimous rule.
- **Guard immediately after.** Two Advanced Succession attempts per season go
  here before anything else.
- Enhancement targets by phase: +5 to +8 while the story is still the main
  activity, +10 as soon as you are farming dungeons, +12 on a piece you are
  confident you are keeping, and +20 plus Breakthrough only on a final weapon.
- Do not chase a first-tier Soul Imprint line on the weapon before every other
  slot has second-tier lines. The Korean phrase for the correct approach is
  "staircase growth": raise the floor everywhere, then lift one slot at a time.

## Costs and math

- A Heroic crafted Draconic weapon is roughly **280 to 300 million Kinah** if
  every material comes from the broker (KR, Season 3). Season shop and
  self-gathered materials reduce this considerably, and material prices fell
  through Season 3 as supply caught up.
- Crafting proficiency raises the success rate, and the maximum proficiency was
  raised from 50 to 70 in April 2026, so pushing proficiency before attempting a
  Heroic craft is cheaper than eating failures.
- Farming a Sanctuary weapon without tickets is slow. A community estimate for
  the first Sanctuary put a class-correct Ludra weapon at roughly 2.3% per cube
  with six cubes a week, about 13.8% weekly, or around seven to eight weeks —
  versus about three weeks using the guaranteed-reward ticket route. Treat the
  exact percentages as **medium confidence**; they come from one Inven post,
  but the conclusion that tickets beat drops is corroborated by the patch notes
  that added the ticket conversions.
- Breakthrough consumes roughly 900,000 Kinah per step in addition to stones.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Buying a weapon you cannot inherit into.** Weapons can only be succeeded
  from the same class's weapon type. Check the target before you spend.
- **Registering the source weapon in a preset.** Gear registered in an
  equipment preset does not appear in the Succession material list. Unregister
  it first.
- **Soul Imprinting a tradable crafted weapon you meant to resell.** Imprinting
  converts it to untradable.
- **Buying the Abyss weapon for PvE.** The Abyss sets pay Abyss PvE Damage Boost
  and PvP stats while subtracting 5% general PvE Damage Boost.
- **Enhancing a stopgap past +10.** Below +10 the enhancement is cheap and each
  level is worth 1 Combat Power; the cost curve steepens sharply afterwards
  while the item's remaining lifespan does not.

### Keeper versus stopgap

Ask three questions before spending on a weapon:

1. **Is there a Succession path off it?** If yes, it is a legitimate keeper even
   if it is only Unique, because your investment moves forward. Crafted
   Draconic weapons and Sanctuary weapons both have documented chains.
2. **Is the next tier gated by content I can actually enter?** If the Heroic
   weapon needs a 10-player Sanctuary you cannot fill, the Unique crafted weapon
   is your real endgame weapon for months and deserves full investment.
3. **Is a season boundary near?** Advanced Succession attempts reset each
   season, and Korean guides explicitly advise not to force a top-tier craft
   just before a season ends.

A stopgap gets +10 and nothing else: no Breakthrough, no Soul Imprint re-rolls,
no superior manastones. A keeper gets everything.

## Global launch notes

Unique is the ceiling. Hard-mode Conquest remains reported-absent.

**Launch Scale Test:** global weapons have **higher raw Attack** than KR
(Chromier dagger min **357 vs 298**; crafted Splendent Ebony Dragon dagger
item level **102 / 424–545** vs **92 / 326–419**; Ludra bow Unique **470–591**
vs KR Heroic **384–483**). Ludra is **one or two unique extensions**, not a
Heroic weapon. Built-in PvE Damage Boost is **missing**; Potential stones were
usable on crafted weapons. Extra soul-bind line is still the crafting argument.
See [[Crafting]], [[Potential Enhancement]], [[Global Launch Differences]].
