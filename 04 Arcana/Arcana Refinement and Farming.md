# Arcana Refinement and Farming

> **In one sentence:** Transcendence dungeons produce cards and crystal shards,
> extraction turns unwanted cards into crystals, and refinement turns crystals plus
> Kinah into a card with the skills you actually chose — and the whole chain is
> throttled by weekly ticket refills, not by skill.

## Why it matters

Arcana is the most time-gated system in the game. Cards do not drop in the open
world, from [[Crafting]] or from [[PvP Progression]]; they come from one dungeon
track with a capped number of weekly reward refills, so your progress is close to
fixed per week regardless of how much you play. Getting the routine right therefore
matters more than getting the theory right: the theory only tells you what to spend
a resource on, the routine determines how much you have. The blunt version, from a
KR crafting guide: start Arcana work when you can farm the top stage, because the
reward gap to the stage below is roughly twofold.

## How it works

**Farming.** Clear a Transcendence (초월) stage, then open the Od Energy reward
cube. Cards, crystal shards, Training Arcana and Kinah come out. Which cards can
appear is a function of the dungeon; which grades can appear is a function of the
stage.

**Extraction (추출).** Feed unwanted cards in. Each card advances a gauge from 0%
toward 100% by an amount set by its grade, with higher enhancement levels adding
more; at 100% you receive one crystal and the gauge resets. The card is consumed.
Vigor, Magic, Frenzy and Pure Blood cards yield Mysterious Crystal; Punishment,
Protection and Indomitability cards yield Noble Crystal.

**Refinement (연성).** Spend crystals plus Kinah to create a card of a chosen slot
and chosen set, designating one or more of its skill lines. This is the only way
to aim at a specific skill. The cooldown was removed on 2026-02-25, so it is
limited purely by materials.

**Enhancement.** Consumes other Arcana cards plus Training Arcana plus Kinah to
raise a card from +0 to +5, rerolling one existing skill line upward per level.
Feeding the identical card grants bonus experience. Training Arcana comes in
Lesser, Intermediate and Greater tiers, and the maximum usable per action rose
from 10 to 100 on 2026-02-25.

### S-clear and stage progression

At launch, moving to the next stage required an S-grade clear, scored on clear
time and party deaths. Guides recommend keeping party resurrections to no more
than about seven on high stages, and note that S-grade requires trash kills as
well as bosses: in Shattered Arkanis the target is 120 enemies and a named monster
credits 45, so the standard route clears the roughly thirty mobs at the entrance.

**This gating no longer exists.** On 2026-02-25 both KR and TW removed the stage
unlock condition on every Transcendence dungeon, including the older ones. S rank
still matters for [[Titles and Achievements]] and ranking rewards, and the same
patch stopped counting mobs killed after the final boss appears toward ranking
score, but it is no longer a progression gate. A global client that still gates
stages behind S-clears is on a pre-February snapshot.

### Which stages are worth opening boxes on

The rule that supersedes everything below: clear the highest stage your item level
supports and open the boxes there, since a high-stage clear also awards lower-stage
rewards.

| Stage | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 1-2 | Do not open the reward boxes. The grade ceiling is too low to be worth the Od Energy. |
| 3 | Rare (green) cards become possible. Chalice becomes possible. |
| 4 | Rare card at 100%, plus a 20% chance of a second. This is where farming genuinely starts. |
| 5 | Chalice guaranteed. |
| 6 | Heritage (blue) grade begins. One community guide recommends repeated stage 6 S-clears here. |
| 7-8 | Heritage available as bonus-roll rewards as well as the base reward. |
| 9 | Unique Compass and Unique Bell become possible. |
| 10 | Unique Chalice becomes possible, and the reward pool is roughly double stage 9. |

### Guaranteed per-clear rewards

Mirror of Scarlet Desire costs 40 tickets to enter and awards, on a three-boss
clear, one Noble and one Splendent Noble Crystal shard per boss at 100%, 250,000
Kinah per boss, 3-5 Intermediate and 4-6 Lesser Training Arcana, a Soul Codex per
boss, 2-6 Unstable Stigma Shards on a weighted roll, and 40 Yustiel's Trace
fragments. Deus Research Base and Shattered Arkanis instead award Amplify Stone
Fragments, a Soul Codex and 100,000 Kinah per boss.

Submerged Life Temple's guaranteed shards scale linearly with stage on a
three-boss clear: 5, 10, 15 and 20 Mysterious Crystal shards for stages 1 to 4,
the same counts again in Harmonious Crystal shards, and 50, 60, 70 and 80 Marcutan
Trace shards. Marcutan Trace feeds the Marcutan guardian-power panel, which belongs
to [[Pantheon Stats]] rather than here, but it is a reason to run this dungeon even
when you do not need cards.

On 2026-05-21 KR adjusted the quantity of Arcana from the Transcendence final boss,
and a later patch raised the Unique Arcana count per stage in Mirror of Scarlet
Desire while removing Rare and Heritage Arcana from that dungeon's Od Energy pool
in favour of Training Arcana. The direction of travel is fewer junk cards, more
fuel and more Uniques.

## Progression path: a weekly farming routine

Assumes you are past the item-level gate for the top tier of your current
generation's dungeon; adjust downward if not. **Daily, about fifteen minutes:**
collect the Transcendence ticket refill, since tickets accumulate but a full bar is
wasted capacity, then run the highest stage you can clear cleanly and open the
boxes there. Never open boxes on stages 1-2.

**Weekly, in this order**

1. **Awakening Battle first.** Push your weekly cumulative score to at least
   3,000, and to 6,000 if you can, for up to five Mysterious Crystals. Highest
   crystal value per unit of time, and it hard-resets weekly.
2. **Spend the whole weekly Transcendence reward refill** on whichever dungeon
   carries the generation you need: Submerged Life Temple for Vigor and Magic
   Chalices, Deus or Arkanis for the slot-specific cards, Mirror of Scarlet Desire
   for Season 3.
3. **Buy Mysterious Crystals from the season shop** at 25,000 Oath Coins while
   your extraction gauge is still unproductive. Stop once you discard Uniques
   regularly.
4. **Storage pass.** Sort the week's cards into keepers with a valid skill line,
   fuel (Heritage and Unique with no useful line), and extraction fodder (Normal
   and Rare with nothing). Extract the third pile, and keep the 800-card cap clear
   enough that it never blocks a good drop.
5. **Refine only for a designated skill you actually planned.** Never
   speculatively. Frenzy or Pure Blood for any older-generation Chalice, never
   Vigor or Magic.
6. **Enhance only the cards you have decided to keep**, and only after the Chalice
   is settled. The KR consensus order is to leave everything roughly enhanced until
   a good Chalice appears, then reroll Parchment and Compass to match it. Do not
   run Expedition content hoping for Arcana; it drops no cards, so save it for the
   nights when your tickets are spent but Od Energy remains.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **The top stage of your current dungeon is the only stage worth farming
  seriously**, because the reward gap to the stage below is roughly twofold.
- **Designate three skills, not four.** The crystal cost scales with the number of
  designations, so three designations buys meaningfully more attempts per week and
  reaches a good roll faster.
- **Grade before enhancement, always.** See [[Arcana Cards and Types]].
- **Buy crystals early, extract late.** The crossover is roughly the point where
  you start discarding Unique cards every week.
- **Chalice before Parchment and Compass**, because the latter two get rerolled to
  match whatever the Chalice gave you.

| Normal | 2% | 50 |
| Rare (green) | 4% | 25 |
| Heritage (blue) | 8% | 12.5 |

Higher enhancement levels add more gauge than the base figure, so a +5 card beats a
+0 card of the same grade; the per-level increment is not published.

**The timing rule matters more than the table.** Early on the gauge fills so
slowly that extraction is not a real crystal source; buy from the season shop and
take the Awakening Battle rewards instead, and save extraction for when you are
routinely discarding Heritage and Unique cards. One well-regarded KR guide advises
not extracting at all while still farming, since a Heritage card is worth more as
enhancement fuel for a Unique than as one-eighth of a crystal. See

### How long graduation actually takes

The most useful KR analysis models weekly refinement throughput after the
2026-06-04 relaxation and converts it into expected calendar time, where "A4 + B3"
means one skill at four levels and a second at three on the same card. Each slot
appears twice, for the four- and three-designation cases.

| Slot | Designations | Refinements per week | A4 + B3 | A4 + B2 or better | A4 | A3 or better |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chalice | 4 | 3.0 to 3.7 | 23.0-28.3 weeks | 4.6-5.7 weeks | 2.6-3.1 weeks | 0.7-0.9 weeks |
| Chalice | 3 | 4.1 to 5.0 | 17.3-21.2 weeks | 3.5-4.2 weeks | 1.9-2.4 weeks | 0.5-0.7 weeks |
| Scales | 4 | 1.5 to 1.8 | 46.1-56.5 weeks | 9.2-11.3 weeks | 5.1-6.3 weeks | 1.4-1.7 weeks |
| Scales | 3 | 2.1 to 2.5 | 34.5-42.4 weeks | 6.9-8.5 weeks | 3.8-4.7 weeks | 1.1-1.3 weeks |
| Parchment, Compass, Bell, Mirror | 4 | 2.8 | 31.1 weeks | 6.2 weeks | 3.5 weeks | 1.0 weeks |
| Parchment, Compass, Bell, Mirror | 3 | 3.7 | 23.3 weeks | 4.7 weeks | 2.6 weeks | 0.7 weeks |

The lesson is not the individual numbers, which are model output rather than
measurement, but the shape: **a perfect two-skill roll is a six-to-thirteen-month
project while a good single-skill roll takes two to six weeks.** Aim for good, not
perfect, and note how badly Scales punishes perfectionism.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Opening boxes on stages 1-2.** Pure Od Energy waste.
- **Extracting before the gauge is productive.** Fifty Normal cards for one crystal
  is a terrible rate; the season shop and Awakening Battle are better. Never
  extract Heritage cards, which are the cheapest enhancement fuel for a Unique.
- **Refining a Vigor or Magic Chalice.** See [[Arcana Sets and Meta]]; the Frenzy
  and Pure Blood versions give the same stat for a fraction of the Kinah.
- **Designating four skills on every refinement.** Four is for closing out a
  build, not for the middle of one.
- **Perfectionism on Scales.** Double crystals, no skill designation at all, and a
  perfect two-skill roll modelled at close to a year of weekly play.
- **Trusting drop percentages as exact.** The AION2 Hub figures are calibrated
  estimates; no official Arcana drop table exists.
- **Following pre-February stage advice.** The S-clear unlock chain is gone, entry
  item levels were rewritten, and the Unique Chalice is no longer stage-10-only.

## Global launch notes

The entire economy here depends on which dungeons exist at global launch. If
global ships with only Deus Research Base and Shattered Arkanis, the Unique
Chalice is stage-10-only again, Scales and Harmonious Crystal do not exist, and
Mysterious Crystal is the only currency. If global ships current, all six dungeons
and five crystal types are live and the routine above applies as written.

Verify before spending anything: which Transcendence dungeons are in the instance
list; whether stages unlock immediately or behind S-clears; entry item levels per
band and whether they match 1,600 and 2,200; the weekly refill count, 7 or 14; the
ticket cost per entry; every refinement Kinah figure, since Kinah faucets were
rebalanced repeatedly in KR and those numbers are the most fragile thing here; and
whether the season shop stocks Mysterious Crystal. Record the answers in
[[Global Launch Differences]] and update this note's `confidence` field.
