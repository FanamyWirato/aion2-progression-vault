# Arcana

> **In one sentence:** Arcana (아르카나 / 阿爾卡那) is the card-collection layer that
> supplies most of your item level outside gear and is the only system that can
> push individual skills past the level ceiling that Mastery and Daevanion reach.

## Why it matters

Arcana is not an optional collection. It does two things nothing else in the game
does as cheaply:

1. **Bulk item level.** Every equipped card adds a flat item level value by grade.
   A full set of five Unique skill cards is +400 item level, which then feeds
   Combat Power through the normal conversion described in
   [[Item Level vs Combat Power]]. That is a larger single block of item level than
   any one gear slot delivers, and it does not require [[Enchanting]] or
   [[Breakthrough]] risk.
2. **Skill levels above the normal ceiling.** [[Skills and Mastery Points]] and
   [[Daevanion Boards]] together take a skill to roughly level 14. Active skills
   unlock specialisation slots at levels 8, 12, 16 and 20, so the last two
   specialisation slots are unreachable without Arcana plus skill-level lines on
   rings and earrings from [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]]. Arcana is the only
   source that exists purely to raise skill levels.
3. **Set effects and main-god stats.** Cards carry a Lord/main-god stat
   (주신 스탯) and belong to a set. The set effects are meaningful percentage
   modifiers (boss damage, critical damage, PvE attack and defence) rather than
   rounding errors.

What it competes with: Transcendence tickets and Od Energy are shared with
[[Dungeons and Raids]] farming for gear, and refinement eats large amounts of
Kinah that would otherwise go to [[Crafting]] and [[Enchanting]]. See
[[Currencies and Economy]] and [[Daily and Weekly Loops]].

## How it works

Arcana has three distinct families of slots, added in three waves. As of the KR
build in early September 2026 there are ten slots.

| Family | Slots | What the card gives | Primary source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skill Arcana (launch) | Chalice, Parchment, Compass, Bell, Mirror | Item level, one main-god stat, 1-4 skill-level lines | Deus Research Base, Shattered Arkanis, Submerged Life Temple |
| Scales / Libra (2026-02) | Scales | Two main-god stats at half value, fully random skill lines | Refinement with Harmonious Crystal only |
| Stat Arcana (2026-07 onward) | Key, Hourglass, Dice, Lantern | A fixed main stat plus four random sub-stats, no skill lines | Abyssal Horn Den (Key, Hourglass), Noiran's Hidden Legacy (Dice, Lantern) |

Full detail on every slot, grade and stat lives in
[[Arcana Cards and Types]]. Set effects and the build meta live in
[[Arcana Sets and Meta]]. The acquisition and upgrade economy lives in
[[Arcana Refinement and Farming]].

The loop itself is short: clear a Transcendence (초월 / 超越) dungeon stage, open
the Od Energy reward cube, keep cards that carry a useful skill line, feed the
rest into either enhancement (as fuel) or extraction (for crystals), then spend
crystals plus Kinah on refinement to force the exact card you want.

## Progression path

The order below is the one Korean guides converge on, and it deliberately
front-loads the cheapest item level and defers the expensive skill chase.

1. **Reach Transcendence and equip anything.** Five Normal cards are +100 item
   level for zero investment. Do not enhance them.
2. **Replace with Rare (green) cards.** Rare drops start around stage 3 and are
   effectively guaranteed from stage 4. Five Rare cards are +200 item level. This
   is the single best item-level-per-hour block available at that stage of
   [[Progression Phases Roadmap]].
3. **Push to the highest stage you can S-clear, then farm it.** Higher stages
   raise the grade ceiling and the number of cards per clear. Stage 10 is roughly
   double stage 9 in reward value.
4. **Plan your skill targets before you spend a single crystal.** Decide which
   two or three skills you want at level 20 and which you will settle for at 16.
   This decision is upstream of every refinement you will ever do. See
   [[Skills and Talents]].
5. **Refine the Chalice first.** It is the only card that can roll any active or
   passive skill, and its Unique version drops only on stage 10, so it is the
   bottleneck. Force it with crystals rather than farming it.
6. **Fill the remainder from Parchment and Compass**, which have small, fixed
   skill pools and therefore high hit rates.
7. **Treat Bell and Mirror as cheap filler.** Enhance them loosely and move on.
8. **Add Scales once you have Harmonious Crystals.** It is pure gambling, since
   its skill lines cannot be designated, but it opens 4+2 and later 4+4 set
   combinations.
9. **Farm Stat Arcana for sub-stats, not skills.** Key, Hourglass, Dice and
   Lantern want four useful damage-amplification or tolerance sub-stats. Skills
   are not on these cards at all.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Grade beats enhancement, always.** Enhancing a Normal or Rare card is close
  to wasted; the item level gain per enhancement level is small compared with
  moving up a grade. Keep low-grade cards as fuel.
- **Stage 10 is the real breakpoint** on the original two dungeons, because
  Unique Chalice appears nowhere else and the stage-10 reward pool is roughly
  twice stage 9.
- **Two or three level-20 skills is the practical target**, not five. Pushing a
  low-contribution active from 19 to 20 is usually worse than putting a tier-one
  passive on the same card. The argument is developed in
  [[Arcana Sets and Meta]].
- **Do not extract early.** The extraction gauge fills so slowly at low grades
  that season shop and Awakening Battle rewards are a better crystal source until
  you are discarding Unique cards.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Enhancing cards you will replace.** Enhancement fuel is consumed permanently
  and low-grade enhancement does not carry forward.
- **Extracting Heritage cards you could have used as Unique fuel.** Unique cards
  are hard to get; Heritage cards are the cheapest path to enhancing them.
- **Buying Mysterious Crystals with season currency after your extraction gauge
  is productive.** The order matters: shop first, extraction later.
- **Chasing 19 to 20 on a skill worth 1-2% of your damage.** It inflates the
  Combat Power number without moving your real output.
- **Investing heavily in Bell and Mirror.** They have been the least stable part
  of the system across patches.
- **Letting storage hit 800.** Cards stop being useful as fuel if you cannot hold
  them; clear the backlog on a schedule.

## Global launch notes

Global early access begins 2026-09-30 for Founder's Pack owners, with the free
release on 2026-10-05 on Steam and PURPLE across North America, South America,
Europe and Asia. A Launch Scale Test ran 2026-09-17 to 2026-09-18.

Everything in this chapter is measured on the KR and TW live services, which have
had ten months of patches since their 2025-11-19 launch. NC has not published the
content snapshot the global build starts from, and has said publicly that dungeons
and PvP were reworked for global. Treat the following as the highest-risk items:

- Whether Scales, Key, Hourglass, Dice and Lantern exist at global launch at all,
  or whether global starts with the five launch slots.
- Whether the 2026-02-25 removal of stage-unlock chaining is already in the
  global build.
- The 800 storage cap, which was raised to that figure only on 2026-03-18 in KR.
- Every Kinah figure, since Kinah faucets were adjusted repeatedly.

Cross-check all of it against the live client after 2026-10-05 and record
differences in [[Global Launch Differences]].
