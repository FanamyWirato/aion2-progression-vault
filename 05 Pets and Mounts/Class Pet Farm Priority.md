# Class Pet Farm Priority

> **In one sentence:** every class starts the same way — **Intelligence pets in
> one dense camp to understanding 5**, then **Nature**, then **Wild**, then
> **Transform** — because possession **Power** and **Precision** cap at **200**;
> class only changes what you **roll later** on Species Understanding and
> **Special** rainbow analysis, not which monster you kill first.

## Why it matters

Players ask "which pet should my class farm first?" Korean guides almost never
mean a unique species per class. They mean:

1. **Which family** to max first (colour of Soul Crystal).
2. **Which camp** has the best mob density for that family.
3. **Which analysis lines** to lock once family level hits 10.

Confusing **held options** on individual Wild pets with **Block on Species
Understanding** is the usual mistake. The nine analysis slots use the **same
pool on all four hunt families**. Block is rolled on slots 1 / 4 / 6 / 7 after
level 10, not by farming Wild before Nature. See [[Pet Collection and Levels]]
and [[Species Understanding]].

## The universal farm order (all classes)

| Step | Family | Why first | Possession you are banking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Intelligence** (blue) | Korean consensus first; funds blue crystals; **Power** toward **200 cap** | HP, Critical, **Power**, Knowledge |
| **2** | **Nature** (spirit) | **Precision** toward **200 cap**; pairs with Accuracy on gear | MP, Critical Resist, **Precision**, Willpower |
| **3** | **Wild** (red) | Additional Accuracy, mount ground speed; largest kill count | Dexterity, Knowledge, Wild-family amp |
| **4** | **Transform** (yellow) | Sprint stamina, Evasion; last hunt family | Constitution, Willpower, Transform-family amp |
| **∥** | **Special** (rainbow) | **Not field souls** — **Rainbow Soul Crystals only** | No possession; Penetration-heavy analysis pool |

**Parallel rule:** from the first week, spend **every rainbow crystal on Special**.
Never feed hunt souls into Special pets. TW Ludens "Special to 10 first" means
**rainbow budget**, not skipping Intelligence camps.

**Method:** finish **one or two dense species per family to Pet Understanding 5**,
then camp them for crystal overflow. Do not spread fifty pets to level 3.
Details: [[Pet Understanding]], [[Pet Collection and Levels]].

### First camp to open (not class-specific)

Pick the Intelligence pack you can already clear at auto-hunt efficiency:

| Faction | High-density Intelligence leads | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elyos | Eternal Isle **Kerubim / Kuru** stacks | Ludens / vault default soft-HP camp |
| Elyos | **Dratona** line (7 o'clock Dratus area) | Dense; crowded at launch |
| Asmodian | Immortal Isle Kerubim equivalent | Mirror of Elyos starter |
| Asmodian | **Mumu tribe** near Dratus | Vortex: strong Intel efficiency; totem quirks |

Then Nature (spirit clusters, Spring of Recovery / Purification Forest **Gravi**,
Spirit's Resting Place water spirits), Wild (Sparkie / Tog pairs, mole fields),
Transform (Krao path worms/slimes, or camps that mix Wild + Transform). Location
threads: Vortex location guide, Ludens TW soul DB — treat spawn names as
**verify on map** leads, not gospel.

**Opposing-faction pets** are optional for the order above; use Spacetime Rift
when you want the other half of the encyclopedia, not before your first family
is at 5.

## What actually differs by class

Differences appear **after** crystals flow, at **Species Understanding** level 10
(and on **Special**). Farm order stays **Intelligence → Nature → Wild → Transform**
for everyone in this vault unless global tooltips prove a retune.

### KR / TW live — analysis targets (family level 10+)

Generic PvE template ([[Species Understanding]], Inven / gamenewb / Vortex):

| Slots | Most DPS & healers | Templar (Guardian) override |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **3 and 9** | **Smite** ≥ 1.5% each (≥ 3% combined), else Damage Amplification | Same — Smite still only on 3 / 9 |
| **2 / 5 / 8** | **Maximum Attack Power** | Same |
| **6** | Damage Resistance / Iron Wall (Chanter: Regeneration contested) | **Block** while Fury unstable, else Damage Tolerance |
| **1 / 4 / 7** | Accuracy, Critical | **Block** early, then Accuracy / Critical |

**Stat priority context** ([[Stat Reference]]):

| Class | Offensive 1st | 2nd | 3rd | Farm order change? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gladiator | Accuracy | Smite | Critical | **No** |
| Assassin | Accuracy | Critical | Smite | **No** |
| Ranger | Accuracy | Critical | Attack Speed | **No** |
| Sorcerer | Accuracy | Smite | Critical | **No** |
| Spiritmaster | (same band as mage) | Smite / Crit | — | **No** — see Arcana exception below |
| Templar | Accuracy | Damage Resistance | HP % | **No** — Block is **roll** target, not Wild-first farm |
| Chanter | Smite scaling (0.3% / passive level) | CDR / Wisdom | — | **No** — Smite on 3 / 9 is **highest lock value** |
| Cleric | Accuracy | Healing | Damage Resistance | **No** — no separate pet note; same family order |

**Chanter and Spiritmaster (Arcana, not farm):** end-game guides allow
**Maximum Attack Power / Perfect / Inspiring Spell** on Mirror and Bell where
other classes take different Arcana lines ([[Chanter Support Systems]]). That
does **not** change which field pet you kill first.

**Templar "Wild before Nature" posts:** some Korean threads reorder to
**Intelligence → Wild → Nature → Transform** because Wild **held options** list
Block. This vault follows Inven/Vortex: **Block is not a Wild possession stat**;
do **not** reorder the farm for Block ([[Templar Support Systems]] vs
[[Pet Collection and Levels]] — **prefer the collection note for farm order**).

### Launch Scale Test / global Genus retune

If the client matches the Launch Scale Test Genus map ([[Global Launch Differences]]),
**Smite on slots 3 and 9 is not the day-one script**. Priorities shift:

| Slot | Default PvE | Class tweak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **4** | PvE Damage Boost **4%** | Dual content: Damage Boost **3%**. **Ranger-likes:** Crit Damage Boost **3%** |
| **1 and 5** | Max Attack **20** | All classes |
| **3, 6, 9** | Accuracy / Crit until dungeon caps | Then Power Shot damage |
| **2 and 8** | HP **+2.4%** | All classes |
| **7** | Damage Tolerance or PvE Attack | Special family: Tolerance-only on 7 |
| **Special 4** | Double Chance **3% (Smite)** default | **Front boost:** Templar, Gladiator · **Back boost:** Assassin |

**Read the tooltip before locking KR guides.** Global may use **ten slots** and
**pet level cap 3 (105 kills)** — possession tables in [[Pet Collection and Levels]]
are KR five-level; re-check on day one.

## Season 1 timing vs other systems

Korean guides rank pets high; **global launch planning** often ranks them **lower**
than on KR because pets were **per-character and expensive** before 2026-01-07
sharing and soul halving ([[Templar Support Systems]], [[Season 1 Launch Plan]]).
Reasonable timing:

- **Level 45+:** start Intelligence camp when main story and item level gates allow
  efficient auto-hunt (often after [[Fast Progression Track]] Track 0).
- **Do not** sacrifice the one-time **~1,400 item level** sweep for pet week —
  pets are long-term; sealed dungeons and Daevanion crystals gate content now.
- **Duty Quest Command** and **Conquest / Hidden Cube** Soul Crystal payouts (global
  test: large cube grants) can shorten field time — see [[Global Launch Differences]].

## Traps

- **Different monster per class for "first pet."** Wrong question — same Intelligence
  camp, same order.
- **Wild before Nature for Templar Block.** Wrong layer; roll Block on understanding
  slots after level 10.
- **Rainbow crystals into Intelligence.** Special-only.
- **Analyzing for stats before family 10** with locks on (slows XP and burns Kinah).
- **KR Smite-on-3/9 on a global character** without checking Genus slot 4 first.
- **SEO guides that say Intelligence → Wild → Nature** (Vortex task order) vs
  **Intelligence → Nature → Wild** (Gametoc / Madimi). Both agree **Intelligence
  first**; this vault uses **Nature before Wild** for **Precision cap** before the
  largest Wild kill budget.
