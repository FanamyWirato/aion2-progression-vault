# Mounts

> **Season 1 verdict:** mounts are the pets. There is no second mount collection, no mount-enhancement track, and no wardrobe-stat for pet skins. Glide at understanding 3 and a short transform at 5 are in. Black Cloud Trading Company is in as a Kinah shop. Odium Storage boxes are not a Season 1 mount farm.

> **In one sentence:** every pet is a mount (탑승물 / 坐騎); you ride it with C, its family possession feeds move-speed and sprint stamina, and understanding 3/5 add glide and a timed costume, not a combat kit.

## Why it matters

Traversal is why you equip a pet. Power is why you collect them. Confusing the two is how people buy a cash-looking shop pet and think they skipped [[Species Understanding]]. The mount you sit on does not change your nine analysis slots; it changes how fast you cross the field and whether you can glide off a cliff without wings.

Wild possession is the ground-speed line. Transform possession is the sprint-stamina line. Both stack from every pet in that family you have levelled, equipped or not — they are possession, not an "equip the fastest model" stat. See [[Pet Collection and Levels]].

## How it works

**Pets and mounts are the same objects.** Official TW copy: collect the monster's souls, the pet appears on the pet list, C mounts it, some Expeditions and similar instances disable riding. Ground pets are ridden; flying species (mutant bees, butterflies and similar) lift the character. Preview UI shows a basic appearance and a riding appearance on the pet page, not in the gear wardrobe.

**Traversal stats** come from family possession, not from a mount-upgrade NPC:

| Family | Mount-facing possession at understanding 5 (per pet) |
| --- | --- |
| Wild | Mount ground speed 6 (units as on the tooltip — verify percent vs flat) |
| Transform | Sprint-stamina consumption −0.6% |
| Intelligence / Nature | No mount-speed line; combat possession only |
| Special | No possession |

Glide (활강) unlocks at **Pet Understanding 3** on that pet. It is wingless gliding, not [[Wings and Flight]]. Transform (변신) unlocks at **5**: a short appearance change (Inven: Empathy Cookie, on the order of five minutes) during which skills still work. It is not a battle transform and it is not a stigma.

Equipping a pet is cosmetic-plus-traversal. Collection rewards and titles come from *owning* many types, not from which one is summoned.

**Wardrobe.** Pet riding skins live on the pet UI. They do not fill [[Wardrobe and Skin Collection]] and they do not pay wardrobe percentage stats. Cash appearances are excluded from wardrobe stats in any case. Do not extract gear "to get the pet look."

## Unlock and gates

First pet → C to mount. Understanding 3 and 5 on that species for glide and transform. Mounting can be blocked inside listed instances (official TW note).

## Progression path

1. Ride whatever you unlocked while levelling. Speed differences are small until Wild possession stacks.
2. After a few Wild pets at 5, ground travel is noticeably faster. Keep collecting Wild even if you never roll Wild slots first.
3. Use glide on any understanding-3 pet when you would otherwise walk a cliff. Keep a flying species favourited if you like the lift animation; it is not a power choice.
4. Check Black Cloud on the hour only for species your dex marks shop-only, and only on a discount. Do not Kinah-snipe cosmetics.
5. Transform is optional flavour. Do not spend a crystal budget on it.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- Wild family 5s are the mount-speed breakpoint. Transform family 5s are the sprint breakpoint.
- Understanding 3 on *one* favourite is enough glide for daily use.
- Shop pets are collection fills, not a Species Understanding shortcut, unless they are Special (then they still need rainbow crystals).

## Costs and math

Black Cloud prices move with the hourly discount (cap 50%). Ludens mentions a Special shop pet around 2,000,000 Kinah (Pacadi / 파카디 class — treat as a TW snapshot, not a price list). No mount-enhancement stone exists in Season 1.

## Traps and mistakes

- Looking for a separate "mount" chapter or NPC. The pet list is the mount list.
- Expecting the equipped pet to change analysis slots.
- Putting pet skins in the wardrobe grind.
- Buying Shugo Festa specials before Wisdom Stones.
- Using Odium Storage as the mount-collect daily at launch.
- Thinking level 5 transform is a combat skill.

## Global launch notes

Mounting, glide, transform, and Black Cloud are launch systems. Server-wide pet lists (so alts' shop purchases feed the main) are 2026-01-07. Species Understanding presets do not add extra mount loadouts. No sailing, fishing, or housing mounts — those systems are not live.
