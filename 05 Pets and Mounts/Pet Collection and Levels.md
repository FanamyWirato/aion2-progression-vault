# Pet Collection and Levels

> **Season 1 verdict:** collecting field monsters as pets is in. **Launch Scale
> Test cap is level 3, 105 kills to max** — not the KR five-level / ~1,010-soul
> track. Possession-from-level-5 KR tables below are the Korean live game.
> Server-wide sharing is still 2026-01-07 — plan per character unless the
> client shows otherwise. Odium Storage is not a Season 1 farm.

> **In one sentence:** you collect field monsters as pets, each pet has a five-level understanding track that pays a family possession effect, and the Season 1 job is to bank Power and Precision from Intelligence and Nature before those stats cap at 200.

## Why it matters

Possession effects (보유 효과) are always-on stats from every pet you have raised. They are small per pet and large in aggregate, they never walk off when you change mounts, and two of them — Power (위력) from Intelligence and Precision (정확) from Nature — hit a **200 cap** that you would otherwise buy on gear. Korean guides therefore finish those two families first so later attunement and manastone rolls can go to Accuracy, Combat Speed and Smite. See [[Stat Reference]] and [[Pet Understanding]].

The collection is also the feed for [[Species Understanding]]. Each new pet and each understanding level-up grants family XP; once a pet is at 5, further souls become the crystals that roll the nine slots.

## How it works

A pet is a field monster. Kill it, collect its souls, and at a soul threshold it enters the pet list and can be equipped as a mount. There is no gacha pack for the hunt families. Special-family pets come from shops, passes and events instead.

## Unlock and gates

Souls drop from the matching monster as soon as you can kill it. First acquire is widely quoted as **10 souls** at KR launch (Daum / LDPlayer, 2025-11-20 and 2025-12-17). After the 2026-01-07 halving that first-acquire number almost certainly moved; verify.

Pet Understanding cap is 5. Species Understanding is a separate ten-level family track — [[Species Understanding]].

## Progression path

Season 1 collection order, Korean consensus:

1. **Intelligence to understanding 5** on the dense camps you can live in. Banks Power and Critical toward the 200 Power cap, and is the usual first Soul Crystal colour.
2. **Nature to 5.** Banks Precision toward the 200 cap.
3. **Wild to 5.** Additional Accuracy plus mount ground speed. Largest family (alias007: ~32,825 kills post-halving, ~41 Intelligence pets × 505 ≈ 20,705 for that family).
4. **Transform to 5.** Sprint stamina and Evasion. Last of the hunt families.
5. **Special** only with rainbow crystals. Never dump hunt-family souls into it.

A Templar-community reorder puts Wild before Nature because some posts claim Wild "held options" include Block. Block is a **Species Understanding slot** option, not a Wild possession line. Do not reorder the farm for Block; roll Block on the family slots in [[Species Understanding]].

Finish **one or two dense species per family to 5**, then camp them for crystals, rather than spreading 50 pets to level 3. Spreading is how you never open the Soul Crystal gate. See [[Pet Understanding]].

## Priorities and breakpoints

- Power 200 and Precision 200 are the reason Intelligence and Nature go first. Possession is the cheap fill; gear is the remainder.
- Understanding 5 on a camp pet is the breakpoint that turns hunting into crystal income.
- Alias007's post-halving family totals (Intelligence ~20,705, Nature ~24,240, Wild ~32,825) assume every pet in the family to 5 at 505 kills. Season 1 without the halving is roughly double. You do not need every pet at 5 to start Species Understanding — you need a few at 5 so crystals flow.

## Costs and math

Launch soul curve, Gameple 2025-12-05, **cumulative** not per-level:

| Step | Souls to next | Running total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| → 1 | 10 | 10 |
| → 2 | 50 | 60 |
| → 3 | 150 | 210 |
| → 4 | 300 | 510 |
| → 5 | 500 | **1,010** |

The "500 souls to level 5" line in later Gametoc copy is the **last step**, not the total. ~200 monster types × 1,010 ≈ 200,000 kills for a full book — that is a completionist figure, not a Season 1 goal.

2026-01-07 halved soul requirements. Madimi's kill curve 5 / 25 / 75 / 150 / 250 = **505 kills** to 5 is the post-patch figure and matches alias007's 41 × 505 = 20,705 Intelligence total. Vortex's "1,000 kills, then a 17 November patch made it 500" is the same confusion between total and last-step, plus an undated CBT/launch tweak. **Verify the tooltip on day one.**

## Traps and mistakes

- Treating 1,010 (or 500) as a per-level kill count. It is cumulative across five levels, and 500 is the last rung.
- Assuming possession is account-wide on day one. At KR launch it was per character.
- Spending rainbow crystals on hunt families.
- Buying Shugo Festa special pets as a free-to-play first purchase (Vortex: not recommended).
- Planning around Odium Storage boxes.
- Copying the Templar "Wild first for Block" farm order. Block is not a Wild possession stat.
