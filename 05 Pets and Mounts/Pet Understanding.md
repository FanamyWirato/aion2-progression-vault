# Pet Understanding

> **Season 1 verdict:** the per-monster five-level kill counter is in. ~1,010 is the **cumulative** launch soul total to level 5, not a per-level count; after KR 2026-01-07 the same curve is halved (~505 kills). Level 5 is a short transform, not a combat skill. Odium Storage is not the Season 1 farm.

> **In one sentence:** Pet Understanding (펫 이해도 / 신수 이해, aliases Pet Insight, Pet Affinity — **not** "Genus Insight") is the per-monster soul counter that pays possession stats, unlocks glide and a short transform, and at level 5 turns further kills into Soul Crystals for [[Species Understanding]].

## Why it matters

This is the farm. Species Understanding does not drop from a dungeon boss; it is fed by finishing individual monsters. Every hour of auto-hunt that is not already on the Kinah cap should land on a pet that is either still climbing to 5 or already at 5 in a family you are rolling. See [[Farming Routes and Kinah]] and [[Daily Routine Main]].

English blogs that say "Genus Insight" are talking about 종족 이해도, the family track. Using that name for this counter will send you to the wrong UI page.

## How it works

Each distinct monster has its own Pet Understanding, cap **5**. Souls from that monster raise only that track. Possession for its family scales with the level (tables in [[Pet Collection and Levels]]). Raising the level also grants Species Understanding XP for that family (Inven: first acquire +200, then +20 at level 2, +30 at 3, and so on).

**What each level unlocks**

| Level | Combat / collection | Traversal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pet enters the list (launch: 10 souls). First possession line | Can mount (C). Some instances forbid mounting |
| 2–4 | Additional possession lines | Still a ground or flying mount according to the species |
| 3 | Possession continues | **Glide** (활강): wingless gliding while on this pet, not flight |
| 5 | Full possession including family Damage Amp 0.1%; **Soul Crystal gate** opens | **Transform** (변신): a short appearance change, not a combat skill |

Transform is a flavour / movement toy. An Inven tips post describes it as a timed change into that pet's look (about five minutes, Empathy Cookie / 펫 교감 쿠키), during which you can still use skills and flight but keep the pet appearance. It is **not** a DPS cooldown. Confidence medium on the duration.

Glide at 3 is widely repeated (Daum launch article 2025-11-20, LDPlayer 2025-12-17, Inven). It is not Daevanion flight and it does not replace [[Wings and Flight]].

**The Soul Crystal gate.** Once Pet Understanding is maxed, further souls from that monster convert automatically into that family's Soul Crystals (영혼 결정). That is the only hunt-family crystal faucet. Special does not convert this way — see [[Species Understanding]].

## Unlock and gates

No quest. The matching monster must be killable. Auto-hunt (자동 사냥) is the intended grind.

## Progression path

1. While levelling, accept whatever souls drop. Do not camp.
2. At 45, pick **one dense Intelligence camp** and finish those species to 5. Eternal Isle Cherubim / Kerubim packs (Ludens) or the equivalent Asmodian Immortal Isle line are the usual "soft HP, 3–4 stacked" recommendation.
3. Stay on a finished species until you have enough crystals to push that family's Species Understanding. Then either stay for more crystals or move to Nature.
4. Do not rotate six maps "to collect the book." Crystal income requires a maxed target.

**Which monsters to finish.** Finish the ones in the camp you can already clear, not the rare named on the other faction's doorstep. Family location maps: Ludens 2025-12-21 (TW) and the Inven soul-location threads it cites. Practical Season 1 camps:

| Family | High-density leads (verify spawns) |
| --- | --- |
| Intelligence | Eternal Isle / Immortal Isle Kerubim–Kuru packs; Drakan watchers on the Drana processing / Dranakus line |
| Nature | Eternal Isle spirit packs; elemental clusters |
| Wild | Light-bugs / Sparkie pairs (farm fruit and spirit stones on the side); Kalnif fields |
| Transform | Krao Cave path (Kinah + two target species, Ludens); slime / Drana mutant clusters |

Fafnite Burial Grounds is the Kinah camp that doubles as understanding if the local mutants sit in the family you need. See [[Farming Routes and Kinah]].

Opposing-faction field pets exist. Reach them through the Spacetime Rift if you care; it is not required to open Species Understanding.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Level 3** is the traversal breakpoint (glide). Fine as a side effect, not a farm target.
- **Level 5** is the only farm target that matters. Possession completes and crystals start.
- A family with **zero** pets at 5 produces no hunt crystals. One pet at 5 in a dense camp beats twenty pets at 3.

## Costs and math

Launch cumulative souls to 5: **10 + 50 + 150 + 300 + 500 = 1,010** (Gameple 2025-12-05). This is the number English notes often paste as "1,010 kills per level." It is not.

Post 2026-01-07: costs −50%. Madimi kill steps 5 / 25 / 75 / 150 / 250 = **505** to 5. Gametoc's "500 souls / 500 kills to reach 5" is the last launch step, reused carelessly as a total.

If global ships the launch curve, budget ~1,010 souls per finished pet. If it ships the January curve, budget ~505. Check one understanding bar before you plan the week.

## Traps and mistakes

- Farming a species that is not yet 5 and expecting Soul Crystals.
- Calling this track Genus Insight / 종족.
- Treating level 5 transform as a combat button.
- Using Odium Storage as the default crystal farm at launch.
- Bouncing maps to "fill the dex" instead of parking on a maxed dense spawn.

## Global launch notes

Pet Understanding existed at KR launch. The January halving changes the time-to-5 by about half and is the main "is my Korean guide lying?" check. Season 3 eased *Species* Understanding XP, not this counter. No Heroic gear interaction.
