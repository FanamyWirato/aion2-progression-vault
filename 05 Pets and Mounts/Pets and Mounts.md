# Pets and Mounts

> **Season 1 verdict:** pets, both understanding tracks, and mounting are in on day one. There is no separate mount system. Species Understanding presets, the April XP ease, and Odium Storage are not Season 1 farms — treat them as roadmap.

> **In one sentence:** every field monster you kill is a collectible that is also your mount, and the real power sits in a two-layer understanding track that Korean guides call the largest non-gear stat source in the game.

## Why it matters

Pets are the permanent-progress layer that does not wait on item level. Korean press and community guides treat Species Understanding (종족 이해도) as the end of "inner substance" (내실): nine rerollable stat slots per family, including Smite and Damage Amplification that gear barely supplies. Pet Understanding (펫 이해도 / 신수 이해) is the farm that feeds it. Mounting is the same object, not a second collection.

What it competes with: field-hunting time against the 2,000,000 Kinah daily cap, Duty Quest Command Soul Crystals, Shugo Festa leftover candy, and — after a pet is maxed — the Soul Crystal funnel that could have been more Kinah. See [[Daily Routine Main]], [[Currencies and Economy]], and [[Alts and Account Wide Progression]].

## The two-layer hierarchy

**Pet Understanding** is the per-monster kill counter. Each distinct monster has its own five-level track. Killing it drops souls; souls raise that monster's understanding; at level 5 the same kills convert into Soul Crystals (영혼 결정) for its family. Understanding level 3 unlocks glide on that pet, level 5 unlocks a short transform. English "Genus Insight" is **not** this counter.

**Species Understanding** (종족 이해도, also Race / Family / Genus Understanding — "Genus Insight" in some English blogs) is the family track above it. Five families, ten levels, nine rerollable analysis slots. You raise it by collecting pets, levelling Pet Understanding, and spending Soul Crystals on Analysis. Higher-grade options appear at family 4 (Heritage), 7 (Unique) and 9 (Heroic *options*, not Heroic gear). Special is the fifth family and is analysis-only.

Possession effects (보유 효과) ride on Pet Understanding of each owned pet and stack inside a family. Analysis slots ride on Species Understanding and are the same nine-slot pool on the four normal families. Do not mix the two.

## Unlock and gates

No separate quest gate is documented. Souls drop from the first field monsters you kill. Species Understanding analysis is on the pet UI from the first collected pet; from 2026-01-07 it opens at family level 1 with no pet required. Season 1 content sits inside character level 45 and the 0–3800 gate band. Heroic-grade *equipment* is out; Heroic-grade understanding *options* at family 9 are still in.

## Progression path

1. Collect whatever you kill during levelling. Do not spend Soul Crystals yet.
2. After cap, finish **Intelligence** pets in one dense camp to understanding 5 so souls become crystals. Then Nature, then Wild, then Transform. Details in [[Pet Collection and Levels]].
3. Raise each family's Species Understanding to 10 with **locks off**. Then roll slots 3 and 9. Details in [[Species Understanding]].
4. Put Rainbow Soul Crystals only into Special. Never into the four hunt families.
5. Use the pets as mounts the whole time. Glide and transform are understanding unlocks, not a shop skill. Details in [[Mounts]].

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Power and Precision cap at 200.** Bank them from Intelligence and Nature possession before you lock those lines on gear. This is why the family order exists.
- **Species Understanding 10 before any serious lock.** Family 9 is the first Heroic-option chance; family 10 raises it (reported 1% → 5%).
- **Slots 3 and 9 are the chapter.** Smite and Damage Amplification live there. Slot 6 is the defence slot. Everything else is filler.
- Do not plan the week around [[Odium Storage]]; it is a 2025-12-24 KR daily and is out of the Season 1 farm list.

## Traps and mistakes

- Treating English "Genus Insight" as the per-monster counter. It is 종족, the family track.
- Copying current Korean guides that assume server-wide pets, halved soul costs, Season 3 presets, and eased Species Understanding XP.
- Locking analysis slots before family 9.
- Spending Rainbow Soul Crystals on Intelligence / Nature / Wild / Transform.
- Farming Odium Storage or waiting on Species Understanding presets.
