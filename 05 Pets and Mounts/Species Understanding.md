# Species Understanding

> **Season 1 verdict:** the family reroll track is in and is still the largest
> non-gear power source in this chapter. **Launch Scale Test rebuilt the ten
> slots** (percent HP, split offence/defence, Smite as Double Chance on special
> slot 4). KR option tables below are the live Korean map, not the global one.
> **Presets are Season 3 — roadmap.**

> **In one sentence:** Species Understanding (종족 이해도 — Genus / Race / Family Understanding; English "Genus Insight" belongs **here**) is the family track that unlocks nine Analysis slots you reroll with Soul Crystals, with better grades at family 4 / 7 / 9.

## Why it matters

This is the reason you finish pets. Korean combat-power guides put Species Understanding next to Pantheon and traces as permanent stats you cannot buy on a dungeon drop. Slots 3 and 9 are among the few places Smite (강타) and Damage Amplification appear as rerollable lines. Slot 6 is the defence slot (Damage Resistance, Iron Wall, Regeneration). See [[Stat Reference]] for why Smite is a doubling and why Accuracy still comes first.

It competes with [[Enchanting]] and [[Arcana Refinement and Farming]] for Kinah once you start locking, and with field-hunting time for crystals. Rainbow crystals compete with nothing useful except Special — spend them anywhere else and you brick the fifth family.

## How it works

Five independent family tracks, cap **10**. The four hunt families (Intelligence, Wild, Nature, Transform) share the same slot-option logic. Special is a fifth page with a different pool and **no hunt-crystal income**.

**XP sources** (Inven 6444/1077, 2026-01-14):

- First acquire of a pet in the family: +200
- Pet Understanding level-ups: +20 at 2, +30 at 3, … (pattern continues)
- Each Analysis: XP equal to the number of **unlocked** slots × 100 (example: family 1 → 100, family 2 → 200, family 3 → 300)
- Special: Analysis only. Hunting does not grant Special family XP

Locked slots raise the crystal and Kinah cost of the next Analysis **and** (Madimi) grant no XP. That is why early locks slow the climb to 10 as well as making each roll dearer.

**Slots and grades**

| Family level | Slots open | Grades that can appear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | 3 | Normal |
| 4–6 | 6 | Heritage (전승) unlocks at 4 |
| 7–8 | 9 (full) | Unique (유일) unlocks at 7 |
| 9 | 9 | Heroic (영웅) *options* unlock at 9 |
| 10 | still 9 | No tenth slot. Heroic chance rises (community: ~1% → ~5%) |

Heroic here is option grade, not Heroic gear. Season 1 has no Heroic equipment and still has this roll.

Until family 6 every slot can roll the common pool. From Unique/Heroic at 7+, each slot adds a unique pool on top of the basics (Accuracy and similar). Inven unique-pool map:

| Slots | Unique / Heroic extras |
| --- | --- |
| 1 / 4 / 7 | Additional Defense, Critical Defense, Rear Defense, … |
| 2 / 5 / 8 | Additional Attack, Maximum Attack Power, Critical Attack, Rear Attack, … |
| 3 / 9 | Damage Amplification, **Smite**, Perfect, family Damage Amp, Weapon Damage Amp, Critical Damage Amp, Rear Damage Amp, … |
| 6 | Damage Resistance, **Iron Wall**, Regeneration, family Damage Resistance, Rear Damage Resistance, … |

Special replaces family Damage Amp / Resistance with **Penetration**, and only slot 6 is defensive — the other eight can all be offensive. That is why rainbow crystals go here first if you have them.

**The four hunt families roll the same analysis pool.** Possession differs by family ([[Pet Collection and Levels]]); the nine slots do not. You are not "building a Wild understanding for Block." You are rolling Block on whichever family's slots you have at 10.

## Unlock and gates

Pet UI → Species Understanding → family → Analyse. Launch: you needed pets. From 2026-01-07 the track opens at family level 1 with no pet. Soul Crystals must match the family; rainbow is Special-only.

## Progression path

1. Do not Analyse for options while climbing. Analyse only as a way to dump crystals into XP if you must, **locks off**.
2. Take each hunt family to **10** with zero locks. Alias007's table: nothing locked at 1–8; at 9 lock only a Unique-maximum; at 10 begin the real work.
3. At 10, roll **slots 3 and 9** until Smite ≥ 1.5% each (combined ≥ 3%) or a high Damage Amplification if Smite refuses to appear. Then slot 6. Then 2 / 5 / 8 (Maximum Attack Power is the PvE default). Then 1 / 4 / 7 (Accuracy, Critical; Block for Templars).
4. Special: dump every rainbow crystal here from the first week. Do not wait until the hunt families are done — Special cannot catch up from hunting.

TW Ludens advice to "push Special to 10 first" is a rainbow-budget statement, not a reason to skip Intelligence possession. Do both: hunt Intelligence, spend rainbow on Special.

## Priorities and breakpoints

**When to spend / lock — verified against the "nothing until 10" line.**

Korean advice is slightly softer than "lock nothing until family 10":

- **Levels 1–8:** lock nothing. This is the hard rule.
- **Level 9:** lock nothing unless a 3 / 6 / 9 line is Unique-maximum and already better than a mid Heroic (Inven: Unique high = Heroic mid; example Additional Attack Unique 6–12 vs Heroic 8–16).
- **Level 10:** this is when you spend. Take Smite 1.5%+ rather than chasing a perfect Heroic forever (alias007's 30,000-gem regret story).

So: nothing until 9 is the community law; **serious spending starts at 10**. "Nothing until 10" is the safe Season 1 paraphrase.

Generic PvE slot template (Inven + Vortex + gamenewb; Templar/Chanter overrides in the class notes):

| Slots | Take |
| --- | --- |
| 3 and 9 | Smite, else Damage Amplification. Smite only exists here |
| 6 | Damage Resistance, else Iron Wall (Chanters: Regeneration is a contested equal) |
| 2 / 5 / 8 | Maximum Attack Power |
| 1 / 4 / 7 | Accuracy and Critical; Templars may put Block here while Fury is unstable |

Vortex end-state lead: Damage Amp ~20% and Damage Resistance ~10% across the relevant slots. Treat as a long-horizon target, not a week-one number.

## Costs and math

Exact crystal and Kinah costs per Analysis, and the multiplier per locked slot, are **not published**. Every serious guide only states:

- Cost rises with the number of locked slots
- Locked slots deny XP (Madimi)
- A full five-family option chase can eat thousands of crystals (Gametoc)

Unique high ≈ Heroic mid, so locking a Unique-max on 3 or 9 at 10 is allowed. Chasing Heroic-max on every slot is how people go broke.

Season 3 **lowered Species Understanding XP requirements** (ZDNet 2026-04-08). Season 1 climbs are slower than current KR blogs imply.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Locking before 9.** The cost and XP penalties compound for the rest of the family's life.
- **Locking Smite on a slot that cannot roll it.** Only 3 and 9.
- **Rainbow crystals into Intelligence.** Irreversible in practice; Special has no other faucet.
- **Building a second Analysis set.** Presets that store extra option pages are Season 3. You have one page per family.
- **Assuming Heroic-grade options need Heroic gear.** They do not.
- **Copying a Special-first rainbow guide as a hunt order.** Rainbow ≠ hunt souls.
- **Using auto-analyse without watching locks** (Inven 2026-08 warning). A convenience feature that can spend a week of crystals.

## Global launch notes

Rerolls are launch content. Sharing of the highest family level is 2026-01-07.
Presets and the XP ease are Season 3 — **out**.

**Launch Scale Test rebuilt Genus Insight.** Welps' hunt-family priority (full
table in [[Global Launch Differences]]):

- Slot **4** is the load-bearing line: PvE Damage Boost 4%, or Damage Boost 3%
  if you also PvP, or Crit Damage Boost 3% on crit-stacking classes.
- Slots **1 and 5:** Max Attack 20 over Attack Bonus 16.
- Slots **3 / 6 / 9:** Accuracy and Crit until the dungeon caps, then Power Shot
  damage.
- Slots **2 and 8:** HP +2.4%.
- Slot **7:** Damage Tolerance 3% (both), PvE Damage Tolerance 4% (PvE-only),
  or PvE Attack 12 (pure damage). Special-family 7 is Tolerance-only.
- Special slot **4:** Double Chance 3% (Smite), else front boost (Templar /
  Gladiator) or back boost (Assassin).

Do not lock the KR Smite-on-slots-3-and-9 map onto a global character until the
tooltip agrees. See [[Global Launch Differences]].
