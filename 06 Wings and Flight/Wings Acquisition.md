# Wings Acquisition

> **In one sentence:** Every distinct pair of wings you acquire is a permanent
> retention effect and roughly +200 Flight Power that you keep forever, so the
> correct policy is to collect all of them and to treat the "best" pair as a
> separate, much smaller decision.

## Why it matters

Because of the retention-effect rule described in [[Wings and Flight]], wing
acquisition is one of very few systems in AION 2 with no diminishing returns and
no wrong answers. A pair you consider useless still contributes item level once
enhanced, still contributes its retention stats, and still adds Flight Power.
Korean guides are unusually unanimous on this point: buy the wings in every shop
that sells them, including the ones you will never equip.

The equip-effect choice is separate and much narrower. In practice two or three
pairs matter for what you actually wear, and the rest are collection fodder that
you enhance in the background.

### Equip effects on the wings that matter

The retention column above is what enhancement grows. The equip effect is a
different, larger block, and it is the reason to actually wear a given pair.
Only a handful have been documented numerically by the Korean community:

| Wings | Equip effect (KR community measurements) | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talisra Wings (공허의 탈리스라) | Additional Accuracy 35, **Cooldown Reduction 4%** | Medium — single Korean blog |
| Nightmare Wings (악몽) | **Boss Damage Amplification 3.5%**, Critical Hit 35 | Medium — single Korean blog, direction corroborated by a Vortex Gaming guide |
| Forest Spirit Wings (숲 정령) | Boss Damage Amplification 3.5%, Boss Attack Power 95 | Medium |
| Dark Veil Wings (어둠의 장막) | Damage Amplification 2.5%, Boss Defence 500 | Medium |
| Black Fragment Wings (검은 파편) | Critical Attack Power 95, Critical Hit 35, HP 500, Additional Evasion 20, Block 10 | Medium |
| Radiant Love Wings (찬란한 사랑) | Defence 400, Evasion 35, MP Natural Regeneration 90, Potion Recovery 5.5%, Aerial Defence 100 | Low — single SEO-flavoured Korean blog |

Every one of these also carries Flight Power +200 in its retention block.

**Cooldown reduction is the standout.** Cooldown reduction is scarce across the
whole game, which is why the season-shop Talisra Wings are described as the most
popular pair in KR despite Nightmare Wings out-damaging them on a pure boss
parse. See [[Stat Reference]].

**Rear Damage Amplification is the other standout.** Korean damage-formula
testing found that rear damage amplification is an independent multiplier, while
the ordinary Damage Amplification / PvE Damage Amplification / Boss Damage
Amplification family all share one additive bucket and therefore lose efficiency
as your gear improves. That makes the Awakened Pride Wings (일깨운 긍지) and the
frontal-amplification Redeemed Wings unusually interesting for high-spec
characters.

### Crafting your own wings

The material conversion menu (Alt+H) turns two gathering-tier materials into
wings:

- **Pure Odyle (순수한 오드)** — a rare field gathering drop. Ten of them craft
  your first pair and permanently unlock the wing crafting menu.
- **Sealed Wing Feather (봉인된 날개 깃털)** — a Sealed Dungeon reward that
  converts 1:1 into wings. Korean guides flag that most players have a stack of
  these sitting in the warehouse unnoticed, because Sealed Dungeons are a daily
  routine and the feathers accumulate silently. Check your warehouse before you
  farm anything.

These are the free, no-fee path to Flight Power for a new character.

## Progression path

A recommended route for a fresh global player, in order:

1. **Ascend immediately.** Take "Fledgling Wings" (Elyos, level 5) or "Spread
   Your Wings" (Asmodian, level 6) the moment it appears. It gates everything.
2. **Bank the free story wings.** Lesser, Intermediate, Superior and Ultimate
   Daeva Wings and Aullaeu Wings all arrive from the main story. That is five
   pairs and roughly 1000 Flight Power for zero effort.
3. **Run Sealed Dungeons daily from level 10.** They pay Enhancement Stones,
   Sealed Wing Feathers, and eventually the Glorious and Sealing Wings.
   Convert every feather; do not let them rot in the warehouse.
4. **Set a gathering route for Pure Odyle.** Craft a pair every time you have
   the materials. This is the free Flight Power engine for the whole early game.
5. **Spend Hidden Cube Keys the day you get them.** Five per day, and the cubes
   are the only source of Golden Emblem Wings and one of two sources of Radiant
   Love Wings.
6. **The first shop wing you buy should be the Season Shop wing.** Korean
   consensus is that the seasonal wing is the highest-value purchase in any shop
   and the one people most regret skipping, because seasons end.
7. **Then Nightmare Wings.** They are the strongest pure boss-damage equip
   effect, and Dream Fragments carry across seasons so you can bank toward them.
8. **Then everything else, cheapest first.** Shugo Festa, Abyss Shop, event coin
   shops. Buy every wing offered; the retention effects justify it even when the
   equip effect is worthless to you.
9. **Expedition wings last**, because they arrive naturally as you farm those
   dungeons for gear anyway.

## Priorities and breakpoints

| Player | Equip this | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PvE boss DPS | Nightmare Wings | Highest measured boss parse; boss damage amplification 3.5% |
| Generalist / PvP | Talisra Wings | 4% cooldown reduction is the scarcest stat in the game |
| Pre-shop stopgap | Forest Spirit Wings | Free from Bakron's Sky Island, and measured about four seconds faster on a boss kill than Dark Veil |
| Tank / survivability | Radiant Love or Black Fragment Wings | Defence, evasion, block, HP |
| High-spec min-max | Whatever carries rear or frontal damage amplification | Independent multiplier, does not decay with gear |

Korean tier consensus, by combat-power gain on swap: Talisra > Nightmare >
Dramata Nest > Forest Spirit > Dark Veil > Fierce Horn Cave > Black Fragment.
Note that the combat-power number and the actual boss damage disagree — Talisra
inflates the score, Nightmare wins the parse.

## Costs and math

- Five story wings, free, roughly +1000 Flight Power.
- Crafted wings from Pure Odyle and Sealed Wing Feathers: gathering time only.
- Nightmare Wings: 160,000 Dream Fragments (confidence medium).
- Talisra Wings: 210,000 Oath Coins (confidence medium).
- Swapping Dramata Nest Wings for Talisra Wings was measured at roughly 800–1000
  combat-power points (confidence medium, single source).

For enhancement costs on top of acquisition, see [[Wings Enhancement]].

## Traps and mistakes

- **Cosmetic wings are a dead end.** Cash-shop wings (for example the "Sunshine
  Wings" sold for ₩29,000 in the 2026-07-01 update), Deva Pass wings and ranking
  reward wings carry no stats and cannot be enhanced. They are Special grade.
  Buy them for looks only, and use the Wardrobe appearance toggle so you never
  trade stats for style. See [[Wardrobe and Skin Collection]].
- **Skipping a seasonal wing.** Season shops rotate. The single most common
  regret in Korean community posts is not buying the season wing before the
  season ended.
- **Dismantling wings for Enhancement Stones.** Around 500 stones per wing, at
  the cost of a permanent retention effect. Almost never correct.
- **Ignoring the warehouse.** Sealed Wing Feathers accumulate passively from
  daily Sealed Dungeon runs and convert straight into wings.
- **Assuming the equip effect is the whole value.** A pair whose equip effect is
  irrelevant to your class is still worth acquiring and enhancing.

## Global launch notes

- KR through 2026-07-08 includes Season 2 and Season 3 wings. Global Season 1
  will have a shorter list.
- **Launch Scale Test rebuilt acquisition.** Not 50 fragments. Last-boss **1.5%**
  pool (50/50 wing or artwork) plus a **separate 5%** individual roll if every
  last boss died. Sealed wing + **10 Radiant Odyle** in Morph = usable wing.
  **Usable wing is bound.** Sealed wing is **tradable** and morphs into
  **10,000** enhancement stones. KR's extract-for-90,000-stones path is closed.
- Nightmare, Season and Abyss shop wings remain the missable set if seasons
  compress.

See [[Global Launch Differences]].
