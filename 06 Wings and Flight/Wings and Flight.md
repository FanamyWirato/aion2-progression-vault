# Wings and Flight

> **In one sentence:** Wings in AION 2 are a real equipment slot and a permanent
> collection at the same time, so every pair you own pays you stats forever
> while the pair you wear decides your combat profile and your ability to cross
> a map that is far too large to walk.

## Why it matters

Wings occupy an unusual position in the AION 2 power budget because they pay out
on three separate axes at once, and the three axes are not interchangeable.

1. **Identity.** Wings are the visual signature of a Daeva. The game grants them
   in a scripted ascension moment rather than at character creation, and the
   [[Wardrobe and Skin Collection]] lets you project any appearance you own over
   whatever pair you actually have equipped. Cosmetics never cost you stats.
2. **Traversal.** Wings supply Flight Power (비행력), the stamina pool that
   governs how far you can fly in one push. Flight Power is what turns a
   [[Hidden Cubes]] route, an [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] route or a
   gathering circuit from an hour into twenty minutes. See
   [[Flight and Traversal]].
3. **Combat stats.** Since the 2026-05-27 KR update, wings enhance to +10 and
   each level adds a real combat stat and item level. This puts wings directly
   in competition with [[Enchanting]] and [[Breakthrough]] for your Kinah. See
   [[Wings Enhancement]].

The reason this chapter matters more than a cosmetic slot normally would is the
**retention effect** rule described below: wings you will never wear still pay
you. That makes wing acquisition an uncapped, permanent, always-worth-it
activity in a way that very few systems in the game are.

## How it works

### Equip effects versus retention effects

Every stat-bearing pair of wings carries two separate blocks of bonuses.

| Effect type | Korean term | When it applies | Typical contents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Equip effect | 착용 효과 | Only while that pair is the equipped pair | The headline combat line: boss damage amplification, cooldown reduction, critical hit, defence |
| Retention effect | 보유 효과 | Permanently, from owning the pair in your collection | Flight Power, plus smaller flat stats; this is what enhancement grows |

Two consequences follow, and they drive the whole chapter:

- You only ever benefit from **one** equip effect at a time, so the equipped
  pair is a genuine build choice comparable to picking a
  [[Titles and Achievements]] title.
- You benefit from **all** retention effects simultaneously, so acquiring a pair
  you consider ugly and weak is still a straight power gain. Korean guides put
  the retention Flight Power at +200 per distinct pair acquired
  (confidence medium — corroborated across two Korean blogs but not by a patch
  note).

Enhancement grows the retention block, not the equip block. That is the single
most counter-intuitive fact in the chapter: enhancing a pair of wings you do
not wear still makes your character stronger, and it still raises your item
level. See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]].

### Where the power actually is

Wings are a mid-sized but very broad power source. Individual numbers are small,
but they arrive on every axis and they never expire.

| Payout | Magnitude | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item level | +1 per successful enhancement step, on every owned pair | 17 Unique pairs at +5 is +85 item level (KR guide worked example) |
| Flight Power | +200 per pair acquired, +10 per enhancement step | Enhancement figure is confirmed by two Korean guides |
| Equip effect | Up to roughly 3.5% boss damage amplification or 4% cooldown reduction on the best pairs | Single strongest slot-level choice outside gear |
| Retention stats | 0.2–0.3% amplification, or 30–500 flat, per pair at +10 | Small individually, cumulative across 30+ pairs |

The best available community comparison found that swapping from a mid-tier pair
to the season-shop pair moved a combat-power score by roughly 800–1000 points,
which is the same order as a meaningful gear upgrade (confidence medium, single
Korean blog).

### Grade names are a translation minefield

The Korean grade ladder for wings and titles is 일반 → 희귀 → 전승 → 유일, plus a
cosmetic-only 스페셜 tier. English sources map these inconsistently, and the
enhancement rules key off the grade, so this matters:

| Korean | Literal | questlog.gg English | Inven Global / patch-note English | Enhancement behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 일반 | Common | Common | Common | Enhanceable, 100% to +10 (inferred) |
| 희귀 | Rare | Rare | Rare | 100% success to +10 |
| 전승 | Heritage | Epic | Legendary | 100% success to +10 |
| 유일 | Unique | Legend | Unique | Success rate decays from +4 |
| 스페셜 | Special | Special | Paid / cosmetic | Cannot be enhanced, no stats |

Throughout this chapter I use the Korean-anchored terms **Rare**, **Legendary**
(전승) and **Unique** (유일), because those are the words the enhancement rule is
written in. If you are reading questlog.gg, mentally translate its "Legend" to
Unique and its "Epic" to Legendary. Logged in [[Glossary KR TW EN]].

### The paid-wings firewall

NCSoft drew an explicit line at the 2026-05-26 developer livestream: wings sold
in the cash shop, wings given as ranking rewards, and wings from the Deva Pass
battle pass **cannot be enhanced and carry no stats**. Only wings earned in game
— quests, dungeon drops, shop currencies, world collection — enter the stat
system. This is the clearest statement of the game's monetisation boundary in
any system, and it means the wing chapter is entirely a play-time chapter. See
[[Seasons and Battle Pass]] and [[Global Launch Differences]].

## Progression path

Read the chapter in this order. Each note assumes the one before it.

1. **[[Wings Acquisition]]** — first, because retention effects mean the cheapest
   power in the chapter is simply owning more pairs. Read this before you spend
   any shop currency anywhere in the game.
2. **[[Wings Enhancement]]** — second, because enhancement only pays off once you
   have a wide collection to spread it across. The correct strategy is
   deliberately not "max one pair".
3. **[[Flight and Traversal]]** — third, but start practising the wing-fold
   technique on day one. Traversal speed compounds with every collection route
   in [[Collections and Exploration]].

Then connect outward:

- [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] and [[Hidden Cubes]] are the world-collection
  routes that flight speed multiplies.
- [[Pantheon]] and [[Pantheon Stats]] are the other big "owning things is power"
  system; wings and Pantheon share the same mental model.
- [[Wardrobe and Skin Collection]] is where appearance and collection state live.
- [[Nightmare Mode]] and [[Seasons and Battle Pass]] hold the two most-wanted
  equip effects in the game.

## Priorities and breakpoints

| Stage | Do this | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ascension to level 30 | Take every free pair. Craft cheap pairs from gathered Pure Odyle. | Flight Power is your bottleneck long before combat stats are |
| Mid game | Buy the wings in every shop that sells them, even the ones you will not wear | Retention effects are unconditional |
| Post-enhancement unlock | Push every owned pair to +5 before pushing anything to +6 | Success rate falls off a cliff at +6 |
| Endgame | Pick one equip pair for PvE bossing and one for PvP, then grind +10 on the highest-value ones only | +6 to +10 costs roughly eight times what +1 to +5 costs |

## Costs and math

The headline numbers, all sourced in the child notes:

- One Unique pair from +0 to +5, assuming no failures: 12,800 Enhancement
  Stones, 27 Tuning Stones (Unique), 3,240,000 Kinah.
- One Unique pair from +0 to +10, expected value including failures: roughly
  78,000 Enhancement Stones, roughly 160 Tuning Stones (Unique) and roughly
  19.6 million Kinah spent directly, which one Korean analyst converted to a
  total imputed cost of about **76 million Kinah**.
- Ten pairs to +10: roughly **1.15 billion Kinah**.

The gap between the +5 target and the +10 target is the defining decision of the
chapter, and it is roughly a factor of twenty in resources for a factor of two
in stats.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Buying cosmetic wings expecting stats.** Cash-shop, ranking-reward and
  battle-pass wings are inert. They cannot be enhanced and they do not add
  retention effects.
- **Dismantling wings for Enhancement Stones.** A spare pair dismantles for
  around 500 Enhancement Stones, but you destroy a permanent retention effect
  and +200 Flight Power to get roughly 0.6% of the stones needed for one pair to
  +10. Only dismantle exact duplicates.
- **Funnelling everything into one "best" pair.** Item level and retention stats
  accumulate across all owned pairs, so an even spread beats a spike.
- **Paying to raise the success rate.** Korean and Taiwanese cost analyses agree
  that the base rate is the cheapest route at every single step.
- **Skipping the ascension quest.** It gates the entire rest of the character.

## Global launch notes

Everything above is drawn from the KR and TW live servers as of the 2026-07-01
Chapter 1 update, several seasons ahead of where a global launch is likely to
start. The material questions for a September 2026 global player are:

- Does global launch with the wing enhancement system at all? It arrived in KR
  six months after launch, in Season 3.
- Which wings exist at global launch? Roughly a third of the current KR list
  comes from Season 2 and Season 3 content.
- Do the season-shop and Nightmare-shop wings rotate out permanently? If they
  do, several of the best equip effects are missable. See
