# Nightmare Mode

> **Season 1 verdict.** Layer I from ~1,000 is in. Recommended item levels
> above 3,800 (Tyrant Tashin 4,050, Layer IV up to 4,950) are later seasons.
> Farm the launch bosses; do not plan around Layer IV.

> **In one sentence:** Nightmare (악몽) is a solo single-boss ladder with two
> attempts per day that pays Dream Fragments, and it is the only reliable
> source of the best [[Pantheon]] colossus and most Pantheon statues.

## Why it matters

Nightmare is the load-bearing content for this chapter. The Season 1
best-in-slot colossus, Zikel's Thoughtform, is bought from the Nightmare
exchange shop for 14,000 Dream Fragments and cannot be obtained any other way
in practice, because the alternative colossus was gated behind more
achievements than the game contained. Three of the four statue slots are
typically filled from the same shop.

It matters for three further reasons beyond the Pantheon:

- The daily attempt counter is the hardest constraint in the chapter. Two
  attempts per day, no more, and a boss must be cleared at challenge levels 1
  through 10 in sequence, so each boss costs at least ten attempts. Missed days
  are permanently lost progress. This is the single most common reason a player
  is months behind on their Pantheon.
- The shop also sells Daevanion crystals and skill point stones, which are
  direct combat power. See [[Daevanion Boards]] and
  [[Skills and Mastery Points]].
- It is a genuine skill check. A Korean player noted that Nightmare bosses are
  a "technique and fake-stat inspection machine": you cannot buy your way
  through them, because they are solo fights with tight timers.

Nightmare is also one of the few endgame systems that needs no group, which
makes it the most reliable progression for a solo player. See
[[Dungeons and Raids]] for how it sits alongside the group content.

## How it works

### Structure

- **Solo only.** One boss per instance, no party.
- **Challenge levels 1 to 10 per boss.** You pick the level, but you clear them
  in order, and each clear advances you one level.
- **Tier gating.** Clearing challenge level 10 of a boss unlocks the next
  boss up the ladder. The exact unlock graph is per-boss rather than per-layer
  in later content: Tyrant Tashin, for example, requires clearing challenge
  level 10 of *either* Incarnated Warding Feather or Modified Bargott, each of
  which has its own two-boss prerequisite.
- **Two attempts per day**, recharging daily. Each stage clear consumes one
  attempt.
- **Layers by season.** Bosses are grouped into tabs called Dream Layers
  (몽층). Season 1 bosses are in Dream Layer I, Season 2 in Layer II, Season 3
  in Layer III, with Layer IV added later. Twenty-six bosses exist across four
  layers as of 2026-09.
- **Random debuffs (Season 3 renewal).** On entry and again on each boss kill,
  a random debuff from a list is applied to you. Environmental objects inside
  the instance grant buffs if you interact with them, which is easy to miss.
- **Ranking season.** Ranks run from Iron up to Challenger 1, with rewards paid
  at season end. Since Season 3 the grade and tier are calculated per class
  rather than server-wide, and the ranking *buffs* that Nightmare used to grant
  were removed, converting season rewards to honorific plus decoration rewards.
- **Repeat rewards scale twice**: with the challenge level, and with your class
  ranking grade. Two players clearing the same level get different amounts.
  Clearing the highest level you can hold consistently is therefore the core
  efficiency rule.
- **Instant Clear tickets (Season 3).** After a normal clear, an Instant Clear
  button lets you claim N further runs' worth of repeat rewards and ranking
  points at once, consuming one Instant Clear Ticket: Nightmare and one attempt
  count per repeat. Tickets cost 30,000 kinah from the Breeze Merchants special
  shop, limited to 14 per server per week. Instant clears count toward the
  season mission "Nightmare Clear".

### Currency

The currency is 몽환의 파편, translated as **Dream Fragments** by the
Fextralife wiki and as **Phantasmal Fragments** by aion2hub. Both refer to the
same item. Note that Korean community posts sometimes write 악몽의 파편
("Nightmare Fragments"), which is a slip for the same currency.

Fragments carry over between seasons and the exchange shop does **not** reset
purchase history when a new season tab is added, so anything you bought stays
bought and anything you saved stays spendable.

## Unlock and gates

| Gate | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| Item level around 1,000 | Layer I challenge level 1, the entry point |
| Ten clears of a Layer I starter boss | The next boss on the ladder |
| Item level around 2,300 | Notun and Gerod challenge level 10, which unlock their statues in the shop |
| Item level around 2,400 to 2,500 | Zikel's Apparition, which unlocks the colossus purchase |

Item level here is what the community calls Combat Power; see
[[Item Level vs Combat Power]]. The listed values are gear checks on the
character, not entry restrictions, so a well-played and well-buffed character
below the suggestion can still clear.

The consumable set a Korean guardian used to clear Notun challenge level 10 at
roughly 2,000 to 2,100 item level, as a concrete baseline:

- Black Dragon Herbal Tea: natural recovery 140, action point recovery +50%
- Black Dragon Chopsteak: max HP +500, spirit +250, defence +250, block +100,
  action points +300
- Shock Mitigation Scroll: absorbs 350 per hit, up to 1,025 total
- Critical Enhancement Scroll: critical hit +100
- Combat Enhancement Scroll: damage amplification +10%, damage tolerance +10%
- Scroll of Courage: combat speed +10%
- Scroll of Blessing: max HP +10%, max spirit +100
- Superior potions, plus stigmas and skills weighted toward HP and spirit
  leeching

Even fully buffed, that clear finished with 40 seconds left on a five-minute
timer, which tells you how tight the DPS check is at the suggested item level.

## Progression path

### Reward and shop tables

orders of magnitude rather than exact current values.

| Reward | Dream Fragments |

### Suggested spend order

1. Colossus: Zikel's Thoughtform, 14,000. Nothing else first.
2. Statue: Fortress Guardian Notun, 8,000.
3. Statue: Furious Feruk, 5,000.
4. Daevanion Ariel Steps 1 and 2, and Wisdom Stone Steps 1 and 2.
5. Daevanion Ariel Step 3.
6. Cosmetics, only once the above are exhausted.

That order spends about 27,000 fragments on the Pantheon and about 34,500 on
Daevanion and skill points, which fits comfortably inside a Season 1 budget.

### Progression plan for a new global player

**Week 1, while levelling.** Do not touch Nightmare yet, but do not spend any
Content Usage Ticket Selection Chests either. Bank them.

**On reaching the item level for Layer I.** Open Nightmare and start
Gatekeeper Pinopi at challenge level 1. Use both attempts every single day
from this point forward. Set a reminder if necessary; the counter is the
bottleneck and it does not bank beyond its charge limit.

**Choose the Notun branch.** Korean guides advise pushing toward Fortress
Guardian Notun first, because Notun's statue is the best Nightmare statue and
it sits on the shortest path to Zikel's Apparition.

**Convert tickets to Nightmare.** If your pass and season missions give you
Content Usage Ticket Selection Chests, take the Nightmare challenge tickets.
A Korean player who converted 19 tickets reached Zikel's Apparition roughly ten
days earlier than a player who did not, which is worth roughly 10,000
additional fragments.

**Push each boss to level 10, then move up.** Do not farm a cleared boss for
repeat rewards while an uncleared boss still has first-clear rewards available.

**At the Zikel's Apparition wall.** Expect a hard DPS and mechanics check
around item level 2,400 to 2,500. Fully consumable-buff the attempt. Clearing
challenge level 1 is enough to unlock the colossus purchase.

**Buy the colossus immediately.** Then keep running the highest boss level you
can hold, and spend the surplus on Daevanion and skill points.

**Then hold the line.** Nightmare becomes a two-minute daily habit that funds
the rest of the chapter. Once the Pantheon items are bought, the shop is a
Daevanion and skill point faucet.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- Never lose a daily attempt. This dominates every other optimisation.
- Highest stable clear level beats safe farming, because both the challenge
  level and your class ranking grade multiply repeat rewards.
- First clears beat repeats. Push new bosses.
- Do not push into a level you fail. A failed attempt still consumes the
  attempt.
- Interact with environmental buff objects in Season 3-era instances. It is
  free damage and easy to overlook.
- Learn the boss patterns rather than out-gearing them. These are solo fights
  with timers, and a Korean guardian's account of Tyrant Tashin makes clear
  that pattern knowledge, not item level, is the gate: red ground markers
  cannot be blocked and must be dodged, orange ones can be blocked, and the
  large jump attack creates a circle that detonates outward a second time.

## Costs and math

- Two attempts per day, ten attempts minimum per boss, so a single boss is a
  five-day project at best.
- Seven Layer I bosses at ten levels each is seventy attempts, or thirty-five
  days of perfect attendance to clear Layer I. This is the real reason the
  Pantheon takes months.
- Season 1 ceiling was about 82,740 fragments; the Pantheon core cost 27,000 of
  them, about a third.
- Instant Clear tickets at 30,000 kinah and 14 per server per week let you
  convert kinah into up to 14 extra repeat-reward payouts per week, but they
  also consume attempt counts, so they accelerate reward income rather than
  ladder progress.

## Traps and mistakes

- Skipping days. Irrecoverable.
- Rushing the difficulty slider. Only raise it when you clear the current level
  comfortably without dying, because failures burn attempts.
- Spending fragments before the colossus. The single most repeated warning in
  Korean guides.
- Buying the 160,000-fragment cosmetic wings. Two seasons of income for an
  appearance.
- Farming a cleared boss instead of pushing an uncleared one, which forfeits
  the much larger first-clear rewards.
- Assuming ranking gives a combat buff. It did in Seasons 1 and 2; the ranking
  buff was removed in Season 3 and season rewards became honorific plus
  decorations.
- Assuming season ranking decorations are permanent. Their effects expire, and
  NC removed the Season 1 ones outright while leaving the items in inventories.
- Letting season-issued challenge tickets expire. Season 3 tickets expired at
  05:00 on 2026-07-01, for example.

## Global launch notes

- Global launch is 2026-10-05, with early access from 2026-09-30 and a launch
  scale test on 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-18.
- Nightmare is expected to ship with **Dream Layer I only**, with higher layers
  added over subsequent months. That means the reachable ceiling at launch is
  Zikel's Apparition and the reachable Pantheon items are the colossus plus the
  Layer I statues. This is a reasonable inference from the staged-content
  pattern rather than a confirmed content list; confidence low, and the
  strongest statement of it comes from a low-trust commercial site.
- The Season 3 quality-of-life features — Instant Clear tickets, random
  debuffs, per-class ranking, and the removal of ranking buffs — arrived five
  months into the Korean service. Whether the global build ships with them is
  unknown and materially changes the daily routine.
- Reward quantities were rebalanced at least once (Season 3 tied repeat rewards
  to class ranking grade), so the fragment figures above may not match the
  global economy.
- Korean season length was roughly two months for Season 1 (2025-11-19 to
  2026-01-21). **Launch Scale Test:** extra Odyle Energy expired **2026-12-16**,
  which Welps reads as global Season 1 lasting about two and a half months.
- Nightmare shop in the test sold **extra Daevanion crystals** (KR: Shugo only)
  and stigma shards. See [[Daevanion Boards]], [[Stigmas]].
