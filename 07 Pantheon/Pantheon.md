# Pantheon

> **In one sentence:** the Pantheon (만신전) is a permanent per-character trophy
> hall where placing one colossus, four statues and twelve artworks converts
> collected decorations into Lord stats, the percentage-based multiplier layer
> that sits on top of your entire stat sheet.

## Why it matters

The Pantheon is the cheapest large power gain in the game and the one most
players forget. It consumes no enhancement stones, no breakthrough materials
and no gear budget. Almost everything that goes into it is a by-product of
content you already run: ascension quest rewards, dungeon clear achievements,
Nightmare currency, Shugo Festa coins and world boss drops. Once a decoration
is placed the bonus is permanent and free.

Two properties make it strategically different from every other system in
[[Progression Phases Roadmap]]:

1. **It pays in percentages, not flat numbers.** Lord stats are expressed as
   percentage modifiers on defence, critical hit, combat speed, cooldown and
   Smite. A Wisdom-heavy Pantheon keeps scaling as your gear improves, whereas
   a flat manastone roll gets diluted.
2. **It does not show up in Combat Power.** A Korean player who measured the
   difference between two colossi confirmed that Lord stats do not raise the
   value the community calls "Combat Power" and the client calls item level.
   See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]]. This is why the Pantheon is invisible in
   gear checks and why so many players under-invest in it, and it is also why
   you must never trade Pantheon progress away for an item level number.

The Pantheon is one of only two meaningful sources of Lord stats, the other
being [[Arcana]]. [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] adds a large one-off block
of Lord stats through collection, and some wings and bracelets add more. All of
these feed the same ten pools with the same 200-point cap per pool, so the
Pantheon should be planned together with those systems, not in isolation. Note
that the Nightmare wings frequently recommended alongside a Pantheon build give
ordinary combat stats such as attack power and boss damage, not Lord stats;
see [[Wings Acquisition]].

## How it works

Open the Pantheon from the navigation bar in the top right of the screen. You
walk into a hall with a large plinth in the centre, four statue pedestals
around it and artwork frames on the walls. The **Pantheon Control Console**
(만신전 조정간) in front of you lists every decoration you own and lets you
place it.

Three decoration classes exist, and they are not interchangeable:

| Class | Korean | Slots | Point scale per item | Practical role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Colossus | 거상 | 1 (centre) | 35–45 per domain | Largest single upgrade in the chapter |
| Statue | 조각상 / 석상 | 4 | 8–12 per domain | Mid-sized, farmed from Nightmare and dungeon achievements |
| Artwork | 명화 / 액자 | 12 | 3–5 per domain | Small each, but twelve of them out-total the colossus |

Each decoration lists one to four **Lord domains** with a point value. Points
from every placed decoration are summed per domain, each domain caps at 200
points, and the total converts into two combat stats per domain. The full
mapping lives in [[Pantheon Stats]]; the slot rules, the statue catalogue and
recommended layouts live in [[Pantheon Statues and Frames]].

Since KR Season 3 (2026-04-08) you can save up to three Pantheon presets and
switch between them, which is what makes a separate PvE and PvP layout
practical. Placement itself is free and reversible at any time, so there is no
irreversible choice in this system.

## Unlock and gates

There is no separate unlock quest for the hall itself. The menu entry appears
during normal levelling and the first decorations arrive as quest rewards, so
in practice the system "opens" when you receive your first statue.

| Gate | What it opens | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 22 ascension (각성) reward chain | Statue: Eternal Artifact (Freedom 10) and Statue: Fragment of Dream (Freedom 8, Death 8); these are the two decorations Korean guides describe as "given to every normal player" | medium — two independent guides attribute them to 각성, and the level 22 ascension step is the one that gates the Spacetime Rift questline, but I did not find a patch note naming the exact quest |
| Level 45 and the end of the launch episode | The point at which Korean coverage tells players to go back and fill every empty slot, because by then a lot of unplaced decorations have accumulated | high |
| Nightmare Layer I, item level 2,400 | Zikel's Apparition challenge level 1, which unlocks the purchase of the Season 1 best-in-slot colossus | high |
| Draupnir Conquest cleared 10 times | The achievement that awards Statue: Transcended Bakarma, the only Unique-grade statue in Season 1 | high |

## Progression path

1. **Place everything you own, immediately.** Empty slots are pure loss and
   placement is free. Do this the moment you notice the menu.
2. **Take the two ascension statues** at level 22 and keep them slotted until
   something strictly better arrives.
3. **Start Nightmare as early as the item level allows** and never waste the
   two daily attempts. The attempt counter recharges daily and the ladder is
   long, so lost days are the single largest source of delay in this chapter.
4. **Bank Dream Fragments for the colossus.** The Season 1 colossus cost 14,000
   fragments; spending fragments on anything else before you own the colossus
   is the classic mistake.
5. **Clear Draupnir Conquest ten times** for Transcended Bakarma. Korean groups
   run last-boss-only lobbies specifically for this achievement.
6. **Buy the two or three cheap Nightmare statues** to fill the remaining
   pedestals.
7. **Fill artworks last and continuously.** They are the slowest and most
   expensive part, but twelve small artworks are worth more than the colossus,
   so do not treat them as optional.
8. **Build a second and third preset** once Season 3-equivalent presets are
   available, splitting PvE damage, PvP and a farming layout.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- Farm order is colossus, then statues, then artworks. This is the consensus
  across Korean and Chinese endgame guides and follows directly from points per
  slot.
- The single largest breakpoint in the chapter is the first Unique colossus.
  Going from the early Rare colossus (Justice 35, Time 35) to Zikel's
  Thoughtform (Justice 45, Time 45, Destruction 45) adds a whole third domain
  at full value.
- For PvE damage the target domain is Wisdom, because Wisdom drives Smite and
  Smite doubles a hit and can itself critically strike. See [[Pantheon Stats]]
  and [[Stat Reference]].
- For PvP and sieges Time is favoured over Wisdom, because combat speed and
  Smite Resist are more reliable than a proc when the enemy is also stacking
  resistances.
- Do not chase 200 in a domain you do not care about. The cap is per domain, and
  reaching it in Wisdom and Illusion is already beyond what a Season 1 or
  Season 2 item pool allows.

## Costs and math

- One point of a Lord domain raises each of its two linked combat stats. The
  in-game Lord effect tooltip, transcribed by a Korean player comparing two
  colossi, reads as 0.1 percent per point per linked stat: a 45-point
  difference in Death showed as critical hit +4.5 percent and regeneration
  penetration +4.5 percent. A Chinese endgame guide and one Korean video
  summary instead report roughly 0.2 percent per point, specifically for
  Wisdom to Smite. Treat 0.1 percent per point as the working figure and the
  Wisdom-to-Smite rate as unresolved. Confidence medium on the exact rate,
  high on the linear per-point behaviour.
- Domain cap is 200 points, after which the domain stops converting. Sourced
  from the aion2hub item database tooltip text.
- A realistic Season 1 endgame Pantheon reached roughly Wisdom 45 and Illusion
  45 from artworks alone, plus 45 in each of Justice, Time and Destruction from
  the colossus and 8 to 12 in each of Freedom, Death and Space from statues.
- Full cost of the Season 1 core: 14,000 Dream Fragments for the colossus plus
  8,000 and 5,000 for two statues, against a season-long ceiling of about
  82,740 fragments for a player who never missed a day.

## Traps and mistakes

- Leaving slots empty. It costs nothing to fill them and the effect is
  immediate.
- Spending Dream Fragments on Daevanion crystals, skill point stones or the
  cosmetic wings before buying the colossus. The wings alone cost 160,000
  fragments, which is roughly two full seasons of income.
- Assuming the Pantheon is account-wide. It is per character, and Korean
  players raising alts have complained about exactly this. Plan it as a
  main-character investment, see [[Alts and Account Wide Progression]].
- Judging the system by Combat Power. Lord stats do not move that number, so
  the Pantheon looks like it does nothing if you only watch item level.
- Treating seasonal ranking decorations as permanent. Several statues and
  artworks awarded for season rankings state that their effect lasts only until
  the end of the following season, and NC did remove Season 1 ranking
  decoration effects while leaving the items in the inventory.
- Confusing Zikel with a statue. Zikel's Thoughtform is a colossus and goes in
  the centre slot. Korean guides warn about this specifically.

## Global launch notes

The global and Western release is scheduled for 2026-10-05 on PC via Steam and
PURPLE in North America, South America, Europe and Asia, with five days of
early access from 2026-09-30 for Founder's Pack owners and a launch scale test
on 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-18. Everything in this chapter describes the Korean
service, live since 2025-11-19, cross-checked against Taiwan where possible.

Expectations for launch:

- The slot structure of one colossus, four statues and twelve artworks is
  original Season 1 design and should be present at launch.
- Nightmare will most likely ship with Layer I only, which means the reachable
  colossus at launch is Zikel's Thoughtform and the reachable statues are the
  Layer I set. Higher layers, and the statues attached to them, arrive with
  later content drops.
- Pantheon presets were added in KR Season 3, five months after Korean launch.
  Whether the global build ships with presets already included is unknown, and
  it matters for whether a separate PvP layout is practical on day one.
- Seasonal ranking decorations are tied to the Korean season calendar. The
  global season numbering will not match, so treat any statue with a season
  name in it as a schedule question rather than a stat question.

See [[Global Launch Differences]].
