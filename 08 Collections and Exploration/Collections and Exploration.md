# Collections and Exploration

> **In one sentence:** the collection systems are the cheapest permanent power in
> AION 2 — they cost time and attention rather than Kinah, gear luck or cash, and
> almost none of them can be bought.

## Why it matters

Every other chapter in this vault competes for the same three scarce resources:
Kinah, Enhancement Stones and dungeon entries. Collections do not. What they
consume is world time — flying to a ledge, opening a chest, extracting a green
item you were going to vendor anyway — and they pay out in the exact currencies
the rest of the game gates behind RNG:

- **Skill points.** The Verteron and Altgard Monoliths hand out 112 Wisdom Stones
  (지혜의 돌), each worth one permanent skill point. This is the single largest
  skill-point source in the game and cannot be purchased. See
  [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] and [[Skills and Mastery Points]].
- **Amulet progression.** The Revelation Amulet (PvE) and Fierce Battle Amulet
  (PvP) can *only* be enhanced with scrolls from Monolith resonance levels. There
  is no drop, no vendor and no auction-house route. See [[Accessories]].
- **A percentage multiplier on your damage.** The Wardrobe grants a Weapon Attack
  Power Increase percentage that multiplies your stat attack. Experienced Korean
  players report 7–10% as normal and 21.2% at the high end — a bigger swing than
  most single gear upgrades. See [[Wardrobe and Skin Collection]].
- **Enhancement Stones at industrial scale.** Surplus feathers convert at 50
  Enhancement Stones each, for roughly 84,000 stones from a full sweep. That is
  described in Korean guides as the largest single-route stone income in the game.
  See [[Enchanting]].
- **Free stat lines you never take off.** Titles give an always-on "Collection"
  bonus for merely owning them, plus a larger bonus when equipped. Registering
  every Kibelisk in your home zone is worth PvE Accuracy 72 and PvE Damage Boost
  3.6% equipped, and PvE Defense 20 permanently. See [[Titles and Achievements]].

The trap is that all of it looks optional. Feathers look like scenery, cubes look
like flavour chests, and the Wardrobe looks like a fashion menu. Korean guides are
unanimous that the visible power gap between two players of the same level and
item level is mostly this chapter.

## Unlock and gates

- **Kibelisks** register from level 1 by walking near them. Nothing to unlock.
- **Wardrobe** opens the first time you extract or dismantle a piece of gear.
- **Monolith** opens through a main-quest step at character level 16, which grants
  a Monolith Express voucher for a free first resonance.
- **Hidden Cubes** are open world from the start, but keys arrive from quests and
  daily missions, and the best rewards want a key.
- **Reshanta (Abyss) traces** need flight and tolerate PvP interference.
- **Eltnen and Morheim** (Chapter 1 zones) need character level 45, item level
  3,300 and combat power 270,000 to enter through the Legion airship portal, per
  the Taiwanese PURPLE Lounge notice.

## Progression path

Order matters here, because two of these systems are gated by flight power and one
of them is almost free.

1. **Levels 1–16, free actions only.** Register every Kibelisk you walk past. Pick
   up every feather you can see from the ground. Register every "NEW" appearance
   the moment a piece of gear becomes obsolete — never vendor an unregistered
   skin. Open every cube you find, with a key if you have one.
2. **Level 16, first resonance.** Do the Monolith main quest, use the Express
   voucher, and start spending Wisdom Stones immediately. Mastery skill reset is
   free, so there is no reason to hoard points.
3. **Levels 16–45, opportunistic.** Do not detour for feathers yet. Many sit on
   tower tops and under cliffs that need real flight power. Keep registering
   Kibelisks, keep extracting gear into the Wardrobe.
4. **Level 45, the sweep.** With fourth-ascension wings you can clear the field
   feather route in bulk. Target 140 pickups first: at 4 traces per pickup that is
   the full 560 needed for Monolith level 30 and every reward on the ladder. Then
   decide whether to finish the remaining ~420 pickups for Enhancement Stones.
5. **Endgame, maintenance.** Reshanta traces for the PvP ladder, the Wardrobe
   grind toward 700 skins, and achievement lines that hand out Pantheon statues.

## Costs and math

Collections are nearly Kinah-free. The real costs are:

- **Time.** A full 560-location feather sweep is reported by Korean route guides as
  a multi-session project even with a completed map, spread across roughly 20
  named sub-areas per faction.
- **Abyss Points**, if you take the shortcut to Unique skins by buying and
  dismantling Guardian Decanus accessories. One English guide puts the net cost at
  about 8,000 AP per guaranteed Unique skin after an 80% AP refund; treat that
  figure as medium confidence.
- **Opportunity cost of extraction.** Extracting gear returns 100% of invested
  Enhancement Stones for normal gear but only 70% for Abyss gear, so route Abyss
  pieces through the Wardrobe deliberately rather than by accident.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Vendoring or selling gear whose appearance you have not registered.** The skin
  is gone. Korean guides state the rule bluntly: if the tooltip says NEW, dismantle
  it, never sell it.
- **Registering other classes' weapon skins and expecting stats.** They count
  toward the 700-skin achievement but contribute nothing to Wardrobe collection
  stats. Only your own class's weapons feed the stat side.
- **Assuming cash-shop skins help.** Appearances obtainable only with cash are
  excluded from collection points.
- **Rushing feathers at low level.** A meaningful fraction are unreachable without
  flight power, and the auto-move shortcut that used to path you to the nearest
  uncollected feather was removed in the 2025-11-22 emergency maintenance.
- **Opening cubes without a key when you have keys.** Keys amplify a cube's energy
  and materially raise the reward grade. Keys are abundant; cube spawns are not.
- **Treating housing as a power system.** True housing is not live yet. The stat
  system people mean is the Pantheon; see [[Pantheon]].

## Global launch notes

The global/Western version enters five-day Early Access on 2026-09-30 and launches
2026-10-05 on Steam and PURPLE, with eight classes rather than the nine on
KR/TW. That eight-class roster strongly suggests global launches on pre-Chapter-1
content, which would mean:

- Verteron, Altgard and Reshanta monoliths present; **Eltnen and Morheim monoliths
  probably absent at launch**.
- Level cap 45 rather than 50, so the flight power needed for the feather sweep
  arrives at the same place in the curve.
- The 4-traces-per-pickup buff, the surplus-to-Enhancement-Stone conversion and
  the Monolith succession feature are all quality-of-life patches from December
  2025 and should be baked into a September 2026 build, but NC has not published a
  content parity list. See [[Global Launch Differences]].
- Housing will not be present. It was announced at the 2026-06-14 Summer Festa as
  a Chapter 1 roadmap item, sequenced after sailing and fishing.

Everything above comes from the KR/TW live service as of Chapter 1 (2026-07-01)
unless stated otherwise.
