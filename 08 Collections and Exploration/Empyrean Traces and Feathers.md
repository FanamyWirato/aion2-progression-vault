# Empyrean Traces and Feathers

> **Global Launch Scale Test:** feathers still exist, but they **do not grant
> Skill Points**. Side quests and Sealed Dungeons do that job while you level.
> Collect the field after the cap for amulet scrolls and leftover Monolith
> rewards, not for mastery. See [[Global Launch Differences]].

> **In one sentence:** the red feathers scattered across the world are the only
> source of 112 permanent skill points and the only source of amulet enhancement
> scrolls, and a full sweep also pays about 84,000 Enhancement Stones.

## Why it matters

This is the highest-value non-gear progression system in AION 2 and it is entirely
free. Three of its outputs have no substitute:

1. **Wisdom Stones.** 112 of them from the level-30 field Monolith, each granting
   one permanent skill point. Korean guides describe this as a gap that money
   cannot close, because Wisdom Stones are not sold. See [[Skills and Mastery Points]].
2. **Revelation Amulet Enhance Scrolls.** The Revelation Amulet (PvE) has no drop
   table, no vendor and no auction-house listing. It is the piece of gear you keep
   from the early game and upgrade forever, and Monolith resonance is the only
   supply of its enhancement scrolls. Same story for the Fierce Battle Amulet
   (PvP) and the Ereshkigal Monolith. See [[Accessories]].
3. **Lord attribute points.** Resonance levels hand out flat points in the ten
   deity attributes, which convert into percentage stat lines. A Vortex Gaming
   free-to-play guide names the traces as the foundation of getting every Lord stat
   above 200. See [[Pantheon Stats]] and [[Stat Reference]].

The amulet is the reason to care early. A Korean beginner guide puts it plainly:
picking up feathers raises your real damage faster than grinding mobs, because the
amulet carries Weapon Damage Amplification and Penetration — first-tier PvE
multipliers — and nothing else supplies them at that stage.

## How it works

Feathers are static world objects — a red feather shape — that you interact with
like a gathering node. Collecting one adds a bound token to your inventory. Tokens
are **zone-specific**: `Empyrean Trace: Verteron`, `Empyrean Trace: Altgard`,
`Empyrean Trace: Eltnen`, `Empyrean Trace: Morheim`, `Empyrean Trace: Reshanta`.
Each token type only resonates with its own Monolith.

You then travel to that zone's Monolith and resonate. Resonance raises the pillar
one level at a time; each level has a **cumulative** token threshold and pays out
stat gains plus items.

### Pickup yield and the 4x patch

Originally one pickup gave one trace, so completing the 560-token reward track
meant finding all ~560 physical locations. In the 2025-12-03 patch (announced on a
developer livestream), each pickup was changed to grant **4 traces**.

| | Before 2025-12-03 | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traces per pickup | 1 | 4 |
| Pickups needed for the full 560-token reward track | 560 | **140** |
| Traces available from a full 560-location sweep | 560 | **2,240** |
| Surplus after completing the track | 0 | **1,680** |

This is the single most-misreported number about the system. "140" is the number
of *pickups* needed, not the number of locations that exist. Roughly 560 locations
exist per faction field, plus about 200 more in the Abyss.

## Unlock and gates

The Monolith opens through a main-quest step at **character level 16** called
"Monolith of the Lord". Growth-support rewards include a **Monolith Express
voucher**: right-click it in front of the pillar to bank a free chunk of resonance
progress and collect the early rewards in one action.

Wisdom Stones are usable from level 4. Feathers themselves have no level gate, but
a meaningful fraction sit on tower roofs, under bridges, on cliff faces and on
floating islands. Korean route guides recommend waiting until level 45 and
fourth-ascension wings before attempting a systematic sweep. See [[Wings and Flight]].

## Progression path

1. **Level 16:** finish the Monolith quest, use the Express voucher, resonate.
2. **Levels 16–44:** pick up every feather you can reach from the ground or a short
   glide. Resonate whenever you pass a Monolith. Spend Wisdom Stones immediately —
   mastery reset is free.
3. **Around 140 pickups (560 tokens):** you have the entire field ladder including
   all 112 Wisdom Stones and all 30 Revelation scrolls. This is the real finish
   line for power.
4. **Level 45+, Reshanta:** collect Abyss traces for the Ereshkigal ladder. Go in a
   group or at off-peak hours; the opposing faction is present.
5. **Optional completion:** finish the remaining ~420 field pickups purely for
   surplus conversion into Enhancement Stones.
6. **Chapter 1 zones:** Eltnen or Morheim traces for 12 more Revelation scrolls and
   the Epic amulet conversion.

## Costs and math

**Skill points.** 112 Wisdom Stones from the field Monolith. Skills level to 10 on
ordinary points; Wisdom Stones are how you push mastery skills toward the 16 cap,
where the specialisation slots at 8, 12 and 16 sit. See [[Skills and Mastery Points]].

**Surplus conversion.** After the 4x patch, a completed field zone yields:

```
560 pickup locations × 4 traces = 2,240 traces
- 560 traces consumed by the reward track
= 1,680 surplus traces
1,680 × 50 Enhancement Stones = 84,000 Enhancement Stones
```

The 50-stones-per-trace rate is a material-conversion recipe (물질 변환) available
for Verteron and Altgard traces. Two independent Korean sources give the rate and
the 84,000 total, and a third describes it as the largest single-route Enhancement
Stone income in the game. It is strictly one-time: once converted, that supply is
gone. See [[Enchanting]] and [[Crafting]].

**Respec cost: free.** The brief that opened this research assumed a Kinah fee for
resetting skill points. That was true at launch, but an emergency update around
2025-11-21 made **mastery skill reset completely free**, and you can reset either
the whole tree or a single skill. Stigma skill reset is the expensive one, at
20,000 Kinah per level for levels 1–4 and 40,000 per level for 5–10, so a level-5
Stigma skill costs 100,000 Kinah to undo. Life-skill resets still cost Kinah. See
[[Stigmas]].

## Cross-faction and cross-character rules

**Cross-faction:** you cannot collect the other faction's field traces. Verteron
traces exist only in the Elyos zone and Altgard traces only in the Asmodian zone,
and the tokens are faction-specific and bound. Reshanta is the only shared trace
pool, and there both factions collect the same `Empyrean Trace: Reshanta` token for
their own Ereshkigal Monolith. Contesting those pickups is a normal part of Abyss
PvP.

**Cross-character:** collection is recorded per character. A December 2025 Korean
FAQ states explicitly that trace collection history and Monolith level are
character-bound and an alt has to collect again. The **2025-12-17 patch added a
succession (계승) function to the Monolith** that shares collection status between
characters on the same server, which materially changes alt planning — do the
route once properly on your main and your alts benefit. What is not yet clear from
public sources is whether succession transfers the resonance *level* or only the
*collection record*. See [[Alts and Account Wide Progression]].

## Efficient route and priority plan

- **Enable the map filter.** Map (M) → filter icon, bottom right → Contents →
  Empyrean Trace. Collected feathers show as ticked, so the map doubles as a
  checklist.
- **Check progress at the pillar.** Interact with the Monolith and press the
  feather "i" button to see per-sub-area collection counts. This is how you find
  the last two or three you are missing without re-walking the whole zone.
- **Expect the 558/559 problem.** Route guides warn that some feathers are filed
  under a sub-area name that does not match where they physically sit — a feather
  categorised under "Legion of Dawn Base" can actually be on a Kantas Valley
  floating island. Cross-reference a community map when you stall.
- **The auto-move shortcut is gone.** Selecting a map point and pressing move used
  to path you to the nearest uncollected feather; that was removed in the
  2025-11-22 emergency maintenance. Some later Korean posts claim map filtering and
  auto-move were improved again, so behaviour may vary by patch.
- **Manage the flight gauge.** Fold your wings and land wherever the feather is
  reachable from the ground. Glide down rather than flying up where the terrain
  allows. Wind currents (바람길) marked on community maps are free altitude.
- **Fold cube hunting into the route.** Feather routes and Hidden Cube locations
  overlap heavily; run them together. See [[Hidden Cubes]].
- **Community tools.** The Inven feather-check pages (Elyos and Asmodian) are
  clickable checklists with wind-current overlays; a Taiwanese interactive map
  provides in-game screenshots per feather; aion2atlas and aion2hub both offer
  filterable maps with local progress saving.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Feather-farming from level 1.** Korean guides actively advise against it. You
  will burn hours failing to reach pickups that need flight power you do not have.
- **Hoarding Wisdom Stones.** Reset is free. Use them.
- **Converting surplus too early.** Convert only after you have banked the
  resonance levels you want, since the reward track needs tokens, not stones.
- **Forgetting to resonate.** Traces sitting in your bag do nothing. Reward payout
  happens at the pillar, not at pickup.
- **Assuming feathers are just an achievement.** They are the sole source of amulet
  scrolls, and the amulet is a semi-permanent slot you never replace.
- **Waiting for the Chapter 1 zones to matter.** They give no Wisdom Stones. Finish
  the level-30 field ladder first.

## Global launch notes

Global Early Access begins 2026-09-30 and full launch 2026-10-05. Expect
Verteron/Altgard and Ereshkigal monoliths, not Eltnen/Morheim.

**Launch Scale Test, 2026-09-17/18:**

- Feathers **do not grant Skill Points.** Side quests: 1 Skill Point + 1
  Daevanion point. Sealed Dungeon clear: 2 + 2. The 112-Wisdom-Stone claim in
  this note is KR. You still pick feathers up after levelling for the rest of
  the resonance track.
- Monolith stat steps are **one point per level**, not the KR 2/2/2/2/2/10/10
  pattern, and the reported **+620 Stamina** (about four extra dodges) is gone.
- Skins from the pillar are gone. Reset Codex, Hidden Cube keys, and Shugo art
  fragments still pay. Artwork itself is buyable in the shop from generic
  fragments (KR forced you into a specific minigame).

4x pickup, surplus conversion, and succession are still *should-be-present*
December 2025 QoL. Diagnostic: if a pickup gives one trace, you are on a
pre-patch yield. See [[Monolith Resonance]] and [[Global Launch Differences]].
