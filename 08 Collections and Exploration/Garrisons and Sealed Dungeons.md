# Garrisons and Sealed Dungeons

> **In one sentence:** the single largest block of one-off permanent power in
> AION 2 — roughly 170 small instances across both factions that pay the only
> Belt Enhance Scrolls in the game, the bulk of your Daevanion points, and about
> 280 points of permanent progression that nothing else replaces.

## Why it matters

Korean guides use the word 내실 (*naesil*, "inner substance") for the category
this content belongs to: growth that does not show up as a shiny new item but
that permanently raises what your character is. Two independent Korean sources
call clearing all of it the highest power-per-hour activity available to a
fresh max-level character, and the KR press framing is blunter still — the
gamevu guide is titled "hit max level, now what? go do Sealed Dungeons and
Garrisons".

Three reasons it is not optional:

1. **Garrisons are the only source of Belt Enhance Scrolls.** The Noble Belt
   (고결의 허리띠) you are handed in the early story is the belt you use forever,
   it cannot be replaced, and it cannot be enhanced without these scrolls. No
   drop, no vendor, no market listing. See [[Accessories]].
2. **Sealed Dungeons are the bulk supply of Daevanion Crystals.** Every clear
   pays 2 Daevanion node points, each point is worth +1 to your progression
   metric, and there are roughly 70 Sealed Dungeons per faction. Your own
   faction's crystals are **not enough** to open all four standard Daevanion
   boards, which is why the enemy-territory set is mandatory rather than
   optional. See [[Daevanion Boards]].
3. **They charge the Ascension Gauge.** The main story deliberately stalls: the
   next chapter will not open until the Ascension Gauge reads 100%, and Sealed
   Dungeons, Garrisons and region quests are what fill it. The final level-45
   ascension quest ("Daeva Sunk in Nightmares") is gated the same way. You
   cannot skip this content and stay on the story. See [[Level and Ascension]].

Everything here is one-off. Clear an instance and it is done forever, which
makes this the only major content block in the game with a hard finish line.

## How they differ

Both appear on the world map as a **question mark** while undiscovered. Walk
close enough and the icon resolves, and that is when you can tell them apart:

| | Sealed Dungeon | Garrison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Map icon once discovered | **Circle** | **Campfire** |
| Korean map path | Map → Exploration → Sealed Dungeon | Map → Exploration → Garrison |
| Setting | A cave or sealed chamber instance | The interior of an enemy encampment |
| Structure | Combat and puzzle rooms; some are boss-only, some are trash-then-boss | Objective-driven: reach a point, open chests, then usually a boss |
| Objective marker | Yellow inverted triangle on the target | Objective list given on entry |
| Size | Smaller | Slightly larger, chest-collection focused |
| Typical clear time | Under 10 minutes | Comparable, more travel inside |
| Count per faction home field | **~70** (see below) | **15** |
| Headline reward | Daevanion Crystal ×2, Enhancement Stones | **Belt Enhance Scroll ×1, guaranteed** |
| Can you skip the fights? | Mostly no, mobs are aggressive | Partly yes — only fight what the objective needs |

Both auto-generate an exit prompt on completion, so you never walk back to the
entrance, and both pay their clear reward immediately.

The practical difference in play is that Garrisons let you route around combat.
Korean guides note that a Garrison drops you inside a base packed with enemies
but you only need to satisfy the stated objective — walk the objective line,
open the chest, kill the boss, leave. Sealed Dungeons want the room cleared, and
because almost every mob is aggressive the standard technique is pulling three
or four at a time and killing them together.

## How many exist, per faction and per zone

This is the weakest-sourced part of the note and the numbers deserve care.

| Set | Sealed Dungeons | Garrisons | Confidence |

| Elyos home field (Poeta, Verteron and their sub-regions) | 60+ per namu.wiki, "about 70" per a KR item-level guide | **15** | Garrisons high (four sources agree); Sealed Dungeons medium |
| Asmodian home field (Ishalgen, Altgard and their sub-regions) | same, mirrored | **15** | same |
| Reshanta / Abyss | present, count unpublished | present, count unpublished | low |
| Eltnen (Chapter 1, Elyos) — roadmap | **20** | **6** | high, from NC's own Summer Festa announcement |
| Morheim (Chapter 1, Asmodian) — roadmap | **20** | **6** | high, same source |

Four separate Korean sources — Inven's core tips round-up, the Inven amulet and
belt guide, a Daum/Gametoc press piece and a savetip guide — all independently
give **15 Garrisons in your own faction's field**, phrased as 각 종족 맵마다
("per racial map"). That is as solid as a community number gets.

The Sealed Dungeon count is softer. namu.wiki says "60-something or more per
race"; a savetip author says "I did not count them myself, but about 70 per
faction". Both are estimates. What nobody publishes is a **per-zone breakdown**,
and I could not find one in Korean, Chinese or English. Do not plan from a
per-zone table you find in an English SEO article — treat those as invented
unless they cite a source.

**The in-game list is the authority and it is better than any guide.** Open the
map, go to the Exploration tab, and pick Sealed Dungeon or Garrison. You get the
complete list for the zone with your clear status on each, which turns the whole
system into a checklist. Use that, not a blog.

Region quests (지역 퀘스트, green icons) sit alongside both and pay the same

almost perfectly. Note that the Ariel and Azphel boards can *only* be opened
with their own named crystals, so ordinary Sealed Dungeon crystals will never
finish those two.

### The level-scaling rule that decides your timing

This is the one mechanic that changes your whole plan, and it is easy to miss.

**Clear rewards are level-independent. Mob drops during the run are not.**
Sealed Dungeon content scales to your level, so the items dropped by the
monsters inside are dramatically better at max level. Inven's guidance is
explicit: do region quests alongside the main story because they charge the
Ascension Gauge, but **bank the Sealed Dungeons and clear them in a sweep after
max level**, because otherwise you throw away the drop table.

The exception is the quest-stall sections. When the story stops and demands a
100% Ascension Gauge, clear whatever is nearest — including Sealed Dungeons —
because the alternative is not progressing at all.

## The Belt ceiling, and why enemy territory is mandatory

The Noble Belt runs the same +10-then-promote loop as the Revelation Amulet:
enhance to +10 with scrolls plus Kinah, unequip, promote through Material
Conversion, repeat from +0 at the higher grade. See [[Accessories]] for the loop
itself and [[Material Conversion]] for the mechanics.

Where the Garrisons come in is the scroll budget, and the numbers are unusually
specific:

| Milestone | Garrison clears needed | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home territory swept | **15** (all of your faction's field Garrisons) | Belt reaches **Heritage grade +5** — described by four Korean sources as exactly, precisely where 15 scrolls land you |
| Promotion to Unique | **5 more**, from enemy territory | Belt reaches **Unique grade**, then continues toward +10 |
| Unique +10 | further clears | The stop line. Beyond Unique +10 the stones-per-level cost climbs steeply for little stat gain |

Inven's phrasing on the 15-clear ceiling is worth quoting because it is the
clearest statement of the design: 시공이나 어비스에 가지 않고 필드 공략만으로
가능한 최대 구간 — "the maximum you can reach by field content alone, without
going to the Rift or the Abyss."

The Unique +10 belt, per a Korean write-up: **Defense 500 (+300), HP 1,100
(+500), HP Potion Recovery +30%, Regeneration 5%.** That is a survivability
block you never take off, which is why the belt outranks most of the accessory
group despite its unimpressive item level.

The "5 more from enemy territory" figure is single-sourced (a savetip guide) and
is the one number here I would most like a second confirmation on — but it is
consistent with the mechanic, and every other source agrees that Unique
specifically requires crossing over. Confidence **medium**.

## Enemy territory: the Rift of Time and Space

You cannot walk to the other faction's Garrisons. You take the Rift of Time and
Space (시공의 균열), a timed portal that drops you into the enemy's field.

| Property | Value | Source and date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening times, original | 2, 6 and 10 o'clock — every 4 hours | KR video guide, late 2025 |
| Opening times, current | **2, 5, 8 and 11 o'clock — every 3 hours** | TW official PURPLE Lounge, post-Chapter-1 rework |
| Time inside | about 30 minutes | TW community guide |
| Quest volume | 120+ quests across own plus enemy territory combined | TW official |
| PvP mode | Toggle added in the rework. Default is PvE. Switching applies a **70-minute cooldown**, and you **cannot switch while inside enemy territory** | TW official |
| What is over there | Region quests (green), Sealed Dungeons and Garrisons, plus extra Abyss Points | KR and TW, multiple |

The PvP toggle detail is the one that changes behaviour: because you cannot
change mode inside enemy land, **set it before you go through the portal**. Get
that wrong and you either spend 30 minutes flagged in hostile territory or you
lose access to the PvP rewards you went for.

Practical advice that recurs across KR and TW guides:

- Wait at the portal location *before* the opening time. The window is short and
  travel eats it.
- **Plant a Kisk immediately on arrival** to secure a safe respawn point.
- Go as a party after max level with a real gear set. A solo undergeared visitor
  is, in one Korean guide's words, a points vending machine.
- Route quests and instances together in one loop rather than doing a dedicated
  Garrison trip — 30 minutes does not allow for backtracking.
- Garrisons are the friendlier target of the two over there. A TW guide notes
  Garrisons can be entered directly from the air with no ground entrance, which
  makes them easier to reach without meeting the locals.

## What the whole block is actually worth

The claim that circulates in English is "300 to 400 item level". The honest
version is close in magnitude but the unit is contested, so here is the full
picture with the disagreement visible.

The mechanism everyone agrees on: **each Daevanion node point is worth +1** to
your progression metric, Sealed Dungeons pay 2 points per clear, and there are
about 70 per faction.

```
~70 Sealed Dungeons x 2 points  = ~140 per faction
Both factions via the Rift      = ~280
Region quests on top            = a further large block, count unpublished
```

The unit conflict:

| Source | Says the unit is |
| --- | --- |
| Inven's post-max-level power guide | **Combat Power +1** per Daevanion point, and adds that titles raise stats but *not* Combat Power |
| Vortex Gaming's translation of a KR video guide | **Combat Power +1** per crystal |
| A KR item-level guide (savetip) | **item level** — "clear your own faction for about 140 levels, both for 280" |

Two sources against one, and the two agreeing sources are the more careful ones,
so **Combat Power is the better reading**. Since Combat Power is the metric
groups actually screen on — see [[Item Level vs Combat Power]] — that makes the
content *more* valuable, not less. The "300 to 400" figure previous research
flagged is plausible as a combined total once region quests are included, but no
source states it directly. Treat 280 from Sealed Dungeons as the corroborated
core and anything above it as an estimate.

For scale, the same Vortex guide gives Combat Power of **900–1,000 at max
level** before any of this content, rising to **1,500–1,700** after the
foundation sweep plus early Expeditions. So this block is roughly a 20–30%
increase on a fresh max-level character. A TW guide puts a post-story character
at about 1,400 after clearing side quests and Sealed Dungeons, against a 1,540
target for entering advanced group content.

## Per character or shared?

**Per character**, with medium confidence. No source states that Garrison or
Sealed Dungeon clears are shared, and the surrounding evidence points the same
way:

- The Monolith needed a **dedicated patch** (2025-12-17) to become server-shared,
  and the patch note names only the Monolith. If exploration clears were already
  shared, that patch would have been unnecessary.
- Chapter 1 separately made **special-grade costumes and pets** server-shared,
  again as a named exception rather than a general rule.
- The Chapter 1 Growth Support event awards Sealed Dungeon and Garrison Express
  vouchers as **per-character bound (각인)** items that cannot be moved through
  the warehouse, which only makes sense if the underlying progress is
  per-character.

So an alt starts from zero on this content. That is a real cost, and it is the
main argument for the Daeva Express items below. See
[[Alts and Account Wide Progression]].

## Daeva Express: the instant-clear items

NC announced **Daeva Express** (데바 익스프레스) at the 2026-06-14 Summer Festa
and shipped it with Chapter 1 on 2026-07-01, described in its own announcement
as a system that "completes Garrisons, Sealed Dungeons and Empyrean Traces at
once". It exists because the developers concluded that making every alt re-walk
170 instances was a retention problem.

It arrives in three forms:

| Form | Cost | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zone Express vouchers — "Verteron Sealed Dungeon Express", "Altgard Garrison Express" and the other three combinations | **500 Cuna each** (paid currency) | **7 per server** |
| The same vouchers, free | Chapter 1 Growth Support event, stages 1 and 2, for clearing one Sealed Dungeon and one Garrison in Verteron or Altgard | event-limited |
| Bundled in the "Exciting First Steps" Growth Support package | package purchase | one-time |

All are **bound (각인)** and cannot be warehoused, so they must be bought on the
character that will use them.

Do not confuse these with the **Instant Complete Voucher: Daily Dungeon**
(즉시 완료권: 일일던전), which is a different item entirely: 30,000 Kinah from
the Wind Breeze Trading Company special tab, 14 per server per week, and it
repeats a *daily dungeon* reward up to 36 times after one manual clear. That one
has nothing to do with Garrisons. See [[Daily and Weekly Loops]].

**Whether Daeva Express exists at global launch is genuinely uncertain.** It is a
Chapter 1 feature, and Chapter 1 is past the expected launch content state.
Plan on walking the routes.

## Achievements and titles

Titles here follow the usual owned-versus-equipped split described in
[[Titles and Achievements]], and per Inven they raise stats but contribute
**nothing to Combat Power** — so do not judge them on the CP display.

The clearest documented example, and a good illustration of how the achievement
layer is structured, is the Verteron chain to the Unique-grade title
**"Beyond the Gate of Life and Death"** (나생문을 넘어선):

1. Clear Sealed Dungeon **Distorted Cave** (일그러진 동굴) — 1 o'clock from the
   Destroyed Abyss Gate; nearest Kibelisk is Verteron Fortress Valley.
2. Clear **Crevice Cave** (틈새 동굴) — 5 o'clock from the Legion of Dawn
   Temporary Outpost, which has its own Kibelisk.
3. Clear **Rift Cave** (균열의 동굴) — 6 o'clock from Artamia Canyon, or 3
   o'clock from the Beast Hunter's Camp; use the Wind Scar Kibelisk.
4. Only then can you enter **Ruins of the Ancient City of Roo**
   (고대 도시 루의 폐허), on the island at the far west of the Verteron map via
   the Aulau Funeral Island Kibelisk. Inside, kill all objective monsters within
   a **15-minute limit**.

All three prerequisite dungeons use the same mechanic — memorise which platforms
light up blue, then step on them — and a KR press piece times the whole chain at
about 30 minutes. The important structural lesson: **some Sealed Dungeons are
locked behind other Sealed Dungeons**, so a "clear everything" sweep needs to
respect prerequisite order, and the in-game Exploration list is where you see
which ones are still greyed out.

An English guide also names a **"Through Hell and Back"** title from finishing
Verteron's local Sealed Dungeons; that is most likely the same title under a
different localisation, but it is single-sourced and low-trust, so treat the
English name as unconfirmed. Beyond these, no source publishes a full
achievement table for a complete Garrison or Sealed Dungeon sweep. What is
documented is that the **Monolith's** achievement rewards were reduced on
2025-12-17 — no equivalent change is recorded for these two systems.

## A launch player's checklist

Built for the Season 1 baseline: level cap 45, gates from 0 to about 3,800, no
Heroic gear, no Chapter 1 zones.

**Phase 1 — while levelling (1 to 44)**

- [ ] Take every region quest (green) as it appears and clear it alongside the
      main story. These charge the Ascension Gauge and pay Daevanion points.
- [ ] Clear **Garrisons as you pass them**. Their reward is level-independent, so
      there is no reason to delay, and the Belt scroll loop wants an early start.
- [ ] **Bank the Sealed Dungeons.** Note them on the map, do not clear them.
      Their mob drops scale to your level and you want them at 45.
- [ ] Exception: when the story stalls on a 100% Ascension Gauge, clear the
      nearest Sealed Dungeons to break through.
- [ ] Never sell or discard the Noble Belt.

**Phase 2 — the max-level sweep, home territory**

- [ ] Map → Exploration → Garrison. Confirm all **15** are ticked. That is
      Heritage +5 on the belt.
- [ ] Map → Exploration → Sealed Dungeon. Work zone by zone, clearing clusters
      that share a travel loop. Expect roughly 70. Respect prerequisite chains
      like Distorted → Crevice → Rift → Ruins of Roo.
- [ ] Spend Daevanion Crystals the moment they arrive. A KR video guide's
      priority: Attack Power, Defense, HP, then active and passive skill levels;
      skip the Spirit lines.
- [ ] Collect Empyrean Traces on the same routes — they overlap heavily. See
      [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] and [[Monolith Resonance]].
- [ ] Fold [[Hidden Cubes]] into the same loops for the same reason.

**Phase 3 — enemy territory, via the Rift of Time and Space**

- [ ] Set your PvP mode **before** entering. You cannot change it inside.
- [ ] Be at the portal before the opening time (2/5/8/11 on the current KR/TW
      cadence).
- [ ] Plant a Kisk on arrival.
- [ ] Priority order inside, given only ~30 minutes: Garrisons first (5 clears
      takes the belt to Unique), then Sealed Dungeons for the second ~140 points,
      then region quests.
- [ ] Go as a party.

**Phase 4 — roadmap, when Chapter 1 arrives**

- [ ] Eltnen or Morheim: 20 Sealed Dungeons and 6 Garrisons in your faction's
      new zone, at level 50.
- [ ] The Abyss Garrisons and Sealed Dungeons in Reshanta.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Clearing Sealed Dungeons at low level.** The clear reward is the same but the
  mob drops are level-scaled. This is the most commonly repeated piece of
  Korean advice about this content and the easiest value to throw away.
- **Selling or discarding the Noble Belt.** It cannot be replaced.
- **Trying to promote the belt past Heritage without crossing over.** Fifteen
  home Garrisons land exactly on Heritage +5 by design. If you are stuck there,
  the answer is the Rift, not more field content.
- **Entering the Rift with the wrong PvP mode set.** 70-minute cooldown, and you
  cannot change it once you are inside enemy land.
- **Going to enemy territory solo and undergeared.** You become Abyss Points for
  someone else.
- **Forgetting the prerequisite chains.** A dungeon you cannot enter looks
  identical to one you have not found. The Exploration list distinguishes them.
- **Buying Express vouchers on your main to give to an alt.** They are bound and
  cannot be warehoused. Buy them on the character that will use them.
- **Judging titles by Combat Power.** They give stats and show zero CP.
- **Assuming an alt inherits any of this.** Only the Monolith got server sharing.
- **Pushing a dungeon accessory or the belt past Unique +10 in the mid-game.**
  See [[Accessories]].

## Global launch notes

Against the KR/TW live state as of Chapter 1:

- **Present at launch:** home-territory Garrisons and Sealed Dungeons, Rift of
  Time and Space, Noble Belt loop to Unique +10. No item-level check of their
  own.
- **Launch Scale Test:** completing a Sealed Dungeon grants **2 Skill Points
  and 2 Daevanion points**. That is a levelling faucet KR used feathers for.
  See [[Skills and Mastery Points]].
- **Roadmap:** Eltnen/Morheim sets, Heroic belt conversion, possibly Daeva
  Express.
- **Uncertain:** the Rift cadence. Global may ship the original 4-hour
  2/6/10 schedule rather than the reworked 3-hour 2/5/8/11 one, and the PvP-mode
  toggle arrived with the same rework. Check the in-game timer rather than a
  guide.
