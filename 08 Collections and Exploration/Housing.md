# Housing

> **In one sentence:** true player housing is announced but not live, and the
> system people mean when they say "housing gives stats" is the Pantheon — a
> personal trophy room inside the Legion airship where placed statues and paintings
> grant real combat stats.

## Why it matters

Two separate answers, because "housing" in AION 2 currently refers to two
different things.

**The Pantheon (만신전) matters a great deal.** It is a personal instanced space
where you place trophies won from dungeons, bosses, achievements and region quests,
and those placements grant character stats. Korean guides list it alongside the
Wardrobe and pet understanding as one of the systems that is easy to forget and
expensive to ignore, and one Korean endgame article calls it "the Pantheon, which
you are highly likely to forget". A Taiwanese progression guide is blunter: "you
must open the Pantheon — after unlocking it, configure it immediately, this is the
core system for raising your attributes."

**Actual housing does not matter yet, and will not at global launch.** It was
announced on 2026-06-14 as a Chapter 1 roadmap item, sequenced *after* sailing and
fishing, and as of September 2026 it is not live. Nothing in the announcement
material suggests it carries stats.

The mechanical detail of the Pantheon belongs to [[Pantheon]],
[[Pantheon Statues and Frames]] and [[Pantheon Stats]]. This note covers the
housing shell: what spaces exist, how you unlock and pay for them, what they grant
mechanically, and whether any of it deserves your time at global launch.

## Terminology and aliases

| Term | Korean | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pantheon** | 만신전 | Your personal trophy room inside the Legion airship. Carries stats. |
| **Legion airship** | 비공정 | The Legion (guild) headquarters, a physical flying area above the world map. Also rendered "Legion Hall", "Legion Spaceship" or "Legion base" in English write-ups. |
| **Legion** | 레기온 | The guild system. See [[Activities and Economy]]. |
| **Housing** | 하우징 | Announced but unreleased personal houses. |
| Painting / frame | 명화 / 액자 | Framed collectibles placed on the Pantheon's outer ring. |
| Statue | 조각상 | Mid-tier Pantheon sculptures. |
| Colossus | 거상 | The single central Pantheon sculpture, the strongest slot. |

Do not confuse the Pantheon with the Legion airship it sits inside. The airship is
shared with your Legion; the Pantheon within it is yours alone.

## How it works

### The three spaces, today

| Space | Scope | Grants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pantheon** | Personal, instanced, inside the airship | Character stats from placed statues, colossus and paintings; up to 3 saved presets since Season 3 |
| **Legion airship** | Guild-wide, a physical visitable location | Crafting tables, merchants, storage manager, voice chat, and (since Chapter 1) portals to the Eltnen and Morheim regions |
| **Personal housing** | Not live | Announced: interior and exterior decorating, fences, statues, furniture, and pets released into the yard |

### Pantheon layout

The Pantheon has a fixed number of placement slots:

| Slot type | Count | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colossus (거상) | **1** | Central. Strongest options. First priority. |
| Statue (조각상) | **4** | Second priority, sourced from boss and dungeon-completion rewards. |
| Painting (명화) | **12** | Individually weak, but twelve of them add up to a meaningful synergy. |

Since the Season 3 update (KR 2026-04-08) you can save up to **three Pantheon
presets** and swap instantly between PvE, PvP and farming configurations.

### Where the display items come from

This is what makes housing a *collection* system rather than a decoration menu.
The items are trophies:

| Item | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Statue: Kibelisk | Registering all 61 Kibelisks in your home zone (see [[Kibelisks and Map Registration]]) |
| Transcended Vakarma statue | Clearing Draupnir Conquest 10 times |
| Zikel's Thought Form (colossus) | Killing Zikel; also purchasable from the Nightmare exchange shop (see [[Nightmare Mode]]) |
| Krome's Desire (colossus) | Temple of Fire stage 4; requires 32 achievements in its tab, of which only 27 are currently achievable |
| Paintings | Region quest rewards, field bosses (which drop "Painting Remnants"), Shugo Festa materials, crafting with Advanced and Highest-Grade Frame Crafting Toolboxes, and events |
| Artwork décor (e.g. Artwork: Urugugu) | Storable and tradable items whose effect activates **only when placed in the Pantheon** |

Because achievements and exploration completion feed the statue and painting
supply, the Pantheon is where several chapters of this vault converge. See
[[Titles and Achievements]].

### The Legion airship: utility, not stats

The airship is genuinely useful but its benefits are convenience and logistics
rather than character power:

- Crafting tables, merchants and a storage manager, positioned for quick access.
- Voice chat and a gathering point for the Legion.
- Return scrolls, bought from general shops anywhere in the world, teleport you to it.
- Since Chapter 1, portals to the Eltnen and Morheim regional bases — subject to
  level 45, item level 3,300 and combat power 270,000.
- Officers can upgrade the airship's facilities using **Legion Coins**.

Notably, NC declined to implement **Legion donation** at the Summer Festa on
abuse-potential grounds, and said it would reconsider "once guild housing arrives"
— which confirms that guild housing is a separate, later thing from the personal
housing on the Chapter 1 roadmap.

### Announced personal housing

From the 2026-06-14 Summer Festa and a subsequent developer presentation on NC's
official channel:

- Starts as an **instanced personal house**.
- Later expands to **field plots on floating sky islands** that you are allotted
  and build on.
- Players decorate **interior and exterior**: fences, statues, assorted
  decorations, and modern-styled furniture.
- **Pets can be released into the yard** to be raised and interacted with. See
  [[Pets and Mounts]].
- Sequencing: sailing and fishing ship first, housing targeted **before Chapter 1
  ends**.

No source — official or community — mentions stat bonuses, buffs, storage
expansion or crafting benefits from personal housing. One low-trust blog post about
Pantheon artwork explicitly states that artwork effects apply only in the Pantheon
and **not in personal housing**, which is at least consistent with housing being
cosmetic. Treat "housing gives no stats" as the working assumption at medium
confidence until the patch notes land.

## Progression path

1. **Join a Legion early** and buy a return scroll so you can reach the airship.
2. **Open your Pantheon the moment it is available.** The Taiwanese progression
   guide treats this as a post-main-story priority, not an optional flourish.
3. **Fill the 12 painting slots first with whatever you have.** They are the easiest
   slots to populate and region quests supply them.
4. **Get a colossus.** This is the biggest single stat item in the space. The
   Nightmare exchange shop route is the reliable one; Korean guides advise buying a
   statue or colossus there **before** spending on wings.
5. **Fill the 4 statue slots** from conquest-difficulty dungeon completions.
6. **Claim the Kibelisk statue** by finishing your zone's Kibelisk registration —
   the cheapest Pantheon item in the game.
7. **Save three presets** once you have surplus items: PvE, PvP and farming.
8. **Ignore personal housing** until it ships and someone confirms whether it has
   mechanical effects.

## Costs and math

The honest summary is that housing has no meaningful cost-benefit math yet, and the
Pantheon's math lives in [[Pantheon Stats]]. What is quantified here:

- **1 colossus + 4 statues + 12 paintings** = 17 placement slots.
- **3 presets** since 2026-04-08.
- A Vortex Gaming free-to-play guide reports **135 Lord stat points** obtainable from
  buying a Unique-grade colossus in the Nightmare shop, and puts Zikel's Thought Form
  at Justice 45 / Time 45 / Destruction 45. A different Korean guide gives
  Transcended Vakarma at Freedom 12 / Death 12 / Space 12 from the Draupnir Conquest
  ×10 reward. These are the scale of numbers involved.
- **32 achievements** needed for Krome's Desire, of which 27 are currently possible —
  so that colossus is temporarily unobtainable.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Assuming housing is a power system.** It is announced, unreleased, and shows no
  sign of carrying stats. The stat system is the Pantheon.
- **Leaving trophy items in your inventory.** Artwork and statue effects only apply
  when placed. This is stated explicitly for artwork.
- **Confusing the Pantheon with the Legion airship.** Airship facilities are
  convenience; the Pantheon inside it is where the stats are.
- **Buying wings before a colossus.** Korean free-to-play guides recommend the
  statue or colossus purchase first out of the Nightmare shop. See
  [[Wings Acquisition]].
- **Optimising layout before upgrading grades.** Raising a piece from Unique to Epic
  moves its numbers far more than rearranging the set.
- **Planning around Krome's Desire.** Its achievement requirement exceeds what the
  current patch allows.
- **Budgeting for a monthly airship fee.** The ~1,000,000 Kinah per month upkeep
  figure is single-source and low trust; do not treat it as fact.

## Global launch notes

**Personal housing will not be in the global launch build.** It was announced on
2026-06-14 as a roadmap item to arrive on KR/TW before the end of Chapter 1, behind
sailing and fishing in the queue. Global launches on 2026-10-05, and given the
eight-class roster it is very likely running pre-Chapter-1 content. Do not factor
housing into any global launch plan.

**Is any of this worth time at global launch?** Yes, but only the Pantheon and only
after the main story. Its items come from content you will be running anyway —
region quests, conquest dungeons, Nightmare, field bosses and Kibelisk completion —
so the marginal cost is placing them rather than farming them. The Legion airship
is worth joining a Legion for on day one, purely for the consolidated crafting,
merchant and storage access.

What is uncertain for global: whether the Season 3 Pantheon preset feature (KR
2026-04-08) is in the launch build, and whether Legion airship pricing has been
localised. See [[Global Launch Differences]] and [[Seasons and Battle Pass]].
