# Kibelisks and Map Registration

> **In one sentence:** walking past a Kibelisk (키벨리스크) registers it for free,
> and registering all 61 in your home zone pays an Epic title worth PvE Accuracy
> 72 and PvE Damage Boost 3.6% plus a Pantheon statue — which is why every guide
> says register everything.

## Why it matters

This is the cheapest reward in the entire vault. The action costs nothing: you walk
within range and the Kibelisk binds itself. Guides insist on it for two reasons,
and most English guides only mention the first.

**The convenience reason.** Kibelisks are the teleport destination network. AION 2's
main story sends you back through zones you have already cleared, repeatedly. A
registered Kibelisk plus a cheap teleport scroll from any general merchant turns a
five-minute flight into an instant jump. The time saved compounds over a full
levelling run, and it is the difference between a smooth region-quest sweep and an
hour of backtracking.

**The power reason.** Completing the set is a real stat reward:

| Achievement | Requirement | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verteron Binding Master (베르테론 각인 마스터) | Activate all **61** Kibelisks in Verteron | Kinah (Bound), **Title: Teleportation Specialist**, **Statue: Kibelisk** |
| Altgard Binding Veteran | Activate all **61** Kibelisks in Altgard | same shape |

| Title | Grade | Equipped effect | Owned effect (always on) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teleportation Specialist (공간이동 능력자) | Epic | PvE Accuracy 72, PvE Damage Boost 3.6% | PvE Defense 20 |

PvE Damage Boost 3.6% from an activity that costs zero resources is a better rate
of return than any enchanting step. And the **Statue: Kibelisk** is a Pantheon
placement item — see [[Pantheon Statues and Frames]] — which means the reward
feeds a second stat system on top of the title. This is the fact that gets lost in
English guides that describe Kibelisks as pure travel convenience.

## Terminology and aliases

| Term | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Kibelisk** / 키벨리스크 | The persistent world binding and teleport point. Preferred term. |
| 각인 (binding / engraving) | The Korean verb for registering one. The achievement family is named after it, which is why questlog renders the achievements as "Binding Master" and "Binding Veteran". |
| Kisk / 키스크 | **A different thing.** A deployable, consumable resurrection obelisk you place for your group, inherited from AION 1. Do not confuse the two. |
| Obelisk | Occasionally used loosely in English write-ups for either. Avoid. |

## How it works

Approach a Kibelisk and it registers automatically — there is no interaction
prompt and no cost. Once registered it appears as a teleport destination on the
world map and minimap.

One nuance that trips people up: the Kibelisk object itself does **not** offer a
teleport menu when you stand at it. You teleport *to* Kibelisks from elsewhere. A
Korean overview guide states this directly ("registers automatically when you go
near, no teleport function"), which reads as a contradiction of the English guides
until you realise they are describing the two ends of the same journey.

The fastest workflow, from an Inven tip post:

1. Open the Journal (J) and select a region quest.
2. Click the map icon next to the Move button — this opens the map centred on the
   quest start point rather than dumping you at world scale.
3. Pick the nearest registered Kibelisk and teleport, or click the green diamond
   to auto-move.

Teleport scrolls are bought from general merchants and are described as cheap.

## The wider map-registration and exploration-completion family

Kibelisks are one of several "touch it once, keep the credit" systems. They are
worth treating as a single levelling habit.

| System | Korean | What registering or clearing grants | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kibelisks | 키벨리스크 | Teleport network; Teleportation Specialist title; Kibelisk Pantheon statue | this note |
| Empyrean Traces | 주신의 흔적 | Skill points, amulet scrolls, Lord attributes | [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] |
| Hidden Cubes | 히든 큐브 | Theostones, wings, skins, Soul Codex | [[Hidden Cubes]] |
| Strongholds / Outposts | 주둔지 | Belt Enhance Scrolls — required to reach the final belt tier | [[Accessories]], [[Equipment]] |
| Sealed Dungeons | 봉인 던전 | Daevanion Crystals and Daevanion Points; "Seal Breaker" titles exist per zone | [[Daevanion Boards]] |
| Region quests | 지역 퀘스트 | Daevanion Points, Pantheon paintings and statues, roughly 4,000,000 Kinah in total across a faction's side quests | [[Daily and Weekly Loops]] |
| Kisks | 키스크 | Achievement lines only: Kisk Installer (install 5/10/30/60/100) and Kisk User (register someone else's, 5/10/30/60/100), paying Status Effect Resist Scrolls and Kinah | this note |

Both Kisk achievement lines are consumable-tier rewards with no stat titles
attached, so they are prestige and convenience rather than power. They do count
toward whatever aggregate achievement tallies exist; see [[Titles and Achievements]].

A Taiwanese progression guide sequences all of this explicitly: after the main
story, go back and clear every region side quest, every sealed dungeon and every
outpost, because those supply Daevanion Crystals, skill points and titles that are
"real combat power increases", and only then chase the Empyrean Traces. That
ordering is worth respecting.

## Unlock and gates

None. Kibelisks register from level 1. The only limits are:

- You must physically travel within range of each one at least once.
- Some Kibelisks sit in awkward places. A Korean expedition guide warns that the
  "Faint Crevice" (희미한 틈새) Kibelisk in particular must be pre-registered
  before you need it, or your group loses the shortcut.
- Chapter 1 zones need level 45, item level 3,300 and combat power 270,000, so
  Eltnen and Morheim Kibelisks arrive late.

## Progression path

1. **Levels 1–45:** register every Kibelisk you pass. Do not detour for them during
   the main story; the story route passes most of them naturally.
2. **After the main story:** open the map, look for unregistered Kibelisk icons in
   your home zone, and clean up the gaps. This is a short trip and it closes out
   Binding Master / Binding Veteran.
3. **Immediately after:** place the Kibelisk statue in your Pantheon and equip
   Teleportation Specialist if nothing better is competing for the slot.
4. **Ongoing:** register Chapter 1 zone Kibelisks as you unlock those regions.
5. **Group content:** pre-register the crevice and outpost Kibelisks before
   attempting conquest-difficulty expeditions.

## Costs and math

- **61 Kibelisks** per home zone, per the achievement step data on questlog.gg for
  both Verteron and Altgard. Counts for Eltnen, Morheim and Reshanta are not
  published.
- **Zero resource cost** to register.
- Teleport scroll price is described as cheap but no source gives the figure.
- The reward is one Epic title, one bound Kinah payout of unknown size, and one
  Pantheon statue.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Making a special trip early.** Every guide says the same thing: register them
  as you pass, do not farm them. The main story route covers most of the 61.
- **Confusing Kibelisks with Kisks.** They share a naming root and an obelisk
  silhouette but do completely different jobs. Kisk achievements are worth almost
  nothing; Kibelisk completion is worth an Epic title.
- **Standing at a Kibelisk expecting a teleport menu.** Teleport from the map or the
  Journal, not from the object.
- **Skipping the last few.** The achievement is binary at 61. Fifty-eight
  registrations pays nothing.
- **Forgetting to place the statue.** The Kibelisk statue does nothing sitting in
  your inventory; Pantheon décor effects only apply when placed.
- **Entering conquest content without pre-registering the route.** Named as a
  specific failure mode in Korean expedition guides.

## Global launch notes

Kibelisks are core traversal content present since the 2025-11-19 KR/TW launch and
should be identical at global launch on 2026-10-05. Expect 61 in Verteron and 61 in
Altgard; Eltnen and Morheim Kibelisks will only exist if global ships with Chapter 1
content, which the eight-class launch roster makes unlikely. The Teleportation
Specialist title and the Kibelisk statue should both be present. See
[[Global Launch Differences]].
