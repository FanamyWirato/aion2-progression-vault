# Monolith Resonance

> **In one sentence:** the Monolith is the machine that turns collected feathers
> into permanent power — 112 skill points, every amulet enhance scroll in the
> game, 240 Empyrean Lord attribute points and 26 Hidden Cube keys — and its
> whole reward track is one-off, so the only decision that matters is the order
> you claim it in.

## How this note divides from the feathers note

[[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] owns the **supply side**: where the feathers
are, how many exist, route order, map filters, flight requirements. This note
owns the **demand side**: the pillar itself, the resonance ladder, what each
level pays, how the payouts convert into character power, and the rules for
sharing progress between characters. If you want to know where to walk, read
that note. If you want to know what you are walking for and in what order,
read this one.

## Why it matters

Resonance is the only source of four things:

1. **Revelation Amulet Enhance Scrolls** and **Fierce Battle Amulet Enhance
   Scrolls.** Neither amulet drops, vendors or lists on the auction house, and
   neither can be enhanced with ordinary Enhancement Stones. The pillar is the
   entire supply chain for a slot you keep from the early story to the endgame.
   See [[Accessories]].
2. **Wisdom Stones** at scale. 112 from the level-30 field ladder. A Korean
   skill-point audit puts a fully finished character at **423 total skill
   points** once every Wisdom Stone source and every one-time Chapter 1 content
   block is banked, which makes the Monolith roughly a quarter of your lifetime
   skill-point budget from a single system. See [[Skills and Mastery Points]].
3. **Empyrean Lord attribute points in bulk.** 240 points across the ten deity
   attributes from the field ladder alone. The Bracelet and Arcana are the only
   other sources, and both are replacement ladders you fight for; this one is
   free and permanent. See [[Pantheon Stats]] and [[Stat Reference]].
4. **PvP defensive stats that cost no Abyss Points.** The Abyss ladder pays
   PvP Defense +300 and PvP Critical Hit Resist +40 for feathers rather than
   rank. See [[PvP Gear]].

## How many Monoliths exist

The commonly repeated figure is **six**, and it needs unpacking, because two of
the six are the same pillar and one of the six does not exist under the name
people give it.

aion2hub's database holds six *entries*, which resolve to **five distinct
pillars**:

| Pillar | Zone | Faction | Levels | Tokens for max | Token item |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Verteron Monolith | Verteron | Elyos | 30 | 560 | Empyrean Trace: Verteron |
| Altgard Monolith | Altgard | Asmodian | 30 | 560 | Empyrean Trace: Altgard |
| Ereshkigal Monolith | Reshanta (Abyss) | Elyos entry | 20 | 300 | Empyrean Trace: Reshanta |
| Ereshkigal Monolith | Reshanta (Abyss) | Asmodian entry | 20 | 300 | Empyrean Trace: Reshanta |
| Eltnen Monolith | Eltnen | Elyos | 15 | 140 | Empyrean Trace: Eltnen |
| Morheim Monolith | Morheim | Asmodian | 15 | 140 | Empyrean Trace: Morheim |

Two corrections to the circulating list:

- **There is no separate "Abyss Monolith".** The Ereshkigal Monolith *is* the
  Abyss pillar — Ereshkigal is a Reshanta location. Any list that names both is
  double-counting one pillar. The Ereshkigal ladder appears twice in the
  database only because each faction has its own entry point into the same
  20-level, identically-costed track.
- **One character can only ever reach three of them:** its home field pillar,
  its home Chapter 1 pillar, and Ereshkigal. Tokens are bound and
  zone-specific, so an Elyos character has no route to Altgard traces at all.

**At the global Season 1 baseline the number drops to two.** Eltnen and Morheim
arrived with Chapter 1 on KR/TW on 2026-07-01, which is well past the content
state a 2026-10-05 launch is expected to ship, so a launch character has
Verteron-or-Altgard plus Ereshkigal and nothing else. Treat the Chapter 1
pillars as **roadmap**.

## How resonance works

1. Collect the zone's traces in the field. They arrive as bound, zone-tagged
   tokens.
2. Travel to that zone's pillar and resonate. Korean guides place the Elyos
   pillar at the Legion of Dawn Base and the Asmodian pillar at Fugitive
   Village.
3. Each resonance level has a **cumulative** token threshold, not a per-level
   cost. Reaching level 20 costs 235 tokens total, not 235 on top of the
   previous 215.
4. Every level pays a stat block plus an item block, both immediately.
5. Interact with the pillar and use the feather "i" button to see per-sub-area
   collection counts — this is the in-game progress checklist.

Traces sitting in your bag do nothing. Nothing pays out at pickup; everything
pays out at the pillar.

## The Lord attribute payout, and a dated conflict

### What aion2hub's table sums to

| Attribute | Lord | Field total | Derived stat at 0.2% per point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Life | Yustiel | +20 | HP and Regeneration +4% |
| Destiny (Fate) | Marchutan | +20 | MP and Endurance +4% |
| Freedom | Vaizel | +20 | Accuracy and Evasion +4% |
| Death | Triniel | +20 | Critical and Regeneration Penetration +4% |
| Illusion | Kaisinel | +20 | Cooldown Reduction and Endurance Penetration +4% |
| Wisdom | Lumiel | +20 | **Smite +4%**, MP Cost Reduction −4% |
| Justice | Nezekan | +20 | Defense and Perfect Chance +4% |
| Destruction | Zikel | +20 | Attack and Perfect Resist +4% |
| Time | Siel | **+40** | Combat Speed and Smite Resist +8% |
| Space | Israphel | **+40** | Movement Speed and Block +8% |

That is **240 attribute points**, eight attributes at +20 and Time and Space at
+40. The derived percentages use the flat 0.2% per point conversion documented
in [[Stat Reference]], which has no diminishing returns, so the arithmetic is
straightforward. The Time and Space double weighting is not an accident of the
table — those two are paid in +10 chunks at levels 5, 10, 15 and 25 while every
other attribute is paid in +2 and +4 dribbles.

The practically important line is **Wisdom +20 → Smite +4%**, because Wisdom is
the only source of Smite, Smite is a flat damage doubling when it procs, and
Korean stat guides treat 30% Smite as a hard minimum. Four percentage points of
a 30-point requirement, free and permanent, from content you were doing anyway.

### The conflict, with dates

| Source | Date | What it says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| aion2hub Monoliths database | read 2026-09-06, post-Chapter 1 KR/TW data | +20 on eight attributes, +40 Time, +40 Space |
| Korean reward summaries circulated around the KR launch window | December 2025 | Justice +12, Destruction +12, Illusion +18, Wisdom +18; Time and Space **not listed at all** |

Both are recorded here rather than reconciled, because they may both be correct
for their own build. Three observations that lean toward the aion2hub figures
being current:

- The aion2hub per-level table sums *exactly* to +20/+20/+40, and the same table
  sums exactly to the community-verified 112 Wisdom Stones and 30 scrolls. A
  table that is right about the items is probably right about the stats.
- The December summaries only ever list four attributes. Omitting Time and Space
  entirely reads like an incomplete write-up rather than a different patch.
- The 2025-12-17 patch explicitly **reduced Monolith achievement reward
  amounts** as compensation for adding trace sharing. That is a documented
  Monolith reward change in exactly that window, so reward values in this system
  demonstrably did move.

Confidence: **high** on the item rewards, **medium** on the attribute totals
until an in-game level-30 screenshot settles it.

### The Stamina unit problem

Summing the field table gives Stamina +62,000, while Korean guides consistently
report the in-UI result as **Action Power (행동력) +320**. These are the same
reward in different units — +62,000 is internal, +320 is what your character
sheet shows. Use +320 when talking to other players.

## Material-conversion gates

Resonance feeds four separate [[Material Conversion]] recipes, and each has its
own gate. This is where most planning mistakes happen.

| Recipe | Gate | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rare → Epic Revelation Amulet | Amulet at +10, item **unequipped** | Substance Morph (Alt + H) refuses equipped and preset-registered items |
| Epic → **Unique** Revelation Amulet | **Resonance level 21** plus amulet at +10 | The single most important breakpoint in the system |
| Unique +10 → **Heroic** Revelation Amulet | Chapter 1 blueprint plus the "Revelation Amulet Enhance Scroll (Engraving)" from the Eltnen/Morheim ladders | Roadmap; Heroic grade is not in the launch build |
| Surplus Empyrean Trace → Enhancement Stones | 50 stones per trace | See below |

The surplus conversion is the one with the strangest constraint. TW's official
PURPLE Lounge growth guide states the 84,000-stone payout is available
**once per server**, not once per character — so this is a server-level windfall
you spend once, not an alt-farmable income. Arithmetic, from a full field sweep
after the 4x pickup patch:

```
560 pickup locations x 4 traces  = 2,240 traces
- 560 traces consumed by the ladder
= 1,680 surplus traces
1,680 x 50 Enhancement Stones    = 84,000 Enhancement Stones
```

Two Korean sources and the TW official guide all give the same 50-per-trace rate
and the same 84,000 total, and one describes it as the largest single-route
Enhancement Stone income in the game. It is strictly one-time. See
[[Enchanting]].

No source documents a resonance-level gate on the trace-to-stone recipe itself;
the practical gate is that the ladder eats tokens first, so converting early
costs you ladder levels.

## Inheritance of God's Traces (KR 2025-12-17)

The single most important patch for anyone running alts, and the one most often
described incorrectly.

**What the patch note actually says.** Use the "Inheritance of God's Traces"
(주신의 흔적 계승) function at any zone's Monolith to share **collection status**
between characters on the same account and the same server. The shared traces
are then used to raise **each character's own resonance level**.

**So it transfers the collection record, not the resonance level.** The Korean
wording — 캐릭터별 공명 레벨, "per-character resonance level" — is explicit, and
aion2hub's English rendering agrees: "inherit collection progress across your
server's characters to raise each character's resonance level". Your alt does
not wake up at resonance 30. Your alt wakes up holding the traces and has to
resonate them itself, which means it claims the entire reward ladder — its own
112 Wisdom Stones, its own 30 scrolls, its own 240 attribute points — without
walking a single step of the route. That is a much better outcome than level
transfer would have been, and it is the reason a serious alt plan in AION 2
starts with one properly finished feather sweep on the main.

This resolves the open question carried in [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] and
[[Collections and Exploration]]. Confidence: **high**, from the official patch
note text as reproduced by Inven, Gamechosun and Gametoc independently.

Three caveats:

- **Same server, same account only.** Cross-server characters get nothing.
- **Monolith achievement rewards were reduced** in the same patch as
  compensation. If you find a pre-December reward figure for a Monolith
  achievement, it is stale.
- **It shipped broken.** Pressing the inheritance button while the "Monolith of
  the Lord" quest was still in progress returned "Inheritance of God's Traces
  failed. Please try again shortly." NC acknowledged it on patch day and fixed
  it on 2025-12-24. Finish the level-16 quest before using the feature.

One terminology trap: 계승 (succession / inheritance) is also the word for
**gear succession**, an unrelated system that carries enhancement levels between
equipment pieces. In January 2026 players destroyed gear by confusing the two
items in the gear succession UI and NC ran a recovery programme for it. When a
Korean guide says 계승, check whether it means traces or equipment.

## Progression path

1. **Level 16.** Finish "Monolith of the Lord". Use the Monolith Express voucher
   if you have one. Resonate whatever you are carrying.
2. **Levels 16–44.** Resonate opportunistically. Spend Wisdom Stones the moment
   they arrive — mastery skill reset has been free since the 2025-11-21
   emergency update, so hoarding buys you nothing.
3. **Push to resonance 21 (255 tokens) before anything else.** This is the
   Unique amulet conversion gate, and Unique is where the amulet's tier-1
   options — Weapon Damage Amplification, Perfect Penetration, Power Shard
   Damage Bonus — become available. Everything below 21 is a warm-up.
4. **Then resonance 30 (560 tokens).** All 112 Wisdom Stones, all 30 scrolls,
   the Saint title, both skin sets. This is the real finish line for power.
5. **Ereshkigal, once you can survive Reshanta.** 300 tokens for 70 Azphel
   crystals, 20 Fierce Battle scrolls and the PvP stat block. Go grouped or
   off-peak; the other faction collects the same tokens.
6. **Convert the surplus.** Only after step 4, and remember it is once per
   server.
7. **Alts: inherit, do not re-walk.** One finished sweep on the main, then
   inheritance at the pillar for every character on that server.
8. **Chapter 1 pillars when they arrive.** 12 more Revelation scrolls, 15 Cube
   Keys, the Epic amulet conversion. No Wisdom Stones, so they are never a
   reason to delay step 4.

| Resonance 30 | 560 | 112 Wisdom Stones, 30 scrolls, 240 attribute points, Saint title |
| Ereshkigal 10 | 85 | Guardian of Ereshkigal; 21 Azphel crystals |
| Ereshkigal 20 | 300 | 70 Azphel, 20 Fierce Battle scrolls, PvP Defense +300 |

## Costs and math

- **Resonance itself is free.** No Kinah, no materials, only tokens.
- **Enhancing the amulet is not.** Each amulet level consumes one scroll plus
  Kinah through Integrated Enhancement. 30 field scrolls carry the amulet
  through three full +10 runs with headroom, which is what "collecting
  everything takes the amulet to Unique +10" means in practice.
- **Soul Codex: Reset is worth more than it looks.** Each one re-rolls all four
  Soul Engraving options on a piece of gear, and using it *also* costs
  **300,000 Kinah**. The 20 from the field ladder are therefore worth six
  million Kinah of re-roll attempts you would otherwise have to fund. Korean
  guides are emphatic that you should not spend them below roughly item level 67
  gear. See [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]].
- **Skill points in context.** 112 of a fully-finished character's 423 lifetime
  skill points. The Shugo Festa shop sells Wisdom Stones at 350 Centuryroot
  Tokens each with a purchase cap, which is the only other bulk source, and it
  is far slower.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Stopping at resonance 20 because the title looks like a milestone.** Level
  21 is the gate that matters. Twenty more tokens.
- **Assuming inheritance skips the resonating.** It shares traces, not levels.
  Your alt still has to hand them in, and still has to do it at each pillar.
- **Using the inheritance button mid-quest.** It fails with a misleading error
  if "Monolith of the Lord" is still open on that character.
- **Converting surplus traces before finishing the ladder.** The ladder wants
  tokens; conversion destroys them. And the conversion is once per server, so
  there is no second attempt.
- **Reading 계승 as gear succession.** Different system, same Korean word,
  irreversible consequences.
- **Spending Soul Codex resets on early gear.** They are the most expensive
  reward on the ladder once you account for the 300,000 Kinah activation cost.
- **Treating the Ereshkigal ladder as optional for a PvE player.** The Amplify
  Stone Fragments and 70 Azphel crystals feed [[PvP Boards and Transformations]],
  and PvP stats contribute nothing to displayed Combat Power — so the ladder
  looks worthless on the CP number and is not.
- **Expecting the Chapter 1 pillars at global launch.** They are roadmap.

## Global launch notes

- **Present:** Verteron or Altgard field pillar (30 levels) and the Ereshkigal
  pillar (20 levels). Unique +10 on the amulet is a genuine endgame goal;
  Heroic conversion is out.
- **Launch Scale Test:** resonance pays **one stat per level**. No +620
  Stamina. No skins. Wisdom Stones from this ladder were **not** the levelling
  faucet — see [[Skills and Mastery Points]]. Other item rewards (Reset Codex,
  Hidden Cube keys, Shugo fragments) still paid.
- **Roadmap:** Eltnen and Morheim pillars.
- **Unconfirmed QoL:** 4-traces-per-pickup, surplus-to-stone conversion,
  Inheritance of God's Traces.
