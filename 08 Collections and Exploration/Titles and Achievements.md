# Titles and Achievements

> **In one sentence:** every title you own grants a permanent passive stat whether
> or not you wear it, you can equip three at once — one Attack, one Defense, one
> Other — so collecting titles is itself a progression system, not decoration.

## Why it matters

Most MMO title systems are cosmetic. AION 2's is not, and the reason is the
**owned effect**.

Each title carries two separate bonuses:

- **Owned effect** (보유 효과), shown in the database as "Collection · always on".
  Applies the moment you acquire the title, forever, regardless of what you are
  wearing. These stack across every title you own.
- **Equipped effect** (장착 효과). Applies only while the title occupies a slot.
  Substantially larger than the owned effect.

Because owned effects stack, a low-effort Rare title you will never equip is still
a permanent stat gain. Korean guides draw the obvious conclusion: collect
everything you can, equip the best three. A Korean title guide puts it as "even
Common, Rare and Transcendence titles should be gathered as much as possible,
because merely owning them raises passive stats".

This makes the achievement list a farmable stat sheet. It is also why the
collection systems in this chapter compound: Monolith resonance, Kibelisk
completion, Hidden Cube opening and Wardrobe milestones each hand out titles whose
owned effects then apply forever.

## How it works

### Equip slots

Titles come in three functional categories and you may equip **one of each**, for
three simultaneous equipped effects:

| Slot | Korean | Typical stats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attack | 공격 | Additional Attack, Critical Hit, Back Critical, Accuracy |
| Defense | 방어 | Additional Defense, Back Defense, Critical Resist |
| Other | 기타 | HP, MP, potion recovery, natural regen, Stamina |

This three-slot model is confirmed by Inven's summary of NC's fifth pre-launch
livestream and independently by a Gameple article. One later Korean blog claims
only one title can be equipped at a time; that is the outlier and is most likely
an error. Confidence: medium-high on three slots.

The chosen title also displays above your character name.

### Grades

Titles are graded like items — Common, Rare, Epic, Unique, Special — and higher
grades add more lines to the equipped effect. Unique-grade titles are described by
Korean guides as "overwhelmingly better" on the equipped side, which is why the
Unique titles listed below are the ones worth planning around.

**Special**-grade titles are the server-first and event trophies. They have **no
stat effects at all** — this was stated as design intent on the pre-launch
livestream and holds in the current database, where every "First to…" title reads
"Cosmetic title — no stats". Examples: First to Become an Officer, First to Bring
Low the God of the New World (first server S-grade clear of Deus Research Base
Transcendence 10), and Daeva Atelier outfit-contest awards.

### Scale

NC's fifth livestream put the launch total at **345 titles**. The current
post-Chapter-1 database is far larger. One English article claims the achievement
system tracks "more than 4,300 milestones"; that figure comes from a Kinah-vendor
site with no corroboration, so treat it as an order-of-magnitude hint only.

### The achievement system

Achievements are grouped into categories that map onto the game's content pillars.
The names visible in the questlog database include:

| Category tag | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `a2.unlock` | "Inner Achievements" — the onboarding and systems-unlock line |
| `a2.seasonmission` | Season and chapter missions (Peering into the Abyss, Purify Reshanta, Scars of Glory and similar) |
| `adventure` | Exploration and map completion, including the Kibelisk lines |
| `craft` | Crafting, gathering and life-skill milestones, including the Fashionista skin ladder |
| Exploration / Dungeon / Resource / Quest / Challenge | The faction-split categories used to sort titles in the title database |

Most achievements are **stepped**: a single achievement entry contains 5–8
thresholds, each paying its own reward. Press the `i` icon on an achievement to see
its step ladder and per-step rewards. Korean players treat this as the primary way
to find achievable titles.

**Achievement tabs also have their own completion rewards.** An Inven post notes
that finishing an entire tab pays out, and gives a concrete example: the colossus
**Krome's Desire** (크로메데의 욕망) requires 32 achievements in its tab, but only
27 are currently achievable, so it is not obtainable yet. That is a useful warning
that some tab-completion rewards are gated on unreleased content. See
[[Pantheon Statues and Frames]].

### The best early Unique title: Through Hell and Back

| Title | Grade | Equipped | Owned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Through Hell and Back (나생문을 넘어선) | Unique | Cooldown Reduction 5%, HP 800, Stamina 200 | Block 10 |

A Gameple article frames this as "a Unique title in 30 minutes", and it is the best
early power-per-time title in the game. Cooldown Reduction 5% and HP 800 on a
single title is exceptional.

The route:

1. Clear the sealed dungeons **Distorted Cave**, **Crevice Cave** and **Rift Cave**
   in that order — they are prerequisites.
2. Enter the sealed dungeon **Ruins of the Ancient City of Roah**, on the island at
   the far west of the Verteron map. The **Aulau Funeral Island Kibelisk** is the
   convenient access point, which is a concrete example of why Kibelisk
   registration matters.
3. Complete all monster-kill missions inside a **15-minute** time limit.

See [[Dungeons and Raids]] and [[Kibelisks and Map Registration]].

## Codex, adventure book and region completion

This is an area where the brief's assumption does not survive contact with the
sources, and it is worth stating clearly.

- **AION 1 had a Storybook System** with three branches — Ancient Artwork, Nature
  Essence and Adventure Book — where you collected pages through story quests and
  registered completed paintings for stat bonuses. That system belongs to the
  original AION and appears on AION PowerBook, not to AION 2.
- **AION 2 has no confirmed codex or adventure-book system** under any name I could
  find in Korean, Traditional Chinese or English sources. The nearest analogues are
  the achievement tab structure described above and the Pantheon's **paintings**
  (명화), which are collectible framed items placed for stats and sourced from
  region quests, field bosses (which drop "Painting Remnants") and crafting. Those
  belong to [[Pantheon Statues and Frames]].
- **Beware a false friend:** the **Soul Codex** (영혼의 서) in AION 2 is a
  soul-imprint reroll consumable from Hidden Cubes and Monolith rewards, not a
  collection book. See [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]].
- **Region completion** exists only as per-system achievements — all Kibelisks in a
  zone, all sealed dungeons in a zone ("Seal Breaker" titles), all outposts, and the
  Monolith ladder. Community map sites talk about "100% region completion" as a
  goal, but no source identifies a distinct zone-completion reward track separate
  from those achievements.

## Unlock and gates

Achievements track from character creation. No unlock. The gates are content
access: dungeon titles need the dungeon, Monolith titles need resonance levels,
Chapter 1 titles need level 45 / item level 3,300 / combat power 270,000.

## Inputs: currencies, materials, sources

Titles cost nothing directly. They are the byproduct of other systems. The
"inputs" are the activities themselves: dungeon clears, life-skill levels, monster
kills, collection milestones, PvP participation and event attendance.

## Progression path

1. **Open the achievement menu early and read it.** Korean guides treat "check the
   achievement list periodically" as a core habit, because many titles are already
   half-complete from ordinary play.
2. **Level 10–20:** the "Inner Achievements" onboarding line and Verteron/Altgard
   Inquirer from Monolith level 10.
3. **As soon as sealed dungeons open:** run the Distorted → Crevice → Rift → Roah
   chain for Through Hell and Back. Best power-per-minute Unique title available.
4. **After the main story:** clean up all 61 Kibelisks for Teleportation Specialist,
   and first-clear every dungeon on both difficulties for the paired titles.
5. **Ongoing:** life-skill entry and expert level 50 milestones, gathering counts,
   monster-race kill counts. These accumulate passively while you do other things.
6. **Endgame:** Monolith level 30 for the Saint title, Ereshkigal 20 for the PvP
   Saint, 700 skins for Empty Closet, 25 wings for Dominator of Dimensions.

## Costs and math

- **345 titles** at launch per NC's fifth livestream; substantially more now.
- **3 equipped effects** simultaneously, one per slot category.
- **Unlimited stacking owned effects** — the real reason to collect broadly.
- Achievement steps commonly run 5–8 thresholds; the Fashionista ladder is
  10/50/100/200/300/500/700/1000.
- A tab-completion reward can be gated on unreleased content: Krome's Desire needs
  32 achievements in a tab where 27 are currently possible.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Treating titles as cosmetic.** The owned effects are the point.
- **Chasing Special-grade titles for power.** Server-firsts and contest wins have no
  stats by design.
- **Ignoring low-grade titles.** A Common title's owned effect is small but free
  and permanent.
- **Only equipping one title.** If you have not filled all three slots — Attack,
  Defense and Other — you are leaving two equipped effects on the table.
- **Chasing the 300 and 500 skin titles specifically.** An Inven player's assessment
  is that they are hard and their effects are weak; let them arrive naturally.
- **Planning around a tab-completion reward.** Check whether the tab is actually
  completable on the current patch first.
- **Assuming a codex system exists.** It does not, in AION 2, as far as any
  available source shows. Do not confuse it with AION 1's Storybook or with the
  Soul Codex consumable.
