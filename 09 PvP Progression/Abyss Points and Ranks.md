# Abyss Points and Ranks

> **In one sentence:** Abyss Points are the PvP wallet and Abyss Ranking Points are
> the PvP scoreboard, they are two separate pools, and almost every constraint in
> the system is a weekly cap rather than a grind wall.

> **Season 1 verdict:** the two-pool split, the weekly hunting / PvP caps and
> [[Merit Medals]] as the real gear gate are all in. **Abyss Potential is out**
> (2026-04-08). Ranking rewards may still carry stats — Korea removed them in
> Season 3, so if they are present they are a limited-time opportunity. Four
> stigma slots, cap 20: 140 shards to take all four to 15, 300 to finish at 20.
> Dungeon drops are the primary shard supply; huge AP totals are the worst case.
> Abyss weekly hours: two models, unresolved — see below.

## Why it matters

Abyss Points (AP, 어비스 포인트) buy the cheap end of the Abyss shop and convert
into [[Merit Medals]], which are what actually gate PvP gear tier — do not
treat AP as the gear currency. They also buy Stigma Shards and Daevanion
Crystal: Azphel. **Abyss gear potential is out** (Season 3, 2026-04-08); do
not budget AP for it. Because the weekly earning ceiling is fixed and generous,
the binding constraint on PvP progression is not how hard you farm but whether
you touch each capped and uncapped source at least once per week. Two players
who both hit their caps progress identically regardless of hours played.

The second reason it matters is the split. In the original AION, Abyss Points were
simultaneously your currency and your rank, so buying gear demoted you. AION 2
separates the two: **Abyss Ranking Points** drive rank and cannot be spent,
**Abyss Points** are spent freely. The tension the original game had is gone in
the current KR/TW build, which is worth knowing because a large amount of English
SEO content still describes the old behaviour.

## How it works

### The two point pools

| Pool | Korean | Earned from | Spent on | Lost on death |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abyss Points | 어비스 포인트 | Abyss hunting, PvP kills, arenas, battlefields, Supply Requests, mission quests, [[Shugo Festa]] | Decanus gear, [[Merit Medals]], Stigma Shards, Azphel crystals. Not Abyss Potential (out) | No, since 2026-02-11 |
| Abyss Ranking Points | 랭킹 포인트 | PvP kills; Abyss PvE only while still under the weekly AP cap | Nothing — sets Abyss Rank | Yes |

Before 2026-02-11, being killed by an enemy player drained Abyss Points. That
patch changed the penalty to Ranking Points instead, and the 2026-03-25 patch
sharply reduced how much Ranking Point loss a death causes. Any source that says
"you lose AP when you die" is describing the launch build.

### Weekly cap structure

The cap system was announced in the 2025-12-02 live broadcast, which converted a
daily 25,000 hunting allowance into a weekly 200,000 allowance and applied the
same figure to PvP.

| Cap | Amount | Counts | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly PvE hunting AP | 200,000 | Abyss monsters, Abyss bosses, Dimensional Cores | high |
| Weekly PvP AP | 200,000 | Enemy player kills and assists in the Abyss | high |
| Combined weekly ceiling from capped sources | 400,000 | — | high |
| Seasonal AP ceiling | 2,500,000 over an 8-week season | All capped sources | high |
| Battlefield, Arena, Supply Requests, [[Shugo Festa]], events | uncapped by the above | — | high |

The developers stated the 2,500,000 seasonal figure is designed to be reachable
in roughly 6 of the 8 season weeks, so missing two weeks is not a permanent loss.
The Chapter 1 patch note (2026-07-01) records that "the Abyss Point acquisition
cap is adjusted to the million unit", so expect these numbers to have moved
upward with the level 50 expansion; the exact new figures are not published in
the translated note.

A separate rule from 2026-02-11: once you have exceeded the AP limit, **PvE no
longer awards Ranking Points** except inside the Abyss Corridor, and Ranking
Points can then only be earned through PvP. This is the mechanism that stops
players from parking a high rank purely through monster grinding.

### The AP bonus buff

At launch, higher Abyss Ranks received larger AP gain bonuses. The 2026-02-11
patch flattened this: **every rank now receives the same +30 percent AP bonus.**
Earlier, the December 2025 broadcast had promised an AP-gain buff specifically for
the lowest ranks (Trainee, Grade 9 Soldier); the February change superseded it.

### Stigma Shards from AP — worst case, not the plan

The Abyss shop sells Stigma Shards at **25,000 AP** each. That is a real row,
and it is also the number that produces the terrifying "7.5 million AP to
finish stigmas" posts. Season 1 is **four slots, level cap 20**:

| Target | Shards per skill | All four skills | AP if bought entirely from the shop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 15 | 35 | **140** | 3,500,000 |
| Level 20 | 75 | **300** | 7,500,000 |

Dungeon drops of Unstable Stigma Shards are the **primary** supply. Convert
them; do not treat the AP column as the plan. Taiwan cut the 100 percent
Unstable → Shard conversion from 1,000,000 Kinah to **200,000** (and the 25
percent recipe from 250,000 to 50,000) on 2026-02-25. Korea later cut
conversion fees again in Season 3. At Season 1 expect the harsher launch
Kinah fee unless the global client inherited the TW cut — *verify at launch*.
See [[Stigmas]] and [[Merit Medals]] for the medal side of the same wallet.

### Abyss access time — two models

Korea and Taiwan run a weekly hour allowance. NCSOFT told Gamescom press that
**free global players will not be time-limited out of the Abyss**. Both models
are live possibilities; do not collapse them.

**Model A — KR/TW weekly allowance (documented).** The Abyss is time-limited
per layer, not persistent.

| Layer | Korean | Entry | Weekly allowance | Recharge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ereshranta Lower | 어비스 에레슈란타 하층 | Level 45, item level 1,000+ | 7 hours (reported) | Lower Abyss Rift Stone |
| Ereshranta Middle | 어비스 에레슈란타 중층 | Item level 3,000 | 7 hours, confirmed in the Season 2 note | Middle Abyss Rift Stone, 1 hour per stone |
| Ereshranta Surface | 어비스 에레슈란타 표층 | Item level 4,300 **or below**, or CP 400,000 or below | Chapter 1 layer — **out** | — |
| Chaotic Abyss | 혼돈의 어비스 | Added 2026-03-25 | later — **out** for planning | — |

The "7 hours per week" figure is documented for the Middle Layer in the Season 2
patch note and was stated for the Abyss generally in the pre-launch broadcast
round-up. The Lower Layer figure is **confidence medium** because I could not find
a patch note that states it explicitly post-Season 2. Membership doubled Abyss
hours on the KR/TW Chengarung product.

**If Model A ships**, the weekly plan is "spend the seven hours on siege night
and corridor windows, extend with Rift Stones only when a medal mission needs
it." Idle time inside the Abyss is wasted progression.

**Model B — global, no time lock (Gamescom press).** Guided.news (2026-09-06)
reports NC saying a free player is not locked out of the main PvP area by a
daily time limit and can enter without a membership and participate for as
long as they want. The statement is press, not a patch note, and it says
"daily" rather than restating the KR weekly figure.

**If Model B ships**, the weekly plan is gated by the AP caps and
[[Merit Medals]] purchase limits, not by a clock. Rift Stones and the
membership hour-double become irrelevant. You can still waste a week by
missing siege and corridor windows; you just cannot be *locked out* of them.

Treat every hour number in this chapter as KR/TW-verified and
global-unconfirmed until the 2026-09-17 public test or the launch client
shows an Abyss timer — or does not.

Middle Abyss Rift Stones are craftable through Substance Morph:

| Recipe | Materials | Success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Middle Abyss Rift Stone | 1 Lower Abyss Rift Stone, 5 Reshanta Aura, 100 Refining Stone, 10 Expert's Refining Stone, 5 Artisan's Refining Stone, 100,000 Kinah | 20% |
| Middle Abyss Rift Stone | 5 Lower Abyss Rift Stone, 25 Reshanta Aura, 500 Refining Stone, 50 Expert's Refining Stone, 25 Artisan's Refining Stone, 500,000 Kinah | 100% |

Reshanta Aura drops from Middle Layer monsters.

Unlimited entry has changed twice. From 2026-03-04, any player at **1-Star
Officer or higher** could always enter regardless of population limits. Season 3
(2026-04-08) removed that perk and replaced it with a much harder condition:
**1,500,000 Abyss Ranking Points and a position inside the overall top 200.**

## Unlock and gates

Abyss access opens with the max-level Abyss introduction quest, which also grants
the [[PvP Gear|Fierce Battle Amulet]]. Rank itself has no unlock — you start at
Trainee and accumulate.

## Progression path

### The rank ladder

Rank names in AION 2 follow the Korean military-grade scheme. The tiers are
Trainee (훈련병), Grade 9 down to Grade 1 Soldier (9급병 – 1급병), 1-Star through
5-Star Officer (1성장교 – 5성장교), then General (장군) and above.

| Rank band | Korean | What it unlocks | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trainee | 훈련병 | Abyss entry | high |
| Grade 9 – 1 Soldier | 9급병 – 1급병 | Silver Merit Medal purchase (Grade 9+); Decanus and Centurion gear | high |
| 1-Star – 5-Star Officer | 1성장교 – 5성장교 | Gold Merit Medal purchase; Tribunus gear; unlimited Abyss entry (2026-03-04 to Season 3) | high |
| General and above | 장군 이상 | Platinum Merit Medal purchase; Legate gear; Guardian General transformation (reported) | medium on the transformation |

Two important qualifications. First, **the top ranks are positional, not purely
threshold-based** — reaching the point requirement puts you in contention, and
only the top N players above that line hold the rank. Second, from Season 3
(2026-04-08) **rank and ranking are calculated per class**, not server-wide, which
made high ranks reachable for classes that previously never competed for them.
The 2026-02-11 patch also lowered the Ranking Point threshold for General and
above.

The precise Ranking Point requirement per rank is not published in any source I
could verify for AION 2. The often-quoted table with 214,100 points for 1-Star
Officer and 488,200 for 5-Star Officer comes from **the original AION**, not AION 2;
do not reuse it. The one AION 2 figure that is firm is the Season 3 unlimited-entry
condition of 1,500,000 ranking points.

### What ranks actually give you

- **Purchase permissions**, which is the main mechanical effect: which medals you
  may buy each week and which gear tiers the Abyss shop will sell you.
- **Weekly medal purchase volume**, which scales with rank and is the real gating
  mechanism on gear tier speed.
- **A higher season-mission AP threshold**, meaning higher ranks are asked to earn
  more AP for the same weekly mission: 200,000 for Grade 1–9 Soldier, 500,000 for
  1–5 Star Officer, 1,000,000 for General and above.
- **The Guardian General transformation** at high rank. See
  [[PvP Boards and Transformations]].
- **Rank icons and titles**, displayed beside your name in the Abyss. See
  [[Titles and Achievements]].
- **PvE Abyss Damage Boost** was added to Abyss rank rewards on 2026-02-11, which
  is one of the few places rank gives a PvE-relevant number.
- **A larger AP reward for killing higher ranks** and a smaller one for killing
  lower ranks, set in the 2025-11-22 TW patch.

Season 3 deliberately reduced the performance value of ranking rewards in favour
of honour rewards, and the Chapter 1 patch went further by removing the stat
effects from Season 2 ranking-reward titles and Pantheon accessories while keeping
the items themselves. **At Season 1 those stat rewards may still be live** —
Korea had them through Season 2 and deleted them on 2026-04-08. If global ships
the launch reward table, treat ranking placement as a limited-time power
opportunity, not as a pillar you will still have in six months. See
[[Trials and Rankings]].

## Priorities and breakpoints

1. Reach **Grade 9 Soldier** to open Silver Medal purchases.
2. Reach **1-Star Officer** for Gold Medals and Tribunus gear. This is the single
   biggest jump in weekly medal throughput.
3. Reach **General** for Platinum Medals and the Legate tier.
4. Only chase **1,500,000 ranking points and top 200** if unlimited Abyss entry is
   worth several hours of scheduled play per week to you.

### An efficient weekly AP routine

This is built to hit both caps and every uncapped source with the least scheduled
play. Adjust to your server's matching group times.

| Slot | Activity | Expected AP | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily, 10 min | 5 mission quest tasks | 2,500/day, 17,500/week | Uncapped, zero risk |
| Daily, 10 min | Daily urgent Supply Request | ~10,000/day | Uncapped; needs crafting level and Kinah |
| Weekly, one session | Weekly Supply Requests | ~30,000 | Uncapped; largest single block |
| Weekly | 12 general order scrolls | 6,000 | Uncapped, no PvP |
| Tue/Thu/Sat 21:00 (Wed/Sat in Season 3) | Artifact siege, then Abyss Corridor per captured artifact | ~10,000 per 5-minute corridor window | Safest hunting AP in the game |
| 2–3 sessions | Abyss hunting to fill the 200,000 hunting cap | up to 200,000 | Best done while your faction holds artifacts for the AP bonus |
| 2–3 sessions | Abyss group fighting to fill the 200,000 PvP cap | up to 200,000 | Also the only Ranking Point source once capped |
| 3 per week | Battlefield, win if possible | 3,000 + Gold Medal per win | Outside the cap; also a medal mission |
| Up to 30 wins / 10 participations | Arena queues | 500 per win | Outside the cap |
| Hourly if convenient | [[Shugo Festa]], Dimensional Invasion | 100–300 each | Outside the cap |

The two highest-value actions on that list for a player who dislikes PvP are the
**weekly Supply Requests** and the **Abyss Corridor**, because both are large,
uncapped or safe, and require no player combat.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Filling your mission quest slots before entering the Abyss.** Order scrolls
  are classed as mission quests, and with the slots full you cannot use them. The
  concurrent mission cap was raised from 5 to 10 in Season 2.
- **Grinding Abyss PvE for rank after hitting the AP cap.** Since 2026-02-11 that
  earns no Ranking Points outside the Corridor.
- **Buying Platinum Medals at 100,000 AP each without checking the gear cost
  first.** A Legate weapon is 240 Platinum, which is 24,000,000 AP if bought
  purely with points — far beyond a season's earning ceiling. Medals must come
  mostly from missions and bosses.
- **Ignoring the Corridor because you dislike the Abyss.** It is a fully protected
  instance the enemy faction cannot enter.
- **Re-entering a used artifact corridor.** One entry per artifact, five minutes,
  no second chance.
- **Reusing the original AION rank point table.** It is a different game.

## Global launch notes

The cap structure and rank ladder above are the KR/TW **launch-through-Season-2**
shape, which is the Season 1 planning baseline. Chapter 1's "million unit" AP
cap, the Surface Layer, Chaotic Abyss and per-class ranking are later.

- **Abyss time:** Model A (≈7 hours/week, Rift Stones, membership double) versus
  Model B (Gamescom: free players are not time-limited out). Unresolved. See
  the section above for what each does to a weekly plan.
- Faction separation by server means all Abyss population is cross-server matched,
  so artifact siege participation numbers and corridor competition may differ
  substantially from Korea.
- **Abyss Potential is out.** Ranking stat rewards may still be in.
- Medal purchase volumes: expect the harsher pre-2026-02-11 tuning. See
  [[Merit Medals]].
