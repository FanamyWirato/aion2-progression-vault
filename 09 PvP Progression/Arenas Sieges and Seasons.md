# Arenas Sieges and Seasons

> **In one sentence:** AION 2's competitive content is a scheduled calendar rather
> than an always-on world, and the whole PvP week is built around three artifact
> siege windows plus a handful of queue slots that pay outside the weekly Abyss
> Point cap.

> **Season 1 verdict:** 1v1 Solitude, 4v4 Cooperation and 8v8 Battlefield — not
> the Chapter 1 5v5 / 10v10 equalised versions. Ranking rewards may still
> carry stats; Korea stripped them in Season 3, so if they are present they
> are a limited-time opportunity. Battlefield Aspel fragments, per-class
> ranking and the Rift Zone event are later. [[Shugo Festa]] is the non-combat
> AP / shop hourly, not a PvP mode. Legions have **no** level, skill tree or
> member buff in any version.

## Why it matters

Almost every high-value PvP reward in AION 2 is attached to a clock. Artifact
sieges run at a fixed hour on fixed days, the Abyss Corridor only opens after a
capture and only for five minutes, battlefields and arenas moved from always-open
to a two-hour evening window in Season 3, and the Spacetime Rift opens on a fixed
interval. A player who logs in at the wrong times can put in more hours than
someone who logs in at the right times and progress slower.

The second reason this note matters is that the queue content — arena and
battlefield — pays Abyss Points **outside** the weekly cap. That makes it the only
way to exceed 400,000 AP in a week. [[Merit Medals]] from battlefields are in
at Season 1; Azphel board fragments on a win are Chapter 1 and are out.

## How it works

The two arena queues run **separate ladders and separate season rewards**, so a
rank in one does not carry to the other.

Pre-launch coverage listed arena modes as "1v1, 2v2, 8v8" and one guide mentions a
third arena named "Strategy" (전략) alongside Solitude and Cooperation. Neither the
2v2 nor a Strategy arena appears in any live patch note; the 8v8 mode is the
Battlefield, referred to in NCSOFT's own community post as "the 8:8 strategic
arena". Treat 2v2 and Strategy arena as **not present** on the live build.

### Matchmaking and equalisation

- **Arena of Solitude has no gear equalisation.** Your build is your rating.
- **Battlefield equalisation is Chapter 1** and is **out** at Season 1: later
  Korea normalises stats, forces Mastery to 20 and Stigmas to 25 (a sixth-slot
  cap that Season 1 does not have — launch is **four slots, cap 20**). At
  launch the battlefield is a raw 8v8. It is still the best early uncapped AP
  source, just not a fair fight.
- **Season 3 restricted Arena and Battlefield matching to solo or 5-player groups**
  and integrated matching across races.
- **From Season 3, season rank and rank tier are calculated per class**, not
  server-wide, for Arena of Solitude, Arena of Cooperation, Abyss, Transcendence,
  Nightmare and Awakening Battle.

### Battlefield mechanics

The battlefield is a payload-and-relic mode rather than a deathmatch.

- **Objective:** push the "torch cargo" toward the target point by standing near it.
- **Relics** decide most matches: red is an instant kill on hit regardless of the
  target's spec, yellow gives 8–9 seconds of area invulnerability to your team,
  and purple turns every enemy into a mushroom for mass CC.
- First relic spawns at a fixed **9 minutes 30 seconds**, then roughly every 2
  minutes from the moment the previous one is taken.
- A match can be as short as 2–3 minutes, there is no loss penalty, and a win pays
  guaranteed Abyss Points. NCSOFT's own community post recommends it as the
  low-pressure PvP reward content.
- Practical advice from Korean guides: take the relic before fighting, never enter
  alone, prioritise healers, and treat the cargo fight as positional rather than
  a kill race.

Battlefield maps rotate by season: Odyle Shard Cradle (Season 1), Stormslumber
Snowfield (Season 2), Sandstorm Temple (Chapter 1).

### Artifact sieges and faction objectives

The artifact siege is the centre of the Abyss economy.

- **Three artifacts in the Lower Layer and three in the Middle Layer**, six total
  since Season 2. Before Season 2 there were only the three Lower Layer artifacts.
- Capture requires landing the finishing blow on the artifact, then defeating the
  **Dimensional Core** that spawns roughly an hour later.
- Holding an artifact grants a faction-wide **Abyss Point gain bonus of 2.5 percent
  per artifact**, reduced from 5 percent when the artifact count doubled.
- Capturing opens the **Abyss Corridor** (어비스 회랑), a protected instance the
  enemy faction cannot enter, with dense low-HP monsters at 50–80 AP each. One
  entry per artifact, maximum five minutes, roughly 10,000 AP per window with full
  buffs. One guide states the capturing faction can access the corridor for 48
  hours after a capture.
- If a faction loses an artifact set 3:0, one corridor portal spawns at the losing
  faction's base with a 30 percent AP reduction, so a total defeat is not a total
  shutout.

**Losing-faction catch-up buffs**, applied separately per layer and lasting until
the next siege. Season 2 raised them:

| Artifacts lost | Original | Season 2 adjusted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:1 | +5% PvP Damage Tolerance | +10% PvP Damage Tolerance |
| 0:2 or 1:2 | +10% PvP Damage Tolerance | +20% PvP Damage Tolerance |
| 0:3 | +15% Tolerance, +10% PvP Damage Boost | +35% Tolerance, +20% PvP Damage Boost |

Losing a siege week therefore makes the following week meaningfully easier, which
is worth planning around rather than despairing over.

**Rewards** from bosses and artifact occupation are Merit Medals, Merit proofs
that convert to Merit Chests, Abyss Points and gear drops, distributed by
contribution ranking. Chapter 1 changed that distribution from overall
contribution ranking to **per-class ranking**, which spreads rewards to more
players, and raised medal amounts.

**Abyss bosses** on the siege calendar: Guardian Lord Nahma (수호신장 나흐마), the
Enraged Guardian Lord Nahma in the Middle Layer, Watcher Kaira (감시자 카이라) and
Spirit King Argo (정령왕 아그로). Nahma spawns 3 times in the Lower Layer and the
Enraged version twice at fixed Middle Layer locations. Boss drop attribution was
changed on 2026-02-11 to final-blow basis, matching the medal rule.

### The Chaotic Abyss

Added 2026-03-25 to fix faction population imbalance. Instead of Elyos versus
Asmodian, players are split into **West (blue) and East (purple)** factions
matched at the server level, so Elyos and Asmodian players can be on the same
team. The Spacetime Rift retains normal race-versus-race matching.

### Spacetime Rift and its Contest

Two distinct things share the name. See [[Flight and Traversal]] and
[[Daily and Weekly Loops]].

**Spacetime Rift (portal):** portals open at random locations inside the enemy
faction's territory. The schedule has changed twice:

| Build | Interval | Times | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Launch (2025-11) | 4 hours | 10, 14, 18, 22, 02, 06 | 200 |
| Dec 2025 onward | 3 hours | 02, 05, 08, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23 | 400 |

A **War Mode** PvP on/off toggle was added in December 2025 with a 70-minute
change cooldown; you cannot change mode while in enemy territory. Base usage time
is one hour and a portal persists for roughly an hour after opening. Rift quests
pay Abyss Points, and you can cross over and complete only the quests without
fighting anyone — the main reason a PvE player ever touches this content.

**Spacetime Rift Contest (쟁탈전):** a scheduled garrison-capture event in Verteron
and Altgard.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule | Monday, Thursday, Saturday at 20:00 and 23:00 rift time |
| Sides | Attack and defence swap weekly — week 1 Verteron with Elyos defending, week 2 Altgard with Asmodian defending |
| Capacity | 25–500 per faction (originally 60–200) |
| Structure | 5-minute prep after the portal spawns, then 25 minutes of contest; the portal accepts entries for 10 minutes |
| Objective | Destroy 3 Monoliths in a garrison to capture it; capturing 3 of 5 garrisons wins for the attackers, holding more than half wins for the defenders |
| Entry | First come, first served, using an Event Ticket; 3 tickets recharge every Monday at 17:00 |
| Restriction | Expedition, Transcendence and Sanctuary content are blocked while the contest runs |
| Weekly completion | 6 sorties available, 3 rewarded |

S-PvP mode activates on participation, so only participants can attack each other.

### Abyss Rift Zone event

Added 2026-06-17. Bosses spawn over time and the two factions race for points.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule | Tuesdays and Thursdays from 22:00, offset by matching group |
| Requirements | Level 45+, item level 3,000+ |
| Capacity | Up to 400 per faction |
| Rewards | Boss rewards go to the race landing the final blow, distributed by per-class contribution ranking |
| Ranking impact | **Does not** affect Abyss season missions or rankings |

### Server matching groups

Because factions are matched across servers, siege and event times are offset by
matching group. The 2026-06-17 note gives Group A at 22:00, B at 22:05 and C at
22:10, with the pairing table published on the official note. Season 3 rebuilt the
matching groups for both the Abyss and the Spacetime Rift after the 2026-04-08
maintenance, using 21:00, 21:05 and 21:10 offsets. Server rematching resets
artifact occupation and the Abyss Corridor.

**Check your own server's group in-game.** Every time listed in this note is a
base time that your group may be offset from by 5 or 10 minutes.

### Season structure and ladder

Main seasons run roughly 8 to 12 weeks with a separate reward-claim window
afterwards. Arena seasons ("Arena of Tactics") run on their own cadence.

| Season 1 (main) | 2025-08-01 – 2025-08-25 | 2025-08-25 – 2025-09-01 |
| Season 2 (main) | 2026-01-21 – 2026-04-08 | 2026-04-08 – 2026-07-01 |
| Season 3 (main) | 2026-04-08 – 2026-07-01 | — |
| Arena of Tactics Season 5 | 2026-04-08 – 2026-07-01 | — |
| Season 4 / Chapter 1 | 2026-07-01 – 2026-11-04 | — |

Note the model change: from Chapter 1 (2026-07-01) NCSOFT moved from "Seasons" to
larger "Chapters". The dates above come from the aion2hub season calendar, which
tracks the KR/TW schedule; the Season 1 window looks short relative to later
seasons, so treat the early rows as **confidence medium**.

**The arena ladder**, lowest to highest: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum,
Diamond, Master, Grand Master, Challenger 3, Challenger 2, Challenger 1. The three
Challenger tiers are placement-based and depend on the server. Ranking Points are
gained on a win and lost on a loss, so a tier is never frozen.

**Season ranking rules have changed twice**, and both changes matter:

- **Season 2 (2026-01-21):** ranking calculation moved from cumulative to
  **best-record**, and rewards moved to **class-specific** leaderboards. Volume
  stopped winning; a single clean run does.
- **Season 3 (2026-04-08):** ranking **buffs were removed** from Arena of Solitude,
  Arena of Cooperation, Transcendence, Nightmare and Awakening Battle, and season
  rewards shifted from stat growth to honour items. Chapter 1 went further and
  removed the stat effects from Season 2 ranking-reward titles and Pantheon
  accessories, keeping the items cosmetically.

The direction of travel in Korea is unambiguous: **PvP ranking became a prestige
system rather than a power system.** At Season 1 that deletion has not happened
yet. If global ships the launch reward table, ladder placement still pays
stats — a limited-time opportunity, not a pillar. Do not follow a mid-2026
Korean guide that says ranking is cosmetic only.

Also relevant: NCSOFT publicly apologised in the 2026-04-07 broadcast for Season 2
ranking issues involving account sharing and an Awakening Battle bug, and chose to
raise rewards to the best bracket rather than strip them.

## Progression path

1. **Battlefield first** for uncapped AP and a [[Merit Medals|Merit Medal]].
   Stat equalisation and Aspel fragments are Chapter 1; at Season 1 it is a
   raw 8v8, so go in with Decanus if you have it.
2. **Arena queues** for uncapped AP and a class-specific ladder position.
3. **Attend one artifact siege per week** for the Silver Medal mission and, more
   importantly, corridor access afterwards.
4. **Add corridor farming after every capture** — the highest safe AP rate in the
   game.
5. **Add the Abyss Rift Zone** on Tuesday or Thursday once you clear item level
   3,000.
6. **Then look at ladder rank.** Season 3 removed the power rewards in Korea;
   at Season 1 they may still be live. If they are, this is the window. If
   they are not, stop at the participation counts.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Item level 3,000** opens the Middle Layer and the Abyss Rift Zone, which is the
  single biggest unlock in this note.
- **Three siege windows per week** is enough; the medal mission only asks for one.
- **Three battlefields per week** covers both the participation and victory medal
  missions.
- **Do not chase Challenger as a permanent plan.** In current Korea the rewards
  are cosmetic titles, wings and Oath Coins. At Season 1, check whether stat
  rewards are still attached before writing the ladder off.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Assuming a base time is your time.** Matching groups are offset by 5 and 10
  minutes, and server rematching resets artifact occupation.
- **Scheduling dungeons on Monday, Thursday or Saturday evening**, when the Rift
  Contest blocks them.
- **Missing the corridor.** The window is five minutes, one entry per artifact,
  and a used artifact cannot be re-entered.
- **Filling mission quest slots before a siege.** Order scrolls are mission quests.
- **Dying immediately after an Abyss boss.** A launch-era bug denied rewards if you
  died after the kill; it was scheduled for a fix on 2026-03-25, but fighting from
  a safe position is still the advice.
- **Assuming the ladder confers nothing.** That is the Season 3 state. At
  Season 1 it may still confer stats; check the reward window before skipping
  it.
- **Expecting a legion skill tree or member buff.** Legions have hideouts and
  storage. They have no level, no skill tree and no member buff in any version.
  See [[Legions]].
- **Bringing a raw-healing healer to a battlefield.** Healing received is reduced
  30 percent in Arena, Battlefield and Abyss zones.

## Global launch notes

Global Season 1 is the **4v4 / 8v8** roster, not the Chapter 1 5v5 / 10v10
equalised versions. English marketing that already prints "10v10 with
normalised stats" is describing later Korea or a hoped-for client feature;
do not build a launch week around equalised battlefields. Ranking stat
rewards are the open question that actually matters: if they ship, they are
a limited-time opportunity.

Because Elyos and Asmodian sit on separate servers globally, all faction content
runs through cross-server matchmaking. During Early Access, Abyss matchmaking is
restricted to Early Access servers; after launch, NCSOFT matches servers by
progression and strength. This means the artifact siege population you meet is a
matched pool rather than your own server's opposing faction, and your siege time
will be set by your matching group.

No global schedule has been published. Every time in this note is the KR/TW
schedule and should be re-verified in-game at launch. See
[[Global Launch Differences]], [[Seasons and Battle Pass]] and
[[Season 1 System Availability]].
