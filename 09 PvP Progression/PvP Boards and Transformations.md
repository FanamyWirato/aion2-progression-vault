# PvP Boards and Transformations

> **In one sentence:** Azphel is the PvP Daevanion board and the only permanent
> source of PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance outside gear, the Guardian General
> transformation is a rank-gated Abyss-only burst window, and Pantheon and Arcana
> contribute through separate PvP presets rather than separate items.

> **Season 1 verdict:** four general boards plus **Azphel**. **Ariel was
> absent** in the Launch Scale Test. Marchutan and Yustiel are out. Learn All
> Nodes is Chapter 1 and is roadmap. Taiwanese 守護力 is Daevanion under a
> different localisation, not a second system. Stat Arcana slots are Chapter 1
> and are out. Generic crystals from [[Shugo Festa]] feed the four general
> boards only.

## Why it matters

Gear can be lost to a tier rotation. Daevanion nodes cannot. The Azphel board is
104 stat nodes, 24 passive nodes, 16 active nodes and 8 specialty nodes of pure
PvP Damage Boost, PvP Damage Tolerance and status-ailment accuracy, and it never
gets replaced by a new patch tier. One English guide's framing is accurate: a
player with a 15 percent PvP Damage Boost from Azphel nodes beats a player with
higher raw attack and no PvP stats.

The transformation matters differently. It is not a progression step you buy — it
is a reward for holding a high Abyss Rank, and it is the closest AION 2 comes to
the "hero rank" power fantasy of the original AION. NCSOFT has explicitly said
transformations are weaker than in AION 1, and community discussion supports that:
players complain it is used as a survival button rather than a kill button.

## How it works

### The Daevanion board roster

**Season 1 has four general boards plus Azphel in the Launch Scale Test**, not
eight and not the KR six. Four are general and share one crystal currency;
Azphel opens at 45 with a dedicated crystal. **Ariel did not appear.** Marchutan
(2026-02-25) and Yustiel (2026-04-08) are later-KR seasonal boards and are
**out** for launch planning.

Taiwanese sources call the whole system **守護力** ("guardian power"). That is
the TW localisation of Daevanion, not a separate panel family. A guide that
lists 守護力 panels alongside Daevanion boards is double-counting.

Nodes open outward one square at a time from the board centre in the four cardinal
directions, so routing matters as much as node choice. **Learn All Nodes**, the
minimum-point route display, the general-board node expansion and the free reset
for those four are Chapter 1 (2026-07-01) and are **roadmap**, not launch QoL.
See [[Daevanion Boards]] for board mechanics and [[Skills and Mastery Points]]
for how Daevanion nodes raise skill levels.

**Correct a common misreading here.** Yustiel is *not* the PvP board, despite
appearing alongside Azphel in some source lists, and it is **out** at Season 1
anyway. It was added with Season 3 on 2026-04-08, it is built around attack and
defence, and its material — "Trace of Yustiel" — drops from the new PvE
expedition *Hall of Illusion* (환영의 회랑, also translated Corridor of
Illusions). Ariel is the PvE board, Azphel is the PvP board, Marchutan covers
weapon damage from expeditions (also out), and Yustiel is a general
attack/defence board on a PvE supply line. The name Yustiel is also reused as a
Pantheon "Life" stat, which is a separate thing entirely.

Aliases to watch: **Azphel** appears as Asphel, Asfel and Aspel; the system name
Daevanion is garbled to "Deinian" and "Devenian" in some machine-translated
guides. See [[Glossary KR TW EN]].

### Board points and crystals

The four general boards run on ordinary Daevanion Points, topped up with generic
Daevanion Crystals. Ariel and Azphel each require their **own** dedicated crystal,
which means there is no opportunity cost between the PvE and PvP boards — filling
Azphel never slows Ariel.

| Crystal | Korean | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daevanion Crystal (generic) | 데바니온 결정 | Level-ups, quests, achievements, Sealed Dungeons, [[Shugo Festa]] shop (7 per character; Inven launch post says 50 — conflict recorded there) |
| Daevanion Crystal: Ariel | 데바니온 결정: 아리엘 | Mission quests, Nightmare exchange shop, fragments from Awakening Battle and Subjugation, broker; 100 Ariel Trace Fragments + 50 Odyle converts at 100 percent |
| **Daevanion Crystal: Azphel** | 데바니온 결정: 아스펠 | **Mission quests, Abyss exchange shop, Arena exchange shop, Monolith rewards, Abyss monster drops, broker** |
| Fragment: Trace of Aspel | 아스펠의 흔적 조각 | Added to **Battlefield victory rewards** in Chapter 1 (2026-07-01) |

Two Azphel supply notes with real planning value. First, the Abyss and Arena
exchange shops sell Azphel crystals for Abyss Points, so **you can convert AP into
permanent PvP power** rather than only into gear. Second, from Chapter 1 the
Battlefield gives Aspel Trace Fragments on a win — that is **roadmap**. At
Season 1 the battlefield still pays AP and medals (see [[Merit Medals]]) but
not board fragments. Season 3 also allows Aspel and Yustiel fragments to be
material-converted into Soul Books; Yustiel is out, so ignore that half.

Monolith rewards are worth calling out. In December 2025 the Empyrean Lord stats
were moved from the Abyss Monolith out to each zone's Monolith, and **PvP-related
stats were added to the Abyss Monolith specifically**. So the Abyss Monolith is a
PvP progression node, not just a collection one. See
[[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]].

### Azphel node priorities

There is no single correct board layout, but the Korean consensus is to run three
saved configurations and switch:

| Situation | Emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| 1v1 Arena of Solitude | PvP Damage Boost, maximum offence |
| Abyss group fighting and Battlefield | Balanced Boost plus Tolerance |
| Healer or tank in any PvP | PvP Damage Tolerance, status-ailment resistance |

A commonly cited target is a **45-point Azphel allocation** as the first
meaningful milestone. Status-ailment accuracy nodes matter only for classes whose
kit depends on landing CC.

### Guardian General transformation

The Guardian General (수호신장) transformation is AION 2's PvP transformation.
Confirmed properties, from the pre-launch broadcast round-up and live-service
patch notes:

| Property | Value | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | 20 minutes | high |
| Cooldown | 120 minutes | high |
| Trigger | Rank-gated; granted by Abyss Rank standing | high |
| Where usable | Abyss only | medium |
| Effects | Unique transformation skills; status-ailment immunity or heavy resistance, increased HP and MP, increased attack and defence, movement and flight speed, increased strike damage | medium |
| Casting | Instant on the current build; community is asking for a cast time as in AION 1 | high |
| Patch note | 2026-03-04: combat speed and movement speed reduction debuffs no longer apply while transformed | high |

What rank unlocks it is the weakest link in this note. A July 2026 Inven thread
from a player who had just reached **General** asks when the transformation is
granted, which implies General is at or near the threshold rather than clearly
past it. The often-quoted figures of "5-Star Officer, 488,200 Abyss Points, top
100 on the server" and the "Seed of Roar" consumable come from **the original
AION**, not AION 2 — do not carry them across. Treat the exact AION 2 unlock as
**confidence low** until an in-game screenshot settles it.

A second transformation exists and is unrelated to PvP: bringing a pet to maximum
level lets your character transform into that pet, with **no combat stats**. See
[[Pets and Mounts]] and [[Pet Collection and Levels]].

There is also a "Guardian General 2" (수호신장2) discussed in the community, which
suggests a second tier or a second form, but I found no patch note describing it.

### PvP Pantheon choices

The Pantheon (만신전) grants stats through a centre Colossus, Statues and Paintings.

| Space (Israphel) | 공간 [이스라펠] | — | — |
| Death (Triniel) | 죽음 [트리니엘] | — | — |
| Wisdom (Lumiel) | 지혜 [루미엘] | — | — |
| Fate (Marchutan) | 운명 [마르쿠탄] | — | — |

Season 3 introduced **three saveable Pantheon presets**, and the standard advice is
PvE, PvP and farming. The PvP preset ordering from Korean guides is:

1. CC resistance and immunity
2. Defence and damage taken reduction
3. Attack power, kept in balance
4. Movement speed, for chasing and escaping in the Abyss

And one thing to avoid: **pure healing output**. Healing received is reduced inside
PvP zones — Chapter 1 set the reduction to 30 percent, down from 40 percent, in
Arena, Battlefield and Abyss areas — so healers should build survival and CC
resistance rather than raw throughput. See [[Pantheon]], [[Pantheon Stats]] and
[[Pantheon Statues and Frames]].

### PvP Arcana choices

Arcana are the same cards in PvE and PvP; what changes is which passives you push
and which slots you forge. A representative Korean 1v1 build for Gladiator pushes
Survivor Stance to +12 and Impact Hit to +10 across the Chalice, Scales, Bell and
Mirror slots, targeting Survivor Stance skill level 36 and Impact Hit 34 with gear
included, and abandons the PvE staple of maxing Cleaving Blow.

The general principle: PvP Arcana builds buy **survivability passives and CC
landing** rather than raw damage multipliers, and the slot order is Scales then
Chalice because those two carry the +4 passive rolls that make or break the build.
Chapter 1 added Key, Hourglass, Dice and Lantern slots and lets you lock one option
with 500,000 Kinah plus a Pure Crystal. Those **Stat Arcana slots are out** at
Season 1; launch Arcana is skill cards only. See [[Arcana]], [[Arcana Cards and Types]],
[[Arcana Sets and Meta]] and [[Arcana Refinement and Farming]].

## Progression path

1. **Open Azphel at 45** and spend the first crystals on PvP Damage Tolerance.
2. **Route toward the specialty nodes deliberately**, using the minimum-point route
   display rather than opening adjacent squares ad hoc.
3. **Reach roughly 45 Azphel points**, the commonly cited first milestone.
4. **Add the Battlefield to your weekly routine** for uncapped AP and medals.
   Aspel Trace Fragments on a win are a Chapter 1 addition and are roadmap.
5. **Build a PvP Pantheon preset** weighted to CC resistance and defence.
6. **Refork your Arcana** into a PvP layout once your Azphel and gear are settled,
   since Arcana refinement is the most expensive layer to redo.
7. **Push Abyss Rank toward General** for the transformation.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Tolerance before Boost on Azphel.** Same logic as gear: you cannot trade if
  the opening CC chain kills you.
- **45 Azphel points** as the first target, then extend toward specialty nodes.
- **Pantheon Freedom** is the PvP-specific stat family; everything else on the
  board is shared with PvE.
- **Do not build healing output for PvP.** It is reduced by 30 percent in all PvP
  zones as of Chapter 1.
- **The transformation is a survival tool in practice.** Plan a 120-minute cooldown
  around the artifact siege window rather than spending it on a skirmish.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Assuming Yustiel is a PvP board.** It is an attack/defence board fed by a PvE
  expedition.
- **Assuming Ariel and Azphel compete for points.** They use separate crystals.
- **Opening nodes without planning the route.** Nodes expand one square at a time,
  so a badly routed board wastes points permanently until a reset is offered.
  Chapter 1 provided resets for the four general boards only.
- **Carrying original-AION transformation data across.** The 488,200 points, top
  100 and Seed of Roar figures belong to AION 1.
- **Building a healer around healing output for PvP.** Reduced by design.
- **Spending Arcana refinement resources on a PvP layout before your gear tier is
  settled**, because the layout you want depends on which passives your gear
  already supplies.

## Global launch notes

Plan on the **four general boards plus Azphel**. **Ariel was absent** in the
Launch Scale Test — no PvE specialised board and no tradable Ariel points.
Azphel was present; fragment-to-tile morphing was not. No Marchutan, no
Yustiel, no Stat Arcana, no Learn All Nodes. See [[Daevanion Boards]] and
[[Global Launch Differences]].
