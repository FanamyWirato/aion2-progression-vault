# PvP Gear

> **In one sentence:** Abyss gear is a parallel equipment ladder bought only with
> Abyss Points and Merit Medals, it is deliberately worse than crafted gear
> outside the Abyss, and the only pieces a PvE player wants are the amulet and the
> cheap Decanus accessories.

> **Season 1 verdict:** Unique Decanus through Legate is the live ladder.
> **[[Merit Medals]], not AP, gate Centurion and above.** No Heroic gear, no
> Praetorian / Captain tiers, no Abyss Potential (2026-04-08), no Succession
> Crafting, no Chapter 1 inheritance-limit removal. Fierce Battle Amulet is in;
> its Heroic upgrade is out. Four stigma slots and a level-20 cap sit next to
> this shop — see [[Abyss Points and Ranks]] for the shard math.

## Why it matters

PvP gear is the only place PvP Damage Boost and PvP Damage Tolerance appear in
bulk. Crafted and dungeon gear carries PvE amplification and no PvP resistance at
all, which is why a Korean guide describes entering the Abyss in PvE gear as
"wearing a cardboard box instead of armour". Six pieces of the entry-tier Decanus
armour are reported to supply roughly 30 percent damage reduction against players,
which is the difference between being one-shot and being able to fight.

It also matters as an item level shortcut. Decanus pieces are item level 67
Unique-grade items, which at the point you first reach max level is higher than
most of what you are wearing. A Decanus ring costs about 31,600 AP and replaces a
level 55 Rare or level 59 Conquest Rare ring. That is the cheapest Unique-grade
accessory in the game, and it extracts back into Abyss Points if you outgrow it.

## How it works

### The tier ladder

Elyos gear is prefixed "Guardian" and Asmodian gear "Archon"; the tier name is
shared. English translations disagree badly, so aliases are listed.

| Tier | Korean | English names seen | Bought with | Rank gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 십부장 | Decanus, Decurion | Abyss Points only | none |
| 2 | 백부장 | Centurion, Captain | AP + Silver Merit Medals | none (Grade 9 Soldier for medal purchase) |
| 3 | 천부장 | Tribunus, Chiliarch, Tribune | AP + Gold Merit Medals | 1-Star Officer or higher |
| 4 | 군단장 | High Commander, Legate, Legionary | AP + Platinum Merit Medals | General or higher |
| 5 | 정예 군단장 | Elite Legate, Elite High Commander | Inheritance craft from tier 4 + Platinum | General (Season 3) — **out** |
| 6 | 친위대장 | Praetorian, Captain | Inheritance craft + Platinum | Chapter 1 — **out** |
| 7 | 정예 친위대장 | Elite Praetorian | Inheritance craft + Platinum | Chapter 1 — **out** |

Originally every tier was purchasable with Abyss Points alone. A later patch added
Merit Medals as an additional requirement for Centurion and above, which is the
change that turned gear tier progression from an AP problem into a medal problem.
The 2026-03-25 patch removed the rank-based purchase restriction on upper Abyss
gear and unified weekly medal purchase counts, so the rank gates in the table
reflect the Season 2 rules and may be looser on the current build —
**confidence medium** on whether Tribunus and Legate are still rank-locked.

Chapter 1 (2026-07-01) added "Guardian / Archon Captain" armour to the Abyss Shop
plus an inheritance-craft design from Legate armour, and Heroic-grade inheritance
designs from Centurion to Legate. **All of that is out** at Season 1. The launch
ceiling is Unique Legate, and there is no Heroic gear line.

### The Fierce Battle Amulet line

There are exactly two amulets in the game and they are permanent, non-droppable
"lifetime" items upgraded rather than replaced.

| Amulet | Korean | Focus | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revelation Amulet | 계시의 아뮬렛 | PvE — Weapon Damage Boost, Penetration, Power Shard bonus | Main story quest, early |
| Fierce Battle Amulet | 격전의 아뮬렛 | PvP — PvP Damage Boost | Max-level Abyss visit quest |

Also localised as "Amulet of Battle". Database values for the Fierce Battle
Amulet at Legend grade, item level 65, +0:

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Perfect Chance | 5% |
| Penetration | 700 (+300 at +10) |
| PvP Damage Boost | 20% |
| Power Shard Damage Bonus | 40 (+30 at +10) |

Both amulets share the same upgrade path: enhance to +10 with Kinah, Enhancement
Stones and an Amulet Enhance Scroll, then Substance Morph to the next grade and
start again. Scrolls come from Monolith Resonance level-ups, which you raise by
handing in Empyrean Traces — see [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]] and
[[Kibelisks and Map Registration]]. Reaching Monolith Resonance level 21 unlocks
promotion to Unique grade. Chapter 1 added Heroic-grade versions of both amulets,
converted from a Unique +10 amulet, and the **Amulet of Battle Enhancement Scroll
is sold in the Abyss Shop**. Heroic amulets are **out** at Season 1; stop at
Unique +10.

Since both amulets occupy the same slot and neither can be bought or traded, there
is no cost to owning both and switching. Every PvP guide reviewed recommends
exactly that.

### PvP stats versus PvE stats

| PvP stat | Korean | Effect | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PvP Damage Boost | PvP 피해 증폭 | Increases damage dealt to players | Azphel nodes, Soul Binding, Abyss gear, gear potential, Fierce Battle Amulet |
| PvP Damage Tolerance | PvP 피해 내성 | Reduces damage taken from players | Azphel nodes, Abyss gear, gear potential |
| PvP Accuracy | PvP 명중 | Negates player evasion | Abyss gear, Soul Binding, Pantheon Freedom stat |
| Evasion | 회피 | Player-viable in PvP in a way it is not versus bosses | Abyss gear, Soul Binding, Pantheon Vaizel stat |
| Status Ailment Accuracy | 상태이상 적중 | CC landing rate | Azphel nodes, some gear |
| Abyss PvE Damage Boost | 어비스 PvE 피해 증폭 | PvE damage, **only inside the Abyss** | Abyss gear set effects, Abyss rank rewards |

The critical asymmetry: **PvE amplification does nothing to players and PvP
amplification does nothing to monsters**, and Season 3 separated the two
coefficient families entirely. Chapter 1 also removed the maximum caps on all
Damage Amplification and Resistance items while capping total damage reduction
from resistance at 80 percent, and added a Frontal stat family (Frontal Damage
Amplification, Resistance, Attack, Defence, Critical, Critical Resistance) that
applies in both PvE and PvP. Recommended Abyss PvP accuracy is 2,000 or higher.
See [[Stat Reference]].

### Abyss gear potential (Season 3) — **out**

A PvP mirror of the PvE potential system, added 2026-04-08. **Do not budget for
it at Season 1.** The values below are recorded so later-KR guides are readable,
not so you can plan around them. Levels are bought with Abyss Points plus
"Potential Amplification Stone: Abyss" (잠재력 증폭석: 어비스), which drops from
Abyss monsters.

| Slot group | Per level | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon / gauntlet | +2.5% PvP Damage Boost | +10% |
| Armour | +0.5% PvP Damage Tolerance | +2% |
| Accessories | +0.5% PvP Damage Boost | +2% |

The weapon and gauntlet lines are by far the best value in later Korea, which
is the one argument for buying a PvP weapon earlier than the defence-first
ordering suggests. At Season 1 this whole layer is absent, so the
accessories-then-armour-then-weapon order stands without that exception.

### Set effects and the PvE penalty

From Season 2 (2026-01-21), Abyss gear set effects were rewritten so the sets are
strong inside the Abyss and actively bad outside it. The published Decanus values
show the pattern:

| Set effect | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abyss PvE Damage Boost | +1,000 |
| PvE Damage Boost | −500 |
| PvP Damage Boost (higher piece counts) | +500 |
| PvP Damage Tolerance (higher piece counts) | added |

The negative PvE Damage Boost is the deliberate cost. Centurion, Tribunus and
High Commander sets were also consolidated from two set effects to one. Practical
consequence: keep a PvE preset and a PvP preset, and never run
[[Dungeons and Raids]] in an Abyss set.

There is a second, subtler set-effect trap. Because set bonuses activate at piece
thresholds, upgrading pieces one at a time can break your active set bonus and
leave you weaker than before. Korean guides recommend inheriting Decanus into
Centurion in an order that preserves a working set threshold at every step rather
than replacing pieces as you can afford them.

- Abyss access at level 45 with item level 1,000 for the Lower Layer.

- Weekly medal purchase caps, which are the real throttle. See [[Merit Medals]].

## Progression path

1. **Fierce Battle Amulet** from the quest. Free.
2. **Decanus rings** at ~31,600 AP each. Two slots, cheapest item level 67 Unique
   in the game.
3. **Decanus earrings, cloak, gloves** at ~42,100 AP each.
4. **Decanus necklace, boots, pauldrons** at ~52,600 AP each.
5. **Decanus chest, pants, weapon** at 60,000–120,000+ AP. Last, because expensive.
6. **Centurion armour** — the five-piece armour purchase is 120 Silver Medals,
   about 2.2 weeks of medal income at Grade 9 Soldier or above.
7. **Tribunus accessories**, then Tribunus armour.
8. **Legate weapon and gauntlet** if you reach General. Elite Legate and
   Praetorian are later-KR and are out.
9. **Do not plan Abyss Potential.** It is a Season 3 system.

Throughout, use [[Succession and Transfer]] to carry enhancement forward if
that system is even present — **Succession Crafting is out** at Season 1
(2026-04-08), so treat invested pieces as much harder to move than Korean
guides written after April imply. Season 2 raised the inheritance attempt cap
from 6 to 20; Chapter 1 removed limits entirely. Expect the tighter launch
rules.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Accessories before armour before weapon before gauntlet.** This is the
  consensus ordering in every Korean PvP guide reviewed, and the reasoning is
  survival first.
- **Six-piece Decanus armour** is the first real breakpoint, for the reported ~30
  percent player damage reduction.
- **Weapon and gauntlet potential** is the highest-value per-point PvP damage
  upgrade in later Korea, at +2.5% per level to +10%. **Out** at Season 1.
- **Stop pushing enhancement past your comfort threshold.** Enhancement is 100
  percent safe to +10 and probabilistic from +11, gear is never destroyed, and
  failure raises the next attempt's chance. See [[Enchanting]] and
  [[Breakthrough]].

### Extraction refunds

Extracting Abyss gear returns Abyss Points. The commonly repeated figure is **80
percent**, stated in pre-launch developer coverage and repeated widely. However,
an Inven guide that measured refunds in November 2025 reported **60–70 percent
depending on slot**. Both are plausible for different builds; treat 80 percent as
the design intent and 60–70 percent as the measured figure, **confidence medium**
either way. Enhancement materials and Enhancement Stones are refunded at 100
percent on extraction for ordinary gear, with Kinah excluded. See
[[Extraction and Refunds]].

The practical effect is that Decanus accessories are close to a loan rather than a
purchase, which is what makes them safe for a PvE player to buy as an item level
bridge.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Running Abyss gear in PvE.** The set effects subtract PvE Damage Boost. This
  is the single most common mistake.
- **Upgrading pieces one at a time and breaking a set threshold.** Plan the
  inheritance order.
- **Buying the weapon early.** Highest cost, lowest survivability return, and the
  slot most likely to be superseded by a new tier.
- **Judging PvP gear by item level.** PvP builds show lower item level because
  their options are specialised; see [[Item Level vs Combat Power]].
- **Chasing Legate before General rank.** The weapon is 240 Platinum Medals and
  Platinum purchase is General-gated, so the tier is unreachable at low rank.
- **Planning a Heroic Fierce Battle Amulet.** The Abyss Shop scroll and the
  Heroic conversion are Chapter 1. Unique +10 is the Season 1 stop.

Unique Decanus-and-up shop, no Heroic, no Praetorian, no Abyss Potential.
**Launch Scale Test: Silver medals only**, shop around **item level 77**.
