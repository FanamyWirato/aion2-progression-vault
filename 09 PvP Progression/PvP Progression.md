# PvP Progression

> **In one sentence:** PvP in AION 2 is a second, parallel progression track with its
> own currency, its own gear line, its own Daevanion board and its own stat pair,
> and it is largely — but not entirely — skippable if you only care about PvE.

> **Season 1 verdict:** Abyss at 45, Unique Decanus–Legate, six Daevanion boards
> (Azphel at 45), four stigma slots cap 20, [[Merit Medals]] as the gear gate.
> **Out:** Abyss Potential, Marchutan / Yustiel, Heroic and Praetorian tiers,
> tradable Medal Boxes, Chapter 1 arena sizes. Ranking stat rewards may still
> be live — a limited-time opportunity. Abyss weekly hours: two models, see
> [[Abyss Points and Ranks]]. Legions have no level, skill tree or member buff.

## Why it matters

AION 2 splits almost every combat number into a PvE half and a PvP half. There is
PvE Damage Boost and PvP Damage Boost, PvE Damage Tolerance and PvP Damage
Tolerance, and from Season 3 (2026-04-08) the two coefficient families are
calculated separately. That split is the single most important fact in this
chapter. It means a fully geared PvE character walking into the Abyss has close
to zero PvP defence, and it means a PvP character's item level looks low while
their effective survivability against players is far higher. A Korean guide
frames the comparison bluntly: a player at item level 3,000 with no PvP defence
loses to a player at item level 2,500 in a full Decanus set, because the PvE
player's numbers do not apply (see [[Item Level vs Combat Power]] and
[[Stat Reference]]).

The consequence for planning is that PvP power is not a stronger version of PvE
power. It is a different budget. Abyss Points cannot buy PvE gear, Kinah cannot
buy Abyss gear, and from Season 2 (2026-01-21) Abyss gear set effects carry an
explicit penalty to non-Abyss PvE damage. Two tracks, two wallets, two sets of
diminishing returns.

## How it works

The chapter has four moving parts, and they feed each other in a loop:

1. **Earn Abyss Points (AP, 어비스 포인트)** from Abyss hunting, player kills,
   arenas, battlefields, Supply Requests and mission quests, inside a weekly cap
   structure. Covered in [[Abyss Points and Ranks]].
2. **Earn Merit Medals (공훈 훈장)** from season missions, Abyss bosses, artifact
   sieges and battlefields — and buy them with AP, gated by Abyss Rank. Also in
   [[Abyss Points and Ranks]].
3. **Spend AP plus [[Merit Medals]]** on the Abyss gear ladder. Medals, not AP,
   gate Centurion and above. Covered in [[PvP Gear]]. **No Abyss Potential**
   at Season 1.
4. **Spend PvP-specific crystals and points** on the Azphel Daevanion board,
   PvP Pantheon and Arcana presets, and the rank-gated transformation. Covered in
   [[PvP Boards and Transformations]].

The competitive content that generates all of this — arenas, battlefields,
artifact sieges, Spacetime Rift raids and season ladders — is scheduled, not
always-on, and is covered in [[Arenas Sieges and Seasons]].

A structural point that trips up players coming from the original AION: **rank and
currency are two separate pools**. Abyss Points are the spendable currency. Abyss
Ranking Points (랭킹 포인트) are a non-spendable score that determines your Abyss
Rank. Spending AP on gear does not reduce your rank, and since the 2026-02-11
patch dying to an enemy player costs Ranking Points rather than Abyss Points.

## Progression path

The order below assumes you have hit level 45 and finished the story, and that
PvP is a real goal rather than a curiosity.

1. **Claim the Fierce Battle Amulet** from the Abyss visit quest. It is free, it
   is permanent, and it carries a flat PvP Damage Boost. Do this even if you never
   fight another player, because it costs nothing but a quest.
2. **Open the Azphel board** and put your first Daevanion Crystals: Azphel into
   PvP Damage Tolerance rather than Boost. Surviving the opening CC chain is what
   lets you play at all.
3. **Buy Decanus accessories with AP** — rings first at roughly 31,600 AP each.
   These are item level 67 Unique-grade pieces, so they double as a PvE item-level
   boost while your dungeon gear is still Heritage or Rare.
4. **Complete the Decanus armour set** for the roughly 30 percent damage
   reduction a six-piece set is reported to give in the Abyss.
5. **Establish a weekly AP and Medal routine.** Both caps and both medal purchase
   limits reset weekly; unused capacity does not bank.
6. **Climb to 1-Star Officer** to unlock Gold Medal purchases and the Tribunus
   tier, then to General for Platinum and the Legate tier.
7. **Layer on Pantheon and Arcana PvP presets** once your gear tier is
   settled. Abyss Potential is out. Stat Arcana slots are out.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Defence before offence.** Every Korean PvP guide reviewed puts accessories,
  then armour, then weapon, then gauntlet. The reasoning is that you cannot
  trade damage if you die inside a single CC chain.
- **Full Decanus set is the first real breakpoint.** Below it you are food; at it
  you are in the fight.
- **1-Star Officer is the second.** It gates Gold Medals and the Tribunus tier.
- **General is the third.** It gates Platinum Medals, the Legate tier and, by
  community report, the Guardian General transformation.
- **Accuracy 2,000+ for Abyss PvP** is the commonly cited stat floor, because
  evasion builds are viable against players in a way they are not against bosses.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Wearing Abyss gear in dungeons.** Since Season 2 the Decanus through Legate
  set effects grant large Abyss PvE Damage Boost but subtract flat PvE Damage
  Boost, so the set is a net loss in [[Dungeons and Raids]]. Use presets.
- **Treating item level as the PvP yardstick.** It is not, for the reasons in
  "Why it matters".
- **Letting weekly caps expire.** Both AP caps and all three Medal purchase
  limits reset weekly and do not accumulate.
- **Buying the weapon first.** It is the most expensive slot and the least
  survivability per point.
- **Assuming rank is bought.** Rank comes from Ranking Points, which come from
  PvP participation. You cannot purchase your way to General.

## Global launch notes

The global PC release runs a free public test on 2026-09-17 and 18, Founder's Pack
Early Access from 2026-09-30, and full free-to-play launch on 2026-10-05 via Steam
and PURPLE. Structural differences that matter for this chapter:

- **Elyos and Asmodian sit on separate servers**, and the two factions meet only
  in the Abyss through cross-server matchmaking. During Early Access, Abyss
  matchmaking is restricted to Early Access servers; after launch NCSOFT matches
  servers by how far along and how strong each one is.
- **Abyss time is unresolved.** Korea runs ≈7 hours per week per layer,
  extendable with Rift Stones. NCSOFT told Gamescom press that free players
  will not be time-limited out of the Abyss. Both models and what they do to
  a weekly plan are in [[Abyss Points and Ranks]].
- **Do not inherit Chapter 1 arena sizes.** Season 1 is 4v4 and 8v8, not 5v5 /
  10v10 equalised.
- **Ranking stat rewards may still be live.** Korea removed them in Season 3.
  If they ship, they are a limited-time opportunity.
- **Legions** have hideouts and storage and nothing else — no level, no skill
  tree, no member buff. See [[Legions]].

## What a PvE-only player can safely skip

- The entire Abyss gear ladder above Decanus accessories. Tribunus and Legate are
  rank-gated, Medal-gated and carry a PvE penalty.
- The Azphel Daevanion board. Put those points nowhere; the crystals are separate
  from Ariel crystals, so there is no opportunity cost in [[Daevanion Boards]].
- Arena and battlefield ladders, ranking rewards and the Guardian General
  transformation.
- Abyss gear potential, which only produces PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance
  and which is **out** at Season 1 anyway.

## What PvP-exclusive power a PvE player still wants

- **The Fierce Battle Amulet** is free from a quest and shares the amulet slot
  with the Revelation Amulet, so it costs nothing to own. See [[PvP Gear]].
- **Decanus accessories**, particularly the two rings at roughly 31,600 AP, are
  item level 67 Unique pieces that raise item level cheaply while your real gear
  catches up, and they refund most of their AP on extraction. See
  [[Extraction and Refunds]].
- **Stigma Shards** are sold in the Abyss exchange shop at 25,000 AP and are
  pure PvE value, but that is the **worst-case** tap. Four slots, cap 20: 140
  shards to take all four to 15, 300 to finish at 20. Dungeon Unstable Shards
  are the primary supply; TW cut the 100 percent conversion to 200,000 Kinah
  on 2026-02-25. See [[Stigmas]].
- **Daevanion Crystal: Ariel** is not sold for AP, but **Abyss and Arena shops do
  sell Azphel crystals**, and Supply Requests — the largest uncapped AP source —
  reward AP for handing in crafted goods, which makes [[Crafting]] an AP faucet
  for PvE players who never fight anyone.
- **Abyss Points themselves** are earnable entirely without PvP: Supply Requests,
  mission quests, order scrolls and the safe Abyss Corridor cover the majority of
  a weekly total.
