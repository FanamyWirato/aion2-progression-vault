# Activities and Economy

> **In one sentence:** every unit of power in AION 2 is bought with a resource,
> and every resource comes from one of about twenty repeatable activities, so
> this chapter is the map you read backwards from the upgrade you want to the
> content that pays for it.

## Why it matters

AION 2 is not a game where you grind an open world and get stronger. Almost all
progression currency is behind a lockout: a daily reset, a weekly reset, a
ticket that recharges on a clock, or an energy pool that caps out. The practical
consequence is that your ceiling is set less by hours played than by how few
lockouts you waste. A player who clears the right eight things per day will
out-pace a player who plays twice as long but lets Odyle Energy sit at cap and
forgets the Wednesday reset.

The second consequence is that this chapter is the bottleneck for every other
chapter in the vault. [[Enchanting]] is gated on Enhance Stones, which come from
Daily Dungeons and Sealed Dungeons. [[Daevanion Boards]] are gated on Daevanion
Crystals, which come from Sealed Dungeons, [[Shugo Festa]] and the Nightmare shop.
[[Arcana]] is gated on Transcendence stage clears. [[Wings Enhancement]] and
[[Pantheon]] are gated on Expedition and event currencies. If you cannot name
the activity that feeds a system, you cannot plan that system.

## How it works

The economy has four layers, and confusing them is the most common planning
mistake.

| Layer | Examples | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time-gated entries | Expedition reward counts, Daily Dungeon 7/week, Nightmare tickets | Recharge on a clock; unspent charges are lost at reset |
| Capped energy | Odyle Energy (오드 에너지) | Regenerates to a hard cap and then stops generating |
| Capped income | Field-hunting Kinah, Abyss Points per corridor | Hard numeric ceiling per day or per run |
| Uncapped income | Gathering, crafting, Market flipping | No lockout; limited only by market prices and your time |

Everything else in the chapter is a variation on those four. Season content
adds a fifth, softer layer: point tracks that convert participation across all
the above into [[Seasons and Battle Pass]] rewards.

## Unlock and gates

- **Levels 1-44:** the campaign, [[Level and Ascension]], Sealed Dungeons and
  Strongholds. This is one-off content, not a loop, but it is where most of your
  early [[Daevanion Boards]] and belt progress comes from.
- **Level 45:** the endgame loop opens. Duty Quests, Expeditions, Daily
  Dungeons, Nightmare, Ascension Trial, [[Shugo Festa]].
- **Item level 1,000:** the first Expedition Conquest dungeons and the Abyss.
- **Item level 1,600 / 2,200:** the two- and three-star Expedition tiers.
- **Item level ~2,700:** Sanctuary 1 (Ludra), 8 players.
- **Item level 3,800:** Season 1 ceiling, not a dungeon door.
- **Level 50 and 3,500 / 4,500 raids:** Chapter 1 / later. **Out.**

See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]] for why the entry number on the door is not
the number you actually need.

## Progression path

1. **Campaign to level 45-50.** Sweep Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds in your
   own territory as you go. See [[Progression Phases Roadmap]].
2. **Foundation sweep.** Clear every remaining Sealed Dungeon, Stronghold and
   Empyrean Trace in both factions' territories. These are one-off, permanent,
   and the single densest source of early Combat Power.
3. **Open the daily loop.** Duty Quests, Expedition Exploration, Daily Dungeon,
   Nightmare. Detail in [[Daily and Weekly Loops]].
4. **Item level 1,600-2,200.** Expedition Conquest becomes the main gear and
   Kinah engine; Transcendence becomes the [[Arcana]] engine.
5. **Item level ~2,700 to 3,800.** Sanctuary 1 (Ludra) opens. The bar tops out
   at 3,800; there is no second raid at launch. See [[Dungeons and Raids]].
6. **Season maintenance.** From here the loop is stable and the variable is how
   completely you convert lockouts into season points and shop currency.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- The three things never to skip: five Duty Quests, the Expedition reward counts
  that recharge at 05:00 / 13:00 / 21:00, and spending Odyle Energy before it
  caps.
- The Wednesday 05:00 reset is the hard wall. Anything weekly that is unspent at
  Tuesday night is gone.
- Odyle Energy is the real currency of the endgame. Entries without energy give
  you nothing worth having, so energy acquisition outranks entry acquisition.
- Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds are one-off. Finish them before optimising
  anything repeatable.

## Traps and mistakes

- Letting Odyle Energy sit at cap. Regeneration stops entirely, so a full pool
  is a silent loss of several hundred thousand Kinah a week.
- Spending entries on content below your gear. Reward tiers scale with
  difficulty and score, so one clear at the right stage beats three below it.
- Treating Bound Kinah as money. It cannot touch the Market, and most endgame
  Kinah income is now Bound. See [[Currencies and Economy]].
- Building alts on the old Korean assumption that entries are per character.
  Chapter 1 moved most weekly counts to per-server. See
  [[Alts and Account Wide Progression]].
