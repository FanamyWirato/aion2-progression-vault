# Currencies and Economy

> **In one sentence:** AION 2 has one tradable currency, one premium currency,
> one bound shadow-currency that looks like money but is not, and about a dozen
> content currencies, and the whole design pivots on the fact that the Market
> and the Kinah-Quna Exchange sit behind a paid Membership.

## Why it matters

Every progression system in this vault ends in a Kinah cost. [[Enchanting]],
[[Breakthrough]], [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]], [[Arcana]] refinement,
[[Succession and Transfer]] and [[Manastones Spirit Stones and Godstones]] all
consume Kinah on top of their materials, and the late-game numbers are large:
20,000,000 Kinah for a Unique-to-Heroic succession, 30,000,000 for a Radiant
Manalight succession, and up to 50,000,000 for a capped inheritance after
Chapter 1 made inheritance guaranteed.

That would be manageable if all Kinah were the same. It is not. From Season 3
onward most endgame Kinah income arrives as Bound Kinah, which cannot touch the
Market. Understanding that split is the single most important economic fact in
the game.

## How it works

### The two Kinah types

| Type | KR name | Aliases | Can be traded | Earned from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal Kinah | 일반 키나 | Kinah, Kina, unstamped Kinah | Yes: Market, personal trade, Exchange | Field hunting, gathering and crafting sales, drop sales, some dungeon rewards |
| Bound Kinah | 각인 키나 | Stamped Kinah, Tied Kina, Soul-bound Kinah, Sealed Kinah | No | Most Expedition, Transcendence and Sanctuary cube rewards, battle pass and mission payouts |

Bound Kinah still pays for growth: enhancement, crafting, manastone engraving,
soul engraving and Arcana refinement all accept it. It cannot buy anything from
another player. The practical effect is that your growth budget and your market
budget are separate wallets and you must farm each one deliberately.

The split was announced in a developer livestream as an anti-bot and
anti-inflation measure. Two framings circulate and they are not the same claim:

- Mein-MMO, reporting the livestream, states that only about 20 percent of
  earned Kinah will be unstamped and tradable, with 80 percent stamped for NPC
  use only.
- Aion2.Online, reporting the 2026-03-18 patch, states that Tied Kina "aims to
  slash market inflation by roughly 20 percent".

Both are the "roughly 20 percent" figure the community repeats, but one is a
share of income and the other is an inflation target. Neither is corroborated by
an official numeric statement in the sources checked, so treat the specific 20
percent as **confidence medium** whichever way it is meant.

The conversion has proceeded in stages, and it is still moving:

| Date | Conversion |
| --- | --- |
| 2026-03-18 | Bound Kinah introduced |
| 2026-04-08 (Season 3) | Krao Cave, Urugugu Canyon, Vakron Sky Island and Draupnir Conquest to 100 percent Bound; Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den Conquest to 50 percent; all Exploration difficulties to Bound |
| 2026-05-27 | 50 percent of Abyssal Forge: Ludra Kinah to Bound |
| 2026-07-01 (Chapter 1) | Conquest Normal Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den to Bound; 50 percent of Dying Dramata's Nest and Cradle of Nihility; Conquest Hard on the four one- and two-star dungeons to Bound |
| 2026-08-26 | Conquest Hard Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den to Bound; Conquest Normal and Hard Dying Dramata's Nest and Cradle of Nihility to Bound; Submerged Life Temple to Bound; Abyssal Forge: Ludra to Bound |

By the 2026-08-26 patch, essentially no dungeon in Korea is a reliable source of
tradable Kinah.

### Quna

Quna (큐나, also written Qunah or Kuna) is the premium currency. It buys shop
cosmetics and consumables, unlocks premium battle pass tracks, pays for
character slots and server transfers, and can be exchanged for Kinah. Quna is
shared across all characters on an account.

### The Market and the Exchange

NC's official global business-model notice of 2026-07-08 defines both:

- **The Market** is "the center of AION 2's player-driven economy". Players list
  and buy with Kinah at prices they set. There is no NPC price floor.
- **The Kinah-Quna Exchange** is player-to-player in both directions: one player
  lists Kinah for sale, another spends Quna to buy it. NC states that "the game
  makes no listings or purchases", so there is no publisher-set rate.
- **An active Membership is required to trade on both.** NC states this twice in
  the notice.

Reported behaviour that is widely repeated but not in NC's notice: free players
can buy from the Market but cannot list, and non-subscribers retain limited
direct player-to-player trade. Those claims come from secondary sites and should
be treated as **confidence medium**.

The Market also charges listing and transaction fees. A commonly repeated
guideline is that flips under a 15 to 20 percent margin lose money to tax, but
the actual fee percentage is not published in any source checked.

For the global launch there is an extra wrinkle: early-access servers run
separate Markets, Exchanges and PvP matchmaking for a period, and there will be
a **Server Market** available from early access plus a **Cross-Server Market**
that opens later, so that founders cannot distort the launch-server economy.

### The trading post

The Black Cloud Trading Company (검은 구름 무역단) is a Kinah-only NPC shop whose
stock rotates every hour. It sells gear appearances, pets and packs. It is worth
a daily look for two reasons: it and the Shugo shop are the only sources for
certain pets, and pets contribute possession effects that are
[[Pet Understanding]] progression rather than cosmetics. Korean guides advise
buying discounted packs over appearances because packs carry possession and
level-up effects.

The Wind Breeze Trading Company (산들바람 상회, also Windbreeze Merchants) is the
membership-only Kinah shop. Its special tab is the most Kinah-efficient purchase
in the game.

global product is a single 15 USD tier.

## Unlock and gates

- Kinah from level 1. Bound Kinah from wherever the content pays it.
- The Market and the Exchange require an active Membership. This is the hardest
  gate in the game's economy.
- Korea set resource gathering to level 45 in January 2026 as an anti-bot
  measure, which also gates the gathering-and-crafting Kinah route behind max
  level.
- Server transfer requires level 45 and costs Quna.

## Progression path

The Kinah income mix changes shape three times.

1. **Levelling.** Campaign and Sealed Dungeon payouts, 15,000 Kinah per
   Stronghold, quest rewards. Kinah is not a bottleneck yet.
2. **Early max level.** Field hunting to the 2,000,000 daily cap plus Expedition
   cube rewards. This is the phase where the daily cap actually matters, because
   2,000,000 is a meaningful share of income.
3. **Mid and late game.** Bound Kinah covers growth; tradable Kinah has to come
   from gathering, crafting and selling drops. This is the phase where players
   without a gathering and crafting pipeline hit a wall, because dungeons no
   longer pay tradable Kinah.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- Fill the 2,000,000 field-hunting cap every day. It resets at 05:00 and is the
  most reliable tradable Kinah in the game. Dense spawn zones such as the
  Fafnite Burial Grounds (파프나이트 매장지) are the standard spot, and it doubles
  as pet-soul farming.
- Build the gathering and crafting pipeline before you need it. It is the only
  uncapped tradable Kinah route and Korean guides rank it as essential from
  Season 3 onward.
- Sell, do not dismantle. Unusable or duplicate Unique drops are worth more on
  the Market than as extraction fodder, and Draconic materials from Draupnir
  Conquest hold a persistently high price.
- Sell Supply Request delivery items instead of handing them in when Kinah is
  tighter than Abyss Points. The rare and heritage-grade delivery gear commands
  high Market prices.
- Buy the Wind Breeze special tab every week. 100,000 Kinah for 40 Odyle Energy
  is the best Kinah-to-power conversion available.

## Costs and math

### Realistic daily Kinah for a mid-game player

The Korean Season 3 efficiency table, converted to a daily budget for a player
around 2,000-2,500 Combat Power who does the full loop:

| Source | Daily expectation | Kinah type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expedition and Transcendence cube rewards | several million to over ten million | Bound |
| Field hunting | up to 2,000,000 | Normal |
| Gathering, crafting and Market sales | several million, price dependent | Normal |
| Drop sales | several million, luck dependent | Normal |
| Supply Request item sales | several million, production dependent | Normal |
| Nightmare instant-complete tickets | 30,000 each, 14 per week, so about 60,000 per day | Normal |
| Challenge ticket selection boxes | 100,000 each, 21 per week, so about 300,000 per day | Normal |

A reasonable planning figure for a mid-game player who runs the whole loop is
**roughly 5 to 10 million tradable Kinah per day plus several million Bound
Kinah**, with the tradable half almost entirely dependent on gathering,
crafting and drop sales rather than dungeons. That is a synthesis of the Korean
table rather than a quoted number, so treat it as **confidence medium**.

### A note on buying currency

Sites that sell Kinah or boosting services advertise heavily against AION 2
search terms. Buying in-game currency for real money outside the official
Kinah-Quna Exchange violates the terms of service and risks the account. The
official Exchange exists precisely to make that unnecessary, and it is
player-to-player. This vault does not recommend third-party currency purchase,
and the SEO content those sites publish is unreliable on specifics — several of
the numbers they quote for AION 2 could not be corroborated anywhere else.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Planning a budget in Kinah without separating the two types.** A player
  with 200 million Bound Kinah and no tradable Kinah cannot buy a single
  manastone from the Market.
- **Letting Odyle Energy cap.** It is functionally destroying Kinah, because
  cube rewards are the largest Kinah payout in the game.
- **Soul engraving before deciding to sell.** A tuned item cannot be listed or
  worn by another character.
- **Assuming the Exchange has a fixed rate.** It is player-set and NC injects
  nothing, so early global weeks will be volatile.
- **Buying tradable goods from the Market to feed growth.** Market prices sit
  above what farming the same material costs in time for most materials, and
  Korean guides say to approach the Black Cloud Trading Company's tradable stock
  only "at a level you can afford".
- **Ignoring the Sunday and Wednesday shop resets.** The Wind Breeze special tab
  is the highest-value Kinah purchase and it is easy to miss.

## Global launch notes

The global economy is documented by NC's own 2026-07-08 notice and is
structurally simpler than Korea's on paper: free to play, two currencies,
player-driven Market, player-to-player Kinah-Quna Exchange, one 15 USD
Membership that unlocks both plus a Kinah-spending member shop, a
cosmetics-and-consumables shop, per-character Daeva Passes, and Founder's Packs
that include a 30-day Special Quai Membership.

What the notice does **not** mention is Bound Kinah. Korea's split arrived four
months before global launch as an anti-bot response, so the global build may
ship with it, may ship without it, or may ship with a different ratio. This is
the largest open economic question for a Western player, because it determines
whether dungeon farming produces spendable money or only growth fuel.

Also global-specific: the early-access period runs separate Markets and
Exchanges, and the Cross-Server Market opens some time after launch rather than
at launch.

**Launch Scale Test:** a **Trust** icon sat next to Kinah. Lost Ark-style:
play content and spend Kinah to raise it; low trust restricts the Market.
Aimed at bots that only list Kinah. Trading was disabled in the test, so
thresholds are unknown. Currency UI also showed **Pending Quna** converting
to Paid Quna after 72 hours — unexplained. See [[Global Launch Differences]].

## Open questions

- [ ] Whether the global build includes Bound Kinah at launch, and at what
      ratio. Settled by the launch client.
- [ ] The exact meaning of the "roughly 20 percent" figure: share of income kept
      tradable, or targeted inflation reduction. Two reputable outlets report it
      differently.
- [ ] The Market listing and transaction fee percentages. Not published in any
      source checked.
- [ ] Whether free players can buy but not sell on the global Market. Widely
      reported by secondary sites, absent from NC's notice.
- [ ] Global Quna pack pricing, which is needed to convert Korean Quna costs
      into a Western budget.
- [ ] Whether per-character and per-server exchange or trade caps exist. No
      source checked documents a numeric Exchange cap, only content purchase
      limits.
- [ ] What the unspecified "additional gameplay benefits" of the global
      Membership are. The Korean tier list is the best proxy: Odyle regeneration
      and cap increases, doubled Abyss hours, doubled Shugo Festa keys, extra
      cube selections.
