# Daily and Weekly Loops

> **In one sentence:** the concrete list of what recharges when, what it pays,
> and the order to spend it in so that nothing expires unspent.

## Why it matters

Nearly all AION 2 growth resources arrive through lockouts. Enhance Stones,
Daevanion Crystals, Arcana cards, Abyss Points, Breakthrough Stones and most
Kinah are all rationed by a charge that refills on a clock. Unspent charges do
not bank past the reset, and the one resource that does bank — Odyle Energy —
stops regenerating at its cap. That makes the daily and weekly routine the
highest-leverage thing a player controls, and it is also why AION 2 has a
reputation for feeling like homework.

The routine also feeds [[Seasons and Battle Pass]]: season weekly point tracks
count participation in Abyss, Arena, Transcendence, Nightmare and challenge
content, so doing the loop pays twice.

## How it works

### Reset clocks

All times are server time. Korea runs KST (GMT+9), Taiwan GMT+8, so the same
event fires exactly one hour apart between the two services.

| Daily | 21:00 | Expedition reward count 3 of 3 |
| Weekly | Wednesday 05:00 | Daily Dungeon entries, Ascension Trial, Abyss access hours, weekly Supply Requests, Orders, Abyss Orders, Battlefield win rewards, Sanctuary, Odyle Energy material conversion, season weekly rewards |
| Weekly | Sunday midnight into Monday | Wind Breeze Trading Company special-tab stock (this was moved to Wednesday 05:00 in the 2026-01-21 patch; older guides still say Monday, confidence medium) |
| Hourly | On the hour | Black Cloud Trading Company stock rotation |
| Every 3 hours | 02:00, 05:00, 08:00, 11:00, 14:00, 17:00, 20:00, 23:00 | Spacetime Rift opening |
| Hourly | :30 past the hour | Dimensional Invasion (moved from the hour in the 2026-04-08 patch) |

## Unlock and gates

- Duty Quests, Supply Requests, Shugo Festa and Nightmare open at or before
  level 45.
- Expedition Conquest needs item level 1,000, with the two- and three-star tiers
  at 1,600 and 2,200. See [[Dungeons and Raids]].
- Abyss needs level 45 and item level 1,000.
- Ascension Trial Easy needs Combat Power 1,000.
- Transcendence is nominally Combat Power 1,200 but Korean guides recommend
  1,400-1,500 before you attempt it seriously.
- Spacetime Rift requires having cleared the opposing faction's Sealed Dungeon,
  which is the one prerequisite people forget.

## Progression path

1. **Week 1 at max level.** Ignore the repeatable loop. Sweep every Sealed
   Dungeon, Stronghold and Empyrean Trace on both maps. This is one-off content
   worth more Combat Power per hour than anything on this page.
2. **Week 2.** Add Duty Quests, Expedition Exploration and the Daily Dungeon.
   Buy the Wind Breeze special tab if you subscribe.
3. **Item level 1,600.** Switch Expedition from Exploration to Conquest. Start
   Transcendence at whatever stage you can clear with an S rank.
4. **Item level 2,200+.** Add Nightmare seriously (for the ranking, not just the
   fragments), the Abyss hours, and the weekly Orders block.
5. **Item level ~2,700.** Sanctuary 1 (Ludra) is the Season 1 raid. 3,500+
   raids are later Korean seasons. The bar itself tops out at **3,800**.

## Priorities and breakpoints

### Minimum viable daily checklist (20-30 minutes)

1. Five Duty Quest: Mission entries. Reroll the offered rewards to fish for
   Wanted-target quests, which have the Unique gear chance.
2. Claim the three Expedition reward counts. If you are short on time, claim
   them on the highest Conquest difficulty you can solo.
3. Spend enough Odyle Energy to stay off the cap.
4. Urgent Supply Request, if you already hold the delivery items.
5. Two Nightmare entries only if your ticket stock is approaching 14.

Everything else can be skipped on a given day without permanent loss.

### Completionist checklist

Daily: five Duty Missions, Duty Commands, urgent Supply Request, three
Expedition reward counts, two Transcendence tickets, two Nightmare entries,
Shugo Festa keys, Dimensional Invasion twice, Black Cloud Trading Company
check, field hunting to the 2,000,000 Kinah cap, one Spacetime Rift.

Weekly (front-load on Wednesday): seven Daily Dungeon entries, three Ascension
Trials, seven Abyss hours (fourteen with membership), every Abyss Corridor
opening, ten Battlefield wins, twelve Orders, twenty-five Abyss Orders, weekly
Supply Requests, Sanctuary to the reward cap, seven Unknown Fissure runs, the
Odyle Energy conversion crafts, and the Wind Breeze special-tab stock.

### What to drop first when short on time

In order, drop:

1. Battlefields and Arenas, unless you are chasing [[Arenas Sieges and Seasons]]
   rank.
2. Abyss Orders. High Abyss Point value but the worst time cost in the game.
3. Spacetime Rift. Fun, high variance, no guaranteed progression.
4. Dimensional Invasion and Shugo Festa, in that order.
5. Abyss hours, if your PvE gear is behind. Korean guides explicitly say to
   defer the Abyss until everything except the hardest Expedition is done.
6. Unknown Fissure.

Never drop: Duty Quests, Expedition reward counts, Odyle Energy management, the
Daily Dungeon block before Wednesday.

| Odyle Energy per week, with membership | 840 passive + 280 crafting + 280 purchased = about 1,400 | high |
| Odyle cost per Expedition or Transcendence cube | 20-40, typically 40 at Conquest and Transcendence | high |
| Odyle cost per Expedition Conquest Hard cube (Chapter 1) | 60 | high |
| Odyle cost per Sanctuary reward | 80 | high |
| Odyle Energy conversion recipe | 25 Odyle + 5 High-Purity Odyle + 1 Pure Odyle + 50,000 Kinah | medium |
| Ascension Trial Extreme payout | 100 Silentium, 40 Ariel's Trace fragments, 20 Engraving boxes | medium |

| Battlefield win | 2,000 Abyss Points, first 10 wins per week | medium |

The Odyle Energy arithmetic is the load-bearing number. At roughly 840 energy
per week without a membership and 40 per cube, a free player can claim about 21
reward cubes per week across all Expedition, Transcendence and Sanctuary
content. That is the real weekly entry budget, regardless of how many entry
charges the UI shows you.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Letting Odyle Energy sit at cap.** Regeneration stops dead at 560 (840 with
  membership). A full pool for two days is roughly 160-240 energy destroyed,
  which is four to six reward cubes.
- **Hoarding entries.** Tickets and charges do not roll over past their reset.
  Korean guides repeat the same warning: spend down so nothing overflows, but do
  not push content your gear cannot clear.
- **Running content below your gear.** Rewards scale with difficulty and with
  clear score. One S-rank Transcendence clear at the correct stage beats several
  A-rank clears one stage down.
- **Forgetting the Wednesday wall.** The single most common loss is a Tuesday
  evening with unspent Daily Dungeon entries and Ascension Trials.
- **Assuming daily means daily.** The Daily Dungeon is a weekly seven-entry
  bundle, and the Expedition "daily" counts stack three times a day and are
  shared across every Expedition dungeon rather than being per dungeon.
- **Handing in Supply Request items when Kinah is the bottleneck.** The delivery
  items are often worth more on the Market than the Abyss Points they return.

## Global launch notes

The daily and weekly skeleton above is the KR/TW Season 4 build and the global
service is expected to launch closer to the early-season structure: fewer
Expedition and Transcendence dungeons, Nightmare Layer I only, and Sanctuary
likely limited to the entry raid. Chapter 1 also changed the shape of the loop
substantially by removing reward-count and kill-count caps from Expedition and
Transcendence in favour of a weekly count that is only consumed when you leave
a cleared dungeon without claiming its reward, and by moving most of those
counts from per character to per server. Whether the global build ships the
pre-Chapter-1 or post-Chapter-1 lockout model is the biggest single unknown for
planning a global routine. Reset times for global regions have not been
published. See [[Global Launch Differences]].
