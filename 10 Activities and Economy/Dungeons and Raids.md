# Dungeons and Raids

> **Season 1 verdict.** The PvE ladder at launch stops at Sanctuary 1 (Ludra,
> ~2,700) and a **3,800 item-level ceiling**. Conquest Hard as a named mode,
> Fallen Daeva at 3,800/4,500, Abyssal Horn Den at 4,100–4,800, Sanctuary 2/3
> at 3,500/4,500, and Nightmare Layer IV are later Korean seasons. Use the
> Season 1 column in [[Item Level vs Combat Power]].

> **In one sentence:** the whole PvE ladder from one-off Sealed Dungeons to the
> launch Sanctuary raid, with the item level that opens each rung.

## Why it matters

Dungeon content is where item level comes from, and item level is what unlocks
the next dungeon, so this ladder is the spine of PvE progression. It is also the
place where the two entry currencies collide: an entry charge lets you in, but
Odyle Energy (오드 에너지) is what turns a clear into loot. Understanding which
rung is worth your energy is the main strategic decision in the game.

The ladder splits cleanly by purpose. Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds are
one-off permanent power. Expeditions are the gear and Kinah engine.
Transcendence is the [[Arcana]] engine. Nightmare is a solo skill ladder and a
shop currency faucet. Sanctuary is the top-end gear source and the reason to
have a legion.

Citadel of the Fallen Daeva is the dungeon behind the "Castle of Ba..." lead;
translations in circulation include Castle of the Fallen Deva and Citadel of the

The tooltip says item level 2,200, but public groups in Korea expected 2,500 or
higher for damage roles, and one guide puts realistic Conquest at 2,700 plus.
Treat 2,200 as "the door opens" and 2,500 as "you will be accepted".

### Transcendence dungeons

Two Season 1 dungeons run ten stages each, and each stage has its own
recommended Combat Power. Clearing a stage with an S rank unlocks the next one;
S rank depends on clear time and party resurrection count.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deus Research Base | 데우스 연구기지 | Easier | Bell, Compass, Grail |

| Abyssal Horn Den | 심연의 뿔 암굴 | Chapter 1, 4 stages | Key and Hourglass |

Stage-to-reward mapping for the ten-stage dungeons, from a Korean guide:

| Stages | Recommended Combat Power | Best Arcana grade available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-2 | 1,500-1,700 | Normal |
| 3-5 | 1,800-2,300 | Rare |

Abyssal Horn Den stage gates are item level 3,800 / 4,100 / 4,500 / 4,800 at
**level 50**. That dungeon is Chapter 1 and **out** at Season 1. Grail raises both active and passive skill levels, Parchment raises
active only, and Bell and Mirror raise passive only, which is why you cannot
farm one dungeon exclusively.

### Nightmare Mode

Nightmare is 26 bosses spread across four Layers, each boss playable at
challenge levels 1 through 10. Suggested item level runs from 1,000 at the
easiest Layer I boss. Recommended item levels above 3,800 (Tyrant Tashin 4,050,
Layer IV up to 4,950) are later seasons and are out. Clearing challenge level
10 of a tier unlocks the next tier, and Chapter 1 added Layer IV with the option
to jump straight to stage 10 and sweep all lower first-clear rewards. Ranks run
from Iron to Challenger 1 and pay out at season end.

The ranking has a counter-intuitive shape: clearing the hardest boss is worth
fewer points than repeatedly clearing challenge level 10 of easier bosses, so
rank pushers farm Gatekeeper Pinopi, Furious Feruk and Fortress Guardian Notun
rather than the Layer capstone. See [[Nightmare Mode]].

### Sanctuary raids

| Raid | KR name | Entry | Players | Season 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abyssal Forge: Ludra | 심연의 재련: 루드라 | ~2,700 | 8 at launch | **In** |
| Corroded Decontamination Facility | 침식의 정화소 | 3,500 (was 3,700) | later 10 | **Out** (Season 2). Below 3,800 as a number; not in the launch raid roster |
| Chalice of Muspel | 무스펠의 성배 | 4,500 | 10 | **Out.** Above the 3,800 bar |

Aliases matter here. "Ludra" and "Rudra" are the same raid; the Korean 루드라 is
transliterated both ways, and English sources also call it Sanctuary Ludra.
"Erosion 2" is community shorthand for the Corroded Decontamination Facility.
The 3,500 item level figure the community circulates is specifically that raid's
entry after the reduction from 3,700, not a general Sanctuary requirement.

Chapter 1 rewrote Sanctuary economics: the final-boss kill requirement for
rewards dropped from 2 to 1 with rewards doubled, the time limit went to two
hours, mid-boss rewards were removed so all loot now comes from the Odyle cube
on the final kill, and the Odyle cost per reward doubled from 40 to 80.

### Loot rules

| Loot source | Binding | Tradable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct boss drops | Unbound on drop, bound on equip | Yes, until equipped |
| Odyle Energy cube loot | Bound on receipt | No |
| Crafted gear | Player-bound after refinement | Yes before binding |

Once a piece has been through [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]] it cannot be
equipped by another character or listed on the Market, which makes the decision
to bind irreversible. Sell duplicates and unusable Unique drops before you tune
them.

## Unlock and gates

- **Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds:** available from the early campaign in
  Verteron, Altgard, Poeta and Ishalgen. Marked with a question mark on the map.
  They gate campaign progress in the "quest stall" sections, where you charge a
  gauge to 100 percent by clearing them.
- **Expedition:** level 45 and item level 1,000.
- **Transcendence:** level 45; stage 1 is nominally Combat Power 1,200 but
  Korean guides recommend 1,400-1,500.
- **Nightmare:** level 45, item level 1,000 at the bottom of Layer I.
- **Sanctuary:** ~2,700 for Ludra only, 8 players.
- **Season 1 ceiling:** 3,800. There is no launch dungeon whose *door* is 3,800.
- **Chapter 1 content (out):** level 50, Fallen Daeva, Abyssal Horn Den, Sanctuary 2+, Doom. Those guides reuse 3,800 as a *floor*.

## Progression path

1. **Sweep the one-off content.** Every Sealed Dungeon and Stronghold in both
   factions' territories, plus [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]]. A Korean guide
   times this at 5-6 hours of hard play and calls it the most effective Combat
   Power per hour in the game. Chapter 1 added Daeva Express items that instantly
   clear a region's remaining Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds.
2. **Item level 1,000-1,600.** Expedition Exploration for gear and Kinah, Daily
   Dungeon for Enhance Stones, Nightmare Layer I for shop currency.
3. **Item level 1,600-2,200.** Expedition Conquest on the two-star dungeons.
   Vakron Sky Island Conquest is the specific target because it pays Wings of
   the Forest Spirit; the Vakron Unique gear set is a long-lived +10 target.
4. **Item level 2,200-2,700.** Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den Conquest.
   Transcendence stages 6-8 for Heritage Arcana.
5. **Item level 2,700-3,500.** Abyssal Forge: Ludra, then the Corroded
   Decontamination Facility. Transcendence 9-10 for Unique Arcana.
6. **Item level 3,800+.** Chapter 1 content: Citadel of the Fallen Daeva and
   Abyssal Horn Den, then Chalice of Muspel at 4,500.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Conquest over Exploration once you can hold it.** Conquest Expedition at
  Combat Power 1,500 was measured at roughly 4.8 million Kinah per hour against
  about 2 million for solo Exploration.
- **S rank, not clear.** In Transcendence, S rank unlocks the next stage and
  scales rewards. Reset a run if you die during the trash phase rather than
  limping to a B.
- **Farm the highest stage you can S-rank, not the highest you can survive.**
  Korean consensus is that stage 6 S-rank farming beats attempting stage 8.
- **Skip Transcendence stages 1-2 boxes entirely.** They pay Normal-grade
  Arcana and waste Odyle Energy.
- **Solo Exploration where you can.** Monster health scales down for solo play,
  so a well-geared character clears faster alone than in a slow pug. Set the
  room to approval-required to keep others out.
- **Fire Temple at 2,500, not 2,200.** The tooltip is not the social
  requirement.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Entering without energy.** An entry charge with no Odyle Energy behind it
  produces almost nothing. Budget energy first, entries second.
- **Binding gear you should have sold.** [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]] is
  irreversible and delists the item from the Market permanently.
- **Enhancing runes from region quests carelessly.** Runes can be destroyed on
  failed enhancement, unlike most gear.
- **Assuming Sanctuary is a 3,500 gate.** Ludra is 2,700 and Muspel is 4,500.
  The 3,500 number belongs to one specific raid.
- **Enhancing the belt or amulet past Unique.** Korean guides call
  post-Unique-grade belt and amulet enhancement inefficient; put the scrolls
  into the Rare-to-Unique steps instead.
- **Forgetting Strongholds.** The Noble Belt Enhance Scroll has no other source
  in the game data, and every belt rung from +0 to +9 needs one.
- **Chasing Neulmu.** Multiple Korean guides warn against obsessing over
  tradable "Neulmu" gear for option rerolls at the expense of the main ladder.

## Global launch notes

Expect Deus Research Base and Fragmented Arcanis as Transcendence, Nightmare
Layer I, and a Season 1 Expedition subset. **Party size is four for dungeons
and eight for Sanctuary** per NC at gamescom — not the Chapter 1 5/10 change.
Earlier drafts of this note that said five and ten were wrong.

**Launch Scale Test:**

- Conquest floors **700 / 1,400–1,500 / 2,100–2,200** (KR 1,000 / 1,600 /
  2,200). See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]].
- Daily dungeon at start: **enhancement stones only** (Bio Research Base).
- Pity boxes include **accessories and armour**.
- Boss telegraphs register the hit **~24 frames** after the circle fills (KR
  ~8). Perfect-dodge is after the fill, Lost Ark-like.
- Cubes paid **1,000 Soul Crystals** (100% on Conquest cube and keyed Hidden
  Cube). Might be test tuning.
