# Legions

> **In one sentence:** legions in AION 2 are a social and logistics container,
> not a progression system — there is no legion level, no legion skill tree and
> no legion-wide stat buff, and this note exists mostly to establish that
> negative result before you go looking for one.

## Why it matters

Every player arriving from another MMO looks for the same thing first: the guild
level bar, the guild skill tree, the guild buff you get for logging in. AION 2
has **none of these**, and several hours of community searching goes into
rediscovering that fact.

What legions *do* provide is real but different in kind: a 128-person roster, a
rentable physical space, shared storage, and eligibility for content that
individuals cannot access. Treat a legion as infrastructure — it changes what
you can do, not what your character's numbers are.

## How it works

### The negative result, stated precisely

There is an important distinction between *confirmed absence* and *unverified
absence*, and I want to be explicit about which applies to what.

| Feature | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Legion level / experience bar | **Confirmed absent.** Addressed directly in NC's pre-launch broadcast series when asked about guild progression |
| Legion skill tree | **Confirmed absent.** Same source |
| Legion-wide stat buff for members | **Confirmed absent** in the shipped build |
| Legion reputation / contribution ranks | **Unverified absence.** Datamined strings reference it; no shipped feature found |
| Legion Coins as a spendable currency | **Unverified.** Datamined; never confirmed live |
| Legion shop | **Unverified.** Datamined; never confirmed live |
| Legion occupation battles / legion-vs-legion PvP | **Unverified.** Datamined; NC publicly characterised related claims as rumour |

The "unverified" rows all come from the same place: a datamining post that
pulled client strings referencing legion levels, decorations with passive
effects, legion coins, a legion shop, legion storage, occupation battles and
legion reputation. **Legion storage from that list did ship.** The others did
not, and NC subsequently addressed circulating claims from that datamine as
rumour rather than roadmap.

That is a genuinely ambiguous situation and it should be recorded as such:
strings in a client are evidence that something was *built or considered*, not
evidence that it exists for players. Do not plan around Legion Coins.

### What does exist

**Roster and membership.** The member cap is **128**. Sources conflict on the
launch value — a lower cap around 20 appears in early material — and the 128
figure is tied to an official Season 2 increase. The safe reading is that the
cap was raised to 128 during Korea's Season 2 and that 128 is the current
Korean number. **Confidence medium on which value a given build uses.**

**Legion Hideouts (레기온 아지트).** A rentable legion space, taken on a
**30-day term** and rented **per region**. Chapter 1 changed the rental
bookkeeping so that the **Eltnen and Morheim hideouts share their rental
state** — renting in one is reflected in the other rather than being two
independent leases. New hideout locations were added alongside new regions.

Inside the hideout there is a **training dummy** for damage testing, which is
the main practical reason non-officers visit.

**The Legion airship (레기온 비공정).** AION 2's legion space is airship-themed,
and Korean sources use 비공정 (airship) and 아지트 (hideout) in overlapping
contexts. I could not cleanly establish whether the airship *is* the hideout or
is a separate rentable asset. **Unverified** — flagged in Open questions.

**Legion storage.** Shared storage with deposit, withdrawal and expansion,
confirmed shipped. This is the single most practically useful legion feature and
the one that matters for [[Material Conversion]] and gearing logistics.

**Legion activity records.** A log of member activity — joins, contributions,
content participation — used by officers for roster management. It feeds
nothing mechanical.

**Upkeep.** Hideout rental is a recurring Kinah cost on the 30-day cycle. I
found a figure of roughly **1,000,000 Kinah per month** for hideout rental and
**100,000 Kinah** to found a legion at around character level 5, but **both of
these come only from shop and boosting-site content (u4n and similar) and are
uncorroborated by any Korean primary source. Treat as low confidence.**

### Legion-gated content

The legion's mechanical relevance is access, not power:

- Sieges and fortress content organise around legions; see
  [[Arenas Sieges and Seasons]] and [[Abyss Points and Ranks]].
- Large-scale Abyss objectives — boss kills, artifact captures — are practically
  legion-coordinated even where the reward is individual. Note that Chapter 1
  moved those rewards to **per-class contribution ranking**, which means the
  legion organises the effort but the reward lands on the individual. See
  [[Trials and Rankings]].
- Endgame raid content in [[Dungeons and Raids]] assumes a standing roster.

### Roadmap

Two announced systems attach to legions and neither has shipped:

- **Sanctum housing.** Announced as a housing system; the legion-facing portion
  would give legions a presence in the capital. Announced, not delivered.
- **Sailing.** Explicitly described as content being built for legions rather
  than individuals. Announced, not delivered.

Both are worth tracking because they would be the first time legion membership
gates content that a well-organised pickup group could not substitute for.

### A naming warning

**AION2 Hub translates 봉인 던전 (Sealed Dungeon) as "hideout" in its levelling
guides.** That is a different system entirely from the Legion Hideout
(레기온 아지트). If you search English sources for "AION 2 hideout" you will get
solo Sealed Dungeon content, not legion housing. Always search with the "legion"
qualifier, or search the Korean term. See [[Glossary KR TW EN]].

## Unlock and gates

- Joining a legion: available from early levels, no gate beyond an invitation.
- Founding a legion: a low character level and a Kinah fee. Reported as level 5
  and 100,000 Kinah, **low confidence — SEO source only.**
- Hideout: rented per region on a 30-day term for a recurring Kinah cost.
- Legion storage expansion: Kinah.

## Progression path

There is no legion progression path, and that is the point of this note. What
there is instead is a checklist:

1. **Join one early** for the social and logistics benefits, not for stats.
2. **Once your legion is stable, rent a hideout** — the training dummy alone
   justifies it for a raiding roster.
3. **Set storage permissions carefully.** Shared storage is the feature with the
   most potential for loss.
4. **Track the 30-day rental clock.** Missing renewal costs you the space.
5. **Organise around siege and Abyss schedules,** since that is where legion
   membership converts into individual reward. See [[Weekly Routine Main]].

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **128 members** — the roster ceiling. Large communities run multiple legions
  and coordinate externally.
- **30 days** — the hideout rental cycle.
- **Chapter 1** — the point at which Eltnen and Morheim hideout rentals became
  linked rather than separate.
- There is **no power breakpoint**, because there is no legion power.

## Costs and math

The honest version of this section is short, because the numbers are not well
sourced.

| Cost | Reported value | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legion founding | ~100,000 Kinah at ~level 5 | **Low** — SEO shop content only |
| Hideout rental | ~1,000,000 Kinah per 30 days | **Low** — SEO shop content only |
| Storage expansion | Kinah, tiered | Confirmed to exist, values unknown |

Against a Kinah economy where enhancement attempts run into the hundreds of
thousands ([[Currencies and Economy]]), a million per month for a hideout is a
real but manageable cost for an active roster and an unjustifiable one for a
small legion that will not use the training dummy.

I would not plan a legion budget on these numbers without checking them in
client.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Looking for the legion level bar.** There isn't one. Confirmed absent, not
  hidden behind a gate.
- **Joining a legion for the buff.** There is no member buff.
- **Planning around Legion Coins.** Datamined strings only; NC treated the
  broader claim set from that datamine as rumour.
- **Searching English sources for "hideout".** You will find Sealed Dungeons.
  Use "legion hideout" or 레기온 아지트.
- **Assuming the 20-member cap from early material.** The current Korean cap is
  128.
- **Letting the 30-day rental lapse.** The Chapter 1 change linked Eltnen and
  Morheim rentals, which helps, but the clock still runs.
- **Treating shop-site legion numbers as fact.** The founding and rental costs
  in circulation trace back to boosting sites, not to patch notes.
- **Expecting Sailing or Sanctum housing at launch.** Both are announced, both
  are unshipped.

## Global launch notes

Legions are launch-day core social infrastructure, so most of this ships. The
uncertainty is about the *numbers*, not the existence.

| Element | Season 1 global player has it? |
| --- | --- |
| Legions as a social system | **Yes.** Core, available from early levels |
| Legion level, skill tree or member buff | **No** — and not later either. This is a design absence, not a roadmap item |
| 128-member cap | **Uncertain.** 128 came from a Korean Season 2 increase; a Season 1 global build may ship the lower original cap |
| Legion Hideouts with a training dummy | **Likely yes**, in the launch regions |
| Chapter 1 linked Eltnen/Morheim rental state | **No.** Chapter 1 change; expect independent per-region rentals |
| Legion storage | **Likely yes** — it shipped in Korea before Chapter 1 |
| Legion activity records | **Likely yes** |
| Legion Coins, legion shop, legion reputation | **No.** Never confirmed to exist in any region |
| Legion-organised sieges and Abyss objectives | **Yes**, as part of the PvP layer |
| Sanctum housing | **No.** Roadmap in Korea, unshipped |
| Sailing as legion content | **No.** Roadmap in Korea, unshipped |

See [[Global Launch Differences]].
