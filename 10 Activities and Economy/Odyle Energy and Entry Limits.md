# Odyle Energy and Entry Limits

> **In one sentence:** an entry charge lets you into a dungeon, but Odyle Energy
> is what turns a clear into loot, and because energy regenerates on a fixed
> clock it — not your free time — is the real ceiling on PvE progression.

## Why it matters

Every reward cube in Expedition, Transcendence and Sanctuary is paid for in
Odyle Energy (오드 에너지). Clear the final boss with an empty pool and you get
the achievement, the story and nothing else. Passive regeneration is fixed, it
stops dead at the cap, and unspent capacity is destroyed rather than banked.

That makes Odyle Energy the one resource in AION 2 that punishes both hoarding
and neglect. Five separate strands of Korean community analysis reach the same
conclusion: the number of dungeons you can profitably run per week is set by
your energy budget, and every entry-count number the UI shows you is a ceiling
you will never reach unless you buy energy with Kinah.

See [[Dungeons and Raids]] for what each rung of the ladder pays, and
[[Daily and Weekly Loops]], [[Daily Routine Main]] and [[Weekly Routine Main]]
for where energy spending sits in the routine.

## How it works

### The name, and the thing it is not

The client term is **Odyle Energy**. Both English-facing databases — questlog.gg
and AION2 Hub — render 오드 에너지 as "Odyle Energy" and use "Odyle" for the raw
material, so that is the spelling this vault uses.

| Alias in circulation | Where it comes from | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Odyle Energy | questlog.gg, AION2 Hub, expcarry | **Correct.** Client spelling. |
| Odd / Ode / Ode Energy | Loose transliterations of 오드 | Same thing |
| Odel / Odell Energy | u4n and similar shop blogs | Same thing, non-client |
| Aetherine / Aetherine Energy | One SEO dungeon guide only | Same thing; no source outside shop content uses it |
| Grace of Odyle | u4n; not an item | Marketing name for the membership perk, **not a real system** |

The important disambiguation is that **Odyle (오드) and Odyle Energy
(오드 에너지) are different resources**. Odyle is a gathering material: it drops
from gathering nodes, appears on the ground after Conquest Expedition clears,
and is consumed by ordinary crafting — a Dark Dragon's Beef Wellington recipe
eats one Odyle. Odyle Energy is the reward-cube resource and is not gathered at
all. Korean guides that say "Odyle is easy to find in the air" are talking about
the material, not the energy. See [[Odyle and Special Resources]] for the
material itself and [[Glossary KR TW EN]] for the naming.

### Regeneration and the cap

| | No membership | With membership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passive regeneration | 10 per 3 hours | 15 per 3 hours |
| Maximum held | 560 | 840 |
| Passive per week | 560 | 840 |

The base 560/10-per-3-hours pair and the membership 840/15 pair both come from
Korean coverage of the Chengarung (now Kwairing Premium) membership. Note that
the weekly passive yield and the cap are the same number in both cases, which is
a deliberate design: **a full pool means you are exactly one week of
regeneration away from having destroyed a week of regeneration.**

Be careful with English sources here. NC's own AI-generated Purple Lounge and
Inven posts state "15 every 3 hours (max 840)" without saying that is the
subscriber rate, and at least one shop blog repeats 15/3h as the base figure.
Treat 10/3h and 560 as the free-player numbers — **confidence medium**, because
no post-Chapter-1 patch note restates them.

### What it is spent on

Odyle Energy is consumed at one moment only: opening the reward cube that spawns
on a final-boss kill. It is never spent on entry, travel, or anything else.

| Content | Odyle per reward cube | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expedition Exploration | 40 | Chapter 1 note, Citadel of the Fallen Daeva |
| Expedition Conquest (Normal) | 40 | Chapter 1 note |
| Expedition Conquest (Hard) | 60 | Chapter 1 note |
| Transcendence | 40 (typical) | Community consensus, not restated in a patch note |
| Sanctuary | 80 — raised from 40 on 2026-07-01 | Chapter 1 note, explicit |

One Korean weekly-economy analysis on Inven models **every** cube open at 80
energy and one bound Odyle Energy item at 40. Its arithmetic only balances at
those values (560 passive + 47 items × 40 = 2,440, ÷ 80 ≈ 30.5 cube opens, which
is the "about 30" it quotes for a single character). Either that analysis is
Sanctuary-focused or cube costs have drifted upward past what the patch notes
record. **Confidence medium on 40 as the universal Expedition figure**; budget
with 40/60/80 by content but do not be surprised by a higher live number.

Two other rules attach to the cube:

- **Contribution floor.** You need a minimum share of the damage and healing to
  be offered a cube at all. Chapter 1 lowered this from 3 percent to 1 percent,
  which is what made low-geared alts viable in carried runs.
- **Boss drop.** Since Chapter 1, an Expedition Conquest boss kill drops Odyle
  Energy for the party: 15 total across five players. It partly refunds the run.

### Faucets

| Source | Yield | Limit | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Passive regeneration | 10 per 3 hours (15 with membership) | Caps at 560 / 840 | medium |
| Material Conversion craft | 40 per craft | Was 7 per character per week; raised to **20 per server per week** on 2026-04-08, and the per-character 4-craft blueprint was deleted | high |
| Wind Breeze Trading Company (membership) | 40 per item | 100,000 Kinah each, **21 per server per week** since 2026-04-08 | high |
| Daily Mission Quests (사명) | Random chance of Odyle Energy | 5 mission quests per day | medium |
| Order scrolls (지령서) | Small Odyle Energy | 12 per week | medium |
| Sprout Badge companion payout | Small Odyle Energy | 14 per server per week, max 2 per run | high |
| Daeva Pass / battle pass premium track | Bundled Odyle Energy | Per pass | high |
| Attendance calendars and events | Bundled | Per event | high |
| Season weekly reward track | Odyle Energy from the 8,000-point tier | Weekly | medium |
| Starter and growth packs | e.g. 5 Odyle Energy in the 1,500-Quna "Paradise" pack | Once per character | high |

The Material Conversion recipe is 25 Odyle + 5 High-Purity Odyle + 1 Pure Odyle
(Bound) + 50,000 Kinah for 40 Odyle Energy. All three inputs are gathered or
drop from Conquest clears, so the real cost is the Kinah and the weekly count.
See [[Material Conversion]] and [[Professions and Mastery]].

Note the shape of the 2026-04-08 change: conversion and shop purchases moved
from **per character** to **per server**. Before, a stable of alts multiplied
your purchasable energy; after, 20 crafts and 21 purchases are all your account
gets on that server no matter how many characters you run. This is the single
biggest nerf to multi-boxing in the game's history and it is why the Korean
eight-character economy analyses read as bitterly as they do. See
[[Alts and Account Wide Progression]].

### The Sprout Badge

Chapter 1 added a Sprout Badge (새싹 뱃지) for new and returning players,
activated through the "New Ascension" growth support plus Kwairing Premium.
The veteran-facing half of it is an Odyle Energy faucet:

- Clearing a target Expedition, Transcendence or Sanctuary alongside a Sprout
  user grants the normal players **Small Odyle Energy (Bound)**.
- Capped at **14 times per week per server**, decremented by cube-open count —
  open two cubes, spend two of the fourteen.
- Only one player per party receives it, and a party with several Sprouts still
  only pays out twice per run.
- The June showcase described the payout as **50 percent of the Odyle Energy the
  Sprout user spent**, which is the mechanism behind the "Small" item.
- Sprout status ends at item level 5,000 or Combat Power 600K.

There is a known exploit-shaped hole: the payout only fires when the Sprout
actually opens their cube. A Sprout who leaves without collecting burns the
veteran's non-collection count and pays nothing.

### Entry counts are not reward counts

This is the distinction that trips up every guide written before July 2026.

**Before Chapter 1**, three separate counters existed: entry charges, weekly
reward-charge counts (Expedition 21 → 14, Transcendence 14 → 7 after
2026-04-08), and a weekly cumulative-play band that scaled your Kinah payout
down as you ran more.

| Weekly cumulative runs | Kinah rate (post-2026-04-08) |
| --- | --- |
| Conquest ≤ 63 / Transcendence ≤ 42 | 100% |
| ≤ 77 / ≤ 49 | 80% |
| ≤ 91 / ≤ 56 | 60% |
| ≤ 105 / ≤ 63 | 40% |
| 106+ / 64+ | 20% |

Those bands were announced at 48/34 in the 7 April broadcast and softened to
63/42 after community pushback — a useful reminder that published numbers move.

**Chapter 1 (2026-07-01) replaced the entry model entirely.** The patch note is
unambiguous:

- Reward-count and final-boss-kill caps are **removed** from Expedition and
  Transcendence.
- Instead, **leaving a cleared dungeon without collecting the reward decrements
  a weekly count.** At zero you can no longer enter. It resets weekly.
- **The count is shared across every character on the same server.**
- Challenge Tickets and Recharge Tickets were **deleted** from inventories and
  warehouses.

So the current model is: enter as often as you like, but every clear you walk
away from without paying energy costs you a shared server-wide charge. Sanctuary
keeps its own structure — final-boss-kill count rather than an entry cap, one
kill for doubled rewards since Chapter 1, two-hour timer, all loot on the final
cube.

The practical consequence is that **your entry budget is now identical to your
energy budget**, because there is no longer any reason to enter a dungeon you
cannot afford to loot.

## Unlock and gates

- Odyle Energy starts accumulating from level 45 and the first Expedition, item
  level 1,000. There is no unlock quest.
- Material Conversion of Odyle Energy needs crafting access; see
  [[Professions and Mastery]].
- The Wind Breeze special tab needs an active Membership; see
  [[Currencies and Economy]].
- The Sprout companion payout needs a Sprout-badged player in the party.

## Progression path

1. **Levelling.** Ignore it. You will not out-earn regeneration.
2. **First max-level week.** Spend on Expedition Conquest at the highest tier
   you clear reliably. Do not touch Exploration cubes once Conquest is open —
   same 40 energy, worse loot.
3. **Once you subscribe.** Buy all 21 Wind Breeze Odyle Energy items and run all
   20 conversion crafts. That is 1,640 extra energy per week for 2.1 million
   plus 1 million Kinah — roughly 20 to 40 extra cubes, and by a wide margin the
   best Kinah-to-power conversion in the game.
4. **Item level 2,700+.** Sanctuary cubes cost 80 but pay Heroic bracelets and
   top-tier weapon materials. Reserve energy for them before the weekly reset.
5. **Item level 4,500+.** Chalice of Muspel and Conquest Hard at 60 become the
   default sink, and energy stops being sufficient for full participation
   without buying every purchasable point.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Never sit at cap.** A full pool for 24 hours destroys 80 energy — one to two
  cubes. Over a week that is a full Sanctuary lockout's worth of loot.
- **Never enter without energy.** Post-Chapter 1 this also burns a
  server-shared non-collection count, so it costs your alts as well as you.
- **Spend downward when you must.** If you are about to cap and cannot form a
  group, an Exploration or low Conquest cube at 40 is worth more than 40 energy
  evaporating. Getting something is better than getting nothing.
- **Buy the Wind Breeze stock first thing on Wednesday.** It is per server per
  week and it is the most-missed purchase in the game.
- **Do the conversion crafts before the reset,** not after. Korean guides have
  repeated this warning since November 2025 and it is still the single most
  common weekly loss.
- **Check the pool before the last boss, not after.** The cube appears on the
  kill; topping up mid-fight is not possible in a timed raid.

## Costs and math

### The weekly claim budget

This is the number that actually governs your week.

| Scenario | Weekly energy | Cubes at 40 | Cubes at 60 | Cubes at 80 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free player, passive only | 560 | 14 | 9 | 7 |
| Free player + 20 conversion crafts | 1,360 | 34 | 22 | 17 |
| Member, passive + 20 crafts + 21 purchases | 840 + 800 + 840 = 2,480 | 62 | 41 | 31 |

Add roughly 100 to 300 more from missions, orders, passes and Sprout payouts
depending on how completionist you are.

The comparison that matters: the pre-Chapter-1 UI offered 14 Expedition and 7
Transcendence reward charges per character per week, and the cumulative-play
bands stayed at 100 percent Kinah out to 63 Conquest runs. **A free player could
never reach either number.** At 560 passive energy they could claim 14 cubes
total across all content. That gap is the entire argument for the Membership,
and it is why Korean players describe Odyle Energy rather than time as the
paywall.

### Kinah cost per point of energy

| Route | Kinah per 40 energy | Kinah per point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material Conversion | 50,000 | 1,250 |
| Wind Breeze special tab | 100,000 | 2,500 |
| Event conversion (2026-06-04, unbound Kinah) | 300,000 | 7,500 |

Conversion is twice as efficient as the shop, so exhaust the 20 crafts before
spending on the 21 purchases. A Korean tip post from 2026-07-22 says exactly
this: "buy this week's Odyle, Daily Dungeon and instant-complete tickets from
Material Conversion, not the shop."

## Traps and mistakes

- **Treating energy as a currency.** It is not tradable, not bankable past the
  cap, and does not appear in the wallet UI. It lives at Menu → Content →
  Expedition, top right, with a charge timer behind the (i) button.
- **Confusing Odyle with Odyle Energy.** Selling your gathered Odyle because you
  "have plenty of energy" destroys your conversion recipe inputs.
- **Believing 15 per 3 hours is the subscriber rate.** That was true in KR.
  **Launch Scale Test: 15 / 3 hours is the base rate for everyone**; membership
  only raises the cap.
- **Planning around entry counts.** Post-Chapter 1 the only counter that binds a
  normal player is energy; the non-collection count only punishes wasted clears.
- **Forgetting that conversion and shop limits are per server.** Alts no longer
  multiply energy purchases.
- **Carrying a Sprout who leaves before opening their cube.** You lose the
  companion payout and a non-collection charge.
- **Running Exploration for energy efficiency.** It costs the same 40 as
  Conquest Normal and pays materially less.

## Global launch notes

Odyle Energy is confirmed for global. Membership still raises **capacity**.

**Launch Scale Test, 2026-09-17/18:** regeneration is **15 every 3 hours for
free players and subscribers.** The only membership difference Welps could see
was a **higher cap** (you can log off longer without wasting regen). KR
free-to-play was **10 / 3 hours**. Extra energy from the test carried a
duration of **2026-12-16**, which is also the working Season 1 end date.

Cube cost (40 vs 80), the exact cap, Conquest Hard at 60, and per-server
purchase limits remain unverified — the test capped at 37 and disabled the
shop. Do not keep using 10/3h in routines. See [[Global Launch Differences]].
