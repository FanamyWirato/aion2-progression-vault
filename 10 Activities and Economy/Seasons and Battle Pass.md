# Seasons and Battle Pass

> **In one sentence:** AION 2 runs four parallel seasonal clocks — main season,
> attendance calendars, arena season and battle pass — and each one issues its
> own expiring currency, so the discipline is spending before the wipe rather
> than earning more.

## Why it matters

Season structure decides two things that matter to a planner. First, it sets the
expiry date on a large slice of your inventory: challenge tickets, Oath Coins,
Odyle Energy issued as a season item, Shugo Festa keys and Abyss Rift Stones all
carry season-limited validity in Korea, and a player who does not read the
season transition notice loses them. Second, since Season 3 the seasonal reward
philosophy changed: season ranking rewards became honour items rather than stat
items, which means seasons are no longer a power treadmill you must ride to stay
competitive.

For a global launch player the calendar is also the best available predictor of
what the first six months look like, because the Korean cadence is documented in
full.

## How it works

### Four parallel tracks

| Track | KR name | Typical length | What it issues |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main Season | 시즌 | 2-4 months in the mature game | Season Missions, ranking for Nightmare, Transcendence, Ascension Trial, Abyss, Arena of Solitude, Arena of Cooperation; Oath Coins; the Season Shop |
| Attendance Calendar | 출석 | 7, 14 or 28 day check-ins, several running at once | Consumables, Odyle Energy, Hidden Cube Keys, challenge tickets, Soul Codex resets, cosmetic headwear |
| Arena of Tactics | 전술 투기장 | Matches the main season | PvP ranking rewards, see [[Arenas Sieges and Seasons]] |
| Battle Pass (Daeva Pass) | 데바 패스 | 3 to 14 weeks, several concurrent | Consumables, upgrade materials, currencies, cosmetics, Deva Pass Shop Coins |

The main season is the anchor. Attendance calendars and battle passes are
layered on top of it, often three or four at a time with different end dates.

### The Korean and Taiwanese calendar

Korea ran eleven short "seasons" during the pre-launch and soft-launch period in
August to November 2025, some as short as a single day. The real cadence starts
with the 2025-11-19 launch.

| Main season | Active window | Reward claim window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Season 1 | 2025-11-18 to 2026-01-21 | to 2026-04-08 |
| Season 2 | 2026-01-21 to 2026-04-08 | to 2026-07-01 |
| Season 3 | 2026-04-08 to 2026-07-01 | to 2026-11-04 |
| Season 4 | 2026-07-01 to 2026-11-04 | to 2027-01-27 |

Season 4 is live as of 2026-09-06 and runs alongside the Chapter 1 content
update.
The pattern is roughly a ten-week season lengthening to about eighteen weeks by
Season 4, with a reward-claim tail that overlaps the following season.

### Season shops and mission currencies

| Shop | Currency | Behaviour at season change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Season Shop | Oath Coin (맹세의 주화), issued per season | Shop is deleted and replaced; the new season issues a new Oath Coin and the old one becomes unusable |
| Pass Shop | Deva Pass Shop Coin (데바 패스 상점 주화), per season | Old season's Pass Shop stays open for a grace period, roughly two extra weeks |
| Nightmare Exchange Shop | Phantasmal Fragment (몽환의 파편) | Persists across seasons with some stock rotation; purchase history is not reset |
| Abyss Exchange Shop | Abyss Points | Persists across seasons |
| Festa Shop | Centuryroot Token (백년삼 증표) | Persists across seasons |

### What resets and what carries over

| Item | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Oath Coins | Expire at the season transition; Season Shop is deleted |
| Deva Pass Shop Coins | Expire about two weeks after the season transition |
| Expedition, Transcendence, Ascension Trial and Nightmare challenge tickets | Season-issued versions expire at the transition; new ones issued |
| Season-issued Odyle Energy, Shugo Festa keys, Abyss Rift Stones, Unknown Fissure tickets | Expire at the transition. Rift Stones obtained in the Abyss or from the Market have no expiry |
| Abyss Points | Persist |
| Phantasmal Fragments and Centuryroot Tokens | Persist |
| Season ranking titles | Time-limited |
| Season ranking Pantheon decorations | Season 1 decorations expired at the end of Season 2. From Chapter 1 onward, past-season decorations keep their appearance but lose their stat effect |
| Subjugation Battle challenge tickets | Deleted outright at the 2026-04-08 maintenance |

The Pantheon decoration change is the most important one. From Season 3 onward,
NC deliberately changed season rewards from stat items to honour items, and
Chapter 1 stripped the stats from older season decorations retroactively. See
[[Pantheon Statues and Frames]].

## Unlock and gates

- Season Missions and season ranking require level 45.
- Season ranking from Season 3 is calculated per class rather than server-wide
  for Transcendence and the Abyss, which widens the reward distribution.
- Some passes are level 45+ purchase only, notably Hero's Invitation.
- Attendance calendars require nothing beyond logging in.

## Inputs: currencies, materials, sources

| Currency | Source | Sink |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Season Points | Abyss, Arena, Transcendence, Nightmare and challenge content | Season weekly reward tiers; a Korean guide flags tier 3 for Resurrection Spirit Stones and the 8,000-point tier for Odyle Energy |
| Oath Coin | Season Missions and seasonal-mode rank rewards | Season Shop: Mysterious Crystals for Arcana refinement, refining stones, crafting materials |

| Quna | Real money or the Kinah-Quna Exchange | Premium pass tracks, level skips, cosmetics |

An Oath Coin Box in the Season 3 shop cost 2 Quna for 100,000 Oath Coins with a
limit of four per server, which is the cheapest bulk route into the Season Shop
if you already hold Quna.

## Progression path

1. **Log in for attendance first.** It costs nothing and several calendars run
   at once. The milestone days at 7, 14, 21 and 28 hold the Soul Codex resets,
   challenge tickets and Odyle Energy.
2. **Activate the free tracks of every pass you qualify for.** On passes where
   the standard track costs 2,000,000 Kinah, weigh that against your Kinah
   income; the returns are consumables and materials, not power.
3. **Bank season points through the normal loop.** You do not need to play
   season content specifically; [[Daily and Weekly Loops]] generates the points
   as a by-product.
4. **Spend Oath Coins on Mysterious Crystals early if your Arcana is behind.**
   The Season Shop is the only reliable bulk source and it is deleted at the
   transition.
5. **Two weeks before the transition, run an inventory audit.** Spend Oath
   Coins, burn season-issued tickets, and buy anything in the Pass Shop.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- Attendance calendars are the highest return per minute of anything in this
  chapter, because the cost is a login.
- Season weekly point tier 3 and the 8,000-point tier are the two thresholds
  worth planning around.
- The premium track is worth buying only if you will finish it. The weekly
  experience cap means a pass with a 50,000 cap and a fourteen-week window
  cannot be rushed at the end; late buyers pay the level-skip price instead.
- Level skips at 50 Quna per rank are the wrong way to finish a pass. Compare
  against simply not buying it.
- Season ranking is no longer a stat race after Season 3, so climbing it is a
  cosmetic and title goal.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Missing the subscribe-by date.** It closes days or weeks before the pass
  window ends, and there is no grace period.
- **Sitting on Oath Coins.** The Season Shop is deleted, not migrated. Korean
  community guides run a "spend your Season 1 currency" reminder before every
  transition for exactly this reason.
- **Assuming Pantheon season rewards are permanent power.** They were stat items
  in Season 1 and 2, they expired, and Chapter 1 removed the stats from the
  survivors.
- **Buying a premium pass with a low weekly experience cap late in its window.**
  The cap makes catching up impossible without paying for level skips.
- **Confusing the season number across tracks.** aion2hub's calendar shows
  "Season 1 Battle Pass, Season 4 Attendance Calendar 2, Season 3 Main Season"
  concurrently. The numbers are per track, not global.

## Global launch notes

Global launch is 2026-10-05, with Founder's Pack early access from 2026-09-30
and a Launch Scale Test on 2026-09-17 and 18. The global Season 1 has not been
detailed, but three things are already known from NC's own notices. Battle
passes will be called Daeva Passes and sold per character with a free Standard
Track and a Quna-priced Premium Track. NC's own wording describes pass contents
as "a combination of cosmetics, items, upgrade materials, and other in-game
currencies", which is deliberately broader than cosmetics-only and has not been
clarified against the Consumables section's explicit disclaimer about gear
progression. All three Founder's Pack tiers include a 30-day Special Quai
Membership, which means every founder starts with the Market and Exchange
unlocked.

Reading the Korean calendar as a predictor: expect a launch season of roughly
ten weeks with a reward-claim tail. **Launch Scale Test:** extra Odyle Energy
carried a duration of **2026-12-16**, which Welps reads as Season 1 lasting
**about two and a half months** from October 5 — similar to KR Season 1. That
is a tooltip, not an NC calendar. See [[Odyle Energy and Entry Limits]] and
[[Global Launch Differences]].
