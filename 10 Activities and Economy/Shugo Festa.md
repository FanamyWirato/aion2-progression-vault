# Shugo Festa

> **In one sentence:** an always-on hourly minigame that pays a dedicated shop
> currency, and the shop is one of the densest progression sinks in the game —
> Skill Points, Daevanion Crystals, Soul Books, Pantheon frame tools, Unique
> wings and a trickle of Abyss Points.

> **Season 1 verdict:** a launch player **will see this**. It was in the Korean
> client on day one (Inven coverage 2025-11-19). Plan around the **launch key
> model** (daily recharge, bank to 28), not the Season 3 weekly-per-server
> 7 / 14 model. Buy Wisdom Stones and generic Daevanion Crystals first; treat
> skins and pets as leftover spend.

## Why it matters

Three other notes in this chapter name Shugo Festa as a source and had nowhere
to send the reader. That is not flavour. The Festa shop is one of the few
places a new character can convert five-minute games into [[Skills and Mastery Points|Skill Points]],
[[Daevanion Boards]] crystals, [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding|Soul Books]] and
[[Pantheon Statues and Frames|Pantheon]] frame tools without touching Odyle
Energy or the Abyss.

It is also easy to underrate. The minigames look like events. They are not
limited-time festivals. They fire every hour, they have fired since Korean
launch, and the shop stock is permanent progression rather than a seasonal
catalogue. Skipping them is the same class of mistake as skipping consumables:
cheap stats left on the table because the content does not look like a dungeon.

### Structure — minigames, keys, cubes, shop

Four moving parts, in order:

1. **An hourly window** opens a rotating subset of the minigame roster. A Shugo
   icon appears next to the minimap. You pick a game, teleport, and talk to the
   NPC to enter. Entry itself does **not** cost a key.
2. **A three-to-five-minute minigame** scores you against the other players in
   the instance. Higher rank unlocks more cube selections.
3. **Monster cubes** (몬스터 큐브). After the game you may spend **Shugo Festa
   Reward Keys** (슈고 페스타 보상 열쇠) to pick cards. Rank sets how many
   picks you are offered; keys set how many you can actually take. You can
   walk away without picking — the key is not consumed. Walking away on a bad
   rank is the correct play.
4. **The Festa shop**, paid in **Centuryroot Tokens** (백년삼 증표; TW 百年蔘標誌).
   Tokens drop from the cubes. The shop is the reason the content exists.

There is no board, no season ladder and no entry ticket separate from the
reward key. "Board" in some English guides is a mistranslation of the shop
or the minigame picker.

## Unlock and gates

- No level or item-level gate worth planning around. [[Daily and Weekly Loops]]
  lists it as opening at or before 45, and launch press treated it as
  something you do on the way to max level.
- The only real gate is **keys to claim cubes**. You can enter every window
  for practice; you only spend a key when you take a card.
- Season 3 added a **100 contribution** floor on Shugo Merchant Protect (and
  Dimensional Invasion). Below 100 you get nothing. That rule is a 2026-04-08
  change; *verify at launch* whether the global client ships it.

**Resolution for this vault.** Account C is the patch-note winner for live
Korea after 2026-04-08. Account A is the winner for a Season 1 global
player, because the 7 / 14 weekly-per-server change is a Season 3 systems
change, not launch content. Account B is recorded so English guides that
quote it are not treated as a fourth system.

Membership doubled keys at launch (Chengarung) and still doubles them after
Season 3 (7 → 14). Season-issued keys expire at a season transition; keys
bought or dropped in the live economy do not. See [[Seasons and Battle Pass]]
and [[Currencies and Economy]].

### Cube payouts (not the shop)

These come from spending keys on monster cubes. They are **uncapped against
the weekly Abyss Point hunting / PvP caps**.

| Reward | Role | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Centuryroot Tokens | Shop currency | high |
| Abyss Points | ~100–300 per claimed event in the launch-era tables; one aggregator claims 200–4,000. Use 100–300 | medium on the number, high that AP is in the cube |
| Unique-grade gear | About **5 percent** of cube lines, per launch guides | medium |
| Manastones, Spirit Stones, Refining Stones, Odyle gathers, generic gear | Filler | high |
| Artwork / painting fragments | Pantheon input, per-minigame | medium |

Dimensional Invasion is the sister hourly event. It pays a similar cube and
**does not** drop painting fragments. Do not substitute it when you are
farming [[Pantheon Statues and Frames]].

## Per character, per server, per account — and expiry

| Thing | Scope | Expires? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reward keys, launch model | Per character, daily recharge, bank 28 | Season-issued keys expire at the season change. Ordinary keys do not |
| Reward keys, Season 3 model | **Per server**, 7 / 14 per week. Engraved refill keys sum across characters | Same season-key rule |
| Centuryroot Tokens | Persist. [[Currencies and Economy]] flags them as a standing currency | No |
| Shop lifetime stock (Wisdom Stones, Daevanion Crystals) | Per character | No — once bought, that character is done |
| Soul Books | Per character, weekly 3 | The weekly count resets; unbought stock is lost |
| Cube Unique gear and AP | Per claim, no weekly AP-cap interaction | Gear is yours; AP does not expire |

Alts do not share Centuryroot Tokens or lifetime shop stock. Under the
Season 3 key model they **do** share the weekly key pool, which is why
[[Alts and Account Wide Progression]] treats Festa as a reason not to
multibox the minigames on every character after April 2026. Under the
launch model each character has its own daily keys, so an alt is a second
Wisdom-Stone tap.

## Progression path

1. **Open the picker the first time a Shugo icon appears** and learn one
   easy game (Merchant Protect or Jump Jump). Do not spend a key until you
   can finish in the top half.
2. **Spend keys on good ranks only.** A last-place claim wastes a charge
   that would have been a Wisdom Stone later.
3. **Dump early tokens into Wisdom Stones and generic Daevanion Crystals**
   while the price is still at the first tier.
4. **Buy the weekly three Soul Books every week** once you can spare the
   tokens. The count does not bank.
5. **Add frame tools** when you are actually placing Pantheon artworks,
   not before. The 90-tool figure is an endgame Pantheon number.
6. **Wings next** for collection stats, then pets, then skins.

## Priorities and breakpoints

Season 1 buy-first versus ignore:

| Priority | Buy | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buy first** | Wisdom Stones, while cheap | Finite Skill Points that otherwise come from Monolith resonance |
| **Buy first** | Generic Daevanion Crystals, while cheap | Feeds Nezekan / Zikel / Vaizel / Triniel. Does **not** feed Ariel or Azphel |
| **Buy every week** | Soul Books ×3 | Weekly lockout. Soul Imprint rate is the cheapest gear-quality lever |
| **Buy when the board is open** | Picture frame tools | Pantheon is launch content, but the tools do nothing in the bag |
| **Buy later** | Unique wings | Collection stats, and some lines sell |
| **Ignore** | Skin sets, Twilight jewellery, the 24,900 costume | Fashion. Wardrobe collection stats are real but this is the wrong shop to chase them from first |
| **Ignore as a plan** | Empathy Candy, Soul Crystals | [[Pet Understanding]] has better dedicated sources |

Stop buying Wisdom Stones and crystals when the doubled price is obviously
worse than the next row. That stop-point is a feel call; no source publishes
the full price ladder.

## Costs and math

A launch-model week without membership is **14 keys** (2 × 7) if you never
bank. Membership is 28. Season 3 cuts that to 7 / 14 **and** doubles cube
payouts, which is why current Korean guides and launch Korean guides
disagree on how "rich" a week feels.

Token income per key is not published. The shop rows that matter have
published unit prices, so the planning question is "how many good-rank
claims until the first five Wisdom Stones", not a tokens-per-hour formula
I would stand behind.

Abyss Point income at 100–300 per claim is a rounding error against the
200,000 weekly hunting cap, which is why Festa is listed as an **uncapped
side faucet** in [[Abyss Points and Ranks]] rather than as an AP strategy.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Spending a key on a bad rank.** Leave. The key stays.
- **Treating Festa as a limited-time event** and ignoring it until a banner
  appears. It is hourly, and it has been hourly since launch.
- **Buying skins first** because they are the expensive row. The cheap
  consumable tab is the progression.
- **Expecting Ariel or Azphel crystals in this shop.** Generic crystals
  only. Ariel fragments come from [[Trials and Rankings|Ascension Trial]]
  and Nightmare; Azphel from Abyss and Arena shops.
- **Following a post-April-2026 key guide at launch.** 7 / 14 per server
  is Season 3.
- **Banking keys to 28 and then forgetting.** Overflow does not wait.
- **Farming only one minigame** if you care about Pantheon artwork
  fragments.
- **Confusing Centuryroot Tokens with season coins.** Season coins expire.
  Centuryroot Tokens persist. Do not dump tokens because a season is ending.
