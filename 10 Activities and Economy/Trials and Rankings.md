# Trials and Rankings

> **In one sentence:** the score-and-ladder layer sits on top of normal dungeon
> content — the same bosses, but with a difficulty dial you set yourself, a
> numeric score instead of a clear flag, and a leaderboard that has been
> steadily stripped of power rewards until only prestige remains.

Korean **각성전** is one piece of content with two English names, both used
in this vault on purpose: **Awakening Battle** (aion2hub's patch archive)
and **Ascension Trial** (community English, Fextralife, this note's heading).
They are the same weekly. Search both; the result sets do not overlap.

At Season 1 the weekly is worth running for two crystal lines, not for a
ladder: **Ariel Trace Fragments** feed [[Daevanion Boards]], and
**Mysterious Crystals** (신비로운 결정) feed [[Arcana]] refinement. It is
not the dense event shop — that is [[Shugo Festa]].

## Why it matters

AION 2's dungeon ladder ([[Dungeons and Raids]]) answers "can you clear it".
The Trial layer answers "how well", and it is where the game puts its
difficulty-seeking players. Three things make it worth understanding:

1. It is **the only content where you choose the difficulty**, which makes it
   the cleanest gear-check in the game.
2. Its rewards run on **separate weekly counters and separate currencies**, so
   it does not compete with your [[Odyle Energy and Entry Limits]] budget the
   way Expedition does.
3. Korean seasons have repeatedly **removed** stat rewards from rankings. If
   you arrive expecting a competitive ladder that pays power, you will be
   disappointed — and that is a deliberate design decision, not an oversight.

## How it works

### Naming, first

Korean **각성전** is **Awakening Battle** in AION2 Hub's patch archive and
**Ascension Trial** in community English. Same weekly, two strings, disjoint
search results. This note uses Ascension Trial in running text and Awakening
Battle when citing aion2hub. Likewise 시련 is "Trial", 토벌전 is "Subjugation".
Cross-reference [[Glossary KR TW EN]] before searching.

### The Trial family — Vakron Sky Island

Added on **2026-07-15**, this is the newest and most explicit expression of the
score-and-ladder idea.

- **Entry gate:** item level **4,800**. This is well beyond the global launch
  band, so it is roadmap content for a Season 1 player.
- **Difficulty is composed, not picked from a list.** You set four separate
  difficulty attributes, each from 1 to 4, giving a **total difficulty between 4
  and 16**. A run at total 16 is maximum difficulty on every axis.
- **Reward scales with the composed total,** not with clear time. The currency
  is **Proof of Overcoming** (극복의 증표), paid out on a curve against the
  difficulty number you actually completed.
- **A weekly Proof of Overcoming shop** converts the currency into progression
  goods. Because the shop stock is weekly, the practical cap on Trial value is
  the shop, not the number of runs.

The design intent is legible: instead of a fixed Hard/Extreme tier list, you
raise your own difficulty until you fail, and the currency pays you for the
highest number you can actually beat. It is a personal ladder rather than a
server-wide race.

### Ascension Trial / Awakening Battle (각성전)

Solo or small-group boss content on a **weekly rotation**, with an entry count
of roughly three per week rather than an energy cost. The two English names
are both correct; aion2hub prints Awakening Battle, community guides print
Ascension Trial.

- **Difficulty tiers** run up through **Extreme**, with **Doom (파멸)** added in
  Chapter 1 at an item level **4,000** gate.
- Chapter 1 also added **two new Ascension Battles** to the rotation.
- Season 3 attached an **automatic timer that starts on entry**, making clear
  time a scored quantity rather than something you self-report.
- A further **Hell** tier is referenced in community discussion but I could not
  confirm it in any patch note or official post. See Open questions — treat it
  as unverified until a patch archive entry names 지옥 as a difficulty.

Scoring combines a base clear value for the difficulty with the remaining time
on the clock, multiplied by a difficulty coefficient. The consequence is that
**at a high enough difficulty a slow clear outscores a fast clear one tier
down**, which is what pushes players to gear-check rather than to speedrun.

### Subjugation (토벌전) — removed

Four-player content, roughly three entries per week, with its own ranking board.

**Subjugation was removed from the game in Season 3 (2026-04-08.)** Its rewards
were folded into other content and outstanding Subjugation entry tickets were
converted to Enhancement Stones at 20,000 apiece. It is documented here because
Korean guides written before April 2026 still describe it as a live weekly, and
because a Season 1 global build may ship it even though Korea no longer has it.
That is a genuine possibility worth flagging rather than assuming away.

### Ranking boards

Ranking boards exist for Ascension Trial, Subjugation (while it lived),
Transcendence and [[Nightmare Mode]]. Two structural facts matter:

- **Boards are per class.** Season 3 moved dungeon rankings onto per-class
  leaderboards so that a Gladiator is not ranked against a Sorcerer's numbers.
  Chapter 1 extended the same idea to PvP, moving Abyss boss-kill and
  artifact-capture rewards from raw contribution to **per-class contribution
  ranking**. See [[Abyss Points and Ranks]] and [[Arenas Sieges and Seasons]].
- **The scored quantities are clear time, deaths and objective counts.** An
  S-grade clear is gated on finishing inside a time threshold with a limited
  number of resurrections; secondary counters such as monsters killed and
  objectives engraved feed the score. Precise S-rank thresholds are per dungeon
  and per difficulty and are not published in patch notes.

### What ranking rewards actually give now

This is the part that has changed most, and it has changed in one direction:
**downward, deliberately, three times.**

| Patch | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Season 2 | Ranking rewards existed for Ascension Trial, Subjugation and Nightmare and included stat-bearing items |
| Season 3 (2026-04-08) | Those ranking rewards were **removed** and replaced with **Quna boxes** — a flat cash-currency payout with no power attached. Ranking buffs were removed at the same time |
| Chapter 1 (2026-07-01) | **Stat effects were stripped from Season 2 titles and Pantheon accessories,** retroactively converting former ranking prizes into cosmetics |

The result is that as of the current Korean build, **finishing high on a dungeon
ranking board pays prestige and a modest Quna payout, not power.** Korean
community reaction was hostile and the Inven threads on it are worth reading if
you want the argument, but the design position is settled: NC does not want
leaderboard placement to compound into a gear advantage.

The same philosophy shows up in the Trial family, where Proof of Overcoming pays
you for *your own* difficulty number rather than for beating other players.

## Progression path

1. **Clear the parent dungeon on normal difficulty first.** Trial scoring is
   built on content you already know; learning the fight and pushing difficulty
   at the same time wastes weekly entries.
2. **Run Ascension Trial / Awakening Battle every week from the moment it
   opens.** The entry count is small and does not roll over, it does not touch
   your energy budget, and at Season 1 it is one of the few repeatable taps
   for Ariel fragments and Mysterious Crystals. Generic Daevanion Crystals
   and Skill Points still come from [[Shugo Festa]], not from this weekly.
3. **Push difficulty until you fail, then step back one.** Because score is
   base-value times a difficulty coefficient plus remaining time, the highest
   difficulty you clear *reliably* beats an unreliable attempt one tier up.
4. **Doom at 4,000 and Vakron Sky Island Trial at 4,800 are Chapter 1 / later.
   Skip them at Season 1.** Extreme at 2,500 is the top Trial door on the
   0–3,800 bar.
5. **Do not chase rank for power.** Season 3 stripped stat rewards; if launch
   still has them, they are a limited window, not a pillar.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Item level 2,500** — Ascension Trial Extreme, the Season 1 top door.
- **Item level 4,000 / 4,800** — Doom and Vakron Sky Island Trial. **Out.**
- **Total difficulty 16** — the maximum composed difficulty at Vakron.
- **Weekly reset** — entry counts, ranking snapshots and the Proof of Overcoming
  shop all cycle. See [[Weekly Routine Main]].
- Trial entries are **not** paid in Odyle Energy, which makes them the correct
  content to run in a week where your energy pool is exhausted.

## Costs and math

The scoring formula, in the shape the Korean community reports it:

```
score = base clear value (per difficulty) + remaining time × difficulty coefficient
```

Two implications fall straight out:

- **Remaining time is worth more at high difficulty,** because the coefficient
  multiplies it. Two minutes saved at Doom is worth several times two minutes
  saved at Normal.
- **A death costs twice** — once directly against the resurrection-count
  condition for an S grade, once indirectly through the time it burns.

For Vakron Sky Island the equivalent statement is that **Proof of Overcoming
scales on the composed difficulty total (4 to 16)**, so the optimisation problem
is "which four difficulty attributes can I raise without pushing the run past my
clear ability", not "how fast can I go".

I do not have published per-difficulty base values or coefficients from a
primary source, so treat the formula as structurally right and numerically
unverified.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Searching for the wrong English name.** "Awakening Battle" and "Ascension
  Trial" are the same content and return different results.
- **Following a pre-April-2026 guide.** Subjugation is gone, ranking stat
  rewards are gone, and ranking buffs are gone. Any guide that lists Subjugation
  as a weekly is at least a season out of date.
- **Expecting rankings to pay power.** They pay Quna and titles. NC removed the
  stat rewards on purpose and then retroactively stripped stats from the titles
  that had already been awarded.
- **Over-pushing difficulty.** A failed run consumes a weekly entry. Failing at
  the ceiling is strictly worse than clearing one tier down.
- **Spending Odyle Energy expecting Trial rewards.** Trials use entry counts;
  the reward-cube energy model belongs to Expedition, Transcendence and
  Sanctuary.
- **Ignoring the auto-timer.** Since Season 3 the clock starts when you enter,
  not when you engage. Buffing up inside the instance costs score.
- **Treating the Trial shop as permanent stock.** Proof of Overcoming shop
  limits are weekly; unspent currency across a reset is fine, but unbought stock
  is gone.

## Global launch notes

Global launches 2026-10-05 with Season 1 content in a 0–3,800 unlock band. This
layer is mostly **later-season Korean content**, so the honest verdict is that a
Season 1 global player sees very little of it.

| Element | Season 1 global player has it? |
| --- | --- |
| Ascension Trial, base difficulties | **Likely yes.** It is long-standing endgame weekly content and the strongest candidate in this note to ship at launch |
| Ascension Trial, Doom difficulty | **No.** Item level 4,000 sits above the 3,800 band, and Doom is a Chapter 1 addition |
| The two Chapter 1 Ascension Battles | **No.** Roadmap |
| A "Hell" difficulty | **Unverified in any region** |
| Vakron Sky Island Trial | **No.** Item level 4,800, added 2026-07-15. Roadmap |
| Proof of Overcoming and its weekly shop | **No.** Ships with Vakron |
| Subjugation | **Uncertain.** Removed in Korea at Season 3, but a Season 1 build predates that removal. It may ship and then be removed later, mirroring Korea |
| Per-class ranking boards | **Uncertain.** The per-class split is a Season 3 change; Season 1 rankings may be undivided |
| Ranking rewards paying stat items | **Possible at launch.** Korea's Season 1/2 rankings *did* pay stat items; the removal came in Season 3. If global ships Season 1 faithfully, expect stat rewards early and their removal later |
| Season 2 titles and Pantheon accessories with stats | **No.** Season 2 content, and their stats were later stripped |

That last row is the interesting one for a launch player: **global Season 1 may
briefly have the powerful ranking rewards that Korea has since deleted.** If so,
they are a limited-time opportunity, not a permanent pillar. Plan accordingly
and see [[Global Launch Differences]].
