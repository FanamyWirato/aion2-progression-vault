# Assassin BiS Roadmap

> **In one sentence:** the Assassin (살성) is a **Dagger back-attack striker** —
> Accuracy and **Critical** first, then **`Illusive Clone` 20** and **`Swift
> Contract`** so **`Insignia Explosion`** / **`Heart Gore`** windows stay inside
> buff uptime.

## Why it matters

Assassin DPS is **position and burst**: rear **`Rear Smite`**, stigma buffs, and
macro **`Quick Slice`** chains. Global **four stigmas** — KR six-stigma loadouts
pick one flex among **`Savage Fang`**, **`Throw Shadowblade`**, **`Evasion Stance`**.

Mechanics: [[Stat Reference]]. Rotation: [[Assassin Skills and Stigmas]].

## Season 1 baseline

Four stigma slots, cap 20. Pet **Special 4:** **back boost** ([[Class Pet Farm Priority]]).

## Stat priority

Assassin row: **Accuracy → Critical → Smite**.

| Rank | Stat | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Accuracy** | Back attacks still miss; **`Heightened Sixth Sense`** is not a floor |
| 2 | **Critical Hit** | **`Exploit Weakness`**, **`Ambush Stance`** |
| 3 | **Smite** | **`Rear Smite`** passive; stigma **`Triniel's Dagger`** |
| 4 | **Combat Speed** | More **`Quick Slice`** / **`Flash Slice`** in a buff window |
| 5 | **Cooldown Reduction** | **`Swift Contract`**, **`Illusive Clone`** cadence |
| 6 | **Perfect / rear damage** | Imprint and titles when Accuracy/Crit are set |

## Phase plan

| Phase | Item level | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–1,000** | Levelling | **`Rear Smite`**, **`Exploit Weakness`**. Dagger + Guard |
| **1,000–1,600** | Krao / Draupnir | **`Ambush`**, **`Heart Gore`**, **`Insignia Explosion`** toward 16 |
| **1,600–2,200** | Conquest band | **`Illusive Clone` 15–20**, **`Triniel's Dagger` 10** |
| **2,200–2,700** | Fire Temple | **`Swift Contract` 10–15**, **`Savage Fang` 1+** if stagger-heavy |
| **2,700–3,800** | Ceiling | Full four-stigma 15–20, Nuakum |

## Stigma headline (four slots)

**`Illusive Clone` 20 → `Swift Contract` 10–15 → `Triniel's Dagger` 10 → `Savage
Fang` 1–15** (PvE progression). Swap **`Savage Fang`** for **`Throw Shadowblade`
5–10** on mobile bosses or **`Evasion Contract` 1–5** for learning patterns.
**`Evasion Stance`** is PvP-weighted — not the Season 1 PvE default.

## Traps

- Clicking the macro instead of **hold** — drops skills (see [[In-Game Macros and Hotbar Lines]]).
- **`Assault Ambush`** in macro — manual opener only.
- Front-facing **`Savage Roar`** filler without rear setup — weak damage.
