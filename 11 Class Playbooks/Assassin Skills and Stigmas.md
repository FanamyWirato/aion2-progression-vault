# Assassin Skills and Stigmas

> **In one sentence:** open with **`Ambush`** or **`Shadowstrike`**, run macro
> **`Insignia Explosion` → `Heart Gore` → `Ambush` → `Savage Roar`**, second step
> **`Quick Slice`**, and keep **`Illusive Clone`** + **`Swift Contract`** stigmas
> inside rear-attack windows.

## Core PvE loop

1. **Opener:** **`Swift Contract`** → **`Illusive Clone`** (if not macro’d) → **`Ambush`** / **`Shadowstrike`** from rear or flank.
2. **Macro (hold, do not click):** damage line → **`Quick Slice`**, 10 ms, LMB.
3. **Manual:** **`Savage Fang`**, **`Throw Shadowblade`**, **`Smoke Bomb`**, **`Defiance`**, **`Illusive Clone`** reposition.
4. **Position:** stay on **rear** for **`Rear Smite`** and **`Heart Gore`**; **`Insignia Explosion`** after mark applied.

## Stigmas — four slots

| Slot | Stigma | KR | Target | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **`Illusive Clone`** | 환영 분신 | **20** | Major damage + CDR synergy |
| 2 | **`Swift Contract`** | 신속의 계약 | **10–15** | Attack speed / burst window |
| 3 | **`Triniel's Dagger`** | 트리니엘의 비수 | **10** | Stigma nuke |
| 4 | **`Savage Fang`** | 맹수의 송곳니 | **1–15** | Groggy / def shred; more stagger → more CDR |

**Flex fourth:** **`Throw Shadowblade` 5–10** (mobility), **`Evasion Contract` 1–5** (learning). **`Evasion Stance`** — PvP default, not Season 1 PvE core.

**Shard order (guide):** Clone **15→20** → Triniel **10** → Swift **10–15** → Shadowblade **5** or Fang **1**.
