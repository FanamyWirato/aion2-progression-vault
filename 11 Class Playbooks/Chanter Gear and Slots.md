# Chanter Gear and Slots

> **In one sentence:** the Chanter wields a **Staff** in the main hand and the
> universal **Guard** off-hand; with no Heroic grade in Season 1 the target is a
> Unique `Enraged Kromede` or crafted Dragon Lord staff, a Unique `Bakarma` or
> `Aulamus` guard, a Unique `Nuakum` armor set, and belt and amulet at Unique +10.

## Why it matters

The Chanter's weapon is the rarest single drop in Season 1 loot tables from a
Chanter's perspective, because Conquest endbosses roll a weapon *type* and Staff
is only one of several. That shapes acquisition order: plan around **guaranteed
reward ceilings and Weapon Change Vouchers**, not drop luck.

The second reason this note exists: the Chanter spends more soul-imprint budget
on **class passive levels** than a damage dealer, because passive levels
simultaneously buy Smite, heal throughput and group buffs. Slot-by-slot imprint
targets therefore differ for this class.

Mechanics live elsewhere: [[Gear Tiers and Sources]] for grades and drops,
[[Weapons]], [[Armor]] and [[Accessories]] for slot mechanics,
[[Enchanting]] and [[Breakthrough]] for upgrade paths,
[[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]] for imprinting and
[[Manastones Spirit Stones and Godstones]] for sockets. Sequence:

## How it works

Three structural facts drive every row below.

**There is no armor class.** AION 2 has no cloth/leather/chain/plate split.
Every class can wear any armor piece; the Chanter competes with the whole party
for the same armor drops — there is no "Chanter set" as such. A piece becomes
Chanter gear through soul-imprint lines and manastones.

**Weapon and Guard are one project.** The Guard (가더) is not a tank shield; it
is the universal off-hand all classes wear, carries attack stats, and **weapon
damage amplification rolls only on weapon and Guard.** Korean gear guides
prioritise both for premium upgrade attempts. On Chanter that matters twice
because the class does not protect a burst window — damage is continuous and
weapon multipliers apply evenly.

**Two slots are permanent and never replaced.** The **Belt of Nobility** and
**Revelation Amulet** grade from Common to Unique via scrolls and Substance
Morph in place and are never swapped for a better item. Everything else is a
replacement ladder. In a launch build without Heroic, **Unique +10 on both is a
real end goal**, not a stop before conversion.

**Manastones (5 sockets):** Accuracy to content floor, then Attack Power, then
Smite. **Godstone:** save it — one socket, belongs on the staff you keep
(Enraged Kromede or Dragon Lord), not Rupture.

**Soul-imprint targets, in order:** Weapon Damage Amplification → Combat
Speed → Multi-hit Accuracy → Power. Multi-hit Accuracy is weighted higher on
Chanter because Onslaught, Incandescent Blow, Heat Wave Blow, Rushing Smash and
Gust Rampage have multi-hit specialisations. Stop rerolling once Combat Speed or
Weapon Damage Amplification lands.

The **seven armor pieces supply seven of the 22 gear-sourced passive levels**;
armor imprints are where the heal-vs.-damage split from [[Chanter BiS Roadmap]]
lands. Specifically: `Blessing of Life` (생명의 축복) on cloak and helm if you are
the only healer; `Attack Preparation` (공격 준비) plus `Impact Hit` (충격 적중) if not.

Two slots confirm [[Armor]] as offensive imprint slots — chest, gloves,
shoulders — matching Korean slot-tier lists.

### Wings

Strictly not gear, but a stat slot. Full detail in [[Chanter Support Systems]];
briefly: **Talisra Wings** (4% cooldown reduction, 210,000 Oath Coins Season
Shop) are the Chanter pick because cooldown reduction is buff uptime; **Forest
Spirit Wings** from Vakron Sky Island as a free stopgap while farming armor there.

## Realistic acquisition order

Sequence for real players, not a wish list.

1. **Level 45 story staff, Ascension Bracelet, Daeva's Ultimate Wings.** Free.
   Claim all three when the story offers them.
2. **Start both permanent-slot loops immediately.** Belt Enhance Scrolls every
   stronghold; Amulet Enhance Scrolls from Monolith stages. Starting late is the
   mistake.
3. **Fill every empty slot with anything.** Empty accessory slot beats Rare.
   Broker is cheap at launch.
4. **Draupnir Conquest to 14-dice ceiling → `Bakarma Guard`.** Before Krao
   Cave, because Guard equals main hand and only drops in Draupnir. Both dungeons
   from the same 1,000 gate.
5. **Krao Cave Conquest to 14-dice ceiling → `Rupture Staff`, helm and
   necklace.** **Bank Weapon Change Vouchers**; do not spend on a weapon you will
   replace.
6. **Even enchants: +10 staff and Guard, +5 to +7 armor.** Do not push one piece
   deep. See [[Enchanting]].
7. **Intermediate manastones and spirit stones everywhere.** Accuracy first until
   blocks and parries stop.
8. **Vakron Sky Island Conquest** from 1,600 gate → full armor set, Guard,
   Forest Spirit wing material from one dungeon.
9. **Urugugu Canyon Conquest to 21-dice ceiling** → `Aulamus` staff or sub-weapon,
   whichever is missing. Second Weapon Change Voucher.
10. **Soul imprint begins, in this order:** Guard → staff → gloves → chest →
    shoulders → cloak → rings. Reroll with Soul Scrolls and **stop once Combat
    Speed or Weapon Damage Amplification lands.**
11. **Breakthrough offensive slots to 5 before armor** — weapon, Guard,
    accessories.
12. **Ferocious Horn Den Conquest** from 2,200 → `Nuakum` armor set, item Lv 84.
13. **Fire Temple Conquest** from 2,200 tooltip / 2,400–2,500 social floor →
    **`Enraged Kromede's Staff`**, Season 1 drop BiS. Godstone on this weapon, not
    earlier ones.
14. **Belt and amulet to Unique +10, then stop.** Without Heroic that is the
    ceiling; further investment has no target.
15. **Crafted Unique Dragon Lord staff and crafted rings** if committed to
    Jewelcrafting. Extra imprint line is the reason.
16. **Abyssal Forge: Ludra** from 2,700 → `Abyssal Bracelet` with Wisdom, Time and
    Death plus Abyssal set pieces for PvP set effects.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Spending Weapon Change Voucher early.** Only guaranteed staff route; it does
  not expire faster than your gear. Bank for Enraged Kromede.
- **Selling Common Belt of Nobility or Ascension Bracelet duplicate.** Both feed
  Substance Morph. Belt slot never gets another item.
- **Treating Guard as an afterthought.** Carries weapon damage amplification — only
  two slots.
- **Buying Superior manastones for Vakron or Rupture gear.** You will discard them.
  Intermediate until Nuakum and Enraged Kromede.
- **Urugugu Canyon for armor.** Vakron Sky Island covers the same item level with
  all seven slots plus Guard.
- **Rerolling soul imprint past a good line.** Rerolls cost; Combat Speed or Weapon
  Damage Amplification is the stop condition, not a minimum to keep chasing.
- **Zero-HP damage profile.** `Earth's Promise` needs own HP ≥75% for group boss
  damage-resistance shred; Chanter buys HP on purpose. Class-specific exception to
  generic offense manastone advice.
- **Assuming a Chanter armor set exists.** No armor class. Same drops as everyone;
  distinction is imprints.

## Global launch notes

- **No Heroic grade, reportedly.** Every Heroic row above has a Season 1
  substitute. Two consequences: crafted Unique Dragon Lord staff and `Enraged
  Kromede` are real endgame items; **Unique +10 belt and amulet is the end line**.
  Medium confidence — no-Heroic from a low-trust source; Korean Season 1
  Conquest endbosses drop Heroic `Ancient Spirit` accessories at 0.5%, which fits
  poorly.
- **Three accessory slots missing.** Pendant, Brooch, Seal. Budget five not eight;
  each slot proportionally more valuable.
- **Item levels 68 / 76 / 84 are Korean Season 1 numbers.** Global gates to
  ~item level 3800, different scale. Take the *relative* ladder — Krao/Draupnir,
  then Vakron/Urugugu, then Fire Temple/Ferocious Horn Den — absolute numbers
  may not transfer.
- **Craft-weapon-is-BiS is Korea meta.** Extra imprint line. If Global tunes craft
  differently, `Enraged Kromede` is the clear answer.
- **Skill names follow live Global client.** See [[Chanter Skills and Stigmas]] and
  [[Glossary KR TW EN]]. **Item names** in the table remain working titles where
  the vault has no confirmed EN string.
