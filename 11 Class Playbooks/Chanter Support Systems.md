# Chanter Support Systems

> **In one sentence:** beyond gear and skills, Chanter strength comes from Arcana
> slots that are *full* not merely good, lord stats weighted toward **Wisdom and
> Illusion**, and a ~30-minute title that grants **5% cooldown reduction**.

## Why it matters

These systems are account-wide, mostly permanent and largely non-refundable. A
Chanter who neglects them and chases gear grade has high Combat Power and worse
group output — because the two stats this class converts into party damage,
**Smite** and **Cooldown Reduction**, barely come from gear. They come from here.

Documentation: [[Arcana Sets and Meta]], [[Arcana Cards and Types]],
[[Pantheon Stats]], [[Pantheon Statues and Frames]], [[Species Understanding]] and
[[Wings Acquisition]]. This sheet is only what a Chanter should target and how
each system ranks relative to the others.

## Relative worth: read this first

Ranked by power per hour invested, Season 1 Chanter:

| Rank | System | Why here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **`Through Hell and Back` title** | Cooldown Reduction 5%, HP 800, Stamina 200 for roughly **30 minutes** of sealed dungeons. CDR is Chanter throughput; HP 800 helps hold the 75% line for `Earth's Promise`. Nothing else in the game is this cheap |
| 2 | **Stigma levels** | In [[Chanter Skills and Stigmas]]; listed here for ranking. `Undefeated Mantra` stigma level 20 is class justification |
| 3 | **Arcana slots filled** | Five filled Rare cards beat two refined Uniques. Largest Odyle Energy sink and largest early jump |
| 4 | **Pantheon plus Empyrean Traces** | Lord stats are not in Combat Power but are real, permanent and cheap. Traces are one-time collectibles |
| 5 | **Other titles** | Three equipped slots. Free permanent stats, gated only on content you run anyway |
| 6 | **Pet family understanding** | Where Power and Precision bank toward the 200 cap and Smite gets locked. High ceiling, slow accumulation |
| 7 | **Wings** | One equip effect plus retention per pair owned. Talisra's 4% cooldown reduction is a real Chanter item |
| 8 | **Wardrobe** | Combat Power and skin-count titles. Real, but costs money/time better spent above |
| 9 | **Consumables** | Small, repeating; unusually cheap for Chanter because no MP potions needed |

## Arcana

### Season 1 reality: five slots, two sets

Korea now has ten slots and seven sets. **Global Season 1: five** — Chalice,
Parchment, Compass, Bell, Mirror — and only **two sets** with pools for five slots:
**Primal Vigor** (원초의 활력) and **Magic Armor** (마력의 갑주). Scales arrived
2026-02-25, sixth slot later, Stat Arcana July/August 2026. Frenzy and Pure Blood
in current KR Chanter guides are **reskins of later seasons — substitute Primal
Vigor and Magic Armor.**

With five slots and two sets, 4-piece bonus is only reachable as **4 + 1.**

### Recommendation

**4 × Primal Vigor + 1 × Magic Armor.**

Chanter-specific synergy: **Primal Vigor 4-piece = bonus PvE Attack above 70% HP**,
and the Chanter **must hold 75% HP or more** so `Earth's Promise` shreds boss
damage resistance for the party. You get paid twice for a discipline you already
need. No other class gets that alignment for free.

**KR debate, documented not resolved.** One class Arcana table puts Chanter at
**4 Magic + 1 Vigor** (support read); a top Inven Chanter runs Magic-heavy and
offensive lord stats. Both agree **Chanter leans Magic-family more than pure DPS**
— disagreement is how far. This note's 4 Vigor + 1 Magic is the Season 1
compromise: HP-conditioned 4-piece the class uniquely synergises with, and what
the five-slot pool allows. Re-evaluate at six slots.

### Which slot carries what

Planning constraint that simplifies Chanter Arcana: **only two actives need skill
level 20 — `Onslaught` and `Dark Crush` — both in the Parchment pool.** Small,
aimable pool. Everything else can be passive.

| Slot | Target | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Parchment** | `Onslaught` **+4**, `Dark Crush` **+3** | Only mandatory level-20 actives. Skill Points + full Daevanion ~14 — this is how you buy 20 |
| **Compass** | Whatever Parchment missed, else `Recuperation` | Overflow for actives |
| **Bell** | `Attack Preparation`, `Impact Hit` | Up to **4 passive levels** — the two endgame Chanters push toward 30 |
| **Mirror** | `Wind's Promise`, `Blessing of Life` | Up to **4 passive levels**. Party critical damage amp or heal scaler as sole healer |
| **Chalice** | **Passives, no minor active** | Up to **4 levels**. Chanter board advice: few level-20 actives needed → Chalice on passives. Largest difference from DPS |

**Grade path.** Fill all five with anything, then upgrade. Cards from
**Transcendence** (Odyle Energy in the Arcana phase) and Heritage Arcana from
S-rank stages 6–8. Do not refine before every slot is occupied — see [[Arcana Sets and Meta]].

**Lord stats on cards.** Each Arcana slot has a main-god stat line. Chanter: same
lord priority as below — Wisdom and Illusion first.

## Pantheon

### Lord stat priority for Chanter

Each lord domain converts points to combat stats (0.1% or 0.2% per point), cap **200
per stat**. Lord stats **do not appear in Combat Power** — undervalued and rewarding.

| Priority | Lord | Domain | Stat | Chanter reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Wisdom** | — | **Smite** | Chanter converts Smite better than any class (0.3% per passive level); bosses have no Smite resist. MP economy matters |
| 2 | **Illusion** (Kaisinel) | — | **Cooldown Reduction** | Buff uptime = Chanter output. Raised above DPS default |
| 3 | **Time** | — | **Combat Speed** | Signature stat; breakpoint experiments tuned on it |
| 4 | **Death** (Triniel) | — | Offense | Ranker bracelet picks |
| 5 | **Life** (Ariel) | — | HP and healing | Floor. Hold 75% HP; HP scales heal size |
| 6 | **Justice** (Nezekan) | — | Combat Speed / CDR-adjacent | Shares the two best axes |
| 7 | **Space** | — | — | Filler |
| 8 | **Fate** | — | — | Filler |
| 9 | **Destruction** (Zikel) | — | Attack / Critical | Real, replaceable from gear |
| 10 | **Freedom** (Vaizel) | — | Accuracy | Only to accuracy floor |

**Evidence for offensive tilt.** KR press: top-five Atull rankers per class —
**Clerics and Chanters take Destruction and Death "beyond support"** for offense.
Ranker behaviour for hybrid in [[Chanter BiS Roadmap]], vs. pure healer lord spread.

**Counterposition.** TW healer guide: **Life and Fate** artworks stack — written for
Cleric. Wrong shape for Chanter; defensible if you default to no-Cleric main heal.

### Frame layout

Three decoration types: one **Colossus**, several **Statues**, several **Artworks**.
Placement rules and grades in [[Pantheon Statues and Frames]].

| Type | Chanter pick | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Colossus** | `Warden Santras` early → **`Zikel's Thoughtform`** | Zikel's Thoughtform: **14,000 Dream Fragments**, Nightmare `Zikel's Apparition` stage 1, ~item level 2,400. Bank Dream Fragments from phase 3; cross-season |
| **Statues** | **`Transcended Bakarma` ×10** first | Draupnir Conquest — **dungeon you farm for Bakarma Guard anyway.** Most efficient statue farm for Chanter |
| **Statues** | then Death- and Space-themed pairs | Conquest and raid bosses |
| **Artworks** | **Wisdom and Illusion** | Chanter split. Smite and cooldown reduction — stats gear barely supplies |

**Empyrean Traces** — one-time collectibles on both faction maps, direct lord points.
Free permanent stats; phase-1 sweep in [[Chanter BiS Roadmap]] for that reason.

## Pet family stat rerolls

System: [[Species Understanding]] — **Family Understanding** per pet race;
**Race Understanding** unlocks rerollable stat slots.

**Family order: Intelligence (지성) → Nature (자연) → Wild (야성) → Transform
(변형).** Each to level 5 before any reaches 10. Retention: Intelligence Critical
and **Power**; Wild extra Accuracy; Nature **Precision**; Transform Evasion and HP.

**Why for Chanter:** **Power (위력) and Precision (정확) cap at 200.** Banking via
pet retention — free, permanent, account-wide — saves manastones for Accuracy,
Combat Speed and Smite. Intelligence first because Power is more valuable.

**Race Understanding and locks.**

- Race Understanding to **level 10**; **lock nothing before level 9.** Early locks
  are regretted.
- **Smite only in slots 3 and 9.** Target **≥1.5% per slot**, **≥3% combined**.
  Highest-value pet lock for Chanter (0.3% per passive level multiplies everything).
- **Slot 6** defensive — Damage Resistance or Iron Wall. Chanter nuance from Inven:
  **Regeneration ~ as practical as Iron Wall for Chanter**; other classes prefer Iron
  Wall. Single source, medium confidence — test yourself.
- Other lock candidates on good rolls: **Combat Speed**, **Damage Amplification**,
  **Accuracy**.

**Pet titles.** Collecting and levelling grants titles with stats — second reason for
the family ladder even after retention is banked.

## Wings

Wing slot with **equip effect**; each pair **retention** (~**+200 Flight Power**),
equipped or not. Policy: *collect all, equip the effect you want.* Mechanics:
[[Wings Acquisition]].

| Pick | Effect | Source | Chanter verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **`Talisra Wings`** | **4% Cooldown Reduction** | Season Shop, **210,000 Oath Coins** | **Chanter pick.** CDR = buff uptime = output. DPS takes boss damage; Chanter does not |
| `Nightmare Wings` | Boss Damage Amplification | Nightmare progression | Parse alternative if you are group DPS and someone else buffs |
| `Forest Spirit Wings` | — | **Vakron Sky Island Conquest** materials | Free stopgap; you farm that dungeon for armor anyway |
| `Daeva's Ultimate Wings` | — | Level 45 main story | Free day one |

**Collection bonus:** **25 pairs** → Unique title `Dominator of Dimensions`. Wings from
story, dungeons, shop, crafting, hidden cubes, gathering — passively accumulate.

## Wardrobe and consumables

**Wardrobe** → Combat Power and skin titles (`Fashionista` 100, `Empty Closet` 700).
For Chanter, both give Combat Speed and Cooldown Reduction. Keep every skin;
register; never delete.

**Consumables.**

| Priority | Item | Why for Chanter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Attack- and Smite-food** (Cooking) | Direct throughput. Keep up permanently |
| 2 | **HP potions** | Not only emergency — **75% line for `Earth's Promise`.** Belt of Nobility HP potion recovery % makes them better than they look |
| 3 | **Resurrection Spirit Stones** | Support without resurrection wastes a wipe |
| 4 | **Scrolls** | Standard buff scrolls; cheap, additive |
| — | **MP potions and MP food** | **Deprioritise.** `Onslaught` restores MP; **autoattack cancel loop** drives MP use near zero. KR Chanter guide: mana not a constraint. Kinah into attack food |

Last row: concrete Chanter savings many miss because every other melee needs mana
consumables.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Refining Arcana before all five slots are filled.** Five Rare >> two refined Uniques.
- **Copying current KR Arcana build.** Ten slots, seven sets. Season 1: five and two;
  Frenzy/Pure Blood = later reskins of Primal Vigor/Magic Armor.
- **Chalice on a minor active.** Active list is short; Chalice = four passive levels free.
- **Locking pet stats before Race Understanding 9.** Expensive, practically irreversible.
- **Locking Smite outside slots 3/9.** It only appears there.
- **Copying Cleric Pantheon artworks.** Life/Fate = dedicated healer. Chanter: Wisdom/
  Illusion; ranker data for offense tilt.
- **Buying MP consumables.** `Onslaught` covers mana; autoattack cancel keeps use low.
  Real savings vs. peers.
- **Ignoring Empyrean Traces.** One-time, free, permanent — not in Combat Power, so
  invisibly missing.
- **Equipping wings for biggest number not right effect.** Boss damage amp for parse; CDR
  for more frequent buffs.
- **Selling/deleting skins.** Two titles with best stats.
