# Cleric BiS Roadmap

> **In one sentence:** the Cleric (치유성) is the dedicated mace healer whose
> real contribution is **party buffs, boss shred and a resurrection**, so the
> plan chases heal-skill breakpoints and HP for `Earth's Grace`, not a personal
> dummy parse.

## Why it matters

Four-player Season 1 groups are short a body compared with Korean five-player
endgame. That makes a Cleric more valuable at launch than a lot of later
Korean "healers are optional" posts imply. The companion class is the
[[Chanter BiS Roadmap]]: a Chanter is melee hybrid support; a Cleric is the
actual healer. If the group has both, the Cleric heals and shreds, the Chanter
holds `Undefeated Mantra` in melee.

Mechanics live in [[Stat Reference]], [[Item Level vs Combat Power]] and
[[Progression Phases Roadmap]]. What to press lives in
[[Cleric Skills and Stigmas]].

## The Season 1 baseline

Same vault baseline as the other playbooks: level **45**, gates **0–3,800**,
**four stigma slots**, stigma cap **20**, Unique gear ceiling, no Heroic.
See [[Global Launch Differences]].

## Stat priority

| Rank | Stat | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Cooldown Reduction** | Mark uptime, Radiant Recovery uptime, Earth Punishment uptime. Chain of Torment needs ~35 percent CDR to stay permanent |
| 2 | **Combat Speed** | Casts and the Earth's Retribution filler. Universal, and it lives on Nezekan |
| 3 | **HP, held high** | `Earth's Grace` is a party damage buff that dies when you are low. Daevanion leftover is HP-first for this reason |
| 4 | **Healing Amplification** | Only scaler on heals. Comes from `Healing Enhancement` passive levels |
| 5 | **Accuracy** | To the content floor, then stop. You still hit the boss with marks and Condemnation |
| 6 | **Double Chance** (Smite) | Cheap via passive levels; **`Earth Punishment`** adds party **`Earth's Blessing`** Double Chance on stigma tiers |
| — | MP | Earth's Retribution restores it. Do not spend like a caster |

## Phase plan

| Phase | Item level | What you are doing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0. Levelling** | 0–1,000 | Story Mace, Ascension Bracelet, green Belt of Nobility. Daevanion into Nezekan oranges and blue heal nodes. Light of Protection as the first stigma |
| **1. First Uniques** | 1,000–1,600 | Krao Cave and Draupnir ceilings. Rupture Mace, Bakarma Guard. Radiant Recovery 16, Healing Light 16, Light of Regeneration 16 |
| **2. Two-star Conquest** | 1,600–2,200 | Urugugu / Vakron. Aulamus Mace or Vakron pieces. Earth Punishment toward 20. Judgment Thunder 16, Condemnation toward 20 |
| **3. Fire Temple band** | 2,200–2,700 | Enraged Kromede Mace or crafted Dragon Lord Mace. Nuakum armour. Radiant Recovery 20, drop Absolution. Ariel crystals once armour is around +12 |
| **4. Ceiling** | 2,700–3,800 | Ludra / Unique +10 Belt and Amulet. Four stigmas at 15–20. Do not chase Heroic |

## Stigma headline

Season 1 default: **Light of Protection 15 → Earth Punishment 20 → Prayer of
Amplification 15 → Salvation 10–20.** Resurrection on progression. Details in
[[Cleric Skills and Stigmas]].

## Gear headline

Mace (Blacksmithing) and the universal Guard. Same dungeon ladder as every
other class — see [[Cleric Gear and Slots]]. The Cleric-specific imprint
budget is **Healing Enhancement** and **Earth's Grace** on earrings and the
cloak, not a fourth damage active.

## Traps

- Building a pure heal-bot and skipping the marks.
- Copying a five-stigma Inven screenshot.
- Copying a Chanter's Wisdom/Illusion Lord spread without taking Life. You
  need the HP floor.
- Crafting a Staff. Mace is Blacksmithing; Staff is the Chanter.
