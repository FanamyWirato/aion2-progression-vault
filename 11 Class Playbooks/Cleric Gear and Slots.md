# Cleric Gear and Slots

> **In one sentence:** a Cleric wields a **Mace** in the main hand and the
> universal **Guard** in the off-hand, and in Season 1 without Heroic grade
> the finish line is a Unique Enraged Kromede or crafted Dragon Lord Mace, a
> Unique Bakarma or Aulamus Guard, Nuakum armour, and Unique +10 Belt and
> Amulet.

## Why it matters

There is no armour class. Every class rolls on the same armour drops; what
makes a piece a Cleric piece is **soul imprint lines and manastones**. The
weapon *type* is the scarce roll — Conquest final bosses pick a weapon family,
and Mace is one of several — so you plan around **ceiling cubes and Weapon
Change Vouchers**, not drop luck.

Slot mechanics live in [[Weapons]], [[Armor]], [[Accessories]],
[[Gear Tiers and Sources]] and [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]]. The
dungeon ladder is the same one [[Chanter Gear and Slots]] and
[[Templar Gear and Slots]] already sourced. This note only changes the
weapon type and the imprint targets.

**Item English names** in the tables are still working translations unless
the vault already has a confirmed string. Skill names follow the live client.

## Main hand — Mace

Crafted under **Blacksmithing**, with Longswords and Guards. Not Jewelcrafting.

| Target | Grade | Item Lv | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Season 1 dropped BiS** | Unique | 84 | **Fire Temple Conquest**, Enraged Kromede weapon table. Use a Weapon Change Voucher if the boss rolled the wrong type |
| **Season 1 crafted BiS** | Unique | tier-dependent | **Blacksmithing** Dragon Lord Mace line. Extra imprint line; Korean PvE default when the economy allows |
| Second step | Unique | 76 | **Urugugu Canyon** Aulamus, 21-cube ceiling |
| First keeper | Unique | 68 | **Krao Cave** Rupture, 14-cube ceiling |
| Levelling | Rare / Epic | 45-band | Main-story Mace at 45 |
| **Not at launch** | Heroic | — | Ludra Heavenbound Mace. **Substitute: Enraged Kromede or crafted Dragon Lord** |

**Manastones:** Accuracy to the content floor, then Attack Power, then Smite.
**Godstone:** save it for the Mace you intend to keep.

**Imprint, in order:** Weapon Damage Amplification → Combat Speed → Heal /
Healing Amplification lines if they roll → Power.

## Off-hand — Guard

Same table as every other class: Bakarma from Draupnir first, then Aulamus or
Vakron. Take the Unique Guard **before** the Unique Mace. See
[[Chanter Gear and Slots]] for the sourced drop rates.

## Armour and accessories

Same Unique ladder: Vakron as the efficient seven-slot set, Nuakum as the
Season 1 ceiling, Decurion earrings for **passive** skill levels.

| Slot | What a Cleric wants on it |
| --- | --- |
| Cloak | Class **passive** level — `Earth's Grace` or `Healing Enhancement` beats any stat |
| Earrings | Passive levels on those two, then HP |
| Rings | **Active** skill levels: `Radiant Recovery` and `Condemnation` (or Judgment Thunder until Condemnation is 20) |
| Belt of Nobility / Revelation Amulet | Upgrade in place to Unique +10. Never replaced |
| Bracelet | Accuracy until the floor, then a Lord stat you actually convert. Life and Illusion both have a case; see [[Cleric Support Systems]] |

## Acquisition order

1. Fill empty slots from story and field gear.
2. Draupnir **Bakarma Guard**.
3. Krao Cave **Rupture Mace**.
4. Vakron armour set (covers seven slots plus a Guard option).
5. Fire Temple Mace or crafted Dragon Lord Mace.
6. Nuakum armour.
7. Unique +10 Belt and Amulet.
8. Stop. Heroic is not the launch ceiling.

## Traps

- Crafting a Staff. That is the Chanter.
- Spending the Cloak imprint on an active. Earrings and cloaks are the
  passive-level slots; rings are the actives.
- Zero-HP damage profile. Earth's Grace turns that into a party-DPS loss.
