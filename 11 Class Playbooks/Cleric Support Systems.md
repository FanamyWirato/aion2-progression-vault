# Cleric Support Systems

> **In one sentence:** a Cleric converts **Cooldown Reduction, Combat Speed
> and HP** from the slow systems, and its Arcana job is to buy
> `Radiant Recovery` and `Condemnation` levels that Skill Points cannot.

## Why it matters

The same account-wide systems as [[Chanter Support Systems]] and
[[Templar Support Systems]]. This note only says what a Cleric should aim
at. Mechanics live in [[Arcana Cards and Types]], [[Pantheon Stats]],
[[Species Understanding]] and [[Wings Acquisition]].

## Relative value

| Rank | System | Why here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Stigma levels** | `Light of Protection` 15 and `Earth Punishment` 20 are party damage. See [[Cleric Skills and Stigmas]] |
| 2 | **Arcana slots filled** | Five filled Rares beat two refined Uniques. Buys the 16/20 heal breakpoints |
| 3 | **Pantheon / Traces** | Cooldown Reduction and HP do not show well on Combat Power and they are this class |
| 4 | **Titles** | Free. Any Cooldown Reduction or HP title outranks a wardrobe grind |
| 5 | **Pets** | Bank Power and Precision; HP understanding helps Earth's Grace |
| 6 | **Wings** | Retention bonus plus one equipped effect |
| 7 | **Consumables** | HP food and heal potions. MP is not the constraint Earth's Retribution already solves |

## Arcana

Season 1: **five slots**, two sets — **Primal Vigor** and **Magic Armor**.
Run **4 × Primal Vigor + 1 × Magic Armor** unless you have a reason not to.
Primal Vigor's 4-piece bonus is PvE Attack above 70 percent HP; a Cleric is
already required to stay high for `Earth's Grace`. Same alignment the Chanter
gets for free.

If you copy the January 2026 Cleric board's mix instead (3 Vitality / 2 Magic,
no 4-piece), you are buying specific Lord stats over the set bonus. That is a
real alternative, not a mistake. Do not sit in between.

### Slot aims

From the January Cleric guide's pool table. Stars are theirs.

| Slot | Pool that matters | Aim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chalice** | every active and every passive | Lock **`Radiant Recovery`**. This is the card you spend Mysterious Crystals protecting |
| **Parchment** | Earth's Retribution, Debilitating Mark, Chain of Torment, **Condemnation**, **Radiant Recovery**, Bolt | Condemnation and Radiant Recovery |
| **Compass** | **Judgment Thunder**, Divine Aura, **Light of Regeneration**, **Healing Light**, Lightning Strike Scattershot, Defiance | The two heals, then Judgment Thunder to 16 |
| **Bell** | Warm Benediction, Empyrean Lord's Grace, **Immortal Veil**, Prayer of Concentration, Survival Willpower | Immortal Veil in PvP; skip-first in PvE |
| **Mirror** | Empyrean Lords' Benediction, **Healing Enhancement**, Heal Block, Radiant Benediction, **Earth's Grace** | **Earth's Grace and Healing Enhancement.** This is the Cleric Mirror |

**Lord stats on those cards, if you are choosing the family:** Time (Combat
Speed) on the Chalice, Illusion (Cooldown Reduction) on the Mirror, Life (HP)
on the Parchment. Destruction on the Bell is a damage luxury.

## Pantheon

Life and Fate artworks are the dedicated-healer stack Taiwanese Cleric guides
recommend. The Chanter note argues Wisdom and Illusion instead — that argument
is **Chanter-specific**. A Cleric should take the healer stack unless parse
nights have already proved Earth's Grace is covered by gear HP.

## Consumables

HP food, heal potions, attack food if the marks are your night. Skip MP
stacks; Earth's Retribution is the mana skill.

## Traps

- Copying the Chanter's Mirror (Attack Preparation / Impact Hit). Cleric
  Mirror is Earth's Grace / Healing Enhancement.
- Filling two Unique cards and leaving three slots empty.
- Life-and-Fate Pantheon *and* a zero-HP Daevanion leftover. Pick a floor and
  hold it.
