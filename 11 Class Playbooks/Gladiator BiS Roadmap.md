# Gladiator BiS Roadmap

> **In one sentence:** the Gladiator (검성) is a **Greatsword bruiser** whose DPS
> is **`Lunge Stance` + `Rage Burst` + `Overhead Slam` uptime** — plan for
> **Cooldown Reduction (~30%)**, **Smite**, and party **`Zikel's Blessing`**
> before raw Crit.

## Why it matters

Hub labels Gladiator **Tank/DPS** with lifesteal and AoE — in practice Korean
endgame treats it as a **frontline DPS** that can off-tank. Buff pairing (same
cooldown bundles) matters more than on ranged classes.

Mechanics: [[Stat Reference]], [[Progression Phases Roadmap]].
Rotation: [[Gladiator Skills and Stigmas]].

## Season 1 baseline

Four **stigma slots**, cap **20**. See [[Global Launch Differences]].

## Stat priority

Melee row: **Accuracy → Smite → Critical** ([[Stat Reference]]).

| Rank | Stat | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Accuracy** | **`Rending Blow`** and **`Overhead Slam`** must land for CDR procs |
| 2 | **Cooldown Reduction** | **`Lunge Stance`** infinite uptime needs ~30% CDR + crit CDR from stance |
| 3 | **Smite** | **`Murderous Burst`**, stigma **`Lunge Stance`** line |
| 4 | **Critical Hit** | Feeds stance CDR and **`Experienced Counterstrike`** |
| 5 | **Multi-hit** | **`Zikel's Blessing`** party line (+50 multi-hit at stigma 15) |
| 6 | **Lifesteal / sustain** | **`Blood Absorption`**, **`Lifestealing Blade`** stigma |
| — | **Block** | **`Focused Block`** stigma for mechanics, not CP chase |

Pet **Special slot 4:** **front boost** or Smite ([[Class Pet Farm Priority]]).

## Stigma headline (four slots)

**`Lunge Stance` 20 → `Zikel's Blessing` 15 → `Lifestealing Blade` 15 → `Rage
Burst` 20.** Drop **`Wave Armor`** unless you farm open-world packs without a
healer; **`Focused Block` 5–15** replaces **`Rage Burst`** only on block-heavy
progression, not for CP.

Shard order: **Lunge Stance first to 20**, then Zikel 15, Lifesteal 15, Rage 5→20 last.

## Traps

- Splitting **Lunge Stance / Lifestealing Blade** buffs mid-macro — bundle same-CD buffs ([Inven 실전딜](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/aion2/6448/16629)).
- **`Wave Armor`** in stigma slot when the party already has sustain — weak fourth slot in dungeons.
- No CDR: **`Overhead Slam`** falls off and damage collapses.
