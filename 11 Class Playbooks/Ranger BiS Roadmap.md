# Ranger BiS Roadmap

> **In one sentence:** the Ranger (궁성) is a **Bow kiting DPS** whose plan stacks
> **Accuracy and Critical**, keeps **`Vaizel's Authority`** up, and runs the
> **Griffon + Gale** macro with **`Deadshot`** charged on a separate key.

## Why it matters

Ranger is pure ranged physical DPS: **`Snipe`** / **`Deadshot`** openers, sustained
**`Tempest Shot`** filler. Stigma **`Vaizel's Authority`** is the highest-ROI
damage buff even at high Perfect — Korean stigma math treats it as non-optional.

Mechanics: [[Stat Reference]]. Rotation: [[Ranger Skills and Stigmas]].

## Season 1 baseline

Four stigma slots, cap 20. Pet slot **4:** **Crit Damage 3%** if global pet test
matches KR ranger-likes ([[Class Pet Farm Priority]]).

## Stat priority

Ranger row: **Accuracy → Critical → Attack Speed** (Combat Speed).

| Rank | Stat | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Accuracy** | **`Focused Eye`**, bow skills at range |
| 2 | **Critical Hit** | **`Concentrated Fire`**, **`Bow of Blessing`** party crit stigma |
| 3 | **Combat Speed** | **`Tempest Shot`**, **`Gale Arrow`** macro throughput |
| 4 | **Crit Damage Boost** | Pet slot 4, imprint when Crit is stable |
| 5 | **Weapon / Damage Amplification** | Standard DPS imprint |
| 6 | **Perfect** | Late polish on gloves/legs |

## Stigma headline (four slots)

**`Vaizel's Authority` 20 → `Griffon Arrow` 15–20 → `Supporting Fire` 15 → `Bow
of Blessing` 15** for party dungeons. Solo / open world: swap **`Bow of Blessing`**
for **`Mother Nature's Breath` 1+** when you have a fifth slot later; on **four
slots** keep **`Supporting Fire`** over **`Mother Nature's Breath`** for raw DPS.
**`Explosive Arrow`** stigma competes with **`Griffon Arrow`** — pick one arrow
stigma line, not both, until extra slots exist.

## Traps

- **`Deadshot` inside macro** — must **hold charge** with macro + LMB running.
- **`Assault Smite`** on flight-disabled bosses — useless in many dungeons.
- Stacking party buff stigmas (**`Bow of Blessing` + `Vaizel`**) without CDR — walk DPS loss.
