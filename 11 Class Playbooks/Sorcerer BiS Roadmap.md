# Sorcerer BiS Roadmap

> **In one sentence:** the Sorcerer (마도성) is a **Spellbook burst mage** whose
> Season 1 plan stacks **Accuracy and Smite**, pushes **`Blaze`** / **`Wish of
> Concentration`** breakpoints, and runs a **fire macro** with **`Delayed
> Explosion`** and stigma buffs on manual keys.

## Why it matters

Sorcerer is the reference **main DPS** in hub copy: instant-cast fire with ice
control. Global launch is **four stigma slots, cap 20** — Korean 25-level stigma
guides over-budget shards.

Mechanics: [[Stat Reference]], [[Item Level vs Combat Power]],
[[Progression Phases Roadmap]]. Rotation: [[Sorcerer Skills and Stigmas]].

## Season 1 baseline

Level **45**, gates **0–3,800**, Unique ceiling, no Heroic. See
[[Global Launch Differences]].

## Stat priority

Mage row in [[Stat Reference]]: **Accuracy → Smite → Critical**.

| Rank | Stat | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Accuracy** | Every fire tick and stigma debuff must land |
| 2 | **Smite** | **`Robe of Flame`**, **`Fire Mark`**, stigma **`Element Enhancement`** |
| 3 | **Critical Hit** | **`Grace of Enhancement`** procs; stop near practical cap |
| 4 | **Weapon / Damage Amplification** | Imprint and Daevanion oranges |
| 5 | **Combat Speed** | More **`Flame Arrow`** weaves and **`Wish of Concentration`** uptime |
| 6 | **Cooldown Reduction** | Burst line and **`Hellfire`** cadence |
| — | **Max MP / sustain** | **`Absorb Essence`**, MP food in long raids |

## Phase plan

| Phase | Item level | What you are doing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0. Levelling** | 0–1,000 | Story Spellbook. Passives: **`Robe of Flame`** → **`Fire Mark`** → **`Absorb Essence`**. **Alchemy** for craft path |
| **1. First Uniques** | 1,000–1,600 | Krao / Draupnir. **`Blaze`** and **`Wish of Concentration`** toward 16; **`Flame Arrow`** after |
| **2. Two-star Conquest** | 1,600–2,200 | Urugugu / Vakron. Stigmas **`Element Enhancement`**, **`Delayed Explosion`** toward 15–20 |
| **3. Fire Temple band** | 2,200–2,700 | Enraged Kromede Spellbook or Dragon Lord craft. **`Blaze` 20**, **`Hellfire`** 16+. Ariel crystals ~+12 armour |
| **4. Ceiling** | 2,700–3,800 | Nuakum, Unique +10 belt/amulet. Four stigmas 15–20 |

## Stigma headline (four slots)

Default PvE: **`Element Enhancement` 20 → `Fire Wall` 20 → `Delayed Explosion`
20 → `Cold Storm` 20** (swap **`Cold Storm`** for **`Steel Barrier` 10–15** when
you need the move-speed shield). Burst opener uses **`Element Enhancement`** →
**`Delayed Explosion`** → **`Winter's Shackles`** manually — see skills note.

## Gear headline

**Spellbook** (Alchemy) + **Guard**. Same Unique ladder as [[Spiritmaster Gear and
Slots]] with weapon type changed. Rings: **`Blaze`**, **`Bittercold Wind`**, **`Firestorm`**.

## Traps

- **`Hellfire` in the macro** — only stage-one charge; full charge is manual.
- Chasing Crit before Accuracy on debuff-heavy kit.
- Copying **five-stigma / 25-level** KR screenshots.
- Blacksmithing instead of **Alchemy** for weapon craft.
