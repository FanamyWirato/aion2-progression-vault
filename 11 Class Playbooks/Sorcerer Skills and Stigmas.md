# Sorcerer Skills and Stigmas

> **In one sentence:** weave **`Flame Arrow`** with **`Bittercold Wind` → `Blaze` →
> `Firestorm` → `Wish of Concentration`**, burst with **`Element Enhancement` →
> `Delayed Explosion` → `Winter's Shackles`**, and never full-charge **`Hellfire`**
> in the macro.

## Core PvE loop

1. **Sustain macro (hold Macro + LMB):** core line from [[In-Game Macros and Hotbar Lines#Sorcerer (마도성)]] — left-click **`Flame Arrow`** weaves between skills.
2. **Burst (manual):** **`Element Enhancement`** → **`Delayed Explosion`** → **`Winter's Shackles`**; add **`Hellfire`** stage-one only when safe.
3. **Ice chain (levelling / control):** **`Ice Chain`** → **`Frost Burst`** when fire talents are under-leveled.
4. **Utility:** **`Steel Barrier`** stigma or active for movement; **`Defiance`** on knockdown bosses.

English names from [AION 2 Hub — Sorcerer](https://aion2hub.com/classes/sorcerer). Korean in parentheses for Inven.

## Stigmas — four slots

KR high-end: **`Element Enhancement`**, **`Fire Wall`**, **`Delayed Explosion`**, **`Cold Storm`**, sometimes **`Steel Barrier`**.

| Slot | Stigma | KR | Target | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **`Element Enhancement`** | 원소 강화 | **20** | Damage amp debuff before burst |
| 2 | **`Fire Wall`** | 불의 장벽 | **15–20** | Sustained fire damage field |
| 3 | **`Delayed Explosion`** | 지연 폭발 | **20** | Resistance shred + burst |
| 4 | **`Cold Storm`** | 냉기 폭풍 | **15–20** | AoE; flex **`Steel Barrier`** for survival |

**Shard order:** **`Delayed Explosion` 5** → **`Element Enhancement` 10** → **`Fire Wall` 10** → push **`Blaze`/`Wish`** actives → cap stigmas 15–20.

## Daevanion (headline)

1. **Nezekan** — **`Blaze`**, **`Wish of Concentration`**, orange CDR / combat speed.
2. **Zikel** — **`Robe of Flame`**, **`Fire Mark`** passives.
3. **Vaizel** — Accuracy to floor.
4. **Triniel** — damage blues; HP only if **`Vitality Evaporation`** build.

See [[Daevanion Boards]].

[[In-Game Macros and Hotbar Lines#Sorcerer (마도성)]] — one-step core line + LMB. Manual burst and **`Hellfire`** as above.

## Traps

- Full-charge **`Hellfire`** in macro — wastes cast and locks movement.
- **`Element Enhancement`** after **`Delayed Explosion`** — wrong order; amp first.
- Ice-only skill tree while fire stigma loadout — mismatched points.
