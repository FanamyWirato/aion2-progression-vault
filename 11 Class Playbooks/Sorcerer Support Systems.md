> **In one sentence:** Arcana buys **`Blaze` 4**, **`Wish of Concentration` 4**, and
> **`Robe of Flame`** levels; Pantheon **Wisdom + Destruction/Time**; pets **Intelligence
> camp** then Smite on Special 4.

## Arcana (pattern)

Mirror **`Robe of Flame`** / **`Fire Mark`**; Parchment **`Blaze` 4** + **`Delayed Explosion`** stigma does not get Arcana — use **`Hellfire`** or **`Winter's Shackles`** flex; Compass **`Wish of Concentration` 4**; Chalice lock **`Blaze`**. Protect rows with Mysterious Crystals.

Sets: **4-piece PvE attack** + **4-piece crit/crit damage** when available — see [[Arcana Sets and Meta]].

## Consumables

Attack food, MP food on long fights without **`Absorb Essence`** levels.
