# Spiritmaster Gear and Slots

> **In one sentence:** Spiritmaster uses an **Orb** (Alchemy) and the universal
> **Guard**, and Season 1 finishes on **Enraged Kromede or crafted Dragon Lord
> Orb**, **Nuakum armour**, and imprint lines that buff **`Combustion`**, **`Elemental
> Fusion`**, and **`Spirit Strike`** passives.

## Why it matters

Armour drops are class-agnostic; the **Orb** is the scarce weapon roll. Crafting
is **Alchemy**, not Blacksmithing — the same profession as Sorcerer spellbooks.

Shared mechanics: [[Weapons]], [[Armor]], [[Accessories]], [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]].
Dungeon ladder matches [[Cleric Gear and Slots]] / [[Templar Gear and Slots]] with
weapon type changed.

## Main hand — Orb

| Target | Grade | Item Lv | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Season 1 dropped BiS** | Unique | 84 | **Fire Temple Conquest** — Enraged Kromede weapon table; Weapon Change Voucher if wrong type |
| **Season 1 crafted BiS** | Unique | tier-dependent | **Alchemy** Dragon Lord Orb line; extra soul-imprint line |
| Second step | Unique | 76 | **Urugugu Canyon** Aulamus |
| First keeper | Unique | 68 | **Krao Cave** Rupture |
| Levelling | Rare / Epic | 45-band | Story Orb |
| **Not at launch** | Heroic | — | Ludra-tier; use Kromede or craft |

**Manastones:** Accuracy to content floor, then Attack Power, then Smite.

**Imprint order:** Weapon Damage Amplification → Combat Speed → Smite / Damage
Amplification.

## Off-hand — Guard

Same as all classes: **Bakarma** (Draupnir) → **Aulamus / Vakron**. Take Unique
Guard before over-investing in Orb enhancement.

## Armour and accessories

Korean Spiritmaster gear guide (Inven 8165) priorities:

| Slot | Imprint / stat focus |
| --- | --- |
| Weapon / Guard | Damage amp, weapon damage amp, combat speed, multi-hit, Might, Accuracy |
| Gloves | **Combat Speed**, Perfect, passives |
| Chest | **Damage Amplification**, passives |
| Legs | Damage resistance, Perfect |
| Cloak | Smite, passives — **`Spirit Strike`**, **`Mental Focus`**, **`Element Unification`** |
| Earrings / necklace | Passives above; Accuracy/Crit if missing |
| Rings | **Active skill levels:** **`Combustion`**, **`Elemental Fusion`**, **`Jointstrike: Curse`**, **`Summon: Fire Spirit`**; add Earth/Water levels if still below 16 |

Belt and amulet: Unique +10 scroll ceiling on global — see [[Accessories]].

## Acquisition order

1. Story/field fill.
2. Draupnir **Bakarma Guard**.
3. Krao **Rupture Orb** (or first Conquest orb).
4. **Vakron** seven-piece armour.
5. Fire Temple Orb or **Alchemy Dragon Lord** Orb.
6. **Nuakum** armour.
7. Unique +10 belt and amulet.
8. Stop at Unique ceiling.

## Traps

- Blacksmithing instead of **Alchemy**.
- Rings imprinted for wrong actives (PvP fear skills).
- Imprinting a tradable Orb you meant to sell — [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]].
