# Spiritmaster Skills and Stigmas

> **In one sentence:** you maintain **three element spirits**, snapshot **buffs
> into `Summon: Ancient Spirit`**, keep **`Jointstrike: Corrode`** up, and spend
> **`Elemental Fusion`** at four elemental stacks — and at Season 1 you do it with
> **four stigma slots capped at 20**, not the six-stigma KR endgame.

## Why it matters

Skill and stigma levels are the cheapest Combat Power on the orb. Spiritmaster
damage is **front-loaded into buff order** and **spirit AI**; the skills note
is the difference between a strong solo farmer and a player who “has no damage
without summons.”

Systems: [[Stigmas]], [[Daevanion Boards]]. Plan: [[Spiritmaster BiS Roadmap]].
Active names below follow [AION 2 Hub — Spiritmaster](https://aion2hub.com/classes/spiritmaster)
(global list, 2026-09). Korean in parentheses for Inven searches.

## Core PvE loop

1. **Pre-pull (optional PvE):** **`Assault Terror`** (강습 공포) if you use it —
   avoids losing buffs to a knockback during cast bar. Else start with **`Flame
   Blessing`** (불길의 축복).
2. **Buffs before Ancient:** **`Flame Blessing`** → **`Enhance: Spirit's
   Benediction`** (stigma, 강화: 정령의 가호) → **`Summon: Ancient Spirit`**
   (stigma, 고정). Hold or tap per UI; Ancient lasts ~30 s and grants **4-element
   stacks** when it casts.
3. **Debuffs:** **`Jointstrike: Corrode`** (stigma, 협공: 부식) with or immediately
   after Ancient; **`Jointstrike: Curse`** (협공: 저주) for DoT and damage taken
   amp on spirits.
4. **Spirits:** **`Summon: Water / Earth / Fire Spirit`** on cooldown (macro slot
   1 in Korean guides). Wind for heal if needed.
5. **Filler:** left-click **`Cold Shock`** procs; spirit hits build stacks →
   **`Elemental Fusion`** (원소융합). **`Dimensional Control`** (공간지배) fires
   off spirit skill usage.
6. **Re-buff:** refresh **`Spirit's Benediction`** ~10 s before Ancient comes
   off cooldown so the next Ancient snapshot is buffed (guides cite ~2–3% from
   tight timing).

**Cancel trick:** use **`Jointstrike: Curse`** to shorten **`Corrode`** cast
recovery in high-CDR setups (Inven cycle posts).

## Stigmas — four slots, cap 20

KR endgame runs **five to six** stigmas (`Destructive Jointstrike`, **`Siphon`**, etc.).
**Global Season 1:** four slots, max **20** — use the core four only.

### Season 1 PvE loadout

| Slot | Stigma | KR | Target | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **`Enhance: Spirit's Benediction`** | 강화: 정령의 가호 | **20** | 10 s PvE damage amp + DR for you **and** spirits; heal tick. **Not affected by CDR increase** — cast before Ancient |
| 2 | **`Summon: Ancient Spirit`** | 소환: 고대의 정령 | **20** | Biggest DPS stigma; spirit casts → fusion charges. **15:** fewer autos before skill; **20:** +20% spirit stats |
| 3 | **`Jointstrike: Corrode`** | 협공: 부식 | **20** | 20 s debuff: spirits deal +10% damage, DoT. Keep uptime |
| 4 | **`Flame Blessing`** | 불길의 축복 | **20** | On-hit procs; pairs with Smite build |

**Later KR flex (not in four-slot launch):** **`Jointstrike: Destructive Attack`**
(파멸의 공세, channeled burst), **`Siphon`** (흡인), **`Assault Terror`**, **`Seize
Magic`** (PvP).

### PvP flex (four slots)

Same core three (**Benediction, Ancient, Corrode**); fourth **`Seize Magic`** or
**`Cry of Terror`** instead of **`Flame Blessing`**. Fear tools are the class
identity in PvP.

### Shard order (newbie board)

1. **`Spirit's Benediction` 10**, **`Corrode` 1**, **`Ancient Spirit` 5**, flex 1.
2. **`Ancient Spirit` 15 → `Spirit's Benediction` 20 → `Ancient Spirit` 20**.
3. Side skills (`Curse` 15, etc.) as needed.
4. **`Flame Blessing` 20**, then **`Corrode` 20**.

KR **25** priority when cap rises: **Ancient → Corrode → Flame Blessing** (Inven
8165).

## Daevanion routing (headline)

From MetaBot and mage-style boards:

1. **Nezekan** — **`Spirit Strike`** skill nodes first (double value with spirits).
2. **Zikel** — **`Consecutive Countercurrent`** and **`Element Unification`** nodes.
3. **Vaizel / Triniel** — orange damage nodes; Accuracy and Smite blues.
4. **Azphel** at 45 on global — PvP board if present; PvE priority stays Nezekan/Zikel.

Full board graphs: [[Daevanion Boards]]; verify node names on your client.

### In-game macro (PvE)

Full setup: [[In-Game Macros and Hotbar Lines#Spiritmaster (정령성)]].

| Hotbar column | Stack **bottom → top** |
| --- | --- |
| **Jointstrike line** | **`Jointstrike: Corrode`** → **`Jointstrike: Curse`** → **`Dimensional Control`** → **`Combustion`** |
| **Spirit line** | **`Elemental Fusion`** → **`Summon: Water Spirit`** → **`Summon: Earth Spirit`** → **`Summon: Fire Spirit`** |

**Macro (2 steps, 10 ms):** Jointstrike column **first**, then Spirit column. **Hold
Macro + LMB.** [Inven 8165](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/aion2/6454/8165) uses the
same split (skills column 2 = fusion combo, column 1 = three summons).

**Manual boss opener:** optional **`Assault Terror`** → **`Flame Blessing`** → **`Enhance:
Spirit's Benediction`** → **`Summon: Ancient Spirit`** + stigma **`Jointstrike: Corrode`**
→ then hold macro. **`Ancient Spirit`**, buffs, and **`Jointstrike: Destructive Attack`**
(charge) stay **out** of the macro. Do not macro-charge **`Elemental Fusion`**.

## Traps

- **Ancient before buffs** — see roadmap.
- **Five-stigma guides** on a four-slot client.
- Maxing **`Soul's Cry`** before **`Combustion` / `Elemental Fusion`** in PvE.
- Forgetting **`Dismiss`** when swapping spirit sets for mechanics.
