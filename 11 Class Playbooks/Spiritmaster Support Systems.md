# Spiritmaster Support Systems

> **In one sentence:** Spiritmaster Arcana is a **skill-level shopping list** for
> **`Combustion`**, **`Elemental Fusion`**, and passives **`Spirit Strike` /
> `Mental Focus`**, with **Max Attack Power** on Mirror/Bell when you run Perfect
> — same exception as Chanter.

## Why it matters

Spirits scale from **your** stats at summon and from **skill levels** on fusion
skills. Arcana and pets are how you reach **16/20** breakpoints without waiting
on weekly stigma shards alone.

Mechanics: [[Arcana Cards and Types]], [[Species Understanding]], [[Pantheon Stats]].
Shared pet farm order: [[Class Pet Farm Priority]] (Intelligence camp first).

| Rank | System | Spiritmaster note |

## Arcana (Inven 8165 targets)

Season 1: **five slots**, two sets — usually **Primal Vigor**-style PvE attack
(see [[Chanter Support Systems]] for the 4-piece rule) unless you lack pieces.

| Slot | Best skill mix (levels) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chalice** | **`Spirit Strike`**, **`Mental Focus`**, **`Elemental Fusion`** or **`Summon: Fire Spirit`**, **`Cold Shock`** or **`Element Unification`** | **`Spirit Strike` 4** if possible |
| **Parchment** | **`Elemental Fusion` 4**, **`Summon: Fire Spirit` 3**, **`Cold Shock` 1**, **`Jointstrike: Curse`** or **`Dimensional Control` 1** | Fusion must be high |
| **Compass** | **`Combustion` 4**, **`Rapid Scattershot` 2+**, **`Summon: Water Spirit` 2**, flex | Combustion 4 is mandatory in guide |
| **Bell** | **`Spirit Strike` 4**, **`Element Unification` 3**, **`Spirit Revitalization` 1**, flex | |
| **Mirror** | **`Mental Focus` 4**, **`Corrode` 3**, flex | **Max Attack Power** lord stat when running Perfect — Chanter/Spiritmaster exception ([[Chanter BiS Roadmap]]) |
| **Key / scale slots** | Damage amp, weapon damage amp, crit damage | Fill with guardian stat cards |

Spend **Mysterious Crystals** protecting **`Elemental Fusion`** and **`Combustion`**
rows first.

## Pantheon

Default mage stack: **Wisdom** (Smite) + **Destruction** or **Time** (Combat Speed).
No Spiritmaster-only artwork requirement in Season 1 sources.

## Pets

Same universal order as other DPS: **Intelligence** family camp first ([[Class Pet Farm Priority]]).
Slot **4** Smite (3% / 9% lines); Accuracy and Crit on 3/6/9 until dungeon caps.

**Arcana note:** Chanter/Spiritmaster guides allow **Max AP / Perfect** on Mirror
and Bell instead of pure Additional Attack — only if you actually stack Perfect
from **`Spirit Strike`** and gear.

## Consumables

Attack food, MP only if you skip Water spirit content. HP food for solo when
Wind spirit is not out.

## Traps

- Empty Arcana slots while refining one card to Unique.
- Copying Chanter **Mirror** (heal passives) — Spiritmaster wants **`Mental Focus`**
  / **`Spirit Strike`**.
- Pet **Special** race before Intelligence camp done.
