# Templar Support Systems

> **In one sentence:** everything outside gear and skills exists, for a
> Templar, to buy two stats that gear cannot supply enough of — **Block**, for
> Fury uptime, and **Cooldown Reduction**, for the Judgment loop — and the
> systems below are ranked by how much of those two they deliver per hour.

## Why it matters

A Templar's stat priority (see [[Templar BiS Roadmap]]) puts Accuracy and
Block at the top as thresholds, then Damage Resistance and Cooldown Reduction.
Gear covers Accuracy comfortably. Gear covers Block only through the Guard's
imprint line, Greaves rolls and Dexterity, and it covers Cooldown Reduction
essentially not at all — [[Wings Acquisition]] calls Cooldown Reduction "the
scarcest stat in the whole game".

That makes the support systems load-bearing rather than optional for this
class specifically. A Templar who skips the Pantheon and the titles is not
slightly behind; they are missing the two stats their build runs on.

## Relative worth, for a Season 1 Templar

Ranked by power delivered per hour invested. This is my judgement built on the
individual system notes, not a sourced ranking — no source publishes
cross-system comparisons.

| Rank | System | What it delivers to a Templar | Cost | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Arcana** | +400 item level at Unique across five slots, **plus** Freedom (Accuracy) and Illusion (Cooldown Reduction) Lord stats, **plus** a set bonus, **plus** the skill levels that get Judgment to 20. Four returns from one currency | Odd Energy and Mysterious Crystals; time | P3 onward, and it is the fastest Combat Power growth in the game |
| 2 | **Titles** | The two single best Templar items in the game: **Block 110** on the Saint title and **Cooldown Reduction 5%** on Through Hell and Back. Plus unlimited stacking owned effects | Time only. Nothing costs Kinah | Through Hell and Back in P1; the Saint in P6 |
| 3 | **Pantheon / Lord stats** | **Space** (Block) and **Illusion** (Cooldown Reduction) as percentage modifiers that keep pace with gear growth and never decay | Collection time; permanent and free thereafter | Trickles from level 22; a real project from P3 |
| 4 | **Pet / Species Understanding** | 500–800 Combat Power per useful reroll, and one of the very few places to buy Block outside gear | Slow. Gated behind pet levelling and understanding level 10 | P4 onward |
| 5 | **Wings** | **Talisra's Cooldown Reduction 4%** is the best single equip effect available to this class. Every pair also gives a permanent retention effect and Flight Power | Cheap to collect, expensive to buy the good ones | Collect from P0; buy Talisra when the season shop opens |
| 6 | **Wardrobe** | Combat Speed 3.7% at 100 skins, and Combat Speed 5% plus Cooldown Reduction 5% at 700. Also contributes to Combat Power directly, which surprises people | Very slow | Background, forever |

The ordering has one important qualification: **items 2 and 3 have the best
per-hour returns in the game and the worst per-*week* returns**, because
Monolith 30 and a filled artwork frame are long projects. Start them early
and let them run; do not try to finish them in a burst.

## Arcana

Season 1 means **five slots** — Chalice, Parchment, Compass, Bell, Mirror —
and **two sets**, Primal Vigor and Magic Armor. The Scales slot arrived KR
2026-02-25, the Stat Arcana in July and August 2026, and Frenzy and Pure Blood
on 2026-02-25. Every "4+2" and "4+4" Korean Arcana guide is therefore
unreadable for a launch Templar. See [[Arcana Cards and Types]] and
[[Arcana Sets and Meta]].

### The set: 4 Primal Vigor + 1 Magic Bell

With five slots and two sets that each cover exactly those five slots, 4+1 is
the only configuration that reaches a 4-piece bonus at all. Four Primal Vigor
gives **+150 PvE Attack while above 70% HP**. The fifth card is a Magic Bell,
and the reason is the inversion that catches everyone: the Lord stat is
attached to the *slot*, not to the set's philosophy, so the "offensive" Vigor
Bell gives you Justice (Defense) while the "defensive" Magic Bell gives you
Destruction (Attack Power).

You get no Magic Armor bonus from one card — the 2-piece needs two. The Magic
Bell is there purely for Destruction.

### Per-slot assignment for a Templar

| Slot | Family | Lord stat | What it converts into | Skill lines it can roll | What a Templar puts there |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Chalice** | Primal Vigor | **Time** | Combat Speed, Smite Resist | actives **and** passives, pool of roughly 22 | The flexible slot and the hardest to aim. Use it for **passive** lines — Fury above all — and to close the last one or two levels on an active. Four lines out of 22 is a bad lottery, so do not plan around it |
| **Parchment** | Primal Vigor | **Life** | Max HP, Regeneration chance | actives only, pool of **6** | **Judgment to 20.** Small pool means you can actually aim this slot. A Unique Parchment carries 4 levels on one line; add a ring and an earring level each and you are at 20 |
| **Compass** | Primal Vigor | **Freedom** | **Accuracy**, Evasion | actives only, pool of **6** | **Pummel** or **Vicious Strike** to 20. The Lord stat here is the Templar's gate stat |
| **Mirror** | Primal Vigor | **Illusion** | **Cooldown Reduction**, Iron Wall Penetration | passives only, pool of **5** | Warding Shield or Ironclad Defense. The Lord stat is the reason this slot is not flexible for a Templar |
| **Bell** | **Magic Armor** | **Destruction** | Attack Power | passives only, pool of **5** | Fury, Enhance Health, Guarding Seal |

### Why the generic launch build happens to be *more* correct for a Templar

The 4 Vigor + 1 Magic Bell layout is the vault's generic launch-era answer.
It is a better fit for a Templar than for any other class, for two reasons
that are worth spelling out because they change what you would trade away:

- **Freedom on the Compass.** [[Pantheon Stats]] deprioritises Freedom because
  "accuracy overflows against most bosses" — true at KR endgame, false for a
  Season 1 Templar who is still climbing to about 1,100 Accuracy. Freedom
  converts at 0.2% per point, so a Vigor Compass is paying directly into the
  threshold that gates your aggro.
- **Illusion on the Mirror.** Cooldown Reduction is generically scarce and
  specifically load-bearing here, because the Warding Strike → Judgment loop
  *is* the class. A Vigor Mirror is worth more to a Templar than to a class
  whose rotation is not cooldown-gated.

### The Block temptation, considered and rejected

A **Magic Chalice** carries **Space**, which converts into Movement Speed and
**Block**. For a class whose damage runs on Block, that looks like an obvious
swap. Do not take it, for three reasons:

1. It breaks the 4-piece Primal Vigor, which is the only set bonus you can
   reach at five slots.
2. It costs you Time's Combat Speed, which is the highest-value utility stat
   in the game and feeds both your damage and your block-roll frequency.
3. **Space is available from Pantheon statues and the Empyrean Traces
   collection, where Time and Illusion are not** — statues supply Freedom,
   Death and Space in Season 1, and the Traces milestone pays a bonus +20 to
   Space specifically. Buy Block from the cheap source, not the expensive one.

That third point is the general principle for this whole note: **each Lord
stat has exactly one or two realistic sources, so allocate by scarcity rather
than by preference.**

### Arcana progression for a Templar

1. **Fill all five slots with whatever grade you hold.** Empty slots are the
   only wrong answer. Even a Common card is +20 item level.
2. **Upgrade by grade, ignoring skill lines.** Common +20 → Rare +40 →
   Heritage +60 → Unique +80 per slot.
3. From item level 1,600 to 2,000, spend **essentially all Odd Energy** on
   Transcendence, not Expeditions. Farm stage 3 S-rank for Rare cards.
4. Only once the grades are settled do skill lines matter. Then: Parchment and
   Compass for your genuine main actives at 20; the Chalice for passive lines
   and gap-filling.
5. **Match a level-2 Arcana line to a skill that will finish at 16**, not a
   level-3 or level-4 line. That is wasted budget.
6. Heritage cards from Transcendence stage 6, Unique Compass and Bell from
   stage 9, Unique Chalice from stage 10 only.

**Cost note that may not apply at launch.** In current KR, nobody crafts a
Vigor or Magic Chalice, because Frenzy and Pure Blood are stat-identical
reskins costing roughly 100,000 to 600,000 Kinah against roughly 5,000,000 for
Vigor. If global ships with Frenzy and Pure Blood available, **craft those
instead** and treat the set-effect difference as the only real choice. If it
ships Season 1-pure, you are paying the Vigor price and there is nothing to be
done.

## Pantheon and Lord stats

The Pantheon is the largest source of Empyrean Lord attributes, Arcana is the
second, and the Empyrean Traces collection is a large one-off block. All three
feed the same ten pools against the same 200-point-per-domain cap, so they
cannot be planned separately. Mechanics in [[Pantheon Stats]] and
[[Pantheon Statues and Frames]].

The property that makes this system matter and also makes people skip it:
**Lord stats do not raise item level or Combat Power.** They are invisible to
gear checks. That is a trap for anyone optimising a number and an edge for
anyone who is not.

### Lord stat priority for a Templar

[[Pantheon Stats]] gives a generic PvE tank ordering of Justice > Life >
Space > Fate > Time > Illusion > Death > Wisdom > Destruction > Freedom. A
Templar should reorder it:

| Rank | Domain | Lord | Converts into | Why it moves for this class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Space** | Israphel | Movement Speed, **Block** | Up from 3rd. Block is simultaneously this class's mitigation and its damage engine. No other tank has that property |
| 2 | **Illusion** | Kaisinel | **Cooldown Reduction**, Endurance Penetration | Up from 6th. The Judgment loop is cooldown-gated, and Cooldown Reduction is otherwise almost unobtainable |
| 3 | **Justice** | Nezekan | Defense, Perfect proc chance | Down from 1st, but still high — and it comes free on the colossus |
| 4 | **Time** | Siel | Combat Speed, Smite Resist | Also free on the colossus. Combat Speed is more block rolls and more Judgment presses |
| 5 | **Life** | Yustiel | Max HP, Regeneration chance | The HP pool that lets you survive a mechanic |
| 6 | **Freedom** | Vaizel | **Accuracy**, Evasion | Up from 10th — **but only while you are under the accuracy threshold.** Once you clear ~1,100, drop it back to last |
| 7 | **Fate** | Marchutan | MP, Endurance proc | MP matters more on this class than most, because the rotation is MP-hungry. Endurance halves incoming damage when it procs |
| 8 | **Death** | Triniel | Critical, Regeneration Penetration | A pure curve |
| 9 | **Wisdom** | Lumiel | Smite, MP cost reduction | Real, but a curve, and expensive because it comes only from artworks |
| 10 | **Destruction** | Zikel | Attack Power, Perfect Resist | Comes free on the colossus; do not spend slots on it |

Note that rank 6 is **phase-dependent**, which is unusual and worth
remembering when you re-read this note later. Freedom is a top-half domain for
a Season 1 Templar climbing to the accuracy threshold and a bottom-of-the-list
domain once past it.

### Frame layout

The domain-to-slot restriction is the binding constraint, and it is what makes
a Templar's Pantheon plan differ from a damage dealer's:

| Slot class | Slots | Domains it can supply | Magnitude | Templar allocation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Colossus** | 1 | Justice, Time, Destruction | 35–45 per domain | Locked. Take the best colossus you can get; it hands you ranks 3, 4 and 10 for free |
| **Statues** | 4 | Freedom, Death, **Space** (Season 1) | 8–12 per domain | **Space first and as many slots as it will fill.** This is the only realistic Space source. Then Freedom while you are under the accuracy threshold, then Death |
| **Artworks** | 12 | Wisdom, **Illusion**, Life, Fate | 3–5 per domain | **Illusion first**, across as many frames as you can fill. A damage dealer grinds artworks for Wisdom; a Templar grinds them for Illusion. Then Life, then Fate |

**The Empyrean Traces bonus is disproportionately good for a Templar.**
Completing the Traces of the Lord collection gives **+20 to all ten domains,
plus an extra +20 to Space and Time** at the 160-trace milestone. That extra
Space is landing directly on your number-one domain, on top of a collection
you should be doing anyway for the Revelation Amulet scrolls. It is the single
cheapest Block in the plan. See [[Empyrean Traces and Feathers]].

**Conversion and scale.** Points convert linearly with no diminishing returns
inside the 200 cap, at a rate that is **disputed between 0.1% and 0.2% per
point per linked stat**. At 0.1%, 45 points in a domain is 4.5% on each of its
two linked stats. A complete Season 1 Pantheon reaches roughly 45 in its best
domains and about 359 points total, so roughly 36 percentage points spread
across twenty combat stats — small per stat, large in aggregate, and free. The
0.1-versus-0.2 question doubles or halves every derived number here, so treat
all of it as **medium confidence**.

## Pet family and Species Understanding

Pets contribute two separate things: each pet's own **held options**, which
differ by family, and the shared **Species Understanding** slots, which are
identical regardless of which family you levelled. Understanding is where the
stats you care about live. Mechanics in [[Species Understanding]].

### Understanding slot allocation

There are nine understanding option slots and the pools differ by slot. Two
templates exist in the Korean community and they conflict, so both are given.

**The generic PvE template** (the one most Korean guides publish):

| Slots | Take | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 and 9 | **Smite (강타)** | **Smite appears only on slots 3 and 9.** Target 1.5%+ per slot and 3%+ combined |
| 2, 5 and 8 | **Maximum Attack Power (최대 공격력)** | Best PvE efficiency of the offensive options |
| 6 (the defence slot) | **Damage Tolerance** or Weapon Damage Tolerance | The generic all-purpose survivability pick |
| 1, 4 and 7 | Accuracy and Critical | Described as the easy slots — fill them slowly |

**The Templar override** from the Korean Guardian board: put **Block on slots
1, 4, 6 and 7**.

**How I reconcile them.** The two templates agree completely on slots 2, 3, 5,
8 and 9 and disagree only on 1, 4, 6 and 7. So:

- **Slots 3 and 9: Smite.** No disagreement, and it is the only place to get
  it.
- **Slots 2, 5 and 8: Maximum Attack Power.** No disagreement.
- **Slots 1, 4 and 7: Block while you are below the Fury threshold**,
  Accuracy and Critical after. These are the cheap slots to redo, so the
  choice is reversible.
- **Slot 6: Block first, then Damage Tolerance.** This is the genuine
  judgement call. Slot 6 is the single defence slot, and you are choosing
  between Fury uptime (Block) and flat percentage mitigation (Damage
  Tolerance), which is rank 2 versus rank 3 in the class's stat priority.
  Take Block while Fury is flickering; take Damage Tolerance once it is
  continuous.

**Efficiency data for the offensive slots**, from an Inven post on special-pet
understanding: **Maximum Attack Power 20 ≈ Additional Attack 16 > Critical
Attack 20 (≈ Additional Attack 10–12) > Soulstone Additional Damage 16 (≈
Additional Attack 5)**, and the gap widens in Maximum Attack Power's favour as
your gear improves, because Maximum Attack Power benefits from Weapon Damage
Amplification and wardrobe percentages while Additional Attack does not.

The same post gives the amplification ordering: in PvE, **Damage Amplification
> Weapon Damage Amplification > Smite** in general — but **Smite > Weapon
Damage Amplification > Damage Amplification when your own Smite value is
low**. A Season 1 Templar's Smite is low, so Smite is the right call at
launch and the ordering inverts later.

### Understanding levels and locking

- **Target level 10.** At level 10 the chance of rolling a Hero-grade option
  goes from 1% to 5%. Almost every Korean guide stops there.
- **Lock nothing before level 9.** Locking an option raises both the Soul
  Crystal and the Kinah cost of every subsequent analysis. At level 9, lock
  only a Unique-maximum roll.
- Souls become **Soul Crystals** once a pet reaches level 5; below that you are
  levelling pets, not understanding.

### Pet families

Each family has different **held** options. Understanding is shared.

| Family | KR | Held options | Soul colour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intelligence (monkey) | 지성 | Attack, Status Effect Chance, Critical | Blue |
| Nature (spirit) | 자연 | Accuracy, Critical, Status Resist, Critical Resist | — |
| **Wild (mole)** | **야성** | Accuracy, Evasion, **Block**, Critical Resist, Status Resist | Red |
| Transform | 변형 | Evasion, HP, Status Resist | Yellow |

The generic collection order is **지성 → 자연 → 야성 → 변형**. **The Templar
reorder is 지성 → 야성 → 자연 → 변형**, because Wild is the only family whose
held options include Block. Intelligence stays first because it pays the
souls that fund everything else.

**Rainbow Crystals go only to special pets**, and ordinary souls must never go
into a special pet. Special pets have no Additional Accuracy or Additional
Evasion in their pool, so their realistic options are Additional Attack,
Maximum Attack Power, Critical Attack and Soulstone Additional Damage.

**Season 1 caveats, and they are large.** Pets became server-shared and their
soul costs were halved on KR 2026-01-07; the pet XP system was reworked
2026-04-08; pet attack and defence caps were raised from 100% to 120% in
Season 3. At launch, expect pets to be **per-character and considerably more
expensive** than every Korean guide describes. That pushes pet understanding
down the priority list at launch relative to where Korean guides put it —
which is why it sits at rank 4 rather than rank 2 in this note's table.

## Wings

Two separate things, and conflating them is the standard mistake. Every
distinct pair you acquire gives a permanent **retention effect** and roughly
+200 Flight Power that you keep forever regardless of what you wear. The
**equip effect** is a separate, larger block and it is the only reason to
actually wear a pair. See [[Wings Acquisition]] and [[Wings Enhancement]].

**Policy: collect every pair. Wear one.**

### What a Templar wears

| Priority | Wings (EN / KR) | Grade | Equip effect | Where | Why for a Templar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Talisra Wings (공허의 탈리스라)** | Unique | Additional Accuracy 35, **Cooldown Reduction 4%** | **Season Shop**, about 210,000 Oath Coins | Both of this class's scarce stats on one item. Nothing else comes close. Korean consensus already calls the seasonal wing the highest-value shop purchase and the one people most regret skipping — **and seasons end** |
| 2 | **Forest Spirit Wings (숲 정령)** | Unique | Boss Damage Amplification 3.5%, Boss Attack Power 95 | **Vakron's Sky Island** Expedition | The free Season 1 pick. You are farming Vakron for armor and the Guard anyway, and the Korean gearing thread says to keep running it until the wing drops |
| 3 | **Nightmare Wings (악몽)** | Unique | Boss Damage Amplification 3.5%, Critical Hit 35 | **Nightmare Exchange Shop**, about 160,000 Dream Fragments | Wins the pure boss parse. Talisra beats it *for a Templar* because Cooldown Reduction gates the rotation. Dream Fragments carry across seasons, so this is a banking target |
| 4 | **Black Fragment Wings (검은 파편)** | Unique | Critical Attack Power 95, Critical Hit 35, HP 500, Evasion 20, **Block 10** | **Krao Cave** Conquest, via material conversion | The defensive option with Block on it, from launch content |
| 5 | **Blue Wave Wings (푸른 파도)** | Unique | not documented; retention is PvE Damage Amplification 0.3%/level | **Draupnir** Expedition | Early and free |

**The Block sleeper: Sealing Wings (봉인).** Legendary grade, from **Sealed
Dungeons**, and its *retention* enhancement option is **Block, 5 per level,
reaching 50 at +10**. That is the cheapest Block in the entire plan and it
comes from daily content you are already running for Enhancement Stones.

**But — wing enhancement arrived on KR 2026-05-27.** Without it, retention
options do not grow, the Sealing Wings Block-50 plan does not exist, and wings
are a traversal item with an equip effect and nothing more. This is the single
largest Season 1 caveat in this note. Check whether the wing enhancement UI
exists on day one; if it does not, wings drop to rank 6 or 7 in the worth
table and Talisra is still worth buying purely for its equip effect.

### Free wing routes, worth doing regardless

- **Five story wings** — Lesser, Intermediate, Superior and Ultimate Daeva
  Wings plus Aullaeu Wings — arrive from the main story for zero effort, and
  are about 1,000 Flight Power between them.
- **Sealed Wing Feathers** convert 1:1 into wings and accumulate silently in
  your warehouse from daily Sealed Dungeon runs. Korean guides flag that most
  players have a stack they have never noticed. Check the warehouse before
  farming anything.
- **Pure Odyle** gathering: ten of them craft your first pair and permanently
  unlock the wing crafting menu.
- **Hidden Cube Keys**: five per day from Duty Missions, and cubes are one of
  two sources of Radiant Love Wings.

**Never dismantle wings for Enhancement Stones.** It is about 500 stones at
the cost of a permanent retention effect, and cash-shop and ranking wings are
Special grade with no stats at all and cannot be enhanced — buy those for
looks and use the wardrobe toggle.

## Titles

Titles are a progression system, not decoration, and the reason is the
**owned effect**: every title you acquire applies its owned bonus forever
whether or not you wear it, and owned effects stack across everything you own.
On top of that you equip **three at once** — one Attack, one Defense, one
Other. Mechanics and the full catalogue in [[Titles and Achievements]].

### The Templar title priority

| Priority | Title (EN / KR) | Grade | Equipped effect | Owned effect | How |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Through Hell and Back (나생문을 넘어선)** | Unique | **Cooldown Reduction 5%**, HP 800, Stamina 200 | **Block 10** | Clear the sealed dungeons **Distorted Cave → Crevice Cave → Rift Cave** in that order, then enter **Ruins of the Ancient City of Roah** on the far-west Verteron island (the Aulau Funeral Island Kibelisk is the access point) and complete all kill missions inside **15 minutes** |
| **2** | **Saint of Verteron / Saint of Altgard** | Unique | Defense Bonus 300, **Damage Tolerance 5%**, **Block 110** | PvE Damage Tolerance 1% | **Monolith level 30** in your home zone, fed by Empyrean Lord's Traces |
| **3** | **Teleportation Specialist** | Epic | **PvE Accuracy 72**, PvE Damage Boost 3.6% | PvE Defense 20 | Register all **61 Kibelisks** in your home zone |
| 4 | **Guardian of Verteron / Altgard** | Epic | PvE Damage Boost 3.7%, Smite 2% | PvE Damage Boost 1% | Monolith level 20 — the way-station to the Saint |
| 5 | **Dominator of Dimensions** | Unique | Defense Bonus 300, Evasion Bonus 90, PvE Damage Tolerance 5.5% | MP 20 | Obtain **25 wings** — free, if you follow the collect-everything wing policy above |
| 6 | **Empty Closet** | Unique | Combat Speed 5%, Natural HP Regen 40, **Cooldown Reduction 5%** | HP 50 | **700 skins.** A long project with two Templar-relevant stats at the end |
| 7 | **Fashionista** | Rare | Combat Speed 3.7% | Critical Hit 5 | 100 skins — the cheap rung of the same ladder |

**Priorities 1 and 2 are the two best items in this entire note.** Through
Hell and Back is roughly 30 minutes of work for Cooldown Reduction 5%, HP 800
and a permanent Block 10 — do it in Phase 1, before anything else in this
file. The Saint title's **Block 110** is the largest single Block source
available to a Season 1 Templar and it also carries Damage Tolerance 5%, which
is rank 3 in the class's stat priority; Monolith 30 is a Phase 6 project but
you should be feeding Traces into the Monolith from Phase 1 anyway for the
Revelation Amulet scrolls.

**Unverified: the slot categories.** [[Titles and Achievements]] confirms
three slots — Attack, Defense, Other — but does not state which category most
of the titles above sit in. Saint of Verteron reads as Defense and
Teleportation Specialist as Attack; Through Hell and Back, Empty Closet and
Dominator of Dimensions are all plausible in more than one slot. **You may not
be able to equip Through Hell and Back and Empty Closet simultaneously**, and
if so you take the Unique one. Check the UI and record the categories.

### Owned effects worth sweeping, on the Templar's own path

These accumulate from things you are already doing. All are owned effects, so
they apply forever after one completion:

- **Blacksmithing expert level 50** → PvE Damage Tolerance 1%. You are
  levelling Blacksmithing anyway; it makes your Longsword and your Guard.
- **Alchemy 300 successes** → PvE Damage Tolerance 0.5%; **Alchemy entry level
  50** → Critical Attack 2; **Alchemy expert 50** → Back Attack 3. You are
  levelling Alchemy to 25 in Phase 1 for Intermediate manastones.
- **Armorsmithing 300 successes** → PvE Damage Amplification 0.5%;
  **Armorsmithing entry 50** → Additional Attack 1.
- **Every dungeon, first clear** → a separate title on Exploration and on
  Conquest difficulty, so two owned effects per dungeon.
- **Rift of Time and Space, 50 uses** → PvE Defense 10. You are crossing to
  the other faction's map anyway for the second half of the Strongholds and
  Traces.

**Do not chase Special-grade titles for power.** Server-firsts and event
trophies carry no stats by design.

## Wardrobe

Low priority, but not zero, and it has one non-obvious property: **the
wardrobe contributes to Combat Power.** Beyond that, the skin ladder pays
titles at 10 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 300 / 500 / 700 / 1000, and two of those
titles are directly Templar-relevant — Fashionista at 100 for Combat Speed
3.7% and Empty Closet at 700 for Combat Speed 5% plus Cooldown Reduction 5%.

Practical policy: accumulate steadily, register every skin you pick up, and do
not chase the 300 and 500 milestones specifically — a Korean player's
assessment is that they are hard and their effects are mediocre. Use the
appearance toggle so you never wear a stat-free cosmetic wing or costume over
a stat-bearing one.

**Season 1 caveat:** the server-wide wardrobe arrived KR 2026-01-21. At launch
skins may be per-character, which makes the 700-skin title a much longer
project than Korean guides imply. See [[Wardrobe and Skin Collection]].

## Consumables

Cheapest and least glamorous item in this note, and worth a paragraph mostly
because two of its interactions are Templar-specific.

- **MP consumables matter more on this class than most.** The high-end
  rotation runs the MP bar to the floor within a minute, which is the entire
  reason the accessible Vicious-Judgment rotation is recommended over the
  Pummel-cancel one in [[Templar Skills and Stigmas]]. MP restoration is also
  why Shield Smite's specialisation 2 is described as essential, and why the
  Magic Armor Arcana 2-piece (1,500 MP when below 20% MP) is not as silly as
  it looks if you ever get a second Magic card.
- **HP potions interact with the Noble Belt.** The belt's Unique lines include
  **HP Potion Recovery %**, which is easy to read as filler and is not — it
  multiplies every potion you drink, on the one gear slot you never replace.
- **Cooking and Alchemy consumables are the least risky Kinah per hour** in
  the whole crafting system, because combo procs *double* the output of food
  and potions rather than producing a Splendent variant.
- **Consumable and skill buffs are excluded from Combat Power by design**, so
  a well-buffed Templar reads no higher than an unbuffed one. Do not let the
  number talk you out of them. (A bug at CP's introduction did count some buff
  skills; it was slated for a fix.)

NC states that the global cash shop's consumables are earnable through normal
play and that shop items "do not affect gear progression or long-term power".
Korea briefly sold combat-affecting consumables at its launch; whether global
does is an open question in [[Global Launch Differences]].

## Costs and math

- Arcana item level by grade, across five slots: Common +20 each (+100 total),
  Rare +40 (+200), Heritage +60 (+300), Unique +80 (+400).
- 4-piece Primal Vigor: +150 PvE Attack while above 70% HP. The 2-piece is +60,
  and the two tiers appear to replace rather than stack because they grant the
  same stat — unresolved, see [[Arcana Sets and Meta]].
- Vigor Chalice refinement roughly 5,000,000 Kinah; Frenzy or Pure Blood
  roughly 100,000 to 600,000 for an identical Lord stat. Two Korean sources
  disagree inside that range; the order-of-magnitude saving is not disputed.
- Lord stat conversion: linear inside a 200-point-per-domain cap, at either
  0.1% or 0.2% per point per linked stat. **Disputed.** A complete Season 1
  Pantheon is roughly 359 points, about 45 in its best domains.
- Empyrean Traces: +20 to all ten domains, plus +20 more to Space and Time at
  the 160-trace milestone.
- One pet understanding option rerolled to a useful stat: 500 to 800 Combat
  Power.
- Pet understanding level 10 raises the Hero-grade option chance from 1% to 5%.
- Talisra Wings about 210,000 Oath Coins; Nightmare Wings about 160,000 Dream
  Fragments. Both **medium confidence**, single Korean blog, and that blog
  carries an item-broker promotion.
- Every distinct wing pair: about +200 Flight Power plus its retention effect.
- Sealing Wings retention: Block +5 per enhancement level, 50 at +10 —
  **contingent on wing enhancement existing at launch**.
- Swapping to Talisra Wings was measured at roughly 800 to 1,000 Combat Power.
  Pre-Chapter-1 figure on a compressed CP scale; do not compare against
  post-Chapter-1 anchors.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Reading a 4+2 or 4+4 Arcana guide at launch.** You have five slots and two
  sets. Check the set list in the Arcana UI before spending anything.
- **Taking a Magic Chalice for the Block.** It breaks your only reachable set
  bonus and Space is cheaper from statues.
- **Upgrading Arcana grades before filling all five slots.** Filling an empty
  slot always beats improving a filled one.
- **Optimising the Pantheon for Combat Power.** Lord stats do not raise it, so
  optimising for CP makes you skip the system entirely.
- **Spending statue slots on Freedom after you have cleared the accuracy
  threshold.** Freedom is the one domain in the table whose rank changes with
  your gear.
- **Grinding artworks for Wisdom as a Templar.** Wisdom is the damage dealer's
  artwork target. Yours is Illusion.
- **Locking a pet understanding option before level 9.** It raises the Soul
  Crystal and Kinah cost of every subsequent analysis.
- **Feeding ordinary souls to a special pet**, or Rainbow Crystals to an
  ordinary one.
- **Skipping the seasonal wing.** The single most common regret in Korean
  community posts. Seasons end and the shop rotates.
- **Dismantling wings**, or buying a cash-shop wing expecting stats.
- **Treating titles as cosmetic**, or equipping only one when you have three
  slots.
- **Chasing Special-grade titles.** Zero stats by design.
- **Chasing the 300 and 500 skin milestones.** Hard, and the effects are weak.
- **Letting Nightmare tickets cap.** They recharge two per day and stockpile
  to 14, and Dream Fragments are how you eventually buy Nightmare Wings.

## Global launch notes

In descending order of how much a wrong guess costs a Templar:

1. **Wing enhancement** (KR 2026-05-27). Without it, retention effects do not
   grow and the Sealing Wings Block plan evaporates. This is the biggest
   single unknown in the note.
2. **Which Arcana sets and slots ship.** Five slots and two sets is the
   Season 1 assumption. Frenzy and Pure Blood arriving early would cut Chalice
   costs by roughly an order of magnitude; Scales arriving would change the
   whole configuration to 4+2.
3. **Server-wide pets** (2026-01-07) and **server-wide wardrobe**
   (2026-01-21). Both determine whether this work is per-character or per
   account, which is the single biggest factor in how much of it is worth
   doing.
4. **Pet soul costs**, halved on 2026-01-07. Launch-era pet work is
   significantly more expensive than every Korean guide describes.
5. **Whether the Combat Power display exists at all.** KR launched without it
   and added it four months in. If global launches without it, expect a
   third-party aggregator to become the party standard, exactly as Atull did.
6. **Which Pantheon statue and artwork domains exist.** The Season 1 statue
   pool is Freedom, Death and Space — the Space availability is what the
   Templar plan rests on.
7. **The English names for Smite, Damage Amplification and the ten Lord
   domains.** "Destiny" and "Fate" both appear for 운명; Smite renders as
   Strike, Heavy Blow and Crushing Blow across sources.

## Open questions

- [ ] Is Block genuinely available on pet understanding slots 1, 4, 6 and 7?
      This is single-source from the Korean Guardian board and it is the
      load-bearing claim in the pet section.
- [ ] Which title slot category each of the seven recommended titles sits in.
      Determines whether Through Hell and Back and Empty Closet compete.
- [ ] Whether the Lord stat conversion is 0.1% or 0.2% per point. Doubles or
      halves every Pantheon number here.
- [ ] Which five actives sit in the Templar's Parchment and Compass pools, and
      which five passives in the Bell and Mirror pools. No source publishes
      per-class Arcana skill pools, and this determines whether Judgment can
      actually be aimed on a Parchment.
- [ ] Whether Fury (격앙) is in the Chalice, Bell or Mirror passive pool. If it
      is not in any of them, the passive-levels plan has to come entirely from
      earring and cloak soul imprint.
- [ ] Talisra Wings' full equip-effect values, and whether the Cooldown
      Reduction 4% figure is right. Single Korean blog with a broker promotion
      attached.
- [ ] Whether the Season Shop exists at global launch and what its currency is
      called, since Talisra is the top wing recommendation.
- [ ] Whether Maximum Attack Power really benefits from wardrobe percentages,
      which is the mechanism behind the pet efficiency ordering. The Inven
      author marks it "verified" but does not show the test.
- [ ] Whether a Season 1 Templar can reach a Block figure at which Fury is
      continuous at all. If not, the entire Space / statue / pet / title Block
      chain is worth much less than this note assumes.
