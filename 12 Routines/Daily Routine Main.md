# Daily Routine Main

> **In one sentence:** three tickable daily lists — about 30 minutes, about 60
> minutes, and completionist — built around a budget of three Odyle reward
> claims a day and a login-to-logout order that only makes you travel twice.

## Why it matters

Only one thing in AION 2 is genuinely daily: the five Duty Quests. Everything
else on this page is either a weekly budget you happen to be spending today, or
a charge that banks for several days. Getting that distinction right is the
difference between a routine you can sustain for a season and one you resent by
week three.

What makes a daily list necessary anyway is the Expedition reward counter and
the Odyle Energy pool. The counter recharges three times a day and does not roll
over. The pool regenerates 10 every three hours and **stops dead at its cap**, so
a full pool left overnight is destroyed energy — roughly 80 per day for a free
player, which is two reward cubes. Those two facts mean a player who logs in
briefly every day genuinely out-earns a player who plays the same total hours in
two long sessions, and it is the only place in the game where that is true.

## How it works

### The clocks this list runs on

| Reset | Time (KR) | Refills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 05:00 | daily | Duty Quests, urgent Supply Request, Nightmare attempt, Shugo Festa keys, Dimensional Invasion, field Kinah cap, Expedition reward count 1 of 3 |
| 13:00 | daily | Expedition reward count 2 of 3 |
| 21:00 | daily | Expedition reward count 3 of 3 |
| On the hour | hourly | Shugo Festa minigames, Black Cloud Trading Company stock |
| :30 past | hourly | Dimensional Invasion |
| Every 3 hours | 02:00, 05:00, 08:00, 11:00, 14:00, 17:00, 20:00, 23:00 | Spacetime Rift |

Global reset times are unpublished as of 2026-09-06; these are **KR-derived**.
See the Global launch notes.

The practical consequence of the three Expedition recharges: if you log in once
a day, log in **after 21:00 server time** and you will have all three counts
available in one session. Logging in at 19:00 leaves one count stranded.

### The daily energy budget

| Player | Energy per day | Reward claims per day at 40 each |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 120 (80 passive + 40 from the weekly conversion crafts amortised) | **3** |
| Membership | 200 (120 passive + 40 conversion + 40 Wind Breeze amortised) | **5** |

Where those 3 claims should go changes with your item level, and this is the
single highest-value decision on the page:

| Your item level | Spend the day's claims on | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000-1,400 | 1 claim on Krao Cave or Draupnir Conquest; let the rest bank | You are still doing one-time content, which pays far better per hour. Just do not let the pool cap |
| 1,400-1,700 | 3 on Conquest Krao Cave and Draupnir | Draupnir's guaranteed reward at 14 openings is a free Unique armour piece. Stop running it once you have taken it |
| 1,600-2,000 | **all 3 on Transcendence** | The most emphatic recommendation in the Korean guides. Arcana return more power per point of energy than any Expedition. Farm the highest stage you can S-rank, not the highest you can survive |
| 2,000-2,400 | 2 Transcendence + 1 Vakron Sky Island Conquest | Vakron is the armour set and the Wings of the Forest Spirit materials |
| 2,400-3,500 | 1-2 Transcendence stage 6+, 1-2 Fire Temple or Ferocious Horn Den Conquest | Heritage Arcana plus the Enraged weapon line |
| 2,700+ on a raid week | Bank 160 energy for Sanctuary | A Sanctuary reward costs **80**, two ordinary cubes. Start banking three days before raid night |

Never open Transcendence stage 1 or 2 reward boxes. Their grade ceiling does not
justify the energy. See [[Arcana Refinement and Farming]].

### Priority ranks used below

| Rank | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **P0** | Permanent loss if missed. Do it before anything else |
| **P1** | Real loss, but recoverable within the same week |
| **P2** | Worth doing; nothing is lost by skipping a day |
| **P3** | Optional. First to cut |

## Unlock and gates

- Duty Quests, Supply Requests, Shugo Festa and Dimensional Invasion: level 45.
- Expedition and Nightmare: level 45 and item level 1,000. **Conquest reward
  counts do not begin to accumulate until item level 1,000**, which is the reason
  to push to 1,000 quickly rather than lingering at 900.
- Transcendence: nominally Combat Power 1,200, realistically 1,400-1,500.
- Spacetime Rift: opens on the three-hour schedule; the route into the opposing
  faction's territory for the one-time sweep.

## Progression path

### Variant A — Express, about 30 minutes

This is the version that protects everything that expires today and defers
everything that banks. Run this on four or five days a week and batch the rest
into one weekend block and you will not fall behind on anything permanent.

Best run **after 21:00 server time**, so all three Expedition counts are up.

- [ ] Claim attendance, mail, pass and season rewards *(2 min · free · P0)*
- [ ] Glance at the Odyle bar. Over 80 percent of cap means cube claims are mandatory tonight *(10 sec · free · P0)*
- [ ] Open the journal and accept all five Duty Quest: Mission entries at once, plus the urgent Supply Request, before travelling anywhere *(1 min · P0)*
- [ ] Reroll the Duty Quest reward offers before accepting. Take Supply Request items, Small Odyle Energy and challenge tickets over raw Kinah, and fish for Wanted-target quests, which carry the Unique gear chance *(1 min · P0)*
- [ ] Complete the five Duty Missions, batching their field objectives into a single trip *(10-12 min · 5 per server per day · Abyss Points, Enhance Stones, Kinah, Hidden Cube Keys · P0)*
- [ ] Hand in the urgent Supply Request if you already hold the items *(3 min · daily tab · ~10,000 Abyss Points · P1)*
- [ ] Claim all three Expedition reward counts, solo, at the highest difficulty you can clear in five minutes or less *(15 min · 3 counts, 120 energy · gear and Bound Kinah · P0)*
- [ ] Deposit everything to the Server Warehouse and log out in a capital *(1 min · P2)*

**Deferred deliberately:** Nightmare (banks 7 days), Dimensional Invasion (banks
7 days), Shugo Festa, field hunting, gathering, Abyss, Market. None of these
lose you anything by tomorrow.

### Variant B — Standard, about 60 minutes

Everything in Variant A, plus the blocks that make the weekly numbers work.

- [ ] Run all of Variant A *(30 min)*
- [ ] Duty Quest: Command track *(5-10 min · separate track · Soul Crystals, Breakthrough fragments · P1)*
- [ ] Two Nightmare attempts at the highest challenge level you clear **without dying** — a failed attempt still consumes the charge *(6-10 min · 2 per day · Phantasmal Fragments, ranking points · P0 while pushing new bosses)*
- [ ] Convert one or two of today's three cube claims into Transcendence rather than Expedition if your item level is between 1,600 and 2,000 *(no extra time; it replaces an Expedition run · P0 in that band)*
- [ ] One or two Shugo Festa minigames on the hour, spreading your entries across different games rather than repeating one *(5-10 min · key-limited · Centuryroot Tokens · P2)*
- [ ] Gathering pass along the route you already walked, for the weekly Odyle conversion materials *(5 min · free · P1)*
- [ ] Twenty minutes of field hunting toward the 2,000,000 Kinah cap, in a dense spawn zone that doubles as pet-soul farming *(20 min · daily cap · tradable Kinah, pet souls · P2)*
- [ ] On Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday only: be in the Abyss for the 21:00 artifact siege, then take one Abyss Corridor entry per captured artifact *(15 min · per-artifact entry · 10,000-20,000 Abyss Points · P1 on those days)*

### Variant C — Completionist, about 2 to 2.5 hours

Everything above, plus the uncapped and scheduled content. This is the ceiling
of what one character's lockouts allow; beyond this, extra hours have to go into
[[Alt Routines]] or into PvP rank.

- [ ] Run all of Variant B *(60 min)*
- [ ] Finish the 2,000,000 Kinah field-hunting cap *(30-40 min more · daily cap · tradable Kinah · P2)*
- [ ] Two Dimensional Invasion claims on the half hour *(5-10 min · 2 per day, banks to 14 · P3)*
- [ ] Black Cloud Trading Company stock check, buying discounted packs over appearances because packs carry pet possession and level-up effects *(1 min · hourly rotation · P3)*
- [ ] One Spacetime Rift window: opposing-faction farming, Abyss Points and PvP. Note the entry window is only about five to ten minutes wide at each opening *(20-60 min · 8 openings per day · P3)*
- [ ] One Abyss hour from the weekly allowance, spent while your faction holds artifacts for the Abyss Point bonus *(60 min · 7 hours per week · Abyss Points, Abyss gear · P2)*
- [ ] Arena or Battlefield queues if you are chasing rank *(15-30 min · weekly win limits · Abyss Points, Merit Medals · P3)*
- [ ] Crafting and gathering block, and list the output on the Market. This is the only uncapped tradable Kinah route and the one most players build too late *(20-30 min · uncapped · tradable Kinah · P2)*
- [ ] Empyrean Trace route segment if any are still uncollected. Map (M) → filter → Contents → Empyrean Trace, and check per-sub-area counts at the pillar itself *(20-30 min · one-off · Wisdom Stones, amulet scrolls · P1 until finished)*
- [ ] Inventory and warehouse pass: sort keeper gear from tuning fodder from sale stock, and **sell before you soul-engrave**, because engraving is irreversible *(5-10 min · P1)*

### The login-to-logout order

The point of this order is that you travel exactly twice: once to town and once
to the field. Everything else is entered from a UI panel.

1. **On login, wherever you are — UI only.** Attendance, mail, pass and season
   claims. Odyle bar check. Open the journal and accept all Duty Missions,
   Commands and Supply Requests *before* deciding where to go, so their
   objectives can be folded into the trips below.
2. **Town block, one trip.** Supply Request hand-in, Black Cloud Trading Company
   stock, Wind Breeze special tab and material conversion (Alt+H) on Wednesdays,
   season and pass shops, warehouse deposit. All of this is in one capital.
3. **Instance block, no travel at all.** Expedition claims, Transcendence,
   Nightmare, and on Wednesdays the Daily Dungeon and Ascension Trial. These are
   all entered from the content menu, so run them back to back while you are
   still standing in town.
4. **Field block, one trip.** Travel once to your hunting zone and complete the
   Duty Mission field objectives, the gathering nodes, the pet-soul grinding and
   the Kinah cap in the same visit.
5. **Scheduled block, wherever you are.** Shugo Festa on the hour, Dimensional
   Invasion on the half hour. Slot these into whichever block the clock lands in
   rather than travelling for them.
6. **Abyss block last.** Corridor, Orders, Reshanta traces. Last because it is
   the only block where the opposing faction can end your evening, and because
   Korean routine guides explicitly recommend scheduling it at the end for that
   reason.
7. **Log out in a capital**, so tomorrow's town block costs no travel.

## Priorities and breakpoints

### What to drop first, in order

1. Arenas and Battlefields, unless you are chasing rank in
   [[Arenas Sieges and Seasons]].
2. Abyss Orders. High Abyss Point value, worst time cost in the game.
3. Spacetime Rift. Fun, high variance, no guaranteed progression — unless you
   are still sweeping the opposing faction's one-time content.
4. Dimensional Invasion, then Shugo Festa.
5. Abyss hours, if your PvE gear is behind. Korean guides say to defer the Abyss
   until everything except the hardest Expedition is done.
6. Field hunting and the crafting block. Uncapped, so nothing expires.

### What never to drop

- Five Duty Quests.
- The three Expedition reward counts, or at minimum enough energy spending to
  keep the pool off its cap.
- Nightmare, once your banked attempts approach 14.

### Breakpoints that change this list

| Breakpoint | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Item level 1,000 | Expedition, Abyss and Nightmare open; Conquest counts start accumulating |
| Combat Power 1,400-1,500 | Transcendence becomes the correct energy sink and stays so to 2,000 |
| Item level 1,600 | Two-star Conquest; switch Expedition from Exploration to Conquest wherever you can hold it |
| Nightmare ladder finished to your item level ceiling | Nightmare drops from P0 to P2 and becomes a two-minute habit |
| Item level 2,700 | Sanctuary weeks begin, and the daily energy budget starts being raided to bank 80-per-reward for raid night |
| Membership active | 5 claims a day instead of 3, and the Wind Breeze special tab becomes the best Kinah-to-power conversion available |

## Traps and mistakes

- **Letting the Odyle pool cap.** Regeneration stops entirely at 560 (840 with
  membership). Two days at cap is roughly four reward cubes destroyed, and cube
  rewards are the largest single payout in the daily loop.
- **Accepting Duty Quest rewards without rerolling.** The offered reward set can
  be refreshed, and Korean guides are unanimous that the Supply Request items,
  Small Odyle Energy and challenge tickets are worth more than the Kinah
  alternatives. One guide adds that the left-most reward slot carries the
  highest probability; that specific claim is single-sourced, so treat it as low
  confidence, but rerolling itself is not in dispute.
- **Logging in before 21:00 for a once-a-day session.** You strand one of the
  three Expedition counts.
- **Pushing a Nightmare challenge level you fail.** A failure still consumes the
  attempt. Only raise the slider when the current level is comfortable.
- **Farming a cleared Nightmare boss while an uncleared one still has first-clear
  rewards.** First clears are worth several times a repeat.
- **Opening reward cubes on Transcendence stage 1 or 2.** Pure waste.
- **Handing in Supply Request items when Kinah is your real bottleneck.** The
  delivery items often sell for more on the Market than the Abyss Points are
  worth to you.
- **Soul-engraving before deciding to sell.** A tuned item can never be listed or
  worn by another character. See [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]].
- **Treating this as an obligation.** The Express variant exists precisely so a
  bad week costs nothing permanent.

## Global launch notes

Written for global Season 1, item level band 0 to about 3,800. Adjustments the
launch build is expected to force:

- **No Heroic-grade gear.** Nothing in this list farms for it; the ceiling is
  Unique-grade Conquest and Sanctuary loot.
- **Launch Scale Test:** first Conquest door is **700**. Odyle regenerates
  **15 / 3 hours** for free players too — drop the "10 is F2P, 15 is sub"
  split in this routine. Feathers are not a daily Skill Point errand.
- **No hard-mode Conquest** is reported for launch, which removes the 60-energy
  cube tier. Single low-trust source; medium confidence.
- **Reset times are KR-derived.** As of 2026-09-06 NC has published no global
  daily reset time and no server-open hour for 2026-09-30 or 2026-10-05. Check
  the in-game timers on your first login. If the daily reset is not 05:00, the
  "log in after 21:00" advice shifts by the same offset, because the three
  Expedition recharges are spaced eight hours apart from the daily reset.
- **Duty Quests may be per server rather than per character.** If they are, this
  list can only be run on one character per server per day, which is the single
  largest constraint in [[Alt Routines]].
- **The Expedition lockout model is unresolved.** Korea's Chapter 1 replaced the
  3-per-day expiring reward counts with a weekly count consumed only when you
  leave a cleared dungeon without claiming. If global ships the Chapter 1 model,
  the Express variant no longer needs Expedition runs every day at all, and the
  whole routine becomes weekly. Verify by watching whether the Expedition
  counter reads "3" and refills at fixed hours, or reads a weekly total.

See [[Global Launch Differences]].

      in-game timers on day one.
- [ ] Whether Duty Quests are 5 per character or 5 per server. Settled by opening
      the journal on a second character.
- [ ] Whether the launch build uses per-day expiring Expedition reward counts or
      the Chapter 1 weekly count. Determines whether the Express variant needs
      Expedition runs daily.
- [ ] Whether Nightmare attempts are per character or per server. No source
      states either way after the per-server rework.
- [ ] The real Duty Quest reward-reroll odds, and whether the left-most slot
      genuinely carries a higher probability. One Korean guide asserts a 100 to
      10 percent gradient across the five slots; nothing corroborates it.
- [ ] Whether the global membership grants the Korean Odyle regeneration and cap
      uplift. If not, membership players also run on 3 claims a day and the
      whole page simplifies.
- [ ] Current Shugo Festa key recharge. Sources give 4 per day capped at 28, 2
      per day, and 7 per server per week with 14 for subscribers, in that
      chronological order.
