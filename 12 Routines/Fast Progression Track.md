# Fast Progression Track

> **In one sentence:** finish the story to 45 and item level 1,000, then spend every
> hour on **one-time content and Daevanion crystals** until you are near **~1,400
> item level**, and only then farm Conquest, Transcendence and the weekly loop.

This note is the **ordered checklist** version of [[Progression Phases Roadmap]] and
the crystal-first day-one route described in community guides (for example
[NoBS — 1,400 item level day one](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3o_-ZMBnIU)).
For calendar dates and Founder access, use [[Season 1 Launch Plan]]. For nightly
clicks after week two, use [[Daily Routine Main]] and [[Weekly Routine Main]].

## The decision rule

| If you are… | Do this first |
| --- | --- |
| Below **item level 1,000** | Story, red Ascension gauge stalls, equip highest piece per slot. **Do not** polish gear |
| **1,000–~1,400** | **One-time sweep only** — regional missions, sealed dungeons, strongholds, spend **every Daevanion crystal** |
| **~1,400–1,700** | Conquest Krao / Draupnir for Unique gear + cheap market fills; **even** enchants (+10 weapon, +5–7 armour) |
| **1,600–2,000** | **All Odyle into Transcendence** (Arcana) — highest CP per energy in the band |
| **2,000+** | Vakron Conquest set → three-star Conquest → Sanctuary prep — see phases in [[Progression Phases Roadmap]] |

**Item level formula reminder:** each base gear point, each +1 enhance, each
breakthrough level and **each Daevanion crystal spent** is **+1 item level**.
See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]].

**Global conquest doors (plan until retail confirms):** T1 **700**, T2 **1,400–1,500**,
T3 **2,100–2,200** — not KR live 1,000 / 1,600 / 2,200. See
[[Global Launch Differences]].

## Track 0 — Levels 1 to 45 (7–10 h)

**Goal:** level cap, main episode done, Monolith filter on.

1. Main story **only** while leveling (side content waits until 45 unless the red
   Ascension gauge hard-stalls you).
2. Level **16:** Monolith on; map filter → Empyrean Traces; collect what you
   pass, no detours.
3. Equip the **highest item level** piece in every slot; empty slots are free loss.
4. Spend Daevanion crystals on **rotation actives** as they arrive (+1 item level
   each); do not bank crystals.
5. **Do not** enhance past ~+3 on leveling gear, **do not** manastone, **do not**
   soul-imprint, **do not** chase PvP or Arcana.

**You should already have from the campaign:** weapon, guard (off-hand), bracelet,
unique amulet and belt (slots exist but need upgrading).

**Exit:** level 45, item level roughly **1,000–1,100** if you only did story.

## Track 1 — Sprint to ~1,400 item level (highest power per hour)

**Goal:** clear **all one-time item level** before repeatable farming. Worth
**~300–400 item level** total across the band; no battle pass required.

Do these in **priority order**. Stop enhancing when a **regional mission or sealed
dungeon** would give the same +1 item level with less Enhance Stone waste.

### 1. Regional missions (“original” / side quest lines)

- Finish **every regional mission** in every zone to completion.
- Each reward includes **Daevanion crystals** (often one crystal = one item level),
  **Kinah**, earrings, rings, **class runes**, and base pieces (~**43** item level
  on accessories in typical campaign gear).
- This is the **main lever** for a fast **~1,400** push on day one post-campaign.

### 2. Sealed dungeons (both factions)

- Your faction: during week-one sweep ([[Season 1 Launch Plan]]).
- **Opposing faction:** Spacetime Rift → enemy territory sealed dungeons
  ([[Garrisons and Sealed Dungeons]]). Reliable **Enhance Stones** and crystals.
- Each sealed clear: **2 Daevanion crystals** (+ Wisdom Stones) in current global
  notes — treat as mandatory one-time income.

### 3. Strongholds

- **Only** source of **Belt of Nobility** and **Revelation Amulet** progression.
- Push both toward **Unique / gold** grade; full slots are large item-level chunks
  (community guides cite ~**65** combined at gold — verify on your client).

### 4. Spend every Daevanion crystal immediately

- Open boards per [[Daevanion Boards]]; unspent crystals are item level you do not
  have. Auditing inventory at each gate is worth 50–150+ item level if you were
  hoarding.

### 5. Extra crystal sources (same day if available)

- **Shugo Festa** shop → Daevanion crystals / Wisdom Stones ([[Daily Routine Main]]).
- **Abyss coin** trade shop → stigma **or** Daevanion crystal (PvP-tagged items
  still count toward item level when equipped or when the crystal is spent).
- **Nightmare** first clears when at **1,000** item level (attempts bank to 14).

### 6. Expedition — milestone only at first

- Reach **1,000** item level **before** relying on Conquest counters (they do not
  accumulate below the gate).
- **Krao Cave** (Exploration): **3 clears** → guaranteed **gold** piece (often
  pants). **Stop at 3** for the milestone unless you still need drops; extra
  Conquest runs trade time vs more regional quests.
- Prefer **Vakron Sky Island** over **Urugugu Canyon** for early gear: class-appropriate
  upgrades vs wrong-class weapons ([[Dungeons and Raids]]).

### 7. Enhancing — targeted, not spread for vanity

| Piece | Suggested cap (fast track) | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon, guard, bracelet | Up to **+10** | Campaign pieces you keep short term; helps clear sealed / expedition |
| Amulet, belt (unique) | **Gold / Unique** grade | Full gear slots; not on the broker |
| Armour / accessories from quests | **+5 to +6** max | Each +1 = +1 item level, but **1 regional quest ≈ 1 crystal ≈ 1 item level** with no stone cost |
| Everything else | Replace via quests and expedition before pushing high + |

**Enhance Stones when broke:** opposing-faction **sealed dungeons** (free),
**Duty** missions (J → Duty), Daily Dungeon later in the weekly loop.

### 8. Empyrean Traces, Monolith, collections

- Traces do not add item level directly but pay **Skill Points** and amulet
  materials — do them on routes you already run for Track 1.
- Do **not** skip strongholds or half the sealed map (see traps below).

**Exit criterion:** **~1,400 item level**, crystals spent, one-time map clean,
Intermediate crafting unlocked if you followed [[Progression Phases Roadmap]] alchemy
steps.

**Movement tip (quests only):** fly → **dodge** to fold wings and keep velocity;
faster regional completion, zero item level — useful for speedruns, optional.

## Track 2 — ~1,400 to ~1,700 (Unique gear)

1. **Conquest** Krao Cave and Draupnir — use reward cubes; **14** Draupnir openings
   → guaranteed Unique armour piece, then stop farming it.
2. Buy **item level 55** Unique armour/accessories on the market for empty slots.
3. **Even** enchants: weapon **+10–12**, armour **+5–7**.
4. Intermediate **manastones** on weapon/armour, spirit stones on accessories —
   keep any high roll; do not perfect-reroll yet.
5. Odyle: mostly **bank or 1 Conquest claim/day** — you are still finishing one-time
   content if anything remains ([[Daily Routine Main]] table at 1,000–1,400).

**Exit:** **~1,600–1,700** item level, ready for Arcana focus.

---

## Track 3 — ~1,600 to ~2,000 (Arcana spike)

1. **100% of Odyle Energy → Transcendence** (stage **3+** S-rank farm; never open
   stage 1–2 reward boxes). See [[Arcana Refinement and Farming]].
2. Fill **all five** Arcana slots before upgrading one card (set effects).
3. **Nightmare** ladder each week; do not let attempts sit at cap.
4. **Abyss Points** → Decurion rings/earrings/necklace ([[Abyss Points and Ranks]]).
5. **Ignore** spending Odyle on Expedition Conquest until item level **2,000**
   unless you are hard-gated on gear with no market fix.

**Exit:** **~2,000** item level, Rare/green Arcana online.

---

## Track 4 — ~2,000 to ~2,700 (set + three-star)

1. Split Odyle: **Transcendence** + **Vakron Sky Island Conquest** (armour set,
   wing materials).
2. Cross **2,200** mechanical / **~2,500** social gate for three-star Conquest.
3. Enchant **only** gear you will keep (Conquest/Sanctuary tier).
4. Bank **160 Odyle** before Sanctuary week (**80** per reward).

**Exit:** first **Sanctuary** (**~2,700** item level gate in vault planning).

## Track 5 — ~2,700 to ~3,800 (launch ceiling)

Optimisation band: breakthroughs, soul imprint on keeper gear, Fire Temple /
Horn Den, second Sanctuary, pets/wings/PvP last. Full phase text:

## Traps (fast players still hit these)

- **Banking Daevanion crystals** — largest hidden item-level loss.
- **Over-enchanting** one piece to hit a gate — same item level, several times the
  stone cost; do another regional mission instead.
- **Skipping regional missions** because they are not “daily” — they are the fast track.
- **Urugugu before Vakron** for gear — wrong-class weapon risk on a speed run.
- **Expedition spam** before **3× milestone** or before **1,000** item level gate.
- **Soul imprint / attune on quest blues** you will replace in a week.
- **Reading KR Chapter 1 gates (1,600 / 2,200)** on global day one — plan
  **700 / ~1,400 / ~2,100** until confirmed ([[Global Launch Differences]]).
