# Routines

> **In one sentence:** the rest of the vault tells you what to build; this
> chapter tells you what to click tonight, in what order, and what to drop when
> you only have twenty minutes.

## Why it matters

Every other chapter in this vault is a description of a system. This chapter is
the only one that is a schedule, and in AION 2 the schedule is the game. Almost
no progression resource is earned by playing longer; it is earned by touching a
lockout before that lockout resets. Two players with identical gear and
identical class knowledge will diverge by hundreds of item levels over a season
purely because one of them let Odyle Energy sit at cap on a Tuesday and forgot
the Wednesday 05:00 wall.

That makes the routine the single highest-leverage thing you control, and it is
also why AION 2 has a reputation for homework. The counter-argument is worth
stating plainly, because it changes how you should read the checklists here: in
the current build there is **exactly one thing that is genuinely daily** — the
five Duty Quests — and almost everything else is a weekly budget you may spend
whenever you like. A player who understands that can run a five-hour week
without falling behind, and a player who does not will feel obliged to log in
every night for content that would have waited.

## How it works

### The four limiting resources

Every task in this chapter is limited by exactly one of four things. Knowing
which one is limiting you right now is how you decide what to cut.

| Resource | Examples | Behaviour | What running out feels like |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Time-gated entries** | Expedition reward counts (3 per day, recharging 05:00 / 13:00 / 21:00), Daily Dungeon (7 per week), Ascension Trial (3 per week), Nightmare (2 per day, banking to 14), Shugo Festa keys | Recharge on a clock, do not roll over past their reset, and most are now counted **per server** rather than per character | The content is greyed out and no amount of play opens it |
| **Capped energy** | Odyle Energy (오드 에너지) | Regenerates 10 every 3 hours to a hard cap of 560 and then **stops generating**. 15 per 3 hours and cap 840 with membership | You can enter but the reward cube is empty, so the run pays almost nothing |
| **Capped income** | Field-hunting Kinah (2,000,000 per character per day), weekly Abyss Point hunting and PvP caps, per-corridor Abyss Points, weekly medal purchase limits | A hard numeric ceiling per day or per run, after which the activity keeps costing time and stops paying | You are still killing things and the counter has stopped moving |
| **Uncapped income** | Gathering, crafting, Market sales, field grinding for pet souls, Empyrean Trace collection | No lockout at all; limited only by your hours and by Market prices | Nothing stops you, which is exactly why this is the first thing to cut on a short evening |

The strategic content of this whole chapter reduces to one rule: **spend the
capped things first and the uncapped things last, and never let capped energy
sit at its ceiling.** Everything else is detail.

The load-bearing arithmetic, which every checklist in this chapter is built
around:

```
Free player, per week
  560 passive Odyle Energy (10 per 3 hours)
+ 280 from 7 material-conversion crafts (40 each)
= 840 Odyle Energy per week
÷ 40 per reward cube
= 21 reward claims per week, or 3 per day

Membership, per week
  840 passive (15 per 3 hours)
+ 280 from conversion crafts
+ 280 from the Wind Breeze special tab (7 at 100,000 Kinah)
= 1,400 Odyle Energy per week
= 35 reward claims per week, or 5 per day
```

Three claims per day is the number to internalise. It is not a coincidence that
the Expedition reward counter also recharges exactly three times a day, at
05:00, 13:00 and 21:00 — the two systems are numerically matched for a free
player who plays every day. See [[Odyle Energy and Entry Limits]] and
[[Daily and Weekly Loops]].

### How to choose between the routine variants

[[Daily Routine Main]] gives three variants and [[Weekly Routine Main]] gives a
day-by-day spread plus a floor. Pick by the honest answer to "how many hours per
week will I actually give this", not by ambition.

| Your real weekly budget | Run this | What you give up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 5 hours | Daily Express (about 30 min) on 4-5 days, plus the weekly floor in one Sunday block | Abyss rank, arena and battlefield seasons, and the Nightmare ladder falls behind by roughly a boss a month |
| 5 to 8 hours | Daily Express every day, plus the weekly floor on Wednesday and Sunday | Sanctuary progression and the uncapped Kinah pipeline |
| 8 to 12 hours | Daily Standard (about 60 min), weekly spread across Wednesday, Friday and Sunday | Nothing structural; you will be one to two weeks behind a completionist on Arcana |
| 12 to 20 hours | Daily Completionist plus the full weekly spread | Nothing. This is the ceiling of what lockouts allow one character |
| Over 20 hours | The above, plus [[Alt Routines]] | Nothing, but read [[Alt Routines]] first — beyond one character the returns are much worse than Korean guides written before April 2026 suggest |

The important thing that table encodes: **above roughly twelve hours a week, one
character cannot absorb any more.** Extra hours have to go into a second
character, into the uncapped gathering and crafting pipeline, or into PvP rank,
because the lockouts on the main are already exhausted.

## Progression path

Read the notes in this order, once, before launch:

1. [[Season 1 Launch Plan]] — because the first two weeks are almost entirely
   one-time content and the daily and weekly routines barely apply yet.
2. [[Daily Routine Main]] — from the day you hit level 45 and item level 1,000.
3. [[Weekly Routine Main]] — from your first Wednesday 05:00 at max level.
4. [[Alt Routines]] — only after your main routinely finishes its own Odyle
   Energy, which for most players is week four at the earliest.

Class-specific spending decisions sit in [[Class Playbooks]]; the routines here
are class-neutral. Where a routine says "spend Odyle on the highest stage you
can hold", which stage that is depends on your gear plan — see
[[Templar BiS Roadmap]] and [[Chanter BiS Roadmap]].

## Priorities and breakpoints

The whole chapter in one screen.

**Never skip, in any variant:**

- Five Duty Quests. The only genuinely daily content, five per server per day.
- Odyle Energy management. Not "spend it all" — just never let it hit the cap.
- The Wednesday 05:00 audit. See the Tuesday-night list in
  [[Weekly Routine Main]].

**Bank rather than grind.** Three lockouts store multiple days, so they are the
correct things to defer on a busy evening and batch at the weekend:

| Lockout | Banks up to | Days of slack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nightmare attempts | 14 | 7 |
| Odyle Energy | 560 (840 with membership) | 7 from empty, realistically 4 before conversion crafts overflow you |
| Dimensional Invasion claims | 14 | 7 |

**Drop order when short on time**, hardest to softest: arenas and battlefields,
Abyss Orders, Spacetime Rift, Dimensional Invasion, Shugo Festa, Abyss hours,
Unknown Fissure, field hunting, gathering and crafting.

**The one thing this chapter disagrees with older guides about.** English alt
guides in circulation describe a world where every entry was per character. That
world ended with the Korean Season 3 patch on 2026-04-08 and Chapter 1 on
2026-07-01. Duty Quests, the Daily Dungeon, Shugo Festa keys, Orders, and the
Expedition and Transcendence weekly counts are now counted per server. Plan for
one main plus at most one utility alt until you have verified otherwise in the
global client. See [[Alt Routines]].

## Traps and mistakes

- **Treating the checklists as obligations.** They are budgets. The Express
  variant exists so that a bad week costs you nothing permanent.
- **Doing weeklies on Tuesday night.** The most common single loss in the game.
- **Opening reward cubes on content below your gear.** Reward tiers scale with
  difficulty and clear score, so one claim at the right stage beats three below
  it. Never open Transcendence stage 1 or 2 boxes at all.
- **Farming entries you have no energy for.** An entry charge with an empty
  energy pool behind it produces almost nothing.
- **Copying a Korean routine written before April 2026.** Half the entry counts
  in those guides are per character and are now per server.
- **Building the routine around Heroic-grade gear.** Heroic gear is reported to
  be absent from the global launch build entirely, so nothing in this chapter
  farms for it.
- **Following a routine at the expense of the one-time sweep.** In the first two
  weeks the repeatable loop is worth far less per hour than sealed dungeons,
  strongholds and Empyrean Traces. See [[Season 1 Launch Plan]].

## Global launch notes

This chapter is written for **global Season 1**: launch 2026-10-05, Founder's
Pack advance access from 2026-09-30, and a content band that runs from item
level 0 to roughly 3,800. Three things about that build shape every routine
here, and only the first is firmly established:

1. **Heroic-grade equipment is not in the launch build.** No routine here farms
   for it. The gear ceiling is Unique-grade Conquest and Sanctuary loot.
2. **Hard-mode Conquest is reported absent at launch**, which removes the
   60-energy cube tier and simplifies the daily energy budget to a flat 40 per
   claim. Reported by a single low-trust source; treat as medium confidence.
3. **Skill and Stigma enhancement is reported capped at level 20 at launch**
   while the character cap is 45. If true, the routines' skill-point and Stigma
   spending stops mattering much sooner than Korean guides imply. Single
   low-trust source; confidence low.

**Reset times are the biggest open item.** As of 2026-09-06 NC has published no
global daily or weekly reset time, and no server-open hour for either
2026-09-30 or 2026-10-05. Every clock in this chapter is therefore
**KR-derived**: 05:00 daily, Wednesday 05:00 weekly, Expedition recharges at
05:00 / 13:00 / 21:00. The shape (one daily reset, a Wednesday weekly reset,
three Expedition recharges spaced eight hours apart) is very likely to carry
over because it is baked into the content design; the wall-clock hour and the
timezone are not. Check the in-game timers on your first login and correct the
times in these notes before relying on them.

**Per-server versus per-character lockouts is the second biggest.** The global
build launches after Korea's per-server rework, so the default assumption in
this chapter is that per-server counting is present. It can be settled in about
thirty seconds at launch: open the Duty Quest journal on a second character and
see whether the counter reads 5 or 0.

See [[Global Launch Differences]] for the full confirmed-versus-inferred
breakdown.
