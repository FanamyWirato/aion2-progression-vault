# Season 1 Launch Plan

> **In one sentence:** for the first two weeks the repeatable routine barely
> matters — one-time content is worth 300 to 400 item level and nothing you can
> grind comes close — so this plan front-loads the sweep, reaches level 45 and
> item level 1,000 as fast as the story allows, and only switches to
> [[Daily Routine Main]] and [[Weekly Routine Main]] in week three.

## Why it matters

There is a specific trap at every MMO launch and AION 2's version of it is
sharper than most. The game presents a rich daily and weekly checklist the
moment you hit level 45, and that checklist is genuinely the endgame. But in the
first fortnight it is the *wrong* thing to do, because the one-time content —
sealed dungeons, strongholds, the Monolith, Empyrean Traces, campaign rewards,
launch-event tracks — pays several hundred item levels for hours that will never
pay that well again. A player who settles into the daily loop at level 45 and
sweeps the one-time content "later" spends the whole season behind a player who
did it in the opposite order, and can never catch up because the sweep pays the
same whenever you do it while the daily loop compounds.

The second reason this note exists is that Founder advance access gives you
**five days of head start**, and those five days land on a different side of the
first weekly reset than open launch does. Using them well is mostly about
reaching level 45 before Wednesday 2026-10-07 so that the first full weekly
budget is available to you.

### The system unlock order

Push systems in this order. The principle is that anything **account-wide or
one-time** comes before anything repeatable, and anything that **gates a
reward counter** comes before anything that merely raises numbers.

1. **Level.** Nothing else matters until 45. Every system's rewards scale with
   your level, so earning them early is earning them small.
2. **Item level 1,000.** This is a *gate*, not a number: Conquest reward counts
   do not accumulate below it. Reaching 1,000 quickly is worth more than any
   amount of gear polish at 900.
3. **The one-time sweep.** Sealed dungeons, strongholds, Monolith, Empyrean
   Traces. Worth 300-400 item level and never repeatable.
4. **Skills and Stigma**, from Wisdom Stones the sweep pays. Cheap, permanent and
   multiplies everything after.
5. **Arcana**, from Transcendence, from item level 1,600 onward. The single
   largest power-per-energy return in the launch band.
6. **Pantheon**, from Nightmare, which starts paying the moment you can enter and
   banks up to 14 attempts so it forgives a slow start.
7. **Enchanting**, from Daily Dungeon Enhance Stones — but only on gear you
   intend to keep, which in the launch band means Conquest and Sanctuary loot.
8. **Pets, mounts and wings.** Real stats, but they trail the above.
9. **PvP rank and Abyss gear.** Last, because the Abyss punishes low item level
   and rewards you no faster for arriving early.

## Unlock and gates

The whole plan sits inside the launch band of item level 0 to about 3,800.
Consequences:

- Both the 2,700 and 3,500 Sanctuary raids exist in the band; the 4,500 raid does
  not, so raid progression tops out at two.
- **Heroic-grade equipment is not in the launch build.** Nothing in this plan
  farms for it and the gear ceiling is Unique-grade Conquest and Sanctuary loot.
- Hard-mode Conquest is reported absent at launch, so every reward cube in this
  plan costs a flat 40 Odyle Energy except Sanctuary's 80.
- Skill and Stigma enhancement is reported capped at level 20 while the character
  cap is 45. If that is true, the skill investment in step 4 above saturates far
  earlier than Korean guides imply. Single low-trust source.

## Progression path

### Week 1 — Founder advance access, Wednesday 2026-09-30 to Sunday 2026-10-04

The goal for these five days is narrow: **level 45 and item level 1,000, with the
Monolith running in the background.** Nothing else.

**Day 1 — Wednesday 2026-09-30 (3-5 h if you have it)**

- [ ] Before you log in, decide your class and your faction, and if you intend to
      run an alt at all, note that it must be on the same server. Read
      [[Class Playbooks]] now rather than at level 30 *(P0)*
- [ ] Pick your server deliberately. The Server Warehouse and every "per server"
      lockout are keyed to it, and server choice is the least reversible decision
      you make all month *(P0)*
- [ ] Claim the Founder's Pack and activate the 30 days of membership immediately,
      so the Odyle regeneration and cap uplift start accruing on day one *(5 min · P0)*
- [ ] Check the in-game content timers and **write down the real daily reset time,
      weekly reset day and Expedition recharge hours**. Correct them in
      [[Routines]], [[Daily Routine Main]] and [[Weekly Routine Main]] before you
      rely on those notes *(5 min · P0)*
- [ ] Open the Duty Quest journal, Daily Dungeon panel and Odyle conversion UI and
      note the entry counts, then create a throwaway second character and check
      the same three numbers. This settles the per-server question that
      [[Alt Routines]] hangs on *(2 min · P0)*
- [ ] Run the main story campaign, and only the main story campaign, at full speed *(P0)*
- [ ] At level 16, unlock the Monolith and turn on the map filter for Empyrean
      Traces: Map (M) → filter → Contents → Empyrean Trace. From here on, collect
      every trace you walk past. Never detour for one during levelling *(2 min setup · P0)*
- [ ] Claim the launch attendance calendar and every event track on offer *(5 min · P1)*
- [ ] Log out in a capital city *(P2)*

**Day 2 — Thursday 2026-10-01**

- [ ] Attendance, mail and event claims *(5 min · P0)*
- [ ] Main story campaign, continued. Resist every side quest that is not on the
      critical path *(P0)*
- [ ] Collect Empyrean Traces on your levelling route only *(free · P1)*
- [ ] Gather every node you pass. You will need the materials for Odyle conversion
      crafts and you have nothing better to spend inventory on yet *(free · P1)*
- [ ] Do **not** enhance anything above +3 or so on levelling gear. It is all
      replaced within days *(P0 as a prohibition)*

**Day 3 — Friday 2026-10-02**

- [ ] Attendance, mail and event claims *(5 min · P0)*
- [ ] Push to level 45 if you have not reached it *(P0)*
- [ ] The moment you hit 45: accept and complete the five Duty Quests, because
      that is the only genuinely daily reward in the game and it starts paying
      immediately *(10-15 min · P0)*
- [ ] Start the Odyle conversion crafts (Alt+H) as soon as you have materials. Each
      is 40 energy, and energy at the start of a season has nowhere to be wasted *(15 min · P0)*
- [ ] Begin the sealed dungeon sweep in your own faction's territory. Sealed
      dungeons are the largest single one-time item level block *(P0)*

**Day 4 — Saturday 2026-10-03 (long session if possible)**

- [ ] Full daily claims and five Duty Quests *(20 min · P0)*
- [ ] Sealed dungeons and strongholds, hard. Strongholds are the **only** source
      of belt and amulet slots, so they are not optional and not repeatable *(2-4 h · P0)*
- [ ] Equip everything the sweep pays out and push toward item level 1,000 *(P0)*
- [ ] Once you cross 1,000, immediately start claiming Expedition reward counts —
      Conquest counters do not accumulate below 1,000, so every hour spent under
      the gate is a wasted counter *(15-25 min · 3 counts, 40 energy each · P0)*
- [ ] Two Nightmare attempts, at the lowest challenge level you clear comfortably.
      Attempts bank to 14, so if you cannot fit them, they wait *(6-10 min · P1)*

**Day 5 — Sunday 2026-10-04**

- [ ] Full daily claims and five Duty Quests *(20 min · P0)*
- [ ] Finish your own faction's sealed dungeons and strongholds *(2-3 h · P0)*
- [ ] Three Expedition reward claims at the highest difficulty you clear quickly *(20 min · P0)*
- [ ] Monolith audit: open the Monolith, see which reward tiers are close, and plan
      one targeted Empyrean Trace route to cross the nearest threshold. Check
      per-sub-area counts at the pillar itself rather than guessing from the map *(30-45 min · P1)*
- [ ] Run the Tuesday-audit list from [[Weekly Routine Main]] two days early,
      because Wednesday 2026-10-07 is your first real weekly reset and you want
      nothing unspent *(5 min · P0)*

### Week 2 — Open launch, Monday 2026-10-05 to Tuesday 2026-10-13

Open launch changes the world in one way that matters: **the field gets crowded.**
Shift field hunting and gathering to off-peak hours and move instance content into
prime time, because instances are unaffected by population.

**Day 6 — Monday 2026-10-05, open launch day**

- [ ] Full daily claims, including any launch-day event tracks that only appear now *(10 min · P0)*
- [ ] Five Duty Quests *(15 min · P0)*

- [ ] Legion or guild hunting. Do this today rather than in week three: Sanctuary

**Day 7 — Tuesday 2026-10-06, first audit day**

**Days 9-11 — Thursday 2026-10-08 to Saturday 2026-10-10**

- [ ] Express or Standard daily routine each day *(30-60 min · P0)*

### The three things that decide your month

If you remember nothing else from this note:

1. **Level 45 and item level 1,000 as fast as the story allows.** Conquest reward
   counters do not accumulate below 1,000, so time under the gate is time earning
   nothing.
2. **Sweep the one-time content before you settle into the routine.** 300-400
   item level, both factions, never repeatable, and it pays the same in week one
   as in week ten while the daily loop compounds.
3. **All Odyle into Transcendence between item level 1,600 and 2,000.** The
   highest power-per-energy conversion in the launch band and the most
   consistently repeated advice in Korean guides.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Settling into the daily routine at level 45.** The single most expensive
  launch mistake. The routine is right from week three, not week one.
- **Lingering below item level 1,000.** Conquest reward counters do not
  accumulate, so the hours pay nothing toward gear.
- **Enhancing levelling gear.** Everything below Conquest grade is replaced inside
  two weeks. Enhance Stones spent on it are gone.
- **Soul-imprinting or tuning transitional gear.** Irreversible, and it makes the
  item unsellable and untransferable. See [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]].
- **Detouring for Empyrean Traces during levelling.** Collect what you pass; route
  deliberately only once you are 45 and can see the Monolith thresholds.
- **Choosing a server casually.** Every "per server" lockout, the Server Warehouse
  and your legion are all keyed to it.
- **Skipping strongholds.** They are the only source of belt and amulet slots.
  There is no second route and no catch-up.
- **Missing the opposing-faction sweep.** Half the sealed dungeons and half the
  Empyrean Traces are behind a Spacetime Rift with a five-to-ten-minute entry
  window. It is easy to postpone for a month by accident.
- **Buying a premium pass late.** Weekly experience caps make late purchases
  impossible to catch up without paying for rank skips.
- **Letting the Odyle pool sit at cap during the levelling week.** Regeneration
  stops dead at the cap, and during week one you have no other use for the energy.
- **Planning around Heroic gear.** It is not in the launch build.
- **Building an alt in the first fortnight.** Your main's lockouts are nowhere
  near saturated and the per-server question may not be settled yet.
