# Weekly Routine Main

> **In one sentence:** everything that resets on Wednesday at 05:00, ordered by
> what it feeds, spread across the week around the fixed siege and raid clock,
> with a Tuesday-night audit list and a floor you can hit in three hours.

## Why it matters

AION 2 is a weekly game wearing a daily game's clothes. The Daily Dungeon is a
seven-entry weekly bundle. Ascension Trial is three per week. Abyss access is
seven hours per week. Orders, Supply Requests, Sanctuary rewards, Odyle
conversion crafts and the entire Wind Breeze shop are weekly. Add it up and the
weekly layer is worth several times the daily layer in materials, which is why
the single most expensive mistake in the game is a Tuesday evening spent
grinding while seven Daily Dungeon entries and three Ascension Trials sit
unspent.

The second reason this note exists is scheduling. Three of the highest-value
weekly activities happen at fixed wall-clock times you cannot move — the
artifact sieges — and one of them (Sanctuary) depends on other people's
calendars. Everything else is flexible, so the trick is to build the flexible
work around the fixed work rather than the other way round.

## How it works

### The Wednesday wall

Weekly reset is **Wednesday 05:00** in Korea. Everything below is zeroed at that
moment, and unspent charges are simply gone.

| What resets Wednesday 05:00 | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Daily Dungeon entries | 7 per week (one source gives 14 per server; see Open questions) |
| Ascension Trial | 3 per week |
| Unknown Fissure challenges | 7 per week |
| Orders (지령서) | 12 per week |
| Abyss Orders | 5 per grade, 25 total |
| Weekly Supply Requests | one tab |
| Abyss access hours | 7, or 14 with membership |
| Sanctuary final-boss reward counts | per raid |
| Odyle Energy material conversion | 7 crafts per character (20 per server in current KR) |
| Wind Breeze special tab | 21 items per server for most lines |
| Expedition and Transcendence weekly reward refills | 14 and 7 per character in current KR |
| Season weekly point tiers | one full track |
| Merit Medal purchase limits and weekly medal missions | 10-20 Silver, 10 Gold, 10 Platinum |
| Battle pass weekly experience caps | 1,000 to 50,000 depending on the pass |

Global reset times are unpublished as of 2026-09-06; **Wednesday 05:00 is
KR-derived**. See the Global launch notes.

### The fixed clock you have to plan around

| When (KR) | Event | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tue / Thu / Sat 21:00 | Artifact siege, Lower and Middle Abyss simultaneously | Each captured artifact grants one Abyss Corridor entry worth roughly 10,000 Abyss Points in five protected minutes, plus a +5 percent Abyss Point bonus while your faction holds it |
| Sat / Sun 22:00 | Guardian General Nahma | Abyss boss, Merit Medal source |
| Tue / Thu 22:00 | Rift of Time and Space Contest | Scheduled large-scale siege in the current KR build; may not exist at global launch |
| Every 3 hours from 02:00 | Spacetime Rift | Route into opposing territory; five-to-ten-minute entry window, one hour inside |
| Your legion's night | Sanctuary | The only weekly item that depends on other people |

Note the awkward interaction: the sieges are Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, but
the reset is Wednesday 05:00. That means **the Tuesday 21:00 siege is the last
Abyss event of the week and the Thursday one is the first of the new week**, so a
player who does Abyss content only on weekends gets two sieges out of three.

### The weekly Odyle arithmetic

The conversion crafts are the highest-priority weekly item on this page and the
one most often forgotten, because they gate everything else.

```
7 material-conversion crafts x 40 energy = 280 energy per week
  materials: 175 Odyle, 35 High-Purity Odyle, 7 Pure Odyle, ~350,000 Kinah
  gathered incidentally during the daily field block

With membership, add:
7 Wind Breeze purchases x 40 energy = 280 energy per week at 700,000 Kinah
```

That is 280 free energy, or 7 extra reward cubes, for about fifteen minutes at a
crafting table. It is the best time-to-power conversion in the weekly routine.
See [[Odyle Energy and Entry Limits]] and [[Currencies and Economy]].

## Unlock and gates

- Daily Dungeon, Ascension Trial, Orders, Supply Requests, Unknown Fissure:
  level 45.
- Abyss access and Abyss Orders: level 45 and item level 1,000. The Middle Layer
  needs item level 3,000, which is inside the launch band.
- Sanctuary: item level 2,700 for the first raid, 3,500 for the second. Both sit
  inside the launch band; the 4,500 raid does not.
- Wind Breeze special tab: active membership only.
- Ascension Trial Easy opens at Combat Power 1,000, but Normal opens at 1,500
  and pays far better — do not burn the three weekly attempts on Easy if you are
  close to 1,500.

## Progression path

### The full weekly checklist

Roughly 6 to 8 hours on top of the daily routine. Tick these off wherever they
fit; the day-by-day spread below is a suggestion, not a requirement.

**Reset-day block — do these first, because they gate the rest of the week**

- [ ] Buy the Wind Breeze special tab: Odyle Energy first, then Unknown Fissure tickets, then Expedition/Transcendence challenge ticket boxes, then Daily Dungeon and Nightmare instant-clears *(5 min · membership · 280 energy and a week of tickets · P0)*
- [ ] Run all 7 Odyle Energy material conversions (Alt+H) *(15 min · ~350,000 Kinah · 280 energy · P0)*
- [ ] Buy the week's 12 Orders from the Order merchant NPC, cheapest grade first — grade does not change the reward *(5 min · Kinah · P1)*
- [ ] Check the season weekly point track and the weekly experience cap on every active pass, so you know what today's play needs to hit *(5 min · free · P1)*

**Materials block**

- [ ] Daily Dungeon ×7, prioritising elite mobs over clear speed because rewards scale with score. Split them across the week if your gear is still climbing — a late-week run at higher item level pays more *(45-70 min · 7 entries · Enhance Stones, Pet Soul Boxes, Breakthrough materials · P0)*
- [ ] Ascension Trial ×3 at the highest difficulty you clear, pushing weekly cumulative score to at least 3,000 and ideally 6,000 for the Mysterious Crystal thresholds *(15-25 min · 3 entries · Silentium, Ariel's Trace, up to 5 Mysterious Crystals · P0)*
- [ ] Unknown Fissure ×7 *(20-35 min · 7 challenges · P2)*
- [ ] Burn down the Nightmare bank if it is near 14, pushing uncleared bosses before farming cleared ones *(30-60 min · banked attempts · Phantasmal Fragments · P1)*

**Abyss and PvP block**

- [ ] Weekly Supply Requests *(10-15 min · crafted stock · ~30,000 Abyss Points · P1)*
- [ ] Complete the 12 Orders. They are classed as mission quests, so clear your quest slots first or they cannot be accepted *(30-45 min · P1)*
- [ ] Abyss Orders ×25, if you can stomach the time cost *(60-90 min · ~1,300,000 Kinah · P2)*
- [ ] Be in the Abyss at 21:00 on at least two of Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, then take one Abyss Corridor entry per captured artifact *(30 min each · P1)*
- [ ] Spend the 7 weekly Abyss hours, ideally while your faction holds artifacts for the AP bonus. Collect Reshanta Empyrean Traces on the same trips *(up to 7 h · 7 hours · P2)*
- [ ] Three Battlefield matches, winning if you can *(30-45 min · P2)*
- [ ] Clear the weekly Merit Medal missions and spend Abyss Points on medals up to your rank's purchase limit *(15 min · P2)*

**Raid block**

- [ ] Bank 80 Odyle Energy per Sanctuary reward you intend to claim, starting three days before raid night *(planning only · P1)*
- [ ] Sanctuary to your reward cap *(60-120 min · P1 at item level 2,700+)*

**Economy block**

- [ ] Gathering circuit for the next week's conversion materials plus crafting stock *(30-45 min · uncapped · P1)*
- [ ] Crafting and Market listing pass. This is the only uncapped tradable Kinah route and the one most players build too late *(20-45 min · uncapped · tradable Kinah · P1)*
- [ ] Arcana pass: sort keepers from fuel from extraction fodder, then run your one Unique refinement for a **designated** skill you actually planned *(20-30 min · 2,000,000 Kinah + 5 Mysterious Crystals · P1)*
- [ ] Spend Oath Coins in the season shop on Mysterious Crystals if your Arcana is behind. The shop is deleted at the season transition, not migrated *(5 min · P1)*

### Suggested day-by-day spread

Built around the Korean fixed schedule. Shift the whole thing if your region's
siege times differ.

**Wednesday — reset day, the heaviest day of the week (2-3 h)**
- [ ] Wind Breeze special tab and all 7 Odyle conversion crafts, before anything else
- [ ] Buy the week's 12 Orders
- [ ] Daily Dungeon ×3 or ×4
- [ ] Ascension Trial ×3
- [ ] Daily routine as normal

**Thursday — first Abyss night of the new week (1.5 h)**
- [ ] Daily routine, front-loaded so you are free at 21:00
- [ ] Artifact siege at 21:00, then one corridor per captured artifact
- [ ] Two of the weekly Abyss hours while artifacts are held

**Friday — raid and Arcana night (1.5-2.5 h)**
- [ ] Daily routine
- [ ] Sanctuary with your legion, with 80 energy per reward already banked
- [ ] Transcendence and the Arcana storage and refinement pass

**Saturday — Abyss night and PvP (1.5-2 h)**
- [ ] Daily routine
- [ ] Artifact siege at 21:00 plus corridors
- [ ] Nahma at 22:00 if your faction is organising it
- [ ] Three Battlefield matches and any Arena queues

**Sunday — the long block (2-3 h)**
- [ ] Full daily routine including the 2,000,000 Kinah field cap
- [ ] Gathering circuit and crafting, then list on the Market
- [ ] Remaining Daily Dungeon entries and Unknown Fissure runs
- [ ] Burn down the Nightmare bank
- [ ] Any remaining Empyrean Traces

**Monday — light day (45 min)**
- [ ] Daily routine only
- [ ] Finish the Orders if any remain

**Tuesday — audit day, and the last siege of the week (1-1.5 h)**
- [ ] Run the Wednesday reset prep list below **before** you do anything else
- [ ] Spend whatever the audit says is unspent
- [ ] Artifact siege at 21:00 plus corridors — this is the week's last one
- [ ] Any remaining Abyss hours

### Wednesday reset prep — the Tuesday-night audit

Run this every Tuesday. It takes five minutes and it is the highest-value five
minutes in the weekly routine.

- [ ] Daily Dungeon entries remaining? Spend them, even sloppily. An unspent entry is worth zero
- [ ] Ascension Trials remaining? Spend all three
- [ ] Odyle conversion crafts remaining? Craft them now — the energy itself banks past reset, the craft charge does not
- [ ] Wind Breeze special tab fully bought? Especially the Odyle Energy and instant-clear lines
- [ ] Orders and Abyss Orders bought **and completed**? Buying them is not enough
- [ ] Weekly Supply Requests handed in?
- [ ] Abyss hours remaining? Any unspent hour is gone
- [ ] Sanctuary reward counts claimed?
- [ ] Unknown Fissure challenges remaining?
- [ ] Season weekly point track: are you past tier 3 and past the 8,000-point tier?
- [ ] Every active pass at its weekly experience cap?
- [ ] Merit Medal weekly missions done, and medals bought up to your rank limit?
- [ ] Odyle Energy pool: will it be at cap by Wednesday morning? If so, spend claims tonight
- [ ] Any season-issued challenge tickets or currencies near an expiry date?

### Minimum to not fall behind

About 2.5 to 3.5 hours a week on top of the Express daily routine. This subset
keeps every permanent progression track moving; everything cut here is either
uncapped or PvP rank, neither of which compounds.

- [ ] All 7 Odyle Energy material conversions *(15 min · P0)*
- [ ] Wind Breeze special tab, Odyle Energy line first, if you have membership *(5 min · P0)*
- [ ] Daily Dungeon ×7 *(45-70 min · P0)*
- [ ] Ascension Trial ×3 *(15-25 min · P0)*
- [ ] Weekly Supply Requests *(10-15 min · P1)*
- [ ] Orders ×12 *(30-45 min · P1)*
- [ ] Sanctuary reward claim, if your item level qualifies and your legion runs *(60-90 min · P1)*
- [ ] The Tuesday audit *(5 min · P0)*

Explicitly dropped in the floor version: Abyss Orders, the seven Abyss hours,
Battlefields, Arenas, Unknown Fissure, Nahma, the gathering and crafting block,
and two of the three artifact sieges. Cutting all of that costs you Abyss rank
and tradable Kinah. It costs you no gear, no Arcana, no Enhance Stones, no
Pantheon and no season progress.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Conversion crafts before everything.** They are 280 energy, which is a third
  of a free player's weekly total, for fifteen minutes of work.
- **Split the Daily Dungeon across the week if you are still gearing.** Rewards
  scale with clear score, so seven runs at Sunday's item level pay more than
  seven runs at Wednesday's. If your gear is static, front-load instead.
- **Ascension Trial Normal at 1,500, not Easy at 1,000.** Three attempts a week
  is few enough that using them on Easy while 1,500 is close is a real loss.
- **Clear your quest slots before buying Orders.** Orders occupy mission-quest
  slots, and the concurrent cap is small.
- **Two sieges out of three is the realistic target.** Tuesday, Thursday and
  Saturday at 21:00 is a lot of scheduled evenings. Pick the two you can hold.
- **Sanctuary energy has to be planned.** At 80 per reward it is the only weekly
  item that reaches back into your daily budget.
- **The gathering and crafting pipeline is the thing to build before you need
  it.** Once dungeon Kinah is Bound, it is the only uncapped tradable route, and
  Korean guides rank it as essential from the third season onward.

## Traps and mistakes

- **Tuesday night with unspent entries.** The defining weekly failure. The audit
  list above exists for this one reason.
- **Buying Orders and not completing them.** The purchase limit resets whether or
  not you ran them.
- **Skipping the conversion crafts because gathering feels like a side activity.**
  It is 7 reward cubes a week.
- **Spending the three Ascension Trials on Easy.**
- **Assuming "Daily Dungeon" is daily.** It is a weekly bundle, and its name is
  the single most misleading label in the game.
- **Hoarding season currency.** Oath Coins expire at the season transition and
  the Season Shop is deleted rather than migrated. Deva Pass Shop Coins get
  roughly two extra weeks. Run an inventory audit two weeks before any
  transition.
- **Buying a premium pass late in its window.** The weekly experience cap makes
  catching up impossible without paying per-rank level skips.
- **Grinding Abyss PvE for rank after hitting the weekly Abyss Point cap.** Once
  capped, PvE stops paying Ranking Points anywhere except the Corridor.
- **Buying Platinum Medals with Abyss Points as your main route.** A top PvP
  weapon costs hundreds of medals; medals have to come mostly from missions and
  bosses.
- **Enhancing or tuning transitional gear.** Breakthroughs, godstones and soul
  imprint work belong on gear you intend to keep. See
  [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]].

## Global launch notes

Written for global Season 1, item level band 0 to about 3,800. Inside that band
both of the first two Sanctuary raids exist (2,700 and 3,500) and the third
(4,500) does not, so the raid block above tops out at two raids.

- **Wednesday 05:00 is KR-derived.** NC has published no global reset time as of
  2026-09-06. The weekly-reset-on-a-midweek-day shape is very likely to carry
  over because the entire content economy is built on it, but the day and hour
  are unconfirmed. Check the in-game weekly timers on your first Wednesday.
- **The siege schedule is the least portable thing on this page.** Korea fixed
  artifact sieges to Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 21:00 in February 2026,
  and one source suggests a later Season 3 change to Wednesday and Saturday.
  Global has published nothing, and the global Abyss is cross-server matched, so
  expect a different schedule entirely. Treat the day-by-day spread as a template
  keyed to "siege nights", not to specific weekdays.
- **Per-server versus per-character counting is unresolved.** If Daily Dungeon
  entries and Odyle conversion crafts are per server on global, the weekly layer
  above is the *account's* weekly layer, not the character's, and
  [[Alt Routines]] changes substantially.
- **No Heroic gear and no hard-mode Conquest** are reported for the launch build.
  Nothing here farms for either. **Launch Scale Test:** Odyle is **15 / 3 hours**
  for everyone; Conquest doors start at **700**; Silver medals only.
- **Sanctuary group size is contested.** NC said eight-player raids at gamescom.
  Assume eight until the client says otherwise.
