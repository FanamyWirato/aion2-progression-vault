# Farming Routes and Kinah

> **In one sentence:** field hunting is capped at **2,000,000 Kinah per
> character per day**, most dungeon Kinah in the KR build is Bound and cannot
> touch the Market, so your tradable income is the daily cap plus whatever you
> gather, craft and sell — and that is the whole shape of the AION 2 economy.

## Why it matters

[[Currencies and Economy]] establishes the structural fact: from Season 3 onward
essentially no Korean dungeon is a reliable source of **tradable** Kinah.
Growth costs — enhancement, Breakthrough, Arcana refinement, imprinting — accept
Bound Kinah. Buying anything from another player does not.

So there are two wallets and they are filled by completely different activities.
This note is about filling the tradable one.

## The daily cap: the single most important number

**Kinah obtained from killing monsters is capped at 2,000,000 per character per
day, resetting at 05:00 server time.**

Three independent Korean sources state this, including a games-press article
and a Season 3 income guide. Everything else about field farming follows from
it:

- The cap is per **character**, which is why alts multiply tradable income and
  why [[Alt Routines]] exists as a topic.
- The cap is on **monster Kinah only**. Drops you sell on the Market, gathered
  materials, crafted goods and Supply Request items are all **outside** the cap.
- Because the cap is fixed, the only variables are **how fast you fill it** and
  **what else you get while filling it**. Mob density is therefore the entire
  selection criterion for a farming spot.

- **Dreadgion Crash Site** (드레드기온 추락지) — dense Drana Slimes and Mutants,
  good for a short burst.
- **Transcendence stage 1 trash** — the standard **burst** technique. Enter
  Transcendence stage 1, **skip the boss**, and farm the normal mobs on repeat.
  Korean guides recommend this specifically when you need the daily cap filled
  fast rather than pleasantly.

  bottleneck. See [[Abyss Points and Ranks]].

Solo beats party for drop feel according to community consensus, though no
official numbers exist for that. **Confidence low** on the solo/party
difference; **high** on the spot list, which is consistent across sources.

## The routes that make real money

Field hunting fills a capped bucket. These do not.

### 1. Conquest Draupnir, solo, in a private room

The single most-cited free-to-play income route in the Korean sources.

- **Requirement:** roughly **Combat Power 1,300–1,400** in the level-45-era
  scale to solo it reliably. Set the room to private and run it alone.
- **What it pays:** Hard Draconic Scale (단단한 용족의 비늘), Solid Draconic Horn
  (견고한 용족의 뿔) and Unique-grade gear. Both draconic materials hold
  **persistently high** Market prices because every crafter needs them.
- Draconyutes are the target kills.

This is the route that turns "I can clear a dungeon" into "I have a tradable
income", and it is the reason the draconic prefix table in
[[Odyle and Special Resources]] is worth memorising.

### 2. Gathering into the Market

Covered mechanically in [[Gathering Nodes and Plants]]. Economically:

- **Gems are the best sellers.** Ruby has the heaviest demand because accessory
  enhancement consumes it continuously; Diamond reportedly carries the better
  margin. All three Pure-grade gemstones plus Pure Odyle are the bulk of the
  Brooch's cost, and every Jewelcrafter needs them.
- **Odyle is the most liquid material in the game**, because it substitutes for
  everything.
- **Flight and mind potions** are flagged by NC's own community post as popular
  items with high Abyss and Rift-of-Time-and-Space demand. Herb gathering into
  potion crafting is the lowest-capital seller available.
- **Conversion products are Bound.** If you want to sell materials you must
  gather them, not convert them.

One Korean route guide claims **500,000 to 2,000,000 Kinah per hour** for a
dedicated gathering route including incidental drops. It is the highest figure
anywhere, from a single blog with no methodology: **confidence low.** A more
conservative Korean income table lists plain herb-and-ore gathering as
"hundreds of thousands per day" as a background activity. Both describe
different intensities honestly.

### 3. Selling drops instead of dismantling them

The rule is simple and people get it wrong constantly: **an unusable or
duplicate Unique drop is worth more on the Market than as extraction fodder.**
This applies to Unique gear you cannot wear, Unique gear you already have, and
Shugo Festa prize gear. See [[Extraction and Refunds]].

### 4. Ariel's Trace fragments into crystals

One Korean income table lists this as a **weekly 700,000+ Kinah guaranteed**
line: collect Ariel's Trace fragments from Ascension Trial and Orders, convert
them into crystals at the conversion bench, and sell. Listed as SS-tier
efficiency for "anyone past max level". **Single source, confidence medium.**

### 5. Silentium, the boring reliable one

Ascension Trial pays Silentium; the general goods NPC pays a flat **5,000 Kinah**
each. It has no other use. A few hundred thousand Kinah a week for zero effort
**on the character that cleared Trial**. **Global:** Silentium does not trade or
warehouse — alts do not feed Silentium to the main. Details in
[[Odyle and Special Resources]].

### 6. Supply Request items — sell or hand in

Delivery items for Abyss Supply Requests are frequently worth **more on the
Market than the Abyss Points they return**, especially the Rare and
Heritage-grade delivery gear. One Korean table puts the value of Supply Request
items at roughly **420,000 to 2,100,000 Kinah per character per week**
depending on your production. Decide per week whether Kinah or Abyss Points is
your bottleneck.

### 7. The weekly Kinah-for-value shop purchases

Not income, but the best conversion of Kinah into progress:

| Purchase | Cost | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Odyle Energy from Wind Breeze | 100,000 Kinah each | Best Kinah-to-power rate in the game; the event conversion charges 300,000 for the same thing |
| Instant Clear Ticket: Nightmare | 30,000 Kinah | Cheap content compression |
| Challenge Ticket Selection Box | 100,000 Kinah | Extra entries |

See [[Currencies and Economy]] for the full Wind Breeze table and its
per-server weekly caps.

## Realistic income

A Korean Season 3 efficiency table, restated as a daily budget for a mid-game
player running the whole loop:

| Source | Daily expectation | Kinah type | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expedition and Transcendence cube rewards | several million to over ten million | **Bound** | medium |
| Field hunting | up to 2,000,000 | Normal | high |
| Gathering, crafting and Market sales | several million, price dependent | Normal | medium |
| Drop sales | several million, luck dependent | Normal | medium |
| Supply Request item sales | 60,000–300,000/day equivalent | Normal | medium |
| Abyss farming | 500,000–2,000,000 | Normal | medium |
| Ariel's Trace crystals | ~100,000/day equivalent (700,000/week) | Normal | medium |
| Nightmare and challenge tickets | ~360,000 | Normal | medium |

A reasonable planning figure for a mid-game player who does the whole loop is
**roughly 5 to 10 million tradable Kinah per day plus several million Bound
Kinah** — with the tradable half almost entirely from gathering, crafting and
drop sales rather than dungeons. That is a synthesis rather than a quoted
number: **confidence medium.**

Every one of those figures is from the KR level-45 or Chapter 1 economy. Global
launch prices will differ, and early-server material prices spike hard, which
favours gatherers in week one.

## Main versus alt

The priorities genuinely diverge, because the constraints are different.

### Your main

The main is **entry-count limited**, not time limited. Its scarce resources are
Odyle Energy, weekly reward counts and its own gear ceiling.

1. Spend every Odyle Energy point and every weekly reward count. Cube rewards
   dwarf everything else, even though they pay Bound Kinah.
2. Fill the 2,000,000 field cap with the time left over, at Fafnite Burial
   Grounds or Artamia Highlands, while grinding Pet Understanding.
3. Run **Conquest Draupnir solo** for tradable draconic materials.
4. Gather Odyle for the weekly conversion. Ten focused minutes at a dense
   aerial spot reportedly covers a week's crafts.
5. Keep one gear profession above its recipe requirements so you are never
   buying materials at Market prices.
6. **Tier-down convert** surplus high-tier drops into the Potential stones your
   best-in-slot items actually need, rather than re-running old dungeons.

### Alts

Alts exist to **multiply per-character caps** and to hold pristine conversion
fodder. Their job list is short and mostly non-combat:

1. **The 2,000,000 daily field cap**, which is per character and is the reason
   alts pay at all.
2. **Per-character conversion counts.** Odyle Energy conversion is 4 per
   character per week on top of the per-server cap, and event recipes have
   explicitly stacked per-character allowances on top of per-server ones.
3. **Supply Request items**, valued at up to ~2,100,000 Kinah per character
   per week.
4. **Sealed Dungeon treasure chests** — a specific one-spot alt farm circulates
   in the Korean community as an alt-only route.
5. **Warehouse mules for un-imprinted, unbound gear** destined for Potential
   conversion.
6. **Levelling gathering on the alt is cheap** (about an hour) and turns every
   alt into a second gathering route.

Two structural cautions. First, Korea has been steadily moving limits from
**per-character to per-server** precisely to reduce alt multiplication, so the
alt case is weaker than it was and may be weaker still at global launch.
Second, **Market access requires a Membership**, and a Membership is per
account, not per character — so alts do not each need one, but the account does.
See [[Alts and Account Wide Progression]].

## Traps and mistakes

- **Budgeting in "Kinah" without separating Bound from tradable.** A player with
  200 million Bound Kinah cannot buy one manastone from the Market.
- Missing the 05:00 reset and losing a day of the field cap.
- Dismantling a duplicate Unique instead of listing it.
- Handing in Supply Request items when Kinah, not Abyss Points, is the
  bottleneck.
- Soul Imprinting or enhancing a drop you meant to sell or convert. Imprinting
  removes tradability permanently.
- Buying tradable goods from the Market to feed your own growth. For most
  materials the Market price sits above what farming the same material costs in
  time.
- Assuming the broker works without a Membership. It does not, and that closes
  most of this note's income routes for a free account.
- Camping a contested field spot when Artamia Highlands respawns faster.
- Grinding field mobs before spending weekly counts. Counts expire; mobs do not.

## A note on buying currency

Sites selling Kinah and boosting services advertise heavily against AION 2
search terms and several of the guide pages that surface in these searches are
theirs. Buying in-game currency for real money outside the official
**Kinah-Quna Exchange** violates the terms of service and risks the account.
The official Exchange exists precisely to make that unnecessary and it is
player-to-player. **This vault does not recommend third-party currency
purchase**, and the SEO content those sites publish is unreliable on specifics —
several numbers they quote for AION 2 could not be corroborated anywhere else.

## Global launch notes

Three things could reshape this note at launch:

1. **Whether Bound Kinah ships.** Korea's split arrived in March 2026 as an
   anti-bot measure, four months before global launch, and NC's global business
   model notice does not mention it. If global launches without it, dungeon
   Kinah is spendable and gathering matters less. If it launches with it,
   everything in this note is load-bearing. This is the largest open economic
   question for a Western player.
2. **Whether the 2,000,000 daily cap ships**, and at what value. It is a
   KR anti-inflation measure of the same vintage.
3. **Whether the level 45 gathering gate ships.** Korean gathering guides
   written after the January 2026 anti-bot patch still describe unrestricted
   levelling, so the gate's scope is unclear.

What is certain: early-access servers run **separate Markets and Exchanges**,
with a Server Market from early access and a Cross-Server Market opening later.
Founder-server prices and launch-day prices will be different economies. Selling
raw materials into the launch spike is the single best early play, and it is
available to anyone who levels gathering while questing.

See [[Global Launch Differences]] and [[Currencies and Economy]].
