# Gathering and Professions

> **In one sentence:** gathering, the five crafting professions, the material
> conversion bench and field farming are not four hobbies — they are one
> economy with a single input (nodes in the world) and four outputs (your own
> gear, your consumables, your weekly Odyle Energy, and tradable Kinah).

## Why this chapter exists

[[Crafting]] already covers the recipes and the combo mechanic that produce
gear. It deliberately stops at the workbench. This chapter covers everything
feeding into that workbench and everything else the same materials buy:

- **What to gather, where** — [[Gathering Nodes and Plants]]

## The one-diagram version

Four systems, one loop:

| Stage | Korean | What it is | Feeds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gathering | 정기 추출 / 채집 | One unified skill covering ore, gem, log, herb, cooking ingredient and Odyle nodes | Every profession, every conversion |
| Professions | 제작 | Five crafting skills with their own mastery ladders | Weapons, armor, accessories, potions, manastones, food |
| Material Conversion | 물질 변환 | A universal trade-in bench, 684 recipes producing 334 items | Odyle Energy, wings, gear ranks, Potential, substitute materials |
| Field farming | 필드 사냥 | Killing things for Kinah, drops and mob-only materials | The 2,000,000 daily tradable Kinah cap, draconic materials, Spirit Stones |

The thing that makes it a loop rather than four lists is **Odyle** (오드). Odyle
is the only gathering material that converts into *any other* gathering
material of the same quality, and it is also the material you convert into
**Odyle Energy**, which is what opens dungeon reward cubes. So a single node
type feeds both your crafting bench and your dungeon loot access. See
[[Odyle and Special Resources]] for why the naming around it is such a mess and
[[Odyle Energy and Entry Limits]] for the energy side.

## What each part actually buys you

**Gathering buys access, not power.** Levelling 정기 추출 to its ceiling takes
roughly an hour of focused play according to every Korean guide checked, and it
costs nothing but time while you quest. It is one of the cheapest things in the
game per unit of long-term benefit, because everything downstream of it is
gated on materials rather than on skill.

**Professions buy your weapon and your consumables.** Crafted weapons out-level
same-era dungeon weapons, and crafted gear is the only high-end equipment that
circulates on the Market. With Heroic grade reportedly absent from the launch
build, the crafted **Unique** tier is the practical ceiling at global launch,
which raises the relative value of a profession rather than lowering it. See
[[Gear Tiers and Sources]].

**Material Conversion buys everything that has no other faucet.** Wings,
Potential Amplification Stones, Amulet and Belt rank-ups, Attunement Stones,
Daevanion Crystals, Kinah boxes, the Unique Brooch, and the weekly Odyle Energy
crafts all live on the conversion bench and nowhere else. If you skip
conversion you are not skipping a crafting minigame, you are skipping several
progression systems.

**Field farming buys tradable Kinah.** Because most dungeon Kinah in the KR
build is Bound Kinah, field hunting to the daily cap plus selling gathered
materials is the only reliable *tradable* income. See
[[Currencies and Economy]] and [[Farming Routes and Kinah]].

## The order to approach this at Season 1

This is the sequence to run at global launch, assuming the launch build sits in
the level-45-era item level band of roughly **0 to 3,800** and tops out at
Unique grade.

1. **From level 1, gather opportunistically.** Open the 정기 추출 window
   (Alt+F) and put your first Specialty Points into **Seeker** nodes, because
   nodes do not appear on the minimap at all until you do. The Taiwanese guide
   order is Odyle → Cooking Ingredient → Herb → Log → Ore → Gem; that ordering
   is about what you can use immediately, not about value.
2. **Level Cooking alongside questing.** Materials are cheap, food buffs are
   used in literally every activity, and combos double the output instead of
   producing an upgrade item you then have to re-craft. Both the Taiwanese
   beginner guides call Cooking the correct first profession, and it is the
   recommendation this chapter endorses. See [[Professions and Mastery]] and
   [[Consumables and Buffs]].
3. **Push 정기 추출 입문 (Novice) to 50 and take the promotion quest.** It
   auto-generates and needs one **Shining Ruby** (빛나는 루비) from Ruby nodes.
   Professional 1–50 opens on the other side, for 100 levels total.
4. **Concentrate Specialty Points on Odyle.** Seven points maxes the Odyle line.
   You cannot afford breadth: full investment across all six categories would
   need 90 Common and 42 Specialty Points, and 100 levels only pay 65 and 14.
   A respec costs 50,000 Kinah.
5. **Start the conversion bench the week you hit max level.** The weekly Odyle
   Energy conversion is the single highest-value repeatable in this chapter and
   it resets Wednesday 05:00. Do not let it lapse. See [[Material Conversion]].
6. **Then, and only then, pick a gear profession.** Take the one that makes
   your own class's weapon, and level it on first-stage refining (ingots,
   lumber, powder) rather than on anything expensive.
7. **Fill the 2,000,000 daily field-hunting Kinah cap** with whatever time is
   left, ideally in a zone where you are also gathering and also grinding Pet
   Understanding. See [[Farming Routes and Kinah]].

The trap in this ordering is doing step 6 before step 3. A craft attempted at
or below the recipe's required mastery level is a materials fire, and mastery
comes from volume, which comes from gathering.

## Global launch notes

The global service launches **2026-10-05** with Founder early access from
**2026-09-30**, and NC ran a Launch Scale Test on 2026-09-17/18. Launch content
is expected to mirror the KR/TW **Season 1** period: the early star-rated
5-player dungeons, Transcendence, Nightmare Layer I, Ascension Trial, the Ludra
raid and Shugo minigames. For this chapter that means:

- The conversion bench will be much smaller than the 684 recipes datamined from
  the current KR client. Wings, Odyle Energy, Potential Stones and the gathering
  substitutions should be present; Chapter-1-era entries such as Draconic
  Essence, the Kaldrix voucher and the Sanctuary succession recipes should not.
- With Heroic grade reportedly missing, profession output should be planned
  toward **Unique** ceilings, which makes the mid-tier Dragon Lord chains the
  end of the road rather than a stepping stone.
- Korea added a **level 45 floor on resource gathering** in January 2026 as an
  anti-bot measure. Korean gathering guides written after that date still
  describe levelling 정기 추출 from level 1 with no such restriction, so it is
  unclear whether the gate applies to all nodes, to specific zones, or was
  rolled back. Assume you can gather while levelling, and verify in the client.
