# Odyle and Special Resources

> **In one sentence:** the client calls it **Odyle**, it is one Korean word
> (오드) doing two jobs — a gathered material and the energy currency crafted
> from it — and it is the most valuable thing in the world economy because it
> converts into everything else and nothing converts back into it.

> **Season 1 verdict.** Odyle itself, all four qualities, the air-gathering
> mechanic, the conversion asymmetry and the Odyle Energy link are all
> Korean-launch systems, so the core of this note applies on day one. **Out at
> launch:** Draconic Essence and the Pendant it enhances, Shining Odyle dungeon
> nodes, the Kaldrix Brooch voucher, Sanctuary succession recipes, and the Core +
> Breath of Magic season-shop route. **Borderline:** Attunement Stones and
> Fragments, which entered Korea 28 days after its launch. With Heroic absent,
> **Radiant is your top prefix** and the Heroic Attunement step is irrelevant.

## The naming problem, solved

The resource is spelled **Odyle** in the shipping game data. Everything else is
a translation artefact.

| Spelling | Where it comes from | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Odyle** | The AION 2 Hub client extract, taken from shipping game data, for both the material and "Odyle Energy" | **Use this.** The client string |
| Ode | Korean guides romanising 오드 letter-by-letter; also used by the Fextralife wiki's sister pages | Alias |
| Odd | Machine translations of 오드 that hit the English word "odd"; AION 2 Hub itself uses "Odd Energy" in some older notes | Alias |
| Odel / Odal | Loose romanisations, mostly on English SEO blogs | Alias |
| **Odella / Odellya** | **Vortex Gaming's English machine translation of 오드.** Its translated gathering guide renders every instance of 오드 as "Odella", including "Odella Energy" and "Odella material conversion" | Alias. Not a game term |
| Aether / Aetherine | Taiwanese guides gloss 奧德 as "（Aether）" because the floating-orb-in-the-sky resource in the original *Aion* was Aether (에테르). "Aetherine" does not appear as a live AION 2 string in any source checked | Legacy *Aion 1* framing, not an AION 2 name |
| Odium | Aion lore term that circulates as a guess | Not a game term |

So: **the resource the user has seen called "Odellya" is Odyle**, and the trail
runs 오드 → Vortex Gaming's translator → "Odella". If you are reading an
English guide that says Odella, it is a translation of a Korean guide and the
underlying claims are usually fine; only the word is wrong.

### Is it the same word as the energy currency? Yes and no

**Same Korean word, two different items.**

| Item | Korean | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Odyle | 오드 | A **gatherable material** from floating air nodes, in four quality tiers |
| Odyle Energy | 오드 에너지 | A **currency** consumed to open dungeon reward cubes, with a regenerating pool and a cap |

They are directly connected: you convert Odyle **into** Odyle Energy at the
material conversion bench, which is exactly why gathering Odyle is a
progression activity rather than a crafting chore. But they are distinct
inventory entries, they are not interchangeable, and the energy is Bound while
the material is tradable. The glossary's separate entries for 오드 and
오드 에너지 are correct and worth keeping separate in your head. See
[[Odyle Energy and Entry Limits]] and [[Currencies and Economy]].

There is a third thing with the same stem, and it causes real confusion:
**Shining Odyle** (빛나는 오드) is a *node that appears on a boss kill* inside
certain dungeons, not an item. More on it below.

## What Odyle is and where it comes from

Odyle is the top-tier gathering material. Lore-side, Taiwanese guides describe
it as fragments of energy left behind by the creator god — effectively divine
power — which is why it slots into every high-end system.

Mechanically:

- It **floats in the open air**, visible as green orbs. You must fly to it and
  hover while extracting, so your route is limited by flight duration. See
  [[Flight and Traversal]].
- **Alt + double-click** an orb to auto-fly and gather it, which is the standard
  way to farm a dense aerial cluster.
- It comes in the same four qualities as every other material:

| Quality | Korean | Client English | What it converts into |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Odyle | 오드 | Odyle | All base equipment crafting; base material conversion |
| Fine Odyle | 순도 높은 오드 | Fine Odyle | **Any Rare-grade gathering material** |
| Pure Odyle | 순수한 오드 | Pure Odyle | **Any Heritage-grade gathering material**; the Brooch craft; wing crafting |
| Radiant Odyle | 찬란한 오드 | Radiant Odyle | **Any Unique-grade gathering material**; the common material for every Unique wing |

Higher tiers roll at a low chance from Odyle nodes, and **Duty/Mission Quests**
(사명 퀘스트) pay them as guaranteed rewards, which is the reliable route when
the field is contested. Shugo Festa also pays base Odyle.

### Why Odyle is the answer to everything

**Odyle converts into any other gathering material of the same quality, and the
reverse does not exist.** That one asymmetry is the whole strategy:

- It is why your Specialty Points should go into the Odyle line rather than
  being spread across six families.
- It is why "I need Asvata Log and the node is camped" is not a problem.
- It is why Odyle is described in Korean guides as simultaneously the base of
  all crafting **and** a currency.

On top of that, Odyle is the input for:

1. **Odyle Energy** (weekly conversion) — the gate on every dungeon reward cube.
2. **Wings** — Pure Odyle for the lower grades, Radiant Odyle as the common
   material for every Unique wing. Each new wing crafted is a permanent
   **+200 flight power**, so this is genuine progression. See
   [[Wings Acquisition]].
3. **Daevanion Crystals** — via conversion. See [[Daevanion Boards]].
4. **Top-tier equipment crafting** at every rung.
5. **The Brooch** — 30 Pure Odyle per Brooch, and two slots. *Post-launch: the
   Brooch slot is KR 2026-05-27, so this is the one Odyle sink a Season 1 player
   should not stockpile toward. See the roadmap section in
   [[Professions and Mastery]].*

### Shining Odyle (빛나는 오드): the dungeon node

Since the 2026-02-11 patch, killing the boss in **Cradle of Nihility**
(무의 요람) and **Dying Dramata's Nest** (죽은 드라마타의 둥지) spawns a
**Shining Odyle** node. Extracting it gives **Fine, Pure and Radiant Odyle at a
higher rate than field gathering does**.

This is the mechanic behind the widely mistranslated claim that you "get 6
Odyle after the instance boss". If your goal is high-quality Odyle rather than
volume, the dungeon node beats the field, and it costs you nothing extra
because you were clearing the dungeon anyway. Both instances are Chapter-1-era
content, so this may not be present at Season 1.

## The other high-value materials

None of these are gathered. They matter because they are what the conversion
bench eats and what the Market pays for.

### Refining Stones (제련석)

The bulk consumable of equipment crafting, in four tiers, all from monster
drops, Expedition and Shugo Festa:

| Tier | Korean | Feeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refining Stone | 제련석 | Common weapons, armor, accessories |
| Artisan's Refining Stone | 장인의 제련석 | Rare |
| Master's Refining Stone | 달인의 제련석 | Unique |
| Master's Superior Refining Stone | 달인의 최상급 제련석 | Heritage |

Top-tier weapon crafts consume these by the hundreds — the Brooch alone wants
**200 Master's Refining Stones**. Do not hand them in on an Abyss Supply
Request, and do not vendor them.

### Draconic materials (용족 재료)

The drop-only half of the crafted gear chain. Pre-Chapter 1 they existed as
three item types × four prefixes, mapped to specific dungeons and specific
crafted weapon families. This is the structure a Season 1 build will have:

| Prefix | Korean | Crafted family | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thick | 두꺼운 | Star Dragon Lord (건룡왕) | Krao Cave, Draupnir |
| Hard | 단단한 | Dark Dragon Lord (흑룡왕) | Urugugu Canyon, Vakron Sky Island |
| Solid | 견고한 | Ebony Dragon Lord (암룡왕) | Fire Temple, Ferocious Horn Den (named mobs) |
| Sturdy | 강고한 | Horned Dragon Lord (기룡왕) | **Sanctuary — Abyssal Forge: Ludra only** |

And the item type decides which profession uses it:

| Item | Korean | Profession |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draconic Horn | 용족의 뿔 | Blacksmithing (weapons) |
| Draconic Leather | 용족의 가죽 | Tailoring/Armor |
| Draconic Scale | 용족의 비늘 | Jewelcrafting (accessories, bow, staff) |

Plus a parallel set of "Thought / Will / Ego / Innocence of Wrath"
(분노의 사념 / 의지 / 자아 / 무고) from the same Conquest dungeons and
Sanctuary, keyed to the equipment grade.

That prefix table also resolves the naming question in [[Crafting]]: the
English database renders 건룡왕 / 흑룡왕 / 암룡왕 / 기룡왕 as **Star / Dark /
Ebony / Horned Dragon Lord**, which is a *tier ladder within* Jewelcrafting's
bow and staff line, separate from the Genesis and Nemesis (천룡 / 마룡) faction
mirrors. **Confidence medium** — this comes from one English wiki's recipe list
cross-checked against the Korean prefix table.

**Market note:** Hard Draconic Scale (단단한 용족의 비늘) and Solid Draconic
Horn (견고한 용족의 뿔) hold persistently high Market prices in the KR economy,
and **Conquest Draupnir solo-farmed in a private room** is repeatedly named the
best free-to-play income route once you can clear it. Korean guides put the
entry point at roughly **Combat Power 1,300–1,400** in the level-45-era scale.
See [[Farming Routes and Kinah]].

### Draconic Essence (용족의 정수)

Chapter 1 **consolidated** the horn/leather/scale materials from Expedition and
Sanctuary into two items: **Draconic Essence** (용족의 정수) and **Radiant
Draconic Essence** (찬란한 용족의 정수).

- Grade: Epic (Heritage in this vault's vocabulary). Bound and unbound versions
  both exist.
- Uses: **enhancing Unique-grade and above Pendants**, and **converting into
  any of the individual draconic materials** at the conversion bench.
- Sources: named field and dungeon bosses (Silent Tartan, Soul Ruler Kashapa,
  Watcher Knash, Researcher Setram and others), Eltnen and Morheim quests, new
  Monolith level rewards.
- Vendor value: 1,000 Kinah, which tells you nothing — its real value is its
  Market price as a Pendant material.

Because it is a Chapter 1 item and Pendant is a Chapter 1 slot, **expect
Draconic Essence to be absent at Season 1** and the four-prefix system above to
be what you actually farm. See [[Brooch Pendant and Seal Slots]] and
[[Accessories]].

### Attunement Stones and Fragments (조율석 / 조율석 파편)

The material for re-rolling Soul Imprint options. Introduced **2025-12-17**, in
the first major post-launch Korean update — 28 days after the Korean launch,
which is why [[Season 1 System Availability]] marks them **Borderline** rather
than in or out. The underlying Attunement system is a launch feature and is a
launch player's only option-fixing tool; the *Stones* are the part that may not
have shipped yet. Plan around Unique fodder as the fallback.

- **Source:** Expedition and Sanctuary rewards, per grade. The Odyle Energy
  cube's payout of Unique and Heroic fragments was **doubled** in the
  2026-02-11 patch. New Chapter 1 field bosses also drop fragments.
- **Two conversion routes**, both at `물질 변환 → 특수 → 소모품`:
  - **12 fragments + 100,000 Kinah → 1 Attunement Stone**
  - **30 fragments → 1 Attunement Stone, free**
- A Unique Attunement Stone can then be converted up to a Heroic one.
- Unique *equipment* can also be converted into Attunement Stones, which is
  what anchors their price.

The community maths on which route to take is worth repeating because it is
counter-intuitive. Valuing an Attunement Stone at the Market price of a Unique
item (about **120,000 Kinah** on the Elyos market in May 2026), the 12-fragment
route implies a fragment is worth about **1,666 Kinah** while the 30-fragment
route implies about **4,000 Kinah** — so the free route makes each fragment
roughly **2.4× more valuable**. If you have fragments and time, take the free
route. The exception is the **Heroic** conversion, where paying the 700,000
Kinah is the better deal. Single-source community analysis;
**confidence medium.** See [[Soul Imprint and Soul Binding]].

### Theostones (신석)

**Naming warning:** "Theostone" is the client's English string for 신석, which
this vault's glossary maps to **Godstone**. They are the same thing. If you see
Theostone on a database page and Godstone in a guide, do not go looking for two
systems.

- **What they do:** slot into the Enhancement menu and grant a low-chance
  on-attack proc. Observed examples: *Kaisinel's Vestige* (3% Blind for 5s, 15s
  cooldown), *Meslamtaeda's Greed* (3% Stun for 3s), *Tiamat's Rage* (1% for
  4,000 fire damage), *Lumiel's Interference* (3% to halve target combat speed
  for 15s). Only one effect applies if you engrave duplicates of the same one.
- **Sources:** Transcendence and Sanctuary, raid and party-dungeon drops,
  Hidden Cubes, and a family of reward chests — Rare Theostone Chest, Radiant
  Theostone Chest, Abyssal Chest, the Abyssal Forge Odyle Energy Cube reward
  chest, Corroded Chest.
- **Tradable, and this is the point.** Unlike most cube contents, the base
  Theostone items are marked Tradable in the database. Vendor price on the Rare
  tier is 9,600 Kinah, but the questlog listing for *Meslamtaeda's Greed* shows
  384,000, so the tiers differ by two orders of magnitude. Duplicates are worth
  listing rather than decomposing.
- They are also **enchantable and decomposable**, and decomposition yields
  materials for higher enhancements.

Requires level 9 to use — effectively no gate. See
[[Manastones Spirit Stones and Godstones]].

### Silentium (실렌티움)

The honest answer on Silentium is that it is **a Kinah token with no other
use**, and knowing that is worth money.

- **Source:** Ascension Trial (각성전) clears, Hidden Cube rewards, paid
  packages and Deva Pass tracks.
- **Use:** sell it. The general goods NPC pays a flat **5,000 Kinah** each,
  because the game classifies it as junk (잡템).
- On **KR/TW**, it **can** be listed on the Market (some players list above 5,000
  hoping buyers misread it). **Do not buy Silentium.**
- **Global release (EU live):** Silentium is **character-bound** — not tradable,
  not Server Warehouse. Alt Ascension Trial runs do not move Silentium to the main;
  only Kinah already on that character (after vendoring) follows normal Kinah
  transfer rules. See [[Twink Playbook - YouTube Ey_xTunLIg0]].

Ascension Trial is a weekly with a small entry count, so Silentium is a
predictable few-hundred-thousand Kinah per week **on the character that ran Trial**
and nothing more. If a future patch gives it a sink, that changes; as of the
sources checked it has none. See [[Daily and Weekly Loops]].

### Magic Crystal and Spirit Stone (마력의 결정, 정령석)

The Alchemy feedstock, and the reason Alchemy is a business.

- **Spirit Stone** (정령석) is a **monster drop**, not gathered. One Spirit
  Stone crafts into **three Spirit Stone Powder** (정령석 가루) at Alchemy
  level 5.
- **Magic Crystal** (마력의 결정) is crafted, and it is the item you make 100 of
  for a single Brooch.
- Powder and crystals are the standard Alchemy mastery-levelling loop and they
  are cheap because the input is field trash. Never vendor a Spirit Stone.

### Breath of Magic and Cores (마력의 숨결, 코어)

Season-shop and conversion items that turn surplus Unique dungeon gear into
draconic equipment: convert an unwanted Unique from Expedition Conquest into a
**Core**, then combine Core + **Breath of Magic** (bought from the season shop,
limit 4 per server) into draconic gear. This is the "my drops were the wrong
class" release valve. See [[Material Conversion]].

*Post-launch.* The recipe and the 4-per-server shop limit both arrive with the
KR **2026-02-11** patch. Season shop stock and per-server limits are also among
the most frequently retuned numbers in the game, so read the limit off the shop
rather than off this note even after it ships. At Season 1 the wrong-class
release valve you actually have is the **Equipment Change Voucher** weapon swap,
itself borderline at KR 2025-12-17.

## Priorities and breakpoints

- **Gather Odyle every time you fly past it.** It is the only material that is
  never wrong to have.
- **Never let the weekly Odyle Energy conversion lapse.** It resets Wednesday
  05:00 and it is the highest-value repeatable in this chapter.
- **Bank Radiant Odyle.** It is the common material for every Unique wing and
  the top rung of every craft. Do not spend it on a mid-tier recipe and do not
  hand it in on a supply request.
- **Sell duplicate Theostones, not decompose them.**
- **Sell Silentium immediately** at the NPC. It has no use and no upside.
- **Take the free 30-fragment Attunement Stone route** unless you need Heroic
  stones now.
- **Farm Draupnir solo** once you can, for the draconic materials, not for the
  gear.

## Traps and mistakes

- Confusing the Odyle **material** with Odyle **Energy** when reading a guide,
  and then wondering why your energy did not go up after gathering. You still
  have to run the conversion.
- Buying Silentium off the Market because it looks like a growth material.
- Handing Radiant Odyle, Sturdy Draconic Scale or Refining Stones to an Abyss
  Supply Request because the request asked for them.
- Paying 100,000 Kinah per Attunement Stone when you have fragments to spare.
- Dropping into a Ferocious Horn Den run expecting Sturdy draconic materials —
  Sturdy comes only from the Ludra Sanctuary.
- Reading "Odella" in an English guide and assuming it is a different resource
  from Odyle. It is the same word; see the alias table at the top.
- Banking Pure Odyle for a Brooch at Season 1. The slot is not in the launch
  build.
- Running out of flight time over open ground while chasing an orb.

## Global launch notes

Odyle itself, its four qualities, the air-gathering mechanic, the conversion
asymmetry and the Odyle Energy link all predate Season 1 and should ship intact.

What should **not** be there:

| Material or route | KR introduced | Season 1 verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Odyle, four qualities, air nodes, conversion asymmetry | Launch | **In** |
| Refining Stones, all four tiers | Launch | **In** |
| Draconic materials, four prefixes × horn/leather/scale | Launch | **In** — this is what you actually farm |
| Theostones (신석) | Launch | **In** |
| Silentium | Launch | **In**, **character-bound** on global; 5,000 Kinah NPC only on the earning character |
| Magic Crystal, Spirit Stone | Launch | **In** |
| Attunement Stones and Fragments | 2025-12-17 | Borderline |
| Core + Breath of Magic, 4-per-server season shop limit | 2026-02-11 | **Out** |
| Shining Odyle nodes (Cradle of Nihility, Dying Dramata's Nest) | 2026-02-11 | **Out** |
| Sanctuary succession recipes | 2026-04-08 / 2026-05-27 | **Out** |
| Kaldrix Brooch voucher, and the Brooch slot it feeds | 2026-05-27 | **Out** |
| Draconic Essence and Radiant Draconic Essence | 2026-07-01 | **Out** |
| Pendant slot, which Draconic Essence enhances | 2026-07-01 | **Out** |

The consolidation matters more than it looks: because Draconic Essence is out,
the **four-prefix draconic system is what a launch player farms**, and the
prefix-to-dungeon table above is the operative one rather than a historical
note. Sturdy still comes only from the Ludra Sanctuary.

With Heroic grade reportedly absent from the launch build, **Radiant** is the
top prefix that matters and the Heroic Attunement Stone conversion is
irrelevant on day one. Plan Odyle stockpiles around Unique ceilings — and
specifically **do not** bank 30 Pure Odyle per Brooch against a slot that is not
in the build. See [[Season 1 System Availability]].
