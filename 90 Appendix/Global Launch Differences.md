# Global Launch Differences

> **In one sentence:** global does not inherit the Korean live service as it
> stands today, it inherits a snapshot of it, and almost everything in this
> vault that was introduced after the Korean launch should be read as a
> roadmap prediction rather than as day-one content.

## Status of this note

Everything below is marked **Confirmed** when it comes from NC's own
announcements, the Steam store page or on-record NC statements at gamescom
2026, **Launch Scale Test** when it was observed in the 2026-09-17/18 client
(level cap 37, market and shop disabled, wipe afterwards), and **Inferred**
when it is derived from the Korea and Taiwan timeline. NC has still published
no global patch note. The Launch Scale Test is the highest-resolution look we
have at how global *systems* differ from KR/TW, but it is not the Advance
Access client. Treat every test number as **medium confidence** until 2026-09-30.

**Confirmed on EU live (2026-10-05):** **character level cap is 45.** There is no
45→50 band, no Eltnen or Morheim, and no sixth stigma slot in this build. Item
level and Combat Power are the progression bar after the story ends. See
[[Season 1 System Availability]] and [[Level and Ascension]].

Note that the widely repeated "September 2026" global window is now only
technically true through the September 30 head start. The free-to-play launch
is 2026-10-05. Regions served are North America, South America, Europe and
Asia. There is no mobile client for global, which is a genuine structural
difference from Korea and Taiwan.

The Launch Scale Test is not a preview of the launch build's content ceiling:
its level cap was set to 37, purchases, the market, currency exchange and
player trading were disabled, and only default outfits were available. It *is*
a preview of several system retunes that Korean Season 1 never had. Those sit
in the next section.

## Launch Scale Test — global systems are not Korean Season 1

**Launch Scale Test, 2026-09-17/18.** Primary source: Welps' day-one walkthrough
of the global test client ([video](https://youtu.be/5klnmd6NXYU)). Corroboration
and extra numbers: AION Strategies' write-up of the AION2 Hub / Madsin KR-vs-
global comparison. Neither is an NC patch note. The pattern across both is the
same: **raw gear numbers went up, percentage bonuses and extra boards went
down, and several KR grinds were replaced rather than delayed.**

Anyone following a Korean Season 1 guide for feathers, Daevanion, pets, wings,
dungeon doors or Odyle Energy will be wrong on day one.

### Wings

Not "collect 50 fragments". Last-boss drop: **1.5%** chance to enter a pool that is **50/50 wing or artwork**. A **separate 5%** individual roll if you killed every last boss in the instance. The two rolls do not combine. Sealed wing + **10 Radiant Odyle** in the Morph tab = usable wing. **Usable wing is bound.** Sealed wing **is tradable** and morphs into **10,000** enhancement stones. KR's extract-a-wing-for-**90,000**-stones whale path is closed. See [[Wings Acquisition]].

### Titles, dodge, season clock

- Title percentages **nerfed** in line with Daevanion. Example: Empty Closet Combat Speed and Move Speed **5% → 3.5%**. See [[Titles and Achievements]].

## The launch content baseline

**Inferred, medium confidence.** Global appears to be launching from
approximately the Korean launch build rather than from Chapter 1.

The strongest evidence is NC's own gamescom material. In a September 2026
hands-on interview NC stated that **eight classes will be available at global
launch** and described group content as **dungeons for four players and larger
raids for eight**. Both figures match the Korean service as it existed before
2026-07-01 and contradict the current Korean service, where the Brawler is the
ninth class, parties are five players, Expedition and Transcendence are
five-player and Sanctuary is ten-player.

Working from that, the most likely day-one baseline is:

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level cap | **45** | 50 | **Confirmed for this vault.** Chapter 1's raise to 50 is out. |
| Item-level unlock range | **0–3,800** | 4,800 | 3,800 is the bar max. 4,000+ is Chapter 1. See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]] |
| Classes | 8 (no Brawler) | 9 | Confirmed by NC at gamescom |
| Party size | 4 | 5 | Confirmed by NC at gamescom |
| Raid size | 8 | 10 | Confirmed by NC at gamescom |

| Regions | Verteron / Altgard and the starter zones | plus Eltnen / Morheim | Inferred, medium |
| Update unit | Unknown; NC has used both Season and Chapter framing | Chapter | Low |

One low-trust source claims content "will roll out at a measured pace rather
than matching the Korean servers immediately" and that some Heroic-grade gear
is removed or limited early. Treat the phrasing as plausible and the specifics
as unverified until NC publishes a global roadmap; see [[Open Questions]].

The practical consequence for the rest of this vault: any note describing
level 50, item level **4,000+**, Chapter 1 regions, the Pendant slot, Stat
Arcana, or the ten-player Sanctuary format should carry a global-launch caveat
saying that the system is expected, not present. The Season 1 item-level bar
is **0–3,800**; see [[Item Level vs Combat Power]].

## Monetisation

**Confirmed for global.**

- Free to play, funded by a cash shop, an optional monthly membership, optional
  battle passes and pre-launch Founder's Packs.
- The membership is a **single optional tier at $15 per month**. It unlocks
  market (auction house) access, the Kinah/Quna exchange, a subscriber-only
  cash shop section and unspecified "additional gameplay benefits".
- Two currencies: Kinah earned in game, Quna bought with money and used for the
  shop and passes.
- NC states the cash shop is cosmetics and consumables, that the items sold
  "do not affect gear progression or long-term power", and that the
  consumables are earnable through normal play.
- Founder's Packs are Standard $24.99, Deluxe $49.99, Ultimate $99.99. All
  three grant the same five-day head start and 30 days of membership, and NC
  has published all contents as appearance-only with no stats.
- Early-access servers run with **separate markets, exchanges and PvP matching**
  for a period after the head start, so Founder's Pack buyers are isolated
  economically and competitively rather than seeded into the general economy.

**How that differs from Korea and Taiwan.** Korea launched with two separate
memberships on 28-day reset cycles, a "comfort" tier and a "content" tier,
estimated by community reporting at roughly $40–50 per month combined before
battle passes, with the cheaper tier gating the auction house and trading.
That structure drew sustained pay-to-win criticism and NC consolidated it into
the single ₩25,000 Kwairing Premium on 2026-06-17. Global therefore skips the
dual-tier era entirely.

**The caveat that matters.** The specific mechanic Korean players objected to
most, subscription-gated market and currency-exchange access, does carry over
to global in a lighter form. Free players can earn Kinah and buy from the
market but cannot list items, trade Kinah freely, or use the Kinah-to-Quna
exchange. For this vault that means every note quoting a market price should
assume the reader may not be able to sell into that market.

## Servers, factions and cross-server structure

**Confirmed.** Servers are hosted in North America, South America, Europe and
Asia. Two waves open: Early Access servers on September 30, then a second wave
of Launch servers on October 5, running side by side afterwards. NC has not
published server names, capacities, data-centre cities, a preload date, or a
character pre-creation event. The global client supports ten languages.

**Confirmed.** The Elyos and Asmodian factions return, and NC has said that
large realm-versus-realm content matches across servers so that fights do not
depend on a single server's faction balance. Korea implements this as an
explicit server-matching system: 42 Korean servers were organised into 21
matching groups for Season 2, using Abyss kills, active combat population and
Artifact capture counts rather than raw headcount, and NC has periodically
re-matched servers since. Expect the same machinery on global, but the
specific grouping is unknown.

**Inferred, low confidence.** Descriptions of factions levelling on
"completely separate server spheres" and meeting only through cross-realm
matchmaking come from a low-trust SEO source and overstate what NC has said.
Korea does allow open-world cross-faction contact through the Rift of Time and
Space, and cross-faction dungeon parties were added on 2026-03-11. Do not plan
around faction separation until NC documents it for global.

## Events, pre-registration and region-specific rewards

**Confirmed for global.** Pre-launch participation is structured around Steam
wishlisting, the Launch Scale Test sign-up and the three Founder's Packs. NC
has not announced a global pre-registration reward track equivalent to the
Korean one.

**Korea and Taiwan, for comparison.** Korea runs a pre-registration campaign
before each Chapter with a dedicated reward box, plus coupon codes announced on
live streams (`WELCOMEBACK`, `AION2DAYS100`, `AION2SEASON3`, `LUCKYAION2` are
documented examples), plus recurring 14-day and 28-day attendance books, plus
anniversary events at 100 and 200 days. Chapter 1's pre-registration paid a
wing box, a 7-day appearance change ticket, 50 daily-dungeon instant clears
and large stacks of buff scrolls. Two things follow for a global player. First,
coupon codes are region-locked and Korean codes will not redeem on global
accounts. Second, the Korean growth-support packages ("An Exciting First Step",
"New Ascension", "New Continent Explorer", "Slow and Steady", and the earlier
"Shugo's Growth Boosting") are catch-up systems designed for a service that is
already old, so global is unlikely to ship with them at launch and likely to
receive an equivalent later.

**Inferred.** Korea and Taiwan also run collaboration events with local IP,
such as the fromis_9 collaboration that ran from 2026-06-17 to 2026-08-12 and
added customisation options, costumes and choreography. Region-locked
collaborations of this kind are the least likely content to appear on global,
and any permanent unlock attached to one is a candidate for being
unobtainable outside Korea. Flag any such unlock in [[Open Questions]] as it
is found.

## Systems most at risk of not arriving with global

Ordered by how much they change a progression plan. **Launch Scale Test
overrides** are marked.

1. **Ariel Daevanion board.** Korean launch content, **missing from the test
   client**. If that survives, there is no PvE specialised board and no tradable
   Ariel-point market. Azphel was present; fragment morphing was not.
2. **Potential** (KR 2026-03-04). This vault marked it out. The test showed the
   Potential UI on crafted gear. **Reopened.** If it ships, duplicate Unique
   gear has a third use again and crafted weapons can take a PvE damage layer
   they no longer roll for free.
3. **Inheritance at 100%** (2026-07-01). Korea launched inheritance in Season 2
   with a failure chance and a count cap, then made it free and guaranteed.
   Global inheriting the early version would make gear replacement far more
   punishing. Hub comparison also reported transfer-crafting of higher Dragon
   Lord tiers as **bound**.
4. **Brooch, Pendant and Seal slots** (2026-05-27, 2026-07-01, 2026-09). Three
   whole gear slots and their material economies. Still out.
5. **Wing enhancement** (2026-05-27). Turns wings from a traversal item into a
   stat item. Still out. Acquisition itself is already retuned (see LST table).
6. **Stat Arcana** (2026-07-01 and 2026-08-12). Four extra Arcana slots on a
   different progression model from skill Arcana. Still out.
7. **Kinah / Soul-bound Kinah split** (2026-03-25). Changes what "I have
   80,000,000 Kinah" means in every cost table in this vault. Bound Kinah was
   not settled in the test (trading disabled).
8. **Server-wide pets and wardrobe** (2026-01-07, 2026-01-21). Pet *levelling*
   was rebuilt (cap 3, 105 kills) independently of sharing. Sharing is still
   *verify at launch*.
9. **Housing, Sailing and Fishing.** Not yet live even in Korea as of
   2026-09-06, so certainly not present at global launch.
