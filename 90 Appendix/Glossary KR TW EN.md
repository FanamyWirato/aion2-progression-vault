# Glossary KR TW EN

> **In one sentence:** the same AION 2 system usually carries three or four
> different English names depending on whether the writer translated from
> Korean, from Traditional Chinese, or from another English blog, and this
> table maps them all back to one canonical term.

## How to read this table

The **English / global term** column is the name this vault uses. Where the
global client's own English string is known (from the AION 2 Hub client
extract, which is taken from the shipping game data), that string wins.
Where it is not known, the column holds the most common English name in
circulation and the entry is flagged in the aliases column.

The **Traditional Chinese** column only contains terms actually observed in
Taiwanese sources; a dash means the term was not found rather than that it
does not exist. Korean terms are given in hangul because search engines will
not match romanisations.

Confidence for this note as a whole is `medium`: the Korean and Traditional
Chinese columns are well corroborated. Class skill names now follow the live
global client. Several system and item names remain community translations.

## Naming pitfalls a global player will hit

**One Korean word, several English words.** 각인 means "engraving" and appears
in at least three unrelated places: manastone and godstone *engraving*,
*Soul Imprint* (영혼 각인), and the *Soul-bound* item tag printed as "(각인)".
An English blog that machine-translated a Korean patch note will render all
three as "engraving", so a sentence like "engraved Kinah" actually means
Soul-bound Kinah and has nothing to do with stones. Similarly 계승 covers both
Inheritance (moving upgrades between items) and Inheritance Crafting (using
old gear as a crafting ingredient), and English sources split it into
"succession", "transfer" and "carry-over" more or less at random.

**Several Korean words, one English word.** "Rune" is the worst offender.
Korean 룬 is the two-slot Rune of Clash and Rune of Devotion system, but SEO
articles also call Manastones (마석) "runes", and Taiwanese sources call the
runes 古文石, literally "ancient inscription stones", which round-trips into
English as "ancient stone" or "glyph". If an article says runes cannot be
destroyed, it is talking about manastones and is wrong about runes, which are
consumed on a failed enhancement from +2 upward.

**Deity names double as stat names.** The ten outer-ring stats are named after
Empyrean Lords: Justice [Nezekan], Destruction [Zikel], Freedom [Vaizel],
Death [Triniel], Illusion [Kaisinel], Wisdom [Lumiel], Life [Yustiel],
Destiny [Marchutan], Time [Siel], Space [Israphel]. The same names are also
Daevanion board names and Pantheon statue names. "Marchutan boost event"
therefore means a Daevanion board event, not a stat event. Ariel and Azphel
appear on the stat wheel but currently convert to nothing.

**Content names collide with item names.** 성배 is both the Chalice Arcana part
and the Sanctuary raid "Muspel's Chalice"; 열쇠 is both the Key Arcana and the
Hidden Cube key; 인장 is both the new Seal gear slot and the generic word for
an insignia on a nameplate. Context, not the word, disambiguates.

**Class names are unstable in English.** 권성 has shipped in English coverage as
Brawler (AION 2 Hub, from the client data), Vandal (Inven Global) and
Fistmaster (pre-launch NC marketing). The eight original classes are stable:
검성 Gladiator, 수호성 Templar, 살성 Assassin, 궁성 Ranger, 마도성 Sorcerer,
정령성 Spiritmaster, 치유성 Cleric, 호법성 Chanter. Note that 수호성 is Templar
and not "Guardian", even though the Korean word means guardian, and that
호법성 is Chanter and not "Protector".

**Numbers travel badly.** Korean guides write large numbers with 만 (ten
thousand), so 700k Combat Power appears as "70만" and gets mistranslated as
"70,000". Combat Power thresholds in older guides (1,400 / 1,500 / 1,600) come
from the level-45 era and are not comparable to the 300K–700K numbers used
after Chapter 1, because NCSoft rescaled the display.

**Taiwan uses semantic translation, Korea uses transliteration.** Korean often
transliterates the English concept (아르카나, 스티그마, 데바니온), while Taiwan
translates the meaning (阿爾卡那 is a transliteration but 烙印技能 "branding
skill" and 守護之力 "power of guardianship" are translations). Searching for
the Korean loanword in Chinese will therefore fail; search the Chinese
translation instead.

## How to search Korean sources effectively

Always search in hangul. The Korean-language internet for this game is an
order of magnitude larger than the English one, and the good material is on
Inven, Purple Lounge, Vortex Gaming, Gametoc and personal Tistory blogs.

Useful query patterns, in rough order of value:

- `아이온2 <system> 정리` — "summary of <system>". The single most productive
  pattern; Korean guide writers title posts this way by convention.
- `아이온2 <system> 공략` — "<system> strategy guide", more tactical.
- `아이온2 성장 요소 정리` — enumerates every progression system at once; this
  is the query to run when you want a completeness check rather than depth.
- `아이온2 내실` — returns exactly the non-gear progression systems, which are
  the ones English sources omit.
- `아이온2 스펙업 우선순위` — priority ordering between systems.
- `아이온2 <date> 패치노트` or `아이온2 <M>/<D>(수) 업데이트 노트` — patch notes.
  Korean maintenance is on Wednesdays, written `(수)`, so the day-of-week
  marker is a reliable filter.
- `site:inven.co.kr 아이온2 <system>` — Inven has separate boards for free
  discussion, tips (팁과 노하우), each class, and an NC-authored AI tips board
  (`NC AI 팁`) that is unusually accurate.
- `site:lounge.plaync.com AION2 <system>` — NC's own community platform;
  official summaries of new systems appear here within days of a patch.
- `아이온2 <system> 확률` — drop rates and success rates. NCSoft publishes a
  legally mandated probability page, and community posts reproduce it.
- `아이온2 <system> 기대값` — expected-value cost calculations, which is where
  the real enchant and enhancement maths lives.

For Taiwan, search `AION2 <system> 攻略` or `AION2 <system> 教學`, and note that
`永恆之塔` refers to the original Aion, not AION 2, so including it will pull in
fifteen-year-old material.

Two practical notes. Korean date formats in patch notes are `YYYY년 M월 D일`,
and content periods usually start after the Wednesday maintenance at 05:00
KST, so a "3월 25일" end date means the content is gone on the morning of the
25th. And Korean guides freely use slang contractions — 봉던 for 봉인 던전,
자사 for 자동 사냥, 투력 for 전투력, 늘무 for a stretched weapon — which search
engines index, so searching the contraction sometimes finds better material
than the full term.

## Terms whose global English name is still unresolved

- 쾌유의 진언 (Mantra of Recovery): not on the current global Chanter skill
  list. Recuperation is 쾌유의 주문; Healing Touch is 쾌유의 손길.
- 인장 (Seal): the slot had not shipped when the client data behind the
  English item database was last extracted, so "Seal" is a translation of the
  Korean rather than a confirmed client string. "Sigil" and "Emblem" are both
  in circulation.
- 권성: Brawler, Vandal and Fistmaster are all live in English coverage.
- 봉혼 / Soul Binding: the Korean stem is inferred from the item name 봉혼석
  and is not confirmed against a Korean patch note.
- 새싹 휘장 / Sprout Badge: the Traditional Chinese 幼章 is confirmed, the
  Korean form is reconstructed.
- 오드 / 오드 에너지: rendered as Ode, Odd, Odyle, Odella and Aether across
  sources; the AION 2 Hub client extract uses Odyle for the material and both
  "Odd Energy" and "Odyle Energy" for the resource in different notes. Use
  Odyle. The "Odella"/"Odellya" spelling was traced to a single site that
  published the same gathering guide in Korean and English: the Korean says
  오드, its own English version says Odella. Treat it as a translation
  artefact, and note that three things share the stem — the material, the
  energy currency, and 빛나는 오드 which is a dungeon node.
