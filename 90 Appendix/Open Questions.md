# Open Questions

Every system note ends with its own "Open questions" checklist. This page is the
consolidated view: the things that matter most, and the ones worth checking in
the first hours of the global client when the answers become cheap to get.

## How to close a question

1. Check the global client directly. Most numbers here come from the KR or TW
   build and global may differ.
2. If the client cannot answer it, check the newest KR patch notes, then a
   Korean guide, then the data on AION2 Hub. See [[Sources]] for trust tiers.
3. Update the note that owns the question, set its `confidence` and `patch`
   frontmatter, and tick the box there. Then remove it from this page.

## Highest-value questions for global launch day

Fill this list from the per-note checklists as you triage. The candidates that
already look important:

- [x] ~~Which chapter, season and level cap does global launch with?~~
      **Settled for planning: Season 1, character level cap 45, item level
      gates 0–3,800, no Heroic gear.** See [[Home]] and
      [[Season 1 System Availability]]. Re-check in the client on day one.
- [ ] Do the Launch Scale Test retunes survive Advance Access? Ariel deleted,
      feathers no longer grant Skill Points, doors at 700 / 1,400–1,500 /
      2,100–2,200, pet cap 3, Odyle 15/3h for everyone, Potential on crafted
      gear. See [[Global Launch Differences]].
- [ ] Are the enchant success rates and the pity ramp on global identical to the
      extracted KR values? Test also showed lower early caps (green +5, blue
      +10). See [[Enchanting]].
- [ ] Which of the newer KR systems exist at global launch, in particular the
      Arcana Libra slot, Advanced Succession and the newest wing lines.
      *Stigma slot expansion is settled: Season 1 has four slots and a level cap
      of 20. See [[Stigmas]].*
- [ ] What are the exact global names for the systems Korean guides call
      신수 이해 and 만신전, since guide-following depends on the wording. See
      [[Glossary KR TW EN]].
- [ ] Are pet possession effects and other account-wide bonuses shared per
      account or per server on global? See [[Alts and Account Wide Progression]].
