# Season 1 System Availability

Which progression systems a global launch player actually has on day one. This
is the note to check before following any Korean guide, because roughly a third
of the systems documented in this vault did not exist at the Korean launch and
arrived over the following ten months.

> **Launch Scale Test (2026-09-17/18) overrides.** Global is not a replay of
> Korean Season 1. Feathers do not grant Skill Points, the Ariel board was
> missing, dungeon doors dropped, pet cap is 3, Odyle Energy is 15/3h for
> everyone, and several percentage boards were cut. Full tables:
> [[Global Launch Differences]]. The rows below still use KR introduction dates
> as the *content* filter; LST is the *numbers* filter. Treat "out" as "do not
> plan around it" rather than "provably absent". Anything marked
> *verify at launch* is cheap to settle in the first hour of play.

## What this changes about the plan

1. **Gear investment is riskier at launch than in Korea today.** Without
   Succession Crafting and Advanced Succession, enhancement poured into a piece
   is far harder to move, so the "keeper item" judgement in [[Enchanting]]
   matters more, not less.
2. **Option rolling is more expensive.** No Soul Binding means no guaranteed
   extra line, and the 2026-02-25 duplicate-roll protection will probably be
   absent too, so budget more fodder than Korean guides imply.
3. **Fewer slots to fill.** No Brooch, Pendant or Seal at launch, which
   concentrates the plan onto weapon, armour and the launch accessories.
4. **The permanent-progress systems are all present, but several pay
   differently.** Traces, Monoliths, Garrisons, cubes, pets, Pantheon, wardrobe
   and titles were all in at the Korean launch. The test rerouted Skill Points
   off feathers, flattened Monolith stats, cut Ariel, and capped pets at 3.
   Front-load the one-time sweeps anyway; do not copy KR payout tables.
