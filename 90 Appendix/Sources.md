# Sources

Every note carries its own Sources section with the exact URLs used for that
system. This page explains how much each class of source is worth, so you know
how hard to trust a number before you spend materials on it.

## Trust tiers

### Tier 1 — primary

Official material. Treat as authoritative for the region and patch it describes.

- Official Aion 2 site and news for Korea and Taiwan, including patch notes.
  Patch notes are the only reliable record of when a system changed, which
  matters because guides rarely say which patch they describe.
- Official developer livestreams and their summaries.
- The global Aion 2 site and Steam page for anything launch-related.

### Tier 2 — data-driven fan sites

Built from extracted game data, so numbers are usually exact, but the extraction
can lag a patch behind and the interpretation can be wrong.

- [AION2 Hub](https://aion2hub.com/) — item and skill database, build planner
  covering gear, Arcana, pets, skills, Daevanion and Pantheon, the Gear Forge
  enchant calculator with per-level ladders and the pity ramp, interactive maps
  including all hidden cubes, crafting calculator, season and battle-pass
  timeline. The site states plainly that its pity model is read from data and
  not confirmed in game, which is the right level of caution to copy.
- [Aion 2 Wiki on Fextralife](https://aion2.wiki.fextralife.com/) — English
  wiki. Good for mechanics summaries, thin on current endgame numbers.

### Tier 3 — Korean and Taiwanese community guides in translation

The best source for how systems are actually played, including priorities that
no data dump can tell you. Watch for translation drift in system names.

- [Vortex Gaming](https://vortexgaming.io/en/) — English write-ups of Korean
  written guides and, importantly, of Korean YouTube guide videos, with the key
  points bulleted. Highest value per page for progression priorities.
- Korean community posts and YouTube guides, searched directly in Korean. See
  the search-pattern section of [[Glossary KR TW EN]].
- Taiwanese guides for the TW client, useful when KR and TW patches differ.

### Tier 4 — Western news and blog coverage

Mixed quality, useful for structure and for global-launch context.

- [mein-mmo.de](https://mein-mmo.de/en/) — has genuinely detailed equipment and
  progression articles.
- Aion2.Online, LDCloud and similar sites — readable overviews, verify numbers.

### Tier 5 — currency and boosting shops

Sites such as u4n, u4gm, mmoexp, aoeah, mmom, madden-sale, boostroom, freebook
and tribbble publish large volumes of AI-assisted SEO articles about Aion 2.
They are frequently the only English text on a niche system, so they are useful
for leads and for terminology, but they paraphrase Korean sources loosely,
garble names (Daevanion becomes "Deinian", Asphel becomes "Asfel") and sometimes
invent plausible-looking numbers.

Rule used throughout this vault: a number from a tier 5 source is either
corroborated by an independent source or marked `confidence: medium` or lower
with an inline caveat. Their sales pitches are ignored; buying currency breaks
the terms of service and is not a progression strategy.

## Regional context to keep in mind

- Korea and Taiwan launched on 19 November 2025 and have received many content
  updates since, so a guide from launch week can describe a system that no
  longer exists in that form.
- The global release is a later build. Some systems in this vault arrived in
  KR/TW months after launch, and global may ship with them already present, or
  hold them back for its own roadmap. [[Global Launch Differences]] tracks this.
- Where a note gives a number, its frontmatter `patch` field says which patch or
  season the number came from.
