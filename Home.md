## The mental model

Aion 2 splits "power" into three things that are easy to confuse:

| Concept | What it is | What it does for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item level | Sum of equipped gear, upgrades and socketed stones | The key that unlocks content. Dungeons and raids check it. |
| Combat Power | Official metric weighing stats, skills, passives, cards | Your real strength. Groups screen you on it. |
| Permanent progress | Collections, pets, Pantheon, traces, wardrobe, titles | Never lost and never re-earned, so it compounds. |

Two traps worth knowing before you spend anything. First, item level opens doors
but does not win fights, so farming it alone leaves you benched by other players
rather than by the game. Second, "permanent" does not mean "shared" — most of
these systems are per character, and Korea has explicitly refused to make the
Pantheon account-wide, so check [[Alts and Account Wide Progression]] before
building a roster. See [[Item Level vs Combat Power]].

A terminology warning that will save you real materials: Korean guides written
before 2026-03-18 use 전투력 to mean **item level**, and guides after that date
use it for the new official Combat Power stat, which is roughly a hundred times
larger. A guide telling you to reach 2,000 and one telling you to reach 500K can
both be current and correct.

## How to use this vault

1. Read [[Progression Phases Roadmap]], then your class playbook.
2. Keep [[Daily Routine Main]] open while you play, and check
   [[Odyle Energy and Entry Limits]] before spending a reward claim.
3. When you unlock a new system, open its note and read "Priorities and
   breakpoints" first, then "Traps and mistakes".
4. Treat every number as provisional until the global client confirms it. Notes
   carry `confidence` and `patch` frontmatter, and every claim traces to a
   source listed at the bottom of the note, so the research can be re-run and
   updated rather than redone.

## Conventions

- Global English term first, Korean term in parentheses on first use.
- `region_verified` says which live server the information comes from.
- `confidence: low` means a single unverified source, usually an SEO blog.
- Item grades are stated with their Korean term, because three different English
  vocabularies are in circulation for the same tiers. See [[Glossary KR TW EN]].
